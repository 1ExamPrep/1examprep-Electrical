## Licensing Steps

The New Jersey Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors provides application options for Class A journeyman electrician licensing, Class A electrical apprentice licensing, Class B wireman licensing, and electrical contractor licensing. The proper path depends on the applicant’s experience, work role, and professional goal.

A Class A journeyman electrician applicant should review the Board’s current application instructions and gather the required documentation. This may include work certification, proof of electrical experience, apprenticeship or training documentation, and other materials required by the Board. Proper documentation matters because New Jersey uses the application record to determine whether the applicant meets the credential requirements.

Applicants should complete the correct Board application and submit all required supporting forms. Incomplete or inconsistent work history can delay the process. Applicants pursuing electrical contractor licensing follow a separate path. Contractor applicants must apply to the Board, receive approval to test, and complete the required PSI examination components. Electrical contractor licensing involves a higher level of responsibility and is separate from holding a journeyman-level worker credential.

After the Board reviews and approves an application, the applicant completes any remaining licensing, registration, or examination steps required for the credential. A strong career plan begins early. Students should keep records of supervised work, training, classroom hours, employment verification, and continuing education as applicable.
