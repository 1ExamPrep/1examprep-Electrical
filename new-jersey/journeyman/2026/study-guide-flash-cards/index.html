Exam Details

New Jersey electrician licensing is handled through the New Jersey Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors under the Division of Consumer Affairs. The Board licenses and regulates electrical contractors, registers or licenses qualified journeyman electricians, and maintains application materials for electrical contractor examination, Class A journeyman electrician licensing, Class A apprentice electrician registration, and Class B wireman licensing.

The New Jersey Class A journeyman electrician license is not the same as the New Jersey electrical contractor license. The Class A journeyman electrician credential is focused on an individual’s qualification to perform journeyman-level electrical work and supervise certain electrical work under New Jersey rules. The electrical contractor license is the credential connected to operating as an electrical contractor and applying for a business permit.

New Jersey application materials for the Class A journeyman electrician license emphasize documentation of practical electrical experience and related classroom instruction. The state’s published application process for electrical contractor examination is separate and includes PSI-administered computer-based examinations for applicants approved by the Board to take the electrical contractor licensing examination.

Licensing Steps

New Jersey Class A journeyman electrician licensing begins with meeting the qualification requirements established by the New Jersey Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors. Applicants use the Board’s Class A journeyman electrician application process and must provide the required documentation for experience and related classroom instruction.

New Jersey published materials identify 8,000 hours of practical experience working with tools in the installation, alteration, or repair of wiring for electric light, heat, or power. The state also identifies a minimum of 576 classroom hours of related instruction. At least 4,000 hours of the practical experience must be within five years of the application date.

A typical New Jersey journeyman path includes registering or working under the appropriate apprentice or employee status, completing supervised electrical work, maintaining accurate records of job experience, completing related classroom instruction, obtaining employer verification, and submitting the required application materials through the Board’s process.

Applicants should keep detailed records of employment dates, job duties, hours worked, supervision, employer information, classroom instruction, apprenticeship completion, and any supporting documentation required by the Board. New Jersey application materials include work certification forms for Class A journeyman applicants, and incomplete or unsupported documentation can delay the application process.

Electrical contractor licensing is a separate advancement path in New Jersey. A person seeking to become a licensed electrical contractor must meet the Board’s contractor licensing requirements and, when approved, take the PSI-administered electrical contractor licensing examination. The contractor license should not be confused with the Class A journeyman electrician license.
