Exam Details

The New Jersey Electrical Contractor Licensure Examination is administered through PSI for the New Jersey State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors. Candidates must receive authorization from the Board before taking the initial examination. Once approved, candidates receive an Examination Eligibility Notice with instructions for paying for and scheduling the examination through PSI.

To qualify for licensure as a New Jersey electrical contractor, candidates must pass the Electrical Contractor examination, the Alarm Systems Electrical Contractor examination, and the Business and Law examination. Candidates taking the examination for the first time under the current procedure are required to take all three examinations on the same day. If a candidate fails one or more portions, only the failed portion or portions must be retaken, subject to the retesting rules in the candidate instructions.

The Electrical Contractor examination includes 100 questions, requires a 70% passing score, and allows 260 minutes of testing time. The examination scope includes the business of contracting to install, erect, repair, or alter electrical equipment used for the generation, transmission, or utilization of electrical energy. The Business and Law examination includes 50 questions, requires a 70% passing score, and allows 130 minutes of testing time.

Electrical trade exam preparation should include knowledge of services, feeders, branch circuits, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, transformers, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, communication systems, and electrical safety. Candidates should also be ready for electrical calculations that require accurate formula use, table navigation, and careful reading.

Open Book Test

The New Jersey Electrical Contractor examination is an open book test. The current candidate instructions identify the National Electrical Code reference allowed for the Electrical Contractor examination and state that the NEC reference is provided at the test center. Candidates may not use their own copy of the NEC reference book during that examination.

Open-book testing does not mean the exam is easy. Electrical contractor exams require candidates to locate code material quickly, read the correct section carefully, understand the question being asked, and apply the requirement accurately. A candidate who has not practiced with the NEC may lose valuable time searching through unrelated sections.

Reference rules matter. The testing program may restrict writing, highlighting, underlining, indexing, loose papers, attached papers, and personal copies of references depending on the examination being taken. Candidates should follow the current PSI candidate bulletin for the exact NEC edition, supplied-reference rules, allowed materials, identification requirements, calculator rules, and exam-day procedures.

Licensing Steps

New Jersey electrical contractor licensing is handled through the New Jersey State Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors. A typical licensing path includes reviewing the Board’s eligibility requirements, completing the application process, submitting required documentation, receiving authorization to test, scheduling the examinations through PSI, preparing with code-based study materials, passing the required examinations, and completing final licensing or business permit requirements with the Board.

The application process should be completed carefully. The Board reviews the applicant’s documentation before authorizing the initial examination. Candidates should keep records organized, including work experience forms, application materials, education or training documentation when applicable, Board correspondence, examination eligibility notice, PSI scheduling confirmation, identification information, score results, and final licensing materials.

After approval from the Board, the candidate is responsible for contacting PSI to pay for and schedule the required examination. PSI administers the computer-based examination process and provides score results after completion. Results are transmitted to the Board as part of the licensing process.

Candidates approved under the current examination process must pass the required examination portions within the limits stated in the candidate instructions. Retesting rules, waiting periods, and reapplication requirements may apply when a candidate does not pass all required examinations within the allowed attempts or timeframe.
