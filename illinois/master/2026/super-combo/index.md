Illinois electrical licensing is local rather than statewide. Candidates should identify the city, village, county, or local authority where they need to be licensed before selecting an exam path. For candidates working in Chicago, the City of Chicago Department of Buildings issues the Supervising Electrician License. A licensed supervising electrician has demonstrated knowledge of the installation, alteration, repair, and maintenance of electrical equipment and apparatus under the Chicago Electrical Code. A licensed electrical contractor in Chicago must be owned by or employ at least one licensed supervising electrician, and electrical work performed by the contractor must be performed by or under the supervision and direction of a licensed supervising electrician.

The Chicago Supervising Electrician licensure examination is administered through Continental Testing Services. It is an open-book examination based on the Chicago Electrical Code reference specified for the exam. The Chicago exam is an important example of how Illinois electrical licensing works at the local level: the license is not issued by the State of Illinois as a statewide master electrician license, but by the local authority that regulates electrical work in that jurisdiction.

Outside Chicago, Illinois municipalities may set their own licensing requirements and examination rules. Some jurisdictions may recognize licenses from other Illinois municipalities, while others may require a local exam, local registration, local contractor licensing, insurance documentation, bonding, permit registration, or other municipal requirements. Candidates should prepare for NEC-based technical questions while also confirming the exact local requirements for the place where they intend to work.
