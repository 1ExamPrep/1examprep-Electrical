Exam Details

Illinois does not use one single statewide journeyman electrician exam for every candidate. Licensing Steps

Illinois journeyman electrician licensing steps depend on the local authority that issues or recognizes the credential. A candidate may need to apply through a city building department, county office, village licensing division, local electrical commission, or other municipal authority. Because Illinois does not issue one statewide journeyman electrician license, candidates should identify the local jurisdiction first.

A typical Illinois journeyman electrician path may include completing electrical apprenticeship or supervised work experience, gathering employer verification, collecting training documentation, submitting a local application, paying required local fees, scheduling an approved journeyman-level exam, passing the required examination, and completing the remaining local licensing or registration steps.

Some jurisdictions may require a certain number of years of field experience, apprenticeship completion, proof of employment under a licensed electrical contractor, continuing education, identification documents, or a local sponsor. Requirements can vary, so candidates should organize documents based on the local application instructions.
