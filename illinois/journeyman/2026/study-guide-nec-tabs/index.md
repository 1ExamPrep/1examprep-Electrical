## Exam Details

Illinois does not administer one statewide journeyman electrician examination for individual electricians. Instead, electrician licensing and examination requirements are commonly determined by the city, county, or municipality where the work is performed. This means an Illinois candidate may encounter different requirements depending on the local authority handling the license or registration.

Chicago is one of the best-known Illinois examples. The City of Chicago issues a Supervising Electrician License through the Department of Buildings. A licensed supervising electrician has demonstrated knowledge of the installation, alteration, repair, and maintenance of electrical equipment and apparatus under the Chicago Electrical Code. A licensed electrical contractor in Chicago must be owned by or employ at least one licensed supervising electrician, and electrical work performed by the contractor must be performed by or under the supervision and direction of a licensed supervising electrician.

The Chicago Supervising Electrician examination is administered through Continental Testing Services. The examination is open book and is based on the Chicago Electrical Code. The Chicago exam is not the same as a statewide Illinois journeyman electrician exam, but it is an important local licensing example for electrical candidates working in Illinois.

Other Illinois municipalities may have their own license categories, local registration rules, examination providers, reference books, application requirements, and renewal procedures. Some jurisdictions may recognize licenses from certain nearby municipalities, while others may require a separate application, local exam, business registration, or proof of experience. Candidates should start with the local building department, electrical commission, or licensing office for the jurisdiction where they plan to work.
