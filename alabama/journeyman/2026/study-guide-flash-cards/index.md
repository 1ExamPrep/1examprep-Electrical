## Exam Details

The Alabama Electrical Journeyman examination is administered through Prov for licensing through the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board. The current Alabama Electrical Contractor Board candidate bulletin lists the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam as an open-book, timed, computer-administered exam.

The Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam contains 80 questions, and the time allowed is 4 hours. The candidate bulletin lists a passing score of 75%. Test results are scored by Prov and sent to the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board, and the Board mails the score to the candidate.

The published Alabama Electrical Journeyman subject areas include conductors and cables, electrical power, fire detection and alarm systems, general electrical knowledge, grounding and bonding, illuminated signs, lighting, low voltage, motors, overcurrent protection, raceways and boxes, safety information, service, feeders, branch circuits, special occupancies and equipment, wiring and protection, and wiring methods and materials.

## Licensing Steps

Alabama journeyman electrician licensing begins with the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board. A candidate seeking the statewide Journeyman examination must submit the required application and documentation to the Board. Applications are reviewed by the Board, and testing eligibility is issued after approval.

For the statewide Journeyman Electrician examination, Alabama application materials state that applicants must document a minimum of 8,000 hours of experience showing the qualifications, training, and technical knowledge needed to install electrical wiring, apparatus, or equipment for light, heat, or power. Work affidavits are used to document qualifying experience, and applicants cannot certify their own electrical hours.

After approval, the candidate schedules the examination with Prov. The candidate bulletin states that the candidate will be eligible to take the exam within 90 days of the approval date. Candidates who fail the exam or fail to test before the end of the 90-day eligibility period must submit a retake form and an additional examination fee to the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board.

Students can use it before applying, while gathering documentation, after receiving approval to test, or during final exam review.
