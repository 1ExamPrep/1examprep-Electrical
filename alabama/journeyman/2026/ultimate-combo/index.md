## Licensing Steps

Alabama statewide journeyman electrician licenses are issued by examination through the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board. Applicants for the statewide journeyman examination must document a minimum of 8,000 hours of qualifying experience showing the training, qualifications, and technical knowledge needed to install electrical wiring, apparatus, or equipment for light, heat, or power.

The Board allows an applicant to substitute one year of education in an approved electrical curriculum or apprenticeship program for 1,000 hours of electrical experience, up to a maximum substitution of 2,000 hours toward the 8,000-hour requirement. The applicant must provide appropriate documentation of the education or apprenticeship program, subject to Board approval.

Applicants submit the examination application, required supporting documents, fee payment, and employment affidavits or apprenticeship documentation. Employment affidavits must be signed and notarized by the employer or employers and must support the applicant’s qualifying electrical experience. The Board application materials also require related tax records or appropriate documentation supporting the employment history.

Once the Board approves the application, the candidate receives scheduling information. The candidate may then schedule the exam through the testing process identified by the Board. Candidates should follow the Board’s instructions carefully, submit complete documentation, and keep copies of all application materials for their own records.

## State Requirements

The Alabama Electrical Contractors Board is the state board responsible for the statewide journeyman electrician examination process. Alabama law requires individuals who engage in electrical contracting business or perform work as an Electrical Contractor or Electrical Journeyman to be licensed.

For the statewide journeyman examination, the Board’s application materials identify 8,000 hours of qualifying experience as the minimum experience requirement. The experience must show the applicant has the qualifications, training, and technical knowledge to install electrical wiring, apparatus, or equipment for light, heat, or power. The Board also requires supporting documents, including notarized employment affidavits or qualifying apprenticeship documentation.

Application materials must be complete, signed, notarized where required, and submitted with the required fee payment. The Board states that complete materials must be received by the Board office by the posted deadline for consideration at the next Board meeting. Candidates should review the current Board application packet before submitting paperwork because forms, deadlines, and administrative instructions may change.

The Alabama journeyman electrician license path is documentation-based as well as exam-based. A candidate needs to prepare for the test and also organize employment records, affidavits, education documents when used, identification documentation, and all Board-required forms.
