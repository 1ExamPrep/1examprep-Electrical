## Open Book Test

The Alabama Electrical Contractors Board examination bulletin states that all exams administered by Prov for the Board are open book and timed. The Alabama master-level electrical examination is therefore an open book test. Candidates may use authorized references during the examination, and Prov recommends that candidates become familiar with the allowed reference materials before test day.

Open book testing still requires serious preparation. The candidate must know how to move through the NEC, identify the correct article, interpret code language, and apply the rule to the question. A candidate who has not practiced reference navigation may spend too much time searching and not enough time answering. ## Licensing Steps

The Alabama master electrician licensing path begins with the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board. An applicant must complete the appropriate application with the Board before testing. Applications are reviewed by the Board, and the Board posts application deadlines online. Once the applicant has been approved for testing, the Board notifies the candidate that they are eligible to contact Prov to schedule the examination.

After approval, the candidate is eligible to take the examination within 90 days of the approval date. Candidates who fail the exam or who do not test within the eligibility period must reapply for testing according to the Board’s retesting process. Prov is not authorized to decide which examination a candidate must take, so license classification questions should be resolved directly with the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board before scheduling.

The examination is administered by computer at a Prov testing center. Candidates schedule the approved exam, bring required identification, follow test-day rules, and use only approved items and authorized references. Prov’s scoring system grades the exam immediately upon completion and automatically forwards the results to the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board. The Board mails the candidate’s score.

After passing, the candidate must contact the Board and apply for the license. Candidates who do not pass must contact the Board to reapply for testing. A practical path includes reviewing eligibility, submitting the correct application, receiving Board approval, scheduling through Prov, preparing with the exam outline and allowed reference rules, taking the examination, and completing the Board’s licensing steps after passing.
