The statewide electrical contractor requirement includes **8,000 hours** of experience in the overall design, planning, layout, and direct supervision of electrical construction activities and the soliciting and installing of electrical power or control systems.

The scope of licensing for an electrical contractor includes installation, design, planning, layout, and direct supervision of electrical construction activities. The supervised work may be commercial, industrial, or residential. Alabama’s electrical contractor license is unlimited in voltage and bid requirements.

Alabama application materials allow qualified electrical education to substitute for a portion of the required electrical experience, up to the maximum allowed by the Board. Experience documentation must be supported through the Board’s required forms and records.
