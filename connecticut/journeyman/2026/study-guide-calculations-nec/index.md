## Licensing Steps

Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson candidates begin by completing the required training or equivalent experience path. The E-2 license is the journeyperson-level unlimited electrical license and is generally connected to completion of a registered apprenticeship program or approved equivalent experience and training.

After meeting the required training and experience qualifications, the applicant submits the appropriate journeyperson application materials to the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection. The application should include the documentation required for the license type requested. Applicants should make sure work experience, classroom instruction, apprenticeship completion, or equivalent training records are complete and accurate.

Once approved for testing, the candidate schedules the E-2 exam through PSI. The exam is computer-based and is taken according to PSI’s testing procedures. Candidates should review the exam bulletin, scheduling rules, identification requirements, and reference rules before test day.

After passing the examination, the candidate follows the licensing instructions provided through the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection. The license process is not complete until all required licensing steps, fees, and documentation are satisfied. Candidates who do not pass must follow the applicable retesting procedures before another attempt.

A strong licensing plan begins early. Candidates should maintain accurate apprenticeship and employment records, complete required classroom instruction, review the E-2 scope of work, and begin study before the exam is scheduled. Electrical exam preparation is usually strongest when it is built over time through repeated topic review, calculations practice, and code navigation.

## State Requirements

Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson applicants must meet the state’s training and experience requirements for the license. The E-2 path is commonly tied to **8,000 hours** of on-the-job training and **720 hours** of classroom instruction, or approved equivalent experience and training accepted for the license classification.

The E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license allows the holder to do the same electrical work as an E-1 Unlimited Electrical Contractor, but only while employed by a contractor licensed for that work. This distinction matters because the E-2 is a journeyperson credential, while the E-1 is the contractor-level license.

Connecticut recognizes multiple electrical license categories, including unlimited and limited classifications. Students should make sure they are preparing for the correct license type. The E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license is different from limited journeyperson classifications and from the E-1 Unlimited Electrical Contractor license.

Applicants using equivalent experience or an equivalent out-of-state license must satisfy the Connecticut requirements for the license classification. Equivalent experience and training must match the classroom and on-the-job training requirements for the license type being requested. Documentation is important because the state reviews whether the applicant’s training and experience meet the required standard.
