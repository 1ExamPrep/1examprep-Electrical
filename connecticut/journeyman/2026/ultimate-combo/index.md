## Open Book Test

The Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson exam is an open book examination. Candidates are responsible for bringing approved reference materials to the examination center. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, annotated, and indexed before the exam session. References may not be written in during the examination session.

Permanent tabs and indexes are allowed when they are part of the approved reference setup. Temporary tabs, such as Post-it notes, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins. Candidates are not permitted to bring additional papers, loose notes, attached notes, or unapproved materials with their references. Writing questions and answers in reference materials is strictly prohibited.

This makes preparation with a tabbed NEC especially valuable. The included National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback with Tabs is useful for building familiarity with code layout, article structure, tables, definitions, wiring methods, grounding and bonding rules, service requirements, overcurrent protection rules, motor provisions, photovoltaic provisions, and other NEC areas that may appear in journeyperson preparation.

Open book does not mean easy. Candidates still need to know where to look, how to interpret code language, and how to apply the correct rule to the question. The best study approach is to practice identifying the subject of each question first, then locating the relevant code article, table, or rule efficiently.

## State Requirements

The Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license allows the holder to perform electrical work as defined by Connecticut law while employed by a properly licensed contractor. To qualify for the E-2 examination, candidates must complete a registered apprenticeship program or have at least four years of equivalent experience and training.

Connecticut’s E-2 pathway commonly involves 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and 720 classroom hours. Applicants using equivalent experience and training must be prepared to document that their background meets the license requirements for the category they are applying for.

Connecticut uses a license classification system for electrical work. The E-1 license is the unlimited electrical contractor license, while the E-2 license is the unlimited electrical journeyperson license. Limited electrical categories also exist for specialized work, but this product is written for the Connecticut journeyperson electrician path associated with the E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson examination.

Passing the examination is one part of the licensing process.
