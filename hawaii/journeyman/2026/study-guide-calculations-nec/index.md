## Licensing Steps

Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician candidates begin by meeting the experience and academic coursework requirements established by Hawaii law and the Board of Electricians and Plumbers. The applicant must document qualifying electrical experience and completion of the required electrical academic coursework before the Board can approve the application for examination.

The candidate submits the application for license to the Professional and Vocational Licensing Division with the required supporting documents. Experience verification must be completed and notarized by each licensed electrician under whom the applicant received experience or training. If experience was earned in a jurisdiction that does not require licensure as a journey worker, supervising, or master electrician and only requires a contractor license, the Board may accept a notarized experience verification form signed by the licensed contractor with verification of the contractor license.

The application must also include verification of completion of the required electrical academic coursework. Hawaii accepts documentation such as a diploma, certificate of completion, certificate of competence, certificate of achievement, transcript with completion information, a letter from the registrar of a University of Hawaii Community College, or a certificate of completion from an approved apprenticeship program when applicable.

After the Board reviews and approves the application, the candidate receives approval to register for the PSI examination. The candidate then schedules the exam through PSI, follows the computer-based testing procedures, and reports to the exam site with the required identification and testing documents.

After testing, the candidate receives score information according to PSI procedures. Passing the exam is part of the licensing process, but the license process is not complete until all Board requirements are satisfied. Candidates should follow the Professional and Vocational Licensing Division’s instructions for final licensing steps after examination.

A strong licensing plan begins early. Candidates should keep accurate employment records, obtain required notarized experience verification forms, complete the required academic coursework, review the exam outline, and build a study routine that includes trade review, calculation practice, and NEC navigation.

## State Requirements

Hawaii requires a license before a person may act as a journey worker electrician, journey worker specialty electrician, supervising electrician, supervising specialty electrician, journey worker industrial electrician, supervising industrial electrician, or maintenance electrician. A person may not hold themselves out as an electrician without the proper license under Hawaii’s electrician licensing law.

For the Journey Worker Electrician license, Hawaii requires the applicant to be at least **18 years old** and provide satisfactory evidence of experience in residential or commercial wiring of at least **5 years full-time** or its equivalent, but not less than **10,000 hours**, in the trade under the supervision of a journey worker or supervising electrician.

Hawaii also requires satisfactory completion of **240 hours** of electrical academic coursework accepted by a University of Hawaii Community College offering an appropriate program of study. This academic coursework requirement applies to journey worker electrician applicants and must be documented as part of the application process.

The Journey Worker Electrician license is different from a contractor license. Hawaii’s requirements state that an electrician’s license does not allow a person to contract to perform electrical work. A contractor’s license is required for contracting activity. Students should understand the difference between being licensed as an individual electrician and operating as an electrical contractor.

Hawaii does not reciprocate with any jurisdiction for these electrician licenses. Applicants with out-of-state experience or licenses must still follow Hawaii’s application rules, submit required verification, and be qualified by the Board before testing.
