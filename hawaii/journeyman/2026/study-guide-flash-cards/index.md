## Exam Details

The Hawaii Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Professional and Vocational Licensing Division, Board of Electricians and Plumbers oversees electrician licensing in Hawaii. PSI Services LLC administers the Hawaii electrician examinations for the state licensing program.

The Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam contains 70 questions. The minimum passing score is 70%, and the time allowed is 180 minutes. The exam is administered by computer through PSI after the candidate has been approved by the Board. Candidates are not allowed to register for the examination until the Board approves the application and sends the required approval notice.

The Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam content outline includes General Electrical Knowledge, Service, Feeders, and Branch Circuits, Grounding and Bonding, Conductors and Cables, Raceways and Boxes, Special Occupancies, Conditions, and Equipment, Electrical Power, Motors, and Equipment for General Use, Low Voltage and Communication Circuits, Lighting, and Safety Information.

The examination may also include a small number of experimental questions that are not scored. These questions are used for future exam development and do not count against the candidate’s score. Candidates receive score information at the end of the examination, and unsuccessful candidates receive diagnostic information showing strengths and weaknesses by examination type.

## Licensing Steps

Hawaii electrician licensing is handled through the Board of Electricians and Plumbers under the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs Professional and Vocational Licensing Division. Candidates must meet the Board’s qualification requirements before being approved to take the Journey Worker Electrician exam.

For the Journey Worker Electrician classification, Hawaii requires applicants to be at least 18 years old and provide satisfactory evidence of residential or commercial wiring experience of at least five years full-time, or its equivalent, but not less than 10,000 hours in the trade under the supervision of a journey worker electrician or supervising electrician.

Hawaii also requires approved electrical academic coursework for the Journey Worker Electrician pathway. Candidates should follow the Board’s current application instructions for acceptable coursework, experience verification, forms, fees, documentation, and exam approval.

After the applicant submits the required application materials, the Board reviews the application. Candidates may not schedule the exam until the Board approves the application and the candidate receives approval to test. Once approved, the candidate schedules the exam through PSI and pays the examination fee.

After passing the required exam and completing the remaining licensing requirements, the candidate follows the Board’s instructions for license issuance. The exam is part of the licensing process, but passing the exam alone does not replace the state application, experience documentation, academic coursework documentation, or final licensing steps.

Students can use it while completing apprenticeship training, while documenting work hours, after submitting an application, after receiving Board approval, or during final review before the scheduled exam date.
