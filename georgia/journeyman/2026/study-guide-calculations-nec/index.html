Licensing Steps

Electricians in Georgia who want to advance toward independent contracting should begin by reviewing the Georgia State Board of Electrical Contractors license classifications. The main electrical contractor classifications include Class I Restricted and Class II Unrestricted. Class I is limited in scope, while Class II is the broader unrestricted electrical contractor classification.

The applicant must complete the proper application through the Georgia Secretary of State licensing process. The Board reviews the application and determines whether the applicant is approved to sit for the examination. Candidates should provide complete, accurate documentation and follow all instructions for the license classification they are seeking.

After approval, the candidate registers and schedules the examination through PSI. The testing process is controlled by the current candidate bulletin. Candidates should review exam scheduling, identification requirements, reference rules, calculator rules, testing center procedures, and score reporting before exam day.

After passing the required examination, the applicant continues through the licensing process as directed by the Board. Passing an exam is not the same as automatically receiving a license. The applicant must satisfy all licensing requirements, submit required documentation, and complete the Board’s process.
