The main electrical contractor classifications include Class I Restricted and Class II Unrestricted. The applicant must complete the proper application through the Georgia Secretary of State licensing process. After approval, the candidate registers and schedules the examination through PSI. The testing process is controlled by the current candidate bulletin.
