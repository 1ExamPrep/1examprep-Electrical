Open Book Test

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam is an open book test. Candidates taking that exam may use approved reference materials during testing, subject to the exam provider’s rules. Open book testing requires strong code-navigation skills because candidates must find information quickly, understand the code language, and apply it correctly under timed conditions.

Open book does not mean the exam is simple. The National Electrical Code is a detailed technical reference, and a candidate who is unfamiliar with its structure can lose valuable time searching through unrelated sections. Licensing Steps

Georgia electrical licensing depends on the level of credential being pursued. Electrical contractors are licensed at the state level through the Georgia State Board of Electrical Contractors. Journeyman-level recognition may be connected to local requirements, apprenticeship completion, employer standards, or a recognized journeyman-level examination.

A candidate preparing for a local Georgia journeyman electrician credential generally begins by identifying the jurisdiction or organization requiring the credential. The candidate then gathers work history, apprenticeship or training documentation, employer verification, identification, and any required forms.
