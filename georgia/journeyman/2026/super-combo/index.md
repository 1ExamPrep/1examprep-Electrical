## Licensing Steps

The Georgia electrical career path commonly begins with apprenticeship or supervised electrical work under a licensed electrical contractor. Georgia does not require an individual statewide journeyman electrician license to work as an electrician under a licensed contractor, but apprentices and developing electricians still need strong journeyman-level trade knowledge to perform safe and code-compliant work.

A practical study and career path begins with gaining supervised field experience and classroom training. Many apprentices complete a structured apprenticeship program that combines on-the-job training with electrical classroom instruction. That training helps build the foundation needed for journeyman-level work and later contractor-level licensing preparation.

For electricians who want to become licensed electrical contractors in Georgia, the next step is selecting the correct license classification. Georgia electrical contractor applicants apply through the Georgia Secretary of State’s licensing process. The state accepts online applications through the GOALS portal. After the Board approves the application, the candidate receives approval to sit for the examination and follows PSI scheduling instructions. The candidate then schedules the approved exam, studies the current exam content and reference list, takes the examination, and follows the Board’s remaining licensing instructions after passing.
