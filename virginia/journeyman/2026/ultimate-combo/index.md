## Open Book Test

The Virginia tradesman examination rules allow candidates to bring reference books. Study guides and sample examinations are not allowed in the exam room. Reference books may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam, but they must otherwise be unmarked and not written in. Open-book testing does not mean the exam is easy.
