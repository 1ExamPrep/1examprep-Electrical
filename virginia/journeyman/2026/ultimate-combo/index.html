Exam Details

The Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation Board for Contractors regulates individual tradesmen in electrical, plumbing, HVAC, gas fitting, and other trades. For Virginia tradesman licensing exams, candidates apply for eligibility through the Board before testing, and PSI administers the examination program. The exam is administered by computer, with one question displayed at a time and a score report provided after the exam.

Open Book Test

The Virginia tradesman examination rules allow candidates to bring reference books. Study guides and sample examinations are not allowed in the exam room. Reference books may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam, but they must otherwise be unmarked and not written in. This makes preparation with the correct reference material and clean code navigation habits especially important.

Open-book testing does not mean the exam is easy. It means candidates must know how to locate information quickly, understand what the question is asking, and apply the correct rule under time pressure. A candidate who has not practiced with the code book may spend too much time searching and not enough time answering. The National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback with Tabs included in this combo helps students practice finding articles, tables, definitions, exceptions, and common electrical rules before exam day.

The best open-book preparation includes repeated code lookup practice. Students should learn the structure of the NEC, become familiar with commonly tested articles, and practice moving from a question to the correct code section. The included study guides, flash cards, and online test success kit help reinforce that process by combining written review, quick recall, and practice-oriented question work.

Licensing Steps

Virginia journeyman electrician candidates begin by reviewing the current tradesman application instructions and confirming that their education and work experience meet the Board for Contractors requirements. The Board approves applications for testing, and candidates must receive eligibility before scheduling the exam. Eligibility is issued through the licensing process and is used when registering for the examination.

A practical path begins with gathering experience documentation, training records, and any required application materials. Candidates then submit the Virginia tradesman exam and license application for the electrical classification. After the Board approves the application, the candidate registers for the examination through PSI and selects an available testing option. PSI provides computer-based testing procedures, score reporting, and scheduling support.

After passing the exam, candidates complete the remaining licensing steps required by Virginia. Candidates should also keep renewal requirements in mind after licensure, including continuing education obligations for active journeyman and master tradesman licenses. State Requirements

Virginia regulates individual tradesmen through the Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation Board for Contractors. Electrical tradesmen must hold the appropriate credential before performing regulated electrical work in the Commonwealth. The state’s tradesman licensing structure includes electrical licensing, and candidates must satisfy the Board’s education, experience, application, examination, and licensing requirements.

Virginia’s tradesman application process requires candidates to use the correct application for individuals seeking electrical, plumbing, HVAC, or gas fitting licensure. The application process is separate from business contractor licensing. A journeyman electrician candidate should focus on the individual tradesman license requirements, not a contractor business license, unless they are also pursuing a separate contractor license for a business entity.

Electrical candidates should also be aware that local ordinances or other requirements may apply depending on where they plan to work. State licensure is a major requirement, but local rules may affect work practices, permitting, inspection procedures, or business operations. Strong exam preparation helps candidates build the technical foundation needed for safe and code-compliant electrical work throughout the licensing process.
