Exam Details

The Virginia Master Electrician examination is administered through PSI for the Commonwealth of Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation. Virginia DPOR’s Board for Contractors approves applications for tradesman examinations, and candidates must receive approval before scheduling the exam with PSI.

The current Virginia PSI candidate bulletin lists the Master Electrician portion as a 90-item exam with 270 minutes to complete the portion. The listed passing score is 63. The exam outline includes both regulatory and electrical trade knowledge areas, which means preparation should cover Virginia tradesman rules as well as practical electrical work and National Electrical Code subject matter.

Major Master Electrician exam outline areas include standards of practice, standards of conduct, renewal and reinstatement, definitions, qualification for licensure, revocation of licensure, general electrical knowledge, requirements for electrical installations, electrical-electronic fundamentals, services, feeders, branch circuits, service and feeder requirements, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies, special equipment, electrical signs and outline lighting, motors and controls, utilization equipment, lighting, general low voltage requirements, communication systems, and fire detection and alarm systems.

Because the Master Electrician exam covers both broad electrical knowledge and targeted code-based topics, students benefit from studying in layers. First, review the exam outline and identify high-value subject areas. Next, build code navigation skills in the NEC. Then, work through calculation practice until the formulas and steps become familiar. Finally, review weaker areas repeatedly rather than spending all study time on topics that already feel comfortable.

Open Book Test

The Virginia tradesman candidate bulletin states that the examinations are open book. Candidates must bring their own references because references are not provided at the test site. The bulletin also states that no study guides are permitted in the examination room, no loose papers or unbound references are permitted, and reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, or indexed before the exam.

Reference materials containing writing are not allowed into the examination. Candidates may not write in reference materials during the examination session. Permanent tabs are allowed, while removable tabs are not allowed. These rules make preparation with your books especially important before exam day. A strong study routine should include locating articles, reading definitions carefully, practicing with the index, and becoming comfortable moving between related NEC sections.

The study guides in this combo are intended for preparation before the exam. They help you organize study time, review key subject areas, and practice concepts. Licensing Steps

Virginia master electrician licensing is handled through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Board for Contractors. Candidates applying for a tradesman license must follow the Board’s application process, meet the required education and work experience requirements, receive approval, and then schedule the applicable examination through PSI.

A practical preparation path begins with confirming the correct Virginia tradesman application for the license type being pursued. The applicant then submits the required application materials to DPOR. After the Board reviews and approves the application for testing, the candidate receives authorization to schedule the examination. Once approved, the candidate schedules through PSI, prepares using the exam outline and approved reference rules, sits for the exam, and follows DPOR instructions for completing the licensing process.

Students preparing for the Virginia Master Electrician exam should keep their study plan aligned with the exam outline. The outline is useful because it shows the major content categories and the number of items assigned to each subject area. State Requirements

Virginia regulates tradesman licensing through DPOR and the Board for Contractors. Electrical tradesman applicants must follow the Board’s requirements for the license designation they are pursuing. The Board requires pre-approval before candidates sit for tradesman exams, and applicants must satisfy the required education and work experience standards before applying for the examination.

Virginia also requires continuing education for active tradesman license renewal. For electrical tradesmen, the continuing education requirement is three hours and must cover a subject directly related to the current edition of the National Electrical Code. Continuing education must be completed through a Board-approved provider and must be finished before the license expiration date.

For exam preparation, students should understand that state licensing requirements and exam preparation are connected but not identical. Licensing requirements determine whether a candidate is eligible to apply, while exam preparation focuses on the knowledge needed to sit for the test.
