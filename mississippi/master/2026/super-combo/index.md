## Licensing Steps

Mississippi electrical contractor licensing is handled through the Mississippi State Board of Contractors. A practical licensing path begins by identifying the correct classification and confirming whether the intended work requires a state contractor license, a local credential, or both. Candidates should review the Board’s current application instructions before beginning the testing process.

Before taking Mississippi contractor examinations, applicants submit a license application to the Mississippi State Board of Contractors. After the application is received, the Board reviews the file, notifies the applicant of missing requirements when needed, sends testing information from the PSI Candidate Information Bulletin, and notifies PSI of the applicant’s eligibility to test. The applicant then receives scheduling instructions from PSI.

Candidates should not attempt to schedule until the eligibility process is complete. After approval, the candidate schedules the required examination through PSI, prepares using the correct references and study materials, completes the examination, and follows the Board’s remaining licensing steps. Depending on the license type, the process may include business documentation, classification selection, financial information, insurance requirements, fees, and renewal responsibilities.

For candidates pursuing a local master electrician credential, the licensing steps may differ. Local jurisdictions may set their own requirements for experience, applications, testing, renewals, permits, or approval. Candidates should follow the instructions of the local authority connected to their work location.
