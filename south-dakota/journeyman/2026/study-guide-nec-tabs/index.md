## Exam Details

The South Dakota journeyman electrician licensing process is overseen by the South Dakota Electrical Commission. Before taking an electrical examination, applicants must meet the requirements for the license they are pursuing and submit an application with the required application fee to the Commission office. Once an application is approved, the Commission provides the applicant with information needed to locate the appropriate exam site and sit for the exam. Candidates may not take the examination without prior written approval from the Commission.

For the South Dakota journeyman electrician path, the Commission states that a candidate is ready to write the journeyman examination after completing four years, for a total of 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor. Experience hours must be licensed in order to count toward the required hours for the examination.

The South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly identified as the ICC 531 South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam. Published exam information lists the exam as a computer-based, multiple-choice test with 80 questions and a 4-hour time limit. A passing score of 70% is commonly listed for South Dakota journeyman electrical testing.

The exam content focuses on the practical electrical knowledge expected of a journeyman electrician. Important subject areas include general electrical knowledge, services and service equipment, feeders, branch circuit conductors, wiring methods and materials, equipment devices, control devices, motors and generators, and special occupancies, equipment, and conditions. Students should also expect to review electrical theory, code definitions, load calculations, conductor sizing, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, raceways, boxes, panelboards, disconnects, receptacles, luminaires, appliances, heating and cooling equipment, emergency systems, and other NEC-based topics.

## State Requirements

South Dakota electrician licensing is administered by the South Dakota Electrical Commission, which operates under the South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation. The Commission administers state laws and regulations related to electrical wiring, inspects wiring installations, investigates complaints, and licenses electricians within the state.

For journeyman electrician examination eligibility, South Dakota states that a candidate must complete four years, totaling 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor. The state also states that experience hours must be licensed in order to count toward the required exam hours.

After becoming licensed as a journeyman, South Dakota lists additional experience requirements for higher electrical licensing paths. For example, it takes another two years as a licensed journeyman, with 4,000 hours of additional work under the employment and supervision of a contractor, including a minimum of 2,000 hours of commercial work, to write the electrical contractor’s exam.

South Dakota licensees are required to meet continuing education requirements for renewal. The Commission states that licensees must complete 16 hours of continuing education, including a minimum of 8 code hours, by the renewal deadline. South Dakota also notes limits on the number of online continuing education hours allowed under its administrative rules.

These state requirements make organized preparation important. Students should keep records of licensed work experience, follow the Commission’s application instructions, prepare with the correct code edition, and build a consistent study schedule before testing.
