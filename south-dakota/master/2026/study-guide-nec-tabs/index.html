Exam Details

South Dakota electrical examinations are administered through the South Dakota Electrical Commission process. Candidates must meet the requirements for the license they have chosen and submit an application with the required application fee to the Commission office before testing. The Commission’s examination information states that all tests are based on the 2023 NEC starting December 2, 2024.

South Dakota exam preparation should be based on the license category being pursued. A journeyman electrician applicant prepares for the journeyman examination after completing the required apprentice training experience. A candidate pursuing the electrical contractor path prepares for the contractor examination after gaining additional licensed journeyman experience under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor.

The South Dakota electrical contractor path requires broader preparation than basic code lookup. Candidates should study the National Electrical Code, South Dakota electrical laws and regulations, services, feeders, branch circuits, conductor sizing, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, raceways, boxes, equipment, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, and practical electrical calculations. Contractor-level preparation also requires understanding the responsibility that comes with performing or supervising regulated electrical work.

The required passing grade for South Dakota electrical examinations is commonly treated as 70%. Candidates should prepare to answer NEC-based questions accurately while also understanding state electrical rules and licensing responsibilities. Because the exam is tied to the currently adopted NEC edition, candidates should use the official code edition required by the South Dakota Electrical Commission for the examination they are scheduled to take.

Licensing Steps

The South Dakota electrical licensing path begins with registration and supervised experience. A person entering the trade generally works as a licensed apprentice electrician under the employment and supervision of a licensed electrical contractor. Apprentice experience builds the foundation needed for journeyman licensing and later contractor-level advancement.

After completing four years, for a total of 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor, the applicant is ready to write the journeyman examination. The journeyman license is an important step in South Dakota’s licensing structure because contractor-level eligibility requires additional experience as a licensed journeyman.

After becoming licensed as a journeyman electrician, the electrician must gain more experience before applying for the electrical contractor examination. South Dakota’s licensing information states that it takes another two years as a licensed journeyman, with a total of 4,000 hours of additional work under the employment and supervision of a contractor, including at least 2,000 hours of commercial work, to write the electrical contractor exam.

The applicant submits the appropriate application and fee to the South Dakota Electrical Commission. The Commission reviews the application for the license category selected. After approval, the candidate follows the Commission’s examination process and takes the required exam. Candidates should prepare with the NEC edition currently used by the Commission and any applicable South Dakota electrical laws and regulations.

After passing the required examination and completing the licensing process, the licensee must continue to follow South Dakota electrical laws, Commission rules, renewal requirements, inspection requirements, permit requirements, and the scope of work authorized by the license held. Electrical contractors also have responsibilities connected to supervision, compliance, and electrical work performed under their authority.

State Requirements

South Dakota electrical licensing is administered statewide by the South Dakota Electrical Commission. The Commission works to protect citizens and property from hazards associated with electricity by administering state laws and regulations concerning electrical wiring, inspecting wiring installations, investigating complaints, and licensing electricians throughout the state.

South Dakota recognizes several electrical licensing categories. Apprentice electricians work under supervision while gaining experience. Journeyman electricians have completed the required training and examination path. Class B electricians and electrical contractors operate within the state’s licensing structure according to the scope and conditions of the credential held.

The journeyman path requires four years and 8,000 hours as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor. The electrical contractor path requires additional licensed journeyman experience, including two more years and 4,000 additional hours under the employment and supervision of a contractor, with at least 2,000 hours of commercial work.

South Dakota also enforces electrical code adoption through the Commission. Starting December 2, 2024, South Dakota electrical tests are based on the 2023 NEC.
