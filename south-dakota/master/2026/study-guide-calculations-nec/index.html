Licensing Steps

South Dakota electrical licensing is handled through the South Dakota Electrical Commission. A typical licensing path includes reviewing the license classifications, confirming eligibility, documenting the required experience, submitting the correct application and application fee, receiving written approval to test, locating the appropriate exam site, preparing with code-based study materials, taking the required examination, and completing final licensing steps with the Commission.

The first step is confirming the correct license path. South Dakota includes apprentice, journeyman, electrical contractor, and other electrical licensing categories. Candidates pursuing an advanced electrical contractor or master-level path should review the requirements for the exact license they plan to obtain before applying.

South Dakota identifies a journeyman path that includes four years, or a total of 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor before writing the journeyman exam. The electrical contractor exam path requires additional licensed journeyman experience after that point.

For the electrical contractor exam path, South Dakota identifies two additional years as a licensed journeyman, with 4,000 hours of additional work under the employment and supervision of a contractor, including a minimum of 2,000 hours of commercial work. Experience hours must be licensed in order to count toward the hours required to take the exams.

Candidates should keep organized records of apprenticeship registration, journeyman licensure, work experience, employer verification, commercial work hours, application materials, payment records, Commission correspondence, written exam approval, score results, bond or insurance documentation when applicable, and final license records. Organized records help reduce delays during application and licensing steps.

After written approval is issued, candidates follow the exam instructions provided by the Commission. Approved candidates should review the time allowed to schedule, exam-site instructions, reference requirements, identification procedures, and any other testing rules before exam day.

State Requirements

South Dakota uses a statewide electrical licensing structure administered by the South Dakota Electrical Commission. The Commission administers electrical wiring laws and regulations, licenses electricians, inspects wiring installations, investigates complaints related to electrical wiring, and provides licensing and examination information for electricians in the state.

Electrical work and licensing in South Dakota are connected to proper supervision, documented experience, exam approval, code compliance, inspections, and ongoing license maintenance. Candidates preparing for an advanced electrical license should understand that the exam is only one part of the overall licensing process.

South Dakota licensees and registrants must complete required continuing education for renewal. The Commission identifies 16 hours of continuing education, including a minimum of 8 code hours, for renewal. Continuing education is separate from initial exam preparation but is an important part of maintaining a South Dakota electrical credential.

Applicants for certain South Dakota electrical licenses may also need to complete additional forms, such as contractor or Class B electrician bond-related forms, depending on the credential. Candidates should follow the current Commission instructions for forms, payments, renewals, continuing education, bond requirements, and final licensing steps.
