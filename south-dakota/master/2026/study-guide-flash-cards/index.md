## State Requirements

The South Dakota Electrical Commission administers the state laws and regulations concerning electrical wiring, inspects wiring installations, investigates electrical wiring complaints, and licenses electricians within South Dakota. The Commission should not be confused with the South Dakota Electrical Council, which is a separate nonprofit industry organization.

South Dakota’s licensing progression begins with apprenticeship experience. After completing **4 years**, for a total of **8,000 hours**, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor, a candidate is ready to write the journeyman exam.

To write the electrical contractor exam, South Dakota requires another **2 years** as a licensed journeyman, with a total of **4,000 additional hours** of work under the employment and supervision of a contractor. Of those additional hours, at least **2,000 hours** must be commercial work.

South Dakota also states that experience hours must be licensed in order for working time to count toward the hours required to take the exams. This makes proper registration, licensing status, supervision, and documentation important throughout the licensing path.

Licensees must complete continuing education for renewal. South Dakota requires **16 hours** of continuing education, including a minimum of **8 code hours**, by the renewal deadline. Only a limited number of online continuing education hours may count under state rules.

Applicants for an Electrical Contractor or Class B Electrician license must submit the required Electrical Contractor and Class B Electricians’ Bond Application as part of the process. Candidates should follow the current South Dakota Electrical Commission forms and instructions for the license classification they are pursuing.
