Exam Details

The Oregon General Journeyman Electrician license exam is part of the licensing process administered by the Oregon Building Codes Division. Candidates apply for the electrical license, submit required documentation, and receive an authorization letter after the application is approved. The authorization letter explains the examination procedure and provides information for scheduling the licensing exam.

Oregon’s General Journeyman Electrician examination information lists the exam as 52 questions, 3 hours, and open book. The approved materials are controlled by Oregon electrical license examination guidelines. Approved examination materials include the National Electrical Code/NFPA 70 and errata with Oregon amendments, the National Electrical Code Handbook, Oregon Revised Statutes Chapter 479, Oregon Administrative Rules Division 918, American Electrician’s Handbook, Electrical Black Book, Ugly’s Electrical References, Ferm’s Fast Finder Index, Tom Henry’s Key Word Index, and a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator.

The exam materials guidelines allow listed published materials to include tabs and personal handwritten or printed notes within the margins. Practice tests and class preparation materials are not allowed in the examination room. Any materials outside the approved list may be removed by the proctor, and electronics other than the permitted calculator are prohibited.

The exam is designed to measure whether the candidate can apply electrical knowledge and use approved references correctly. Candidates should prepare for questions involving electrical theory, code definitions, calculations, conductors, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, raceways, boxes, motors, transformers, equipment installation, special occupancies, and Oregon electrical rules.

After the exam is completed, Oregon’s licensing process provides exam results by mail no later than two weeks after the exam. If the candidate passes, the Building Codes Division issues the license and mails the photo identification card. The exam is only one part of the licensing process, and candidates must complete the application and documentation requirements connected to the license.

Open Book Test

The Oregon General Journeyman Electrician exam is open book. This makes code-book navigation one of the most important skills a candidate can build before test day. Open book testing gives candidates access to approved references, but it does not remove the need to study. Candidates still need to understand the subject of each question, locate the correct rule, interpret the code language, and apply the rule accurately.

The National Electrical Code can be challenging because related information may appear across definitions, article requirements, tables, exceptions, notes, and cross-references. A candidate who understands the general topic but cannot find the correct section quickly may lose valuable exam time. Practicing with the NEC before the exam helps reduce hesitation and improves familiarity with the structure of the book.

Because Oregon’s exam material rules allow tabs and margin notes in approved published materials, candidates should use study time to build a clean, organized reference system. Effective open-book preparation may include marking major articles, practicing with the index, using the table of contents, reviewing definitions, and learning how to move between a question topic and the applicable rule.

Licensing Steps

The Oregon journeyman electrician licensing process begins with meeting the qualifications for the General Journeyman Electrician license. The Oregon Building Codes Division application instructions require applicants to review the license information, determine whether they meet the qualifications, complete the electrical license application, and submit the required documentation, photograph, and fees.

For the General Journeyman Electrician license, Oregon lists several qualification paths. A candidate may qualify by completing an approved apprenticeship program in Oregon. Another path includes official transcripts verifying 576 hours of classroom training and verification of 8,000 hours of on-the-job experience obtained outside Oregon. Oregon also identifies an alternate experience-based path involving 16,000 hours of on-the-job experience obtained outside Oregon with required category breakdowns.

Application documentation may include the completed application form, signed affidavit, work experience verification from each employer, proof of completion of an Oregon-approved apprenticeship or training program when applicable, proof of completion of an out-of-state apprenticeship program when applicable, official classroom training transcripts when required, proof of high school diploma, GED, equivalent, or college degree, a passport-style photo, and payment of the required fee.

After the application is received and approved, the Building Codes Division sends the candidate a letter of authorization to take the examination. The candidate is responsible for scheduling the examination with the test site office. If the application is denied, the division sends a denial letter explaining the candidate’s hearing rights.

Once the candidate completes the exam, Oregon allows time for grading and license issuance. If the candidate passes, the Building Codes Division issues the license and mails the photo identification card. During each three-year license period, Oregon electrical licensees are required to complete continuing education based on the license type.

State Requirements

Oregon licenses electricians at the state level through the Oregon Building Codes Division. The General Journeyman Electrician license is identified by the license type “J” and is governed under Oregon electrical licensing rules. The license authorizes the holder to make electrical installations under the supervision, direction, and control of a general supervising electrician, unless the work requires the direction of a limited supervising electrician or does not require supervision.

The Oregon Business Xpress license listing for the General Journeyman Electrician license identifies the license fee, three-year renewal cycle, renewal date, continuing education requirement, processing information, prerequisite paths, required documentation, and examination information. Renewal is every three years on October 1, and the listing identifies 24 total continuing education hours for renewal.

The primary Oregon qualification path is completion of an approved apprenticeship program in Oregon. Candidates qualifying through out-of-state training and experience must provide documentation that meets Oregon’s requirements, including classroom training and on-the-job experience when applicable. Work experience documentation must identify required work categories and hours.

Because Oregon’s licensing process is documentation-based, candidates should make sure their application packet is complete. Missing work history, missing employer verification, incomplete category breakdowns, missing training transcripts, missing photo, or missing fee payment can delay evaluation. A complete application helps the Building Codes Division determine whether the candidate is eligible for examination.
