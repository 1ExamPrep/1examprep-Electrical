Exam Details

The Maryland Master Electrician examination is administered through PSI Examination Services for the Maryland State Board of Electricians. Candidates apply through the examination process, provide required documentation, and schedule the exam after receiving approval. The Maryland master electrician exam is designed to measure knowledge of applicable codes, laws, electrical installation principles, and the practical competency needed for state licensure.

The Maryland Master Electrician examination includes 90 questions, scored as 100 points, with a minimum passing score of 70 percent and a time allowance of 240 minutes. The exam content includes electrical theory, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special communications, and calculations. Calculation content is a major part of master-level preparation, so candidates should spend dedicated time reviewing formulas, code tables, and step-by-step problem solving.

Maryland master electrician candidates must meet the state experience requirements before taking the examination. The state requires applicants to have been engaged or employed regularly and principally in providing electrical services for all types of electrical equipment and apparatus for at least seven years while under the direction and supervision of a master electrician or a similarly qualified employee of a governmental unit. The Board may allow up to three years of credit toward that experience requirement for qualifying formal study or professional training in electrical installation.

Open Book Test

The Maryland Master Electrician examination is an open-book examination. Candidates are allowed to bring approved reference material into the testing room under the rules listed in the official PSI candidate bulletin. Open book does not mean easy. It means candidates must know how to find, understand, and apply code language accurately while working under time limits.

The official testing rules control which reference edition, tabs, highlighting, notes, and materials are allowed in the exam room. Study guides are not allowed as exam-room references. Reference books may be indexed and may contain highlighted or underlined text, but they must be unmarked, may not contain written notes, and may not contain additional loose or attached papers. Candidates should always follow the current Maryland and PSI examination instructions for the exact reference edition and materials permitted for their exam date.

The included National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback with Tabs supports code-navigation practice during study. Tabs help candidates become more familiar with the layout of the NEC, including chapters, articles, tables, definitions, and major subject areas. During preparation, candidates should practice locating answers directly in the code book instead of relying only on memory. This builds the speed and confidence needed for timed, open-book electrical questions.

Open-book electrical exams often require more than finding a keyword. Candidates may need to connect multiple code sections, understand definitions, use tables correctly, apply exceptions, complete calculations, and compare answer choices against the exact wording of the code. A candidate who is unfamiliar with the structure of the NEC can lose valuable time searching through the book.

Licensing Steps

The Maryland master electrician licensing process begins with confirming that the candidate meets the state’s experience requirement. Master electrician applicants need qualifying electrical experience under the direction and supervision of a master electrician or a similarly qualified governmental employee. Candidates with formal electrical education or professional training may be eligible for credit toward part of the required experience, subject to Board review.

After confirming eligibility, the candidate applies for the Maryland Master Electrician examination through the approved examination process. Required documentation may include certification of experience and evidence of formal electrical training when applicable. Once PSI reviews and approves the examination application, the candidate receives instructions for payment and scheduling.

The next step is exam preparation. This Ultimate Combo is designed to support that technical preparation by helping candidates study the 2026 NEC, review Maryland master electrician subject areas, practice electrical calculations, work with flash cards, and answer online exam-style questions. Candidates should also review the current PSI bulletin for scheduling rules, identification requirements, reference rules, and testing procedures.

After passing the Maryland Master Electrician examination, candidates complete the licensing process with the Maryland State Board of Electricians. Passing candidates receive documentation that becomes part of the official application for licensure. Maryland may also have waiver or reciprocity provisions for certain qualified applicants holding appropriate licenses or local registrations, but those applications are reviewed by the Board.

State Requirements

Maryland uses a state-level licensing structure for the master electrician license. The Maryland State Board of Electricians issues the state master electrician license, and many local jurisdictions may use the state master license as a prerequisite for local electrical licensing or registration. Local jurisdictions may also issue lesser electrical licenses, registrations, or specialty credentials, so electricians should review the rules for the county or municipality where they plan to work.

The Maryland master electrician license is an advanced credential for experienced electrical professionals.
