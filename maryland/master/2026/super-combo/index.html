Exam Details

The Maryland Master Electrician examination is administered through PSI for the Maryland State Board of Electricians. Before making final application to the Board for a Master or Journeyperson license, an individual is required to pass the written examination. The examination is designed to test knowledge of applicable codes, laws, and principles of electrical installation and to determine fitness for a Maryland state license.

The Maryland Master Electrician exam is listed with 90 questions, scored as 100 points, a 70 percent minimum passing score, and 240 minutes of testing time. The content outline includes Electrical Theory, Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Special Communications, and Calculations. Electrical Theory includes definitions, Ohm’s Law, techniques, troubleshooting, equipment and devices, instruments, materials, math, conversions, diagrams, and symbols.

The exam outline shows why a balanced study plan is important. Calculations carry a major portion of the listed scoring value, and theory also requires serious attention. Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, and Special Communications all require familiarity with NEC organization and electrical installation principles. Candidates should be prepared to move between math-based questions, code-reference questions, and trade knowledge questions during the same testing session.

The current Maryland PSI bulletin lists the National Electrical Code, NFPA 70, 2020 edition, as the only reference allowed in the testing room for the Master Electrician exam. Open Book Test

The Maryland Master Electrician exam is identified as an open book examination. Candidates may bring in reference books, but the rules are strict. Study guides are not allowed in the testing room. Reference books may be indexed and may contain highlighted or underlined text, but all materials must be unmarked and may not contain written notes or additional papers, whether loose or attached.

The Maryland PSI bulletin states that the National Electrical Code reference listed for the Master Electrician exam is not provided at the test center and is the only reference allowed in the testing room. The bulletin also provides rules for tabs. Acceptable tabs must be permanent tabs, while removable flags and tabs that can be taken off without ripping the page are not acceptable. Tabs with paper inserts are not allowed for exam use.

Open book testing does not make the exam easy. It changes the way candidates need to prepare. Instead of memorizing every detail, candidates must know how to find information efficiently, read the exact code language, check table notes and exceptions, and apply the rule to the question being asked. Candidates who are unfamiliar with the NEC layout can lose valuable time searching for articles, definitions, tables, and exceptions.

The National Electrical Code 2026 paperback with tabs included in this Super Combo supports code-navigation practice during study. Tabs can help you move through the code book more efficiently, but they work best when paired with repeated lookup exercises and practice questions. Each study session should include locating the correct article, reading the surrounding section, checking related tables, and confirming whether an exception changes the answer.

The flash cards also support open book preparation by helping reinforce concepts that should become familiar before the exam. The more quickly you recognize formulas, definitions, and common topics, the more time you can spend on careful code application and calculations.

Licensing Steps

Maryland master electrician licensing is handled through the Maryland State Board of Electricians. The licensing process for Master and Journeyperson electricians begins with applying for and passing the examination administered by PSI. After passing the examination, the candidate applies for the appropriate license through the Board.

A practical licensing path begins by confirming that the Master Electrician license is the correct credential for the work you plan to perform. Candidates should review the Board’s experience requirements, gather required documentation, apply online to PSI for examination approval, receive approval to schedule, pay the examination fee, complete the exam, and then apply for the license after passing.

Maryland’s published requirements state that an applicant for a master electrician license examination must have been engaged or employed regularly and principally in providing electrical services for all types of electrical equipment and apparatuses for at least seven years under the direction and supervision of a Maryland licensed master electrician or a similarly qualified employee of a governmental unit. The State Board may allow up to three years of credit toward the required experience with proof of formal study or professional training in electrical installation comparable to the required experience.

The study portion of the licensing path should be treated as a planned process. Start with the content outline, then divide your study time among theory, calculations, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, and special communications. Use the study guide for organized review, the calculations guide for step-by-step math practice, the NEC for reference navigation, and the flash cards for quick reinforcement.

State Requirements

The Maryland State Board of Electricians regulates qualifications for examining and licensing Master, Journeyperson, and Apprentice Electricians. The Board operates under Maryland law and regulations connected with electrical licensing and is responsible for the licensing process after the required examination is completed.

For the Maryland Master Electrician examination, candidates must satisfy the required work experience before registering. The state’s published requirements identify seven years of qualifying electrical services experience under the direction and supervision of a Maryland licensed master electrician or a similarly qualified governmental employee. The Board may grant limited credit toward experience for formal electrical study or professional training that is comparable to the required experience.

Maryland also provides examination waiver and reciprocity pathways under certain circumstances. The Board may grant a waiver for an individual who holds a local master registration if the applicant meets the required qualifications and obtained the local registration after passing an equivalent examination and meeting equivalent licensing requirements. The Board also identifies certain out-of-state and reciprocal pathways for applicants who meet the Board’s conditions.

Candidates should follow the current Board and PSI application process, submit required documentation within the required process, and use the correct examination authorization.
