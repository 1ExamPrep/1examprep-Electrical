## Exam Details

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician examination is administered by PSI Services LLC for the Maryland State Board of Electricians. Before making final application to the Board for a journeyperson license, an individual must pass the required written examination unless the applicant qualifies for a specific waiver pathway.

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam includes 70 questions, requires 49 correct answers for a 70% passing score, and allows 210 minutes of testing time. PSI also notes that a small number of experimental questions may be administered during the examination. Experimental questions are not scored.

The exam content outline includes electrical theory, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, and special communications. Electrical theory and wiring and protection are the largest listed sections, with 20 questions each. Wiring methods and materials includes 12 questions, equipment for general use includes 6 questions, special occupancies includes 4 questions, special equipment includes 2 questions, and special communications includes 6 questions.

Electrical theory topics include definitions, Ohm’s Law, techniques, troubleshooting, equipment and devices, instruments, materials, math, conversions, diagrams, and symbols. Wiring and protection topics include NEC Article 100, Article 110, Articles 200 through 210, Articles 215 through 225, Article 230, Article 240, and Articles 250 through 285. Wiring methods and materials include NEC Article 300, Articles 310 through 314, Articles 320 through 330, Articles 332 through 356, Articles 358 through 374, and Articles 376 through 398.

## Open Book Test

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam is an open book test. Candidates may bring reference books into the exam room, but study guides are not allowed. Reference books may be indexed and may contain highlighted or underlined text. All materials must be unmarked, not written in, and may not contain loose or attached additional papers.

The official PSI bulletin states that the exam reference will not be provided at the test center and that the listed National Electrical Code reference is the only reference allowed in the testing room. Candidates preparing for the exam should understand the difference between study materials and exam-room-approved references. Study guides, practice exams, flash cards, and online test kits are for preparation before exam day.

Open book does not mean easy. Candidates still need to know how to find the right NEC article, interpret the wording, apply the rule, and move efficiently through the exam. The National Electrical Code is detailed, and slow searching can cost valuable time. A prepared candidate should be able to identify the question topic, narrow down the applicable article or table, and answer accurately.

## Licensing Steps

Maryland journeyperson electrician licensing is handled through the Maryland State Board of Electricians. The process includes meeting the experience or approved apprenticeship requirements, applying to sit for the examination through PSI when required, passing the exam, and then applying for the appropriate license through the Board.

A typical Maryland journeyperson electrician path by examination includes gaining qualifying electrical experience under the direction and supervision of a Maryland licensed master electrician or a similarly qualified employee of a governmental unit. The applicant must have been regularly and principally engaged in providing electrical services for all types of electrical equipment and apparatuses while training to become a master electrician for at least four years.

After meeting the experience requirement, the applicant submits the required examination registration and documentation through PSI. Once PSI approves the registration materials, the candidate receives instructions to pay for and schedule the examination. After passing the examination, the candidate applies to the Board for the journeyperson license.

Maryland also provides a journeyperson license pathway by apprenticeship program. The Board waives the experience and examination requirements when the applicant provides written proof of completing an electrician apprenticeship program approved by the Maryland Apprenticeship and Training Council or the Federal Office of Apprenticeship. The program must include at least 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of work experience, and the certificate must have been received within two years of applying to the Board.

## State Requirements

Maryland issues statewide electrician licenses through the Maryland State Board of Electricians. The Board regulates master electricians, journeyperson electricians, and apprentice electricians under Maryland licensing law and Board regulations.

To take the Maryland journeyperson electrician license examination, the applicant must have been engaged or employed regularly and principally in providing electrical services for all types of electrical equipment and apparatuses in training to become a master electrician for at least four years under the direction and supervision of a Maryland licensed master electrician or a similarly qualified employee of a governmental unit.

Maryland may allow credit toward the experience requirement when the applicant provides proof of formal electrical installation study or professional training comparable to the required experience. Candidates using education or training credit should organize documentation carefully because that review can add processing time.

Maryland also recognizes a waiver pathway for applicants who completed an approved electrician apprenticeship program consisting of at least 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of work experience. This pathway applies when the applicant received the qualifying certificate within two years of applying to the Board.

Maryland has reciprocal or waiver processes for certain applicants with local journeyperson registrations and limited out-of-state circumstances. Applicants using a local examination pathway must meet the Board’s requirements for valid local registration, examination history, and time licensed or registered in the local jurisdiction.
