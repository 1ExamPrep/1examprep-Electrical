## Exam Details

Ohio does not operate one single statewide journeyman electrician license that applies uniformly to every individual electrician in every city. The Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board, commonly known as OCILB, issues state licenses for electrical contractors who perform commercial work. Individual journeyman electrician licensing and local credential requirements may be handled by municipalities or local authorities.

Because of that structure, exam details can vary depending on the path being followed. A candidate preparing for an Ohio local journeyman electrician exam should review the requirements issued by the applicable city or local licensing authority. A candidate preparing for an Ohio electrical contractor license should follow OCILB instructions, including the examination application process and PSI exam requirements.

For state electrical contractor licensing through OCILB, applicants must be approved before taking the licensing examination. OCILB identifies electrical, HVAC, refrigeration, plumbing, and hydronics contractors as trades licensed by the Board for commercial work. The Board’s examination application materials state that applicants must be at least 18 years of age, be a U.S. citizen or legal alien, and have been a tradesperson in the type of licensed trade for which the application is filed for not less than five years immediately prior to the application date.

Ohio electrical contractor exams are administered through PSI after eligibility is determined by OCILB. The PSI candidate information for the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board explains that the Board determines eligibility and PSI conducts the examination program through computer examination centers. Candidates should follow the official PSI and OCILB instructions for scheduling, identification, examination rules, reference materials, scoring, and retesting.

Local journeyman electrician exams may use different application requirements, question counts, time limits, fees, and reference rules. For example, some Ohio municipalities maintain their own journeyman electrician application and examination process. This is why candidates should confirm the exact licensing path before test day. The study guide and NEC combo is designed to support NEC-based preparation, but the official requirements of the local authority or state Board control the actual exam process.
