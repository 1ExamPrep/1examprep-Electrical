Exam Details

Ohio electrical contractor licensing examinations are administered through PSI for candidates approved through the Ohio Construction Industry Licensing Board. The testing process includes a trade examination for the electrical contractor classification and a business and law examination or approved business and law course pathway, depending on the current licensing instructions.
