Exam Details

Alaska electrical administrator exams are scheduled through PSI after a candidate obtains approval from the State of Alaska. The State of Alaska requires applicants to submit a complete application with supporting documentation before registering for the examination. Once approved, candidates may schedule through PSI, with Alaska test locations including Anchorage, Fairbanks, and Juneau.

Open Book Test

The Alaska electrical administrator examination allows specific reference materials into the examination center. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own allowed references. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, annotated, and indexed before the exam session, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index in the references during the examination session.

The PSI bulletin also allows a silent, nonprinting, non-programmable calculator in the examination center. Because open book exams still require speed and accuracy, preparation should include regular practice using the code book and tabs. Licensing Steps

Alaska electrical administrator applicants must obtain approval from the State of Alaska before sitting for the examination. The process begins by submitting the appropriate Electrical Administrator application and required supporting documentation to the State of Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing. Online applications are filed through a MyAlaska account, and paper applications are also available through the state licensing page.

After the State of Alaska reviews and approves the application, the applicant may schedule the examination through PSI. Once the exam is passed, examination results must be submitted to the Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing. Alaska states that examination results are valid for one year.

State Requirements

Alaska electrical administrators are regulated by the State of Alaska Department of Commerce, Community, and Economic Development, Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing. The division staffs the electrical administrator program, carries out licensing regulation, makes final licensing decisions, and handles disciplinary action for violations of licensing laws.

Electricians in Alaska are separately regulated by the Department of Labor, Labor Standards and Safety, Mechanical Inspection Section. This distinction is important for candidates because Alaska electrical work and contractor supervision may involve different regulatory responsibilities depending on the credential, work category, and business structure.

The Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator category covers supervision of inside wiring work under Alaska law. Inside wiring includes electrical work subject to the National Electrical Code within buildings and within property lines, beginning at the secondary side of the transformer or applicable point of attachment for conductors serving buildings or structures within the property lines. The category does not include linework consisting of poles and towers, including wires, cables, and apparatus supported by them, or outdoor substations or work defined under the unlimited linework category.

Candidates preparing for Alaska master electrician or electrical administrator work should study both trade knowledge and Alaska-specific statutes and regulations. The exam content outline includes Alaska statutes and regulations as a tested area, so preparation should not be limited to NEC study alone.
