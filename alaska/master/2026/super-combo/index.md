## Open Book Test

Alaska electrical administrator examinations are open book exams. The PSI electrical administrator bulletin lists approved reference material for each examination category and states that candidates may use a silent, nonprinting, non-programmable calculator in the examination center.

The current PSI bulletin allows approved references into the examination center. References may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index references during the examination session. Additional loose or attached papers are not permitted with approved references. References may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it notes or other removable tabs, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins.

Open-book testing still requires serious preparation. ## Licensing Steps

The Alaska advanced electrical path begins with identifying the correct credential. Electricians are regulated by the Department of Labor, Labor Standards and Safety, Mechanical Inspection Section, while electrical administrator licensing is staffed by the Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing. A person may not act as an electrical administrator in Alaska without the required license issued by the department, and a person licensed as an electrical administrator may perform work only in the category for which the person is licensed.

An electrical administrator must be assigned to a licensed or registered contractor. Candidates should select the correct administrator category before applying. The Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator category is commonly associated with broad inside wiring supervision, while other categories apply to linework, residential wiring, communications, controls, and related specialty scopes.

The application-by-examination process requires a signed, completed application, required fees, verification of examination score, and official transcripts when education is used to qualify. Examination results are valid for one year. The application instructions state that electrical administrator license examinations are currently conducted by PSI Exams and that approved candidates contact PSI for examination scheduling information.

After submitting the application and supporting documentation, the applicant obtains state approval before taking the exam. Once approved, the candidate schedules the appropriate PSI examination, studies the approved references and content outline, follows the open-book reference rules, and takes the examination.

After passing the required examination with a score of at least 70%, the candidate submits the required examination results and completes the remaining licensing process through the Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing. A practical preparation path includes reviewing the correct Alaska administrator category, gathering required documentation, applying for approval, studying the NEC and Alaska rules, practicing calculations, building reference navigation skills, using flash cards for recall, passing the PSI exam, and completing the licensing process.

## State Requirements

Alaska regulates electrical administrators through the Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing. The Department of Commerce, Community, and Economic Development adopts regulations governing electrical administrators, makes licensing decisions, and handles disciplinary action for violations of licensing law.

Alaska electrical administrator licensing is category-based. Categories listed by the state include Unlimited Linework Outside, Inside Communications, Controls and Control Wiring, Outside Communications, Unlimited Commercial Wiring, and Residential Wiring. Each category has its own scope, examination content, and approved reference list.

The Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator category authorizes supervision of all inside wiring work in accordance with Alaska law, including work defined under inside communications, residential wiring, and controls and control wiring. Inside wiring includes installation, construction, operation, maintenance, and repair of electrical work subject to the National Electrical Code within buildings and within property lines, beginning at the secondary side of the transformer or the first point of attachment for conductors supplying buildings or structures within the property lines.

Alaska’s application instructions require verification that the applicant obtained, within the 12 months immediately preceding the application date, a score of at least 70% on an approved electrical administrator examination administered by PSI Services. Examination results are valid for one year.
