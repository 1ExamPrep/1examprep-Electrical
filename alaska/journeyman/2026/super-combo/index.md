## Exam Details

Alaska electrician Certificates of Fitness are administered by the Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Labor Standards and Safety, Mechanical Inspection section. The Mechanical Inspection section verifies training, experience, and registration requirements for applicants, conducts testing, and issues Certificates of Fitness in both the plumbing and electrical trades.

The Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development testing information states that applicants must have their application packet processed and approved before scheduling a test. Department testing is offered in Anchorage, Juneau, and Fairbanks on the first and third Wednesday of each month. Proctored testing in other Alaska cities is offered through Department of Labor Job Centers, and Alaska-based plumbing and electrical unions may also offer testing at their facilities.

Alaska testing information states that a government-issued picture ID must be presented at the time of testing. The state lists a four-hour maximum time limit on exams and states that a score of at least 70 percent is required for passing all tests except Boiler Operator 2nd Class, which has a different passing standard.

## Licensing Steps

The Alaska journeyman electrician licensing path is handled through the Certificate of Fitness process. Applicants seeking an Electrical Journeyman Certificate of Fitness must apply through the Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Labor Standards and Safety, Mechanical Inspection section.

The current Alaska Certificate of Fitness application materials list the Electrical Journeyman Certificate of Fitness experience requirement as 8,000 hours of work in the trade. A minimum of 6,000 hours must be commercial or industrial. Up to 1,000 hours of trade-related classroom training may be substituted for 1,000 hours of the commercial or industrial requirement. Up to 2,000 hours may be residential. Maintenance hours do not apply toward the Electrical Journeyman requirement.

A practical licensing path begins with gaining qualifying electrical experience under the appropriate supervision. Applicants then complete the Certificate of Fitness application and provide the required documentation. Alaska testing information states that the application packet must be processed and approved before a test is scheduled. After approval, the applicant schedules and takes the examination.

After passing the exam and meeting the state’s licensing requirements, the applicant completes the remaining Certificate of Fitness process through the Department. The issued Certificate of Fitness allows the holder to perform work within the scope of the electrical journeyman classification under Alaska rules.

Students should begin studying before the exam date is selected. Journeyman electrician content is broad, and the best preparation usually comes from consistent review over time.
