Licensing Steps

Alaska issues an Electrical Journeyman Certificate of Fitness through the Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Mechanical Inspection Section. The Mechanical Inspection Section verifies training, experience, and registration requirements, conducts testing, and issues Certificates of Fitness in the electrical and plumbing trades.

Applicants seeking testing must submit a current application packet before scheduling. Application materials state that testing applicants must submit a current application, original verification of the appropriate number of experience hours, and the application/test fee together as a packet before a test date is scheduled.

For the Electrical Journeyman Certificate of Fitness, Alaska requires a minimum of 8,000 hours of work experience subject to the National Electrical Code. A minimum of 6,000 hours must be commercial or industrial. A maximum of 2,000 hours may be residential. A maximum of 1,000 hours of trade-related classroom training and/or 1,000 hours of power lineman experience may be included as part of the 8,000-hour requirement. Maintenance hours do not apply.

After the application packet is processed and approved, candidates may schedule testing through the Mechanical Inspection office or approved testing locations. State testing information notes that the application packet must be processed and approved before scheduling a test. Candidates must present a government-issued picture ID at the time of testing.

If a passing score is not obtained, Alaska testing information states that the candidate must wait 30 days from the date of the last exam before retesting. Exam results are emailed, and results for tests taken outside Anchorage may take several working days to process.

This study guide and flash card combo supports the preparation stage of the Alaska Certificate of Fitness process. Students can use it while gathering experience documentation, after submitting an application, after testing approval, or during final review before the scheduled exam.

State Requirements

Alaska electrical trade work is regulated through state Certificate of Fitness requirements. The Mechanical Inspection Section states that, with limited exceptions, a Certificate of Fitness is required to perform work subject to Alaska electrical safety standards. The Electrical Journeyman Certificate of Fitness authorizes the holder to perform work subject to National Electrical Code standards adopted by Alaska.

State requirements for the Electrical Journeyman Certificate of Fitness include passing the required examination and submitting documented proof of qualifying experience. Alaska regulations recognize documented proof on a notarized state form, notarized employer statements on letterhead, or an Alaska-based union affidavit that demonstrates the applicant meets the required experience standard.

Students should carefully follow current Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development instructions for application forms, experience verification, testing, fees, certificate issuance, renewals, and continuing education. Requirements can involve both experience documentation and successful exam completion, so exam preparation should be paired with a complete and accurate application packet.

For renewal, Alaska application materials state that Electrical Journeymen are required to obtain at least 16 hours of continuing education within the 24-month period before renewal request. Candidates and certificate holders should follow current state renewal instructions for accepted education, proof of completion, deadlines, and fees.
