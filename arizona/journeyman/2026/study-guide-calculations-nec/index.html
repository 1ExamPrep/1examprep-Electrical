Exam Details

Arizona electrical licensing is administered through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. The state issues contractor licenses rather than a single statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Electrical contracting classifications include residential electrical, commercial electrical, and dual electrical classifications. Candidates pursuing an Arizona electrical contractor license should use the Arizona Registrar of Contractors license classification requirements to determine which examinations apply to the classification they are seeking.

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors uses PSI for trade-specific contractor examinations. Candidates may also be required to complete the Arizona Statutes and Rules Exam, commonly referred to as the SRE. The SRE is a computer-based course and exam maintained by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors for applicants who need to demonstrate knowledge of Arizona statutes and rules connected to contractor licensing.

Arizona electrical contractor applicants may be required to pass a trade-specific electrical exam. Applicants for certain electrical classifications may also have the option of using the NASCLA electrical examination program in place of the Arizona ROC trade exam. The correct exam path depends on the classification selected, the applicant’s role as qualifying party, and the current Arizona Registrar of Contractors requirements.

The Arizona C-11 Electrical Commercial contractor trade exam content outline lists 110 questions, a 70% minimum passing score, and 240 minutes of testing time. Subject areas include general electrical knowledge, service feeders and branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, electrical power, motors, low voltage, lighting, illuminated signs, fire detection and alarm systems, safety information, and overcurrent protection.

Even though this product is titled for Arizona Journeyman Electrician study, the state’s licensing structure should be understood clearly. The study materials are intended to help students build journeyman-level electrical knowledge and prepare for electrical exam content, especially code and calculation topics that appear across electrical licensing and contractor trade exams. Candidates seeking a state-issued contractor license should follow the Arizona Registrar of Contractors process for the exact license classification they plan to pursue.

Licensing Steps

Arizona’s licensing path for electrical work is contractor-focused. A person or business seeking an Arizona electrical contractor license begins by selecting the proper license classification. Electrical classifications may include residential electrical, commercial electrical, or dual electrical, depending on the scope of work the applicant intends to perform.

After selecting the classification, the applicant must determine which examinations apply. The Arizona Registrar of Contractors license classification requirements identify whether the Statutes and Rules Exam, trade exam, or other examination requirements apply. The person serving as the qualifying party is responsible for completing the exam requirements tied to the license application.

The applicant must complete the required exams before submitting a completed license application for processing. Arizona’s contractor licensing process may also include business formation, background checks, identification documents, bonding, fees, and submission through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors application process. The application should match the applicant’s legal business structure and classification.

Electrical applicants should prepare for both the administrative side and the technical side of the process. The administrative side includes choosing the correct classification, completing forms accurately, meeting bonding and background requirements, and following Arizona Registrar of Contractors instructions. The technical side includes studying electrical trade topics, practicing code navigation, and working calculations before taking the trade exam.

Students using this combo can build a study schedule around the major electrical knowledge areas connected to journeyman-level work and Arizona electrical contractor exam content. A practical plan may include several study sessions each week, with separate time for NEC navigation, trade review, and calculations. Instead, electrical contracting is regulated through contractor license classifications. Electrical work performed as contracting work in Arizona must be done under the proper contractor license classification. For electricians, this means the licensing structure is tied to the contractor or business license and the qualifying party rather than a standalone statewide journeyman card.
