The Arizona Registrar of Contractors licenses and regulates residential and commercial contractors throughout the state. Arizona’s electrical contractor classifications include residential, commercial, and dual electrical classifications, and applicants may be required to complete the Arizona Statutes and Rules Exam and the applicable trade-specific examination.

Licensing Steps

The Arizona electrical licensing path begins by identifying the correct license classification through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Arizona electrical contractor applicants may pursue residential, commercial, or dual electrical classifications depending on the scope of work the business intends to perform. The qualifying party must satisfy the applicable exam requirements unless a waiver applies.

A practical path begins with reviewing the Arizona ROC license classification requirements. The applicant determines which examination or examinations are required for the electrical classification being pursued. New license applicants may be required to complete the Arizona Statutes and Rules Exam, which is provided and maintained by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. In addition to that requirement, an applicant may need to pass a trade-specific examination administered by PSI.

After the required examination path is identified, the candidate registers for the applicable PSI trade examination. The PSI candidate bulletin explains that candidates receive eligibility information, create a PSI account, select the examination, choose a test center or remote proctored option, pay the required examination fee, and schedule the appointment.

On test day, the candidate must follow PSI identification and security procedures. The bulletin requires valid identification containing the candidate’s photo, signature, and matching name. PSI provides scratch paper and a pencil at the test center, and candidates may use an approved basic calculator according to PSI rules.

After passing the required examination or examinations, the applicant submits the completed license application and original score report to the Arizona Registrar of Contractors within the required timeframe. The license application cannot be accepted until all examination requirements have been completed.
