Licensing Steps

Arizona electrical contractor licensing begins with identifying the correct license classification through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. The applicant must determine whether the work falls under residential electrical, commercial electrical, dual electrical, or another applicable classification. The qualifying party for the license must meet the required experience, knowledge, and skill standards for that classification.

Once the classification is identified, the applicant must complete the examination requirements assigned to that classification. The Arizona Registrar of Contractors candidate bulletin states that applicants may need to complete the Arizona Statutes and Rules Exam and a trade-specific examination. PSI administers the trade-specific examination program for the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

After the required examinations are passed, the applicant submits the completed license application and required documents to the Arizona Registrar of Contractors for processing. Examination results must be submitted within the time period required by the licensing process. The application cannot be accepted until all required examinations have been completed.
