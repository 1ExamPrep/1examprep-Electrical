## Open Book Test

The Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam is an **open book test**. The Nevada contractor candidate bulletin identifies approved references and states that candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination center. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session. ## Licensing Steps

The Nevada licensing path begins with identifying the credential needed. A person who wants to operate as an electrical contractor in Nevada generally works through the Nevada State Contractors Board and applies for the appropriate classification, such as C-2 Electrical Contractor. A person seeking an individual master or journeyman credential should also check the requirements of the local jurisdiction where the work will be performed.

For Nevada contractor licensing, the applicant submits the contractor license application to the Nevada State Contractors Board. The Board reviews the application and verifies the applicant’s qualifications and experience. Nevada contractor applicants are generally tested through PSI after the Board determines eligibility.

Once approved, the applicant receives an eligibility letter and follows the PSI scheduling instructions. The candidate schedules the required examination portion or portions, reports to the examination center with proper identification, and brings only the approved references allowed for that exam. Nevada PSI rules address reference condition, tabs, highlighting, underlining, additional papers, and testing-room security.

After the examination is completed, the score is given immediately. PSI forwards passing results to the Nevada State Contractors Board for processing. If a candidate does not pass, Nevada rules allow three attempts before the application becomes void. After a third failed attempt, the applicant must reapply after the required waiting period.
