## Licensing Steps

New York electrician licensing is local, so the steps may vary depending on where the candidate plans to work. A candidate preparing for a journeyman electrician license should begin by identifying the correct licensing authority for the city, county, or municipality where the work will be performed.

In general, the licensing path may include reviewing the local licensing classification, confirming eligibility requirements, documenting electrical experience, completing any required application, paying applicable fees, receiving exam authorization when required, passing the required exam, and submitting final documents to the licensing authority. Some jurisdictions may require apprenticeship records, employer verification, proof of supervised work, insurance documents, local registration, continuing education, or additional approvals.

Because of these differences, candidates should not assume that one New York electrical exam path applies everywhere in the state.
