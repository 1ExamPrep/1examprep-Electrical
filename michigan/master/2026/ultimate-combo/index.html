Exam Details

The Michigan Master Electrician examination is part of the state licensing process administered through the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs, Bureau of Construction Codes. Candidates must meet the state’s eligibility requirements before sitting for the master electrician examination. Once approved, candidates follow the state’s examination scheduling process through the approved testing provider.

Michigan requires a master electrician applicant to be at least 22 years old and to have at least 12,000 hours of experience obtained over a period of 6 years or more in electrical construction, electrical wiring, electrical equipment, or maintenance of buildings. The experience must be performed under the supervision of a master electrician. Michigan also requires applicants to hold and maintain a journeyperson electrician license for at least 2 years before qualifying for master electrician licensure.

The Michigan Master Electrician exam is designed to test whether the candidate has the knowledge needed to work at the master level and take responsibility for code compliance. State Requirements

Michigan uses a state-level electrical licensing structure.
