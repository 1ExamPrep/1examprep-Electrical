## Licensing Steps

The North Dakota master electrician licensing path begins with meeting the experience requirements established by the North Dakota State Electrical Board. A Master Electrician applicant must have completed one year of experience as a licensed journeyman and must have at least 2,000 hours of experience working as a licensed journeyman electrician under the supervision of a contracting master electrician or master of record.

After meeting the required experience, the applicant completes the examination application for an electrical license and submits the required employment verification. North Dakota’s application process requires electrical construction work to be performed in a jurisdiction with similar licensing and inspection rules to North Dakota and under the supervision of a master electrician.

Once the application is reviewed and approved by the Board, the applicant receives an invitation to take the examination. The invitation outlines available exam dates. The applicant then contacts the Board and selects the exam date. If the examination is not taken within the allotted time, the applicant may be required to complete another application and pay the required fee.

After taking the exam, a passing grade of 70% is required. Candidates who do not pass must follow the Board’s re-examination process and waiting periods. A score of 60 to 69 has no waiting period, a score of 50 to 59 carries a 3-month waiting period, and a score of 0 to 49 carries a 6-month waiting period.

After passing the exam and completing the licensing process, the electrician may be issued the appropriate North Dakota master electrician license category. Licensees must continue to follow North Dakota laws, rules, wiring standards, renewal rules, continuing education requirements, inspection requirements, and the responsibilities tied to the type of master electrician license held.

## State Requirements

North Dakota electrical licensing is regulated by the North Dakota State Electrical Board. The Board is responsible for examining applicants and issuing licenses to individuals who demonstrate the necessary qualifications and knowledge in the laws of electricity and electrical codes. Electrical licensing in North Dakota is statewide and applies to individuals performing regulated electrical work.

North Dakota recognizes three categories of master electricians. A **contracting master** is responsible for following all laws and rules of the North Dakota wiring standards and must show proof of liability insurance. A **master of record** is responsible for following the laws and rules of the wiring standards for a partnership, company, corporation, limited liability company, or association and must be covered by the organization’s liability insurance. A **non-contracting master** has the same responsibility as a journeyman electrician and performs electrical work under the supervision of a contracting master or master of record.

The Master Electrician experience requirement includes one year as a licensed journeyman and at least 2,000 hours of work as a licensed journeyman electrician under the supervision of a contracting master electrician or master of record. This makes the master license an advancement credential for electricians who have already achieved journeyman licensing and gained additional supervised experience.

Journeyman licensing in North Dakota requires 8,000 hours of experience, which may not be obtained in less than three years, while registered as an apprentice electrician under proper supervision and in a jurisdiction with licensing and inspection rules similar to North Dakota. Credit may be granted for approved electrical schooling and certain qualifying experience categories according to Board rules.
