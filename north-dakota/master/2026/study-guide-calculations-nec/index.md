## Licensing Steps

North Dakota master electrician licensing is handled through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. A typical licensing path includes reviewing the master electrician requirements, completing the required application, submitting documentation, receiving an exam invite, preparing with code-based study materials, taking the required examination, passing the exam, and completing final licensing steps with the Board.

A North Dakota Master Electrician must have completed one year of experience as a licensed journeyman and must have at least 2,000 hours of experience working as a licensed journeyman electrician under the supervision of a contracting master electrician or master of record. This experience requirement is separate from study preparation and should be documented according to Board instructions.

After becoming a master electrician, North Dakota recognizes different master license statuses. A contracting master is responsible for following the laws and rules of the North Dakota wiring standards and must show proof of liability insurance. A master of record is responsible for wiring standards for the organization covered by that master of record. A non-contracting master has the same responsibility as a journeyman electrician and does not function in the same contracting role.

Candidates should keep copies of all important records, including license information, experience documentation, application materials, exam invite, scheduling details, identification information, score results, insurance documentation when applicable, and correspondence from the Board. Organized paperwork can help reduce delays between application, testing, and final license status selection.
