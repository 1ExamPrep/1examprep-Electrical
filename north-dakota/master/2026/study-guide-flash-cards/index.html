State Requirements

North Dakota electrician licensing is regulated by the North Dakota State Electrical Board. The Board examines applicants and issues licenses to individuals who have the necessary qualifications and knowledge of the laws of electricity and electrical codes.

A North Dakota Master Electrician must have completed one year of experience as a licensed journeyman and must have at least 2,000 hours of experience working as a licensed journeyman electrician under the supervision of a contracting master electrician or master of record.

North Dakota identifies three categories of master electricians. A contracting master is responsible for adhering to all laws and rules of the North Dakota wiring standards and must show proof of liability insurance. A master of record is responsible for adhering to all laws and rules of the North Dakota wiring standards for a partnership, company, corporation, limited liability company, or association and must show proof of liability insurance coverage through that organization. A non-contracting master is responsible for adhering to all laws and rules of the North Dakota wiring standards and has the same responsibility as a journeyman electrician.

The journeyman pathway in North Dakota includes 8,000 hours of experience, which may not be obtained in less than three years, while registered as an apprentice electrician under appropriate supervision. Credit may be granted for a graduate of a two-year or longer electrical school accepted by the Board. North Dakota also identifies specific experience-credit categories for practical electrical work, fire alarm technology circuits, process control circuits, power-limited systems, and qualifying armed forces electrical construction experience.

North Dakota also offers a Class B electrician license. A Class B electrician must complete 18 months and 3,000 hours of experience in farmstead or residential wiring in one-family or two-family dwellings under the supervision of a master or Class B electrician. This classification has a restricted scope and is different from the Master Electrician license path.
