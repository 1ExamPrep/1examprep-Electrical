Exam Details

North Dakota electrician licensing is handled through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. The Board examines applicants and issues electrical licenses to qualified individuals who demonstrate the required knowledge of electricity, electrical laws, electrical rules, electrical codes, and North Dakota wiring standards.

Candidates must apply and be approved before signing up for an exam date. After approval, the exam invitation provides the available testing dates, and the candidate must test within six months of receiving the invitation. Candidates who do not receive a passing score must submit the required re-exam application and fee after the applicable waiting period before receiving another exam invitation.

The North Dakota journeyman electrician examination is based on fundamental electricity, North Dakota laws, North Dakota rules, North Dakota wiring standards, and the current edition of the National Electrical Code. The passing grade is 70%. The North Dakota State Electrical Board provides code books, calculators, and pencils for the exam.

The North Dakota Journeyman exam includes a closed-book portion and an open-book portion. The journeyman closed-book portion is listed as 1 hour, and the journeyman open-book portion is listed as 3.5 hours. This format requires students to prepare for both memory-based understanding and reference-based code application.

Open Book Test

The North Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam includes an open-book portion. The open-book portion is listed as 3.5 hours, and the North Dakota State Electrical Board provides code books, calculators, and pencils for the exam. The exam also includes a 1-hour closed-book portion, so candidates should not prepare as though every answer can be found during the test.

Open book does not mean easy. Licensing Steps

North Dakota journeyman electrician licensing begins with meeting the qualifications established by the North Dakota State Electrical Board. Applicants must complete the required apprentice experience, complete apprentice electrician training, submit the required application materials, receive approval to test, pass the journeyman examination, and complete the remaining licensing steps required by the Board.

A North Dakota journeyman electrician must complete 8,000 hours of experience registered as an apprentice electrician. That experience may not be obtained in less than three years. The experience must be under the supervision of a contracting master or master of record licensed electrician in an area where electrical construction work is performed in a jurisdiction with licensing and inspection rules similar to North Dakota.

North Dakota allows up to 3,000 hours to apply under the qualifications of a Class B electrician. Two thousand hours of credit may be granted for a graduate of a two-year or longer electrical school accepted by the Board. Practical experience consists of a minimum of 4,000 hours, and up to 8,000 hours of credit may be granted for wiring for and installing electrical wiring, apparatus, and equipment.

Additional experience credit may be allowed in limited areas. North Dakota identifies possible credit for repairing electrical wiring, apparatus, and equipment for light, heat, and power; wiring fire alarm technology circuits or systems; wiring process control circuits or power-limited systems; and certain electrical construction experience gained in the armed forces when the Board determines the experience is equivalent to work performed under the supervision of a North Dakota licensed electrical contractor.

After completing the required experience and training, the candidate applies through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. Once the application is reviewed and approved, the candidate receives an exam invitation and signs up for an available exam date. Passing the examination is part of the licensing process, but candidates must complete all Board requirements before the license is issued.
