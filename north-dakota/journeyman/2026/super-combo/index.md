## Exam Details

The North Dakota Journeyman Electrician examination is administered through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. Applicants must apply and be approved before signing up for an exam date. After approval, the applicant receives an exam invitation listing available testing dates and must select an exam date through the Board process.

The North Dakota electrical examinations are based on fundamental electricity, North Dakota laws, North Dakota rules, North Dakota wiring standards, and the current edition of the National Electrical Code. The passing grade is 70%. Applicants must test within six months of receiving an exam invite.

## Licensing Steps

North Dakota candidates begin by confirming that the Journeyman Electrician license is the correct license classification. The North Dakota State Electrical Board issues electrical licenses and reviews applicant qualifications before authorizing candidates to take the examination.

A practical licensing path begins with registering and working as an apprentice electrician under the supervision of a contracting master or master of record licensed electrician in a jurisdiction with similar licensing and inspection rules to North Dakota. Candidates should complete the required experience and apprentice electrician training, document qualifying work, submit the examination application and employment verification, receive Board approval, select an available exam date, take the examination, and complete the licensing process after passing.

The Board states that a Journeyman electrician must have completed 8,000 hours of experience, which may not be obtained in less than three years, while registered as an apprentice electrician. Up to 3,000 hours may apply under Class B electrician qualifications. The experience must be under the supervision of a contracting master or master of record licensed electrician in an area with similar licensing and inspection rules to North Dakota, and the applicant must have successfully completed apprentice electrician training.

The Board may grant 2,000 hours of credit for a graduate of a two-year or longer electrical school accepted by the Board. Practical experience consists of wiring for and installing electrical wiring, apparatus, and equipment. Additional experience credit may be allowed in areas such as repair work, fire alarm technology circuits or systems, process control circuits or power limited systems, and qualifying electrical construction experience gained in the armed forces.

After the Board approves an application, the applicant receives an invitation to take the examination. If the examination is not taken in the allotted time, the applicant may be required to complete another application and pay the necessary fee. Passing the examination is an important milestone, but licensing is completed through the Board process.

## State Requirements

The North Dakota State Electrical Board regulates electrical licensing and has jurisdiction over electrical installations in the state. The Board examines applicants and issues licenses to those who have the required qualifications and knowledge of electrical laws and electrical codes.

North Dakota license classifications include master electrician, journeyman electrician, Class B electrician, and power limited electrician classifications. The Journeyman Electrician classification is intended for individuals who have completed the required apprentice experience, training, and examination process.

For a journeyman electrician license, North Dakota requires 8,000 hours of qualifying experience, which may not be obtained in less than three years, while registered as an apprentice electrician. The experience must be under proper supervision and in a jurisdiction with similar licensing and inspection rules. The applicant must also successfully complete apprentice electrician training.

North Dakota journeyman licenses expire each year on March 31. The annual journeyman renewal fee is $25. To renew, the licensee must have 8 hours of continuing education on file with the Board, including 4 hours of code. License renewals begin January 1 each year.

Because licensing requirements are controlled by the North Dakota State Electrical Board, candidates should follow the Board’s current forms, exam invitations, renewal requirements, and instructions. Study materials help candidates prepare, but the Board determines eligibility, exam approval, license issuance, renewal compliance, and enforcement of licensing requirements.
