## Licensing Steps

North Dakota journeyman electrician candidates begin by registering and working through the state’s apprenticeship or qualifying experience path. Apprentices must gain practical electrical construction experience under proper supervision and maintain records that support future licensure. Candidates should follow North Dakota State Electrical Board instructions for apprentice registration, experience documentation, and approved training records.

The standard journeyman path requires substantial electrical construction experience. North Dakota requires applicants to document qualifying experience under proper supervision, and the Board may grant credit for graduates of an electrical school accepted by the Board. The state’s licensing information states that **2,000 hours** of credit may be granted for a graduate of a two-year or longer electrical school accepted by the Board.

After gaining the required experience and completing the application process, the candidate applies to the North Dakota State Electrical Board for approval to take the journeyman examination. The application process should include all required experience records, training records when applicable, fees, and supporting documentation requested by the Board.

Once approved, the candidate follows the Board’s examination scheduling instructions. North Dakota examination information states that applicants must test within **six months** of receiving an exam invite. Candidates should review the current exam dates, exam location instructions, reference rules, identification requirements, score requirements, and rescheduling procedures before exam day.

After passing the examination, the candidate completes the remaining licensing requirements through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. Passing the exam is an important step, but the licensing process is not complete until the Board’s requirements are satisfied and the journeyman electrician license is issued.

## State Requirements

North Dakota requires electricians to be licensed through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. The Board has responsibility for examining applicants and issuing licenses to individuals who have the required qualifications and knowledge of electrical laws and electrical codes. This statewide licensing structure applies across North Dakota and is separate from local-only journeyman licensing systems used in some other states.

A North Dakota journeyman electrician applicant must document qualifying electrical construction experience under the supervision required by the Board. The commonly used experience path is built around **8,000 hours** of electrical experience, with Board-approved education credit available in limited circumstances. A graduate of a two-year or longer electrical school accepted by the Board may receive up to **2,000 hours** of credit.

North Dakota’s licensing requirements also include standards for where and how electrical construction work is performed. The Board’s application information explains that requirements include electrical construction work being done in a jurisdiction with licensing and inspection rules similar to North Dakota and under the supervision of a master electrician.

The North Dakota journeyman electrician license is different from a master electrician license. A master electrician applicant must first complete experience as a licensed journeyman and document the required hours working as a licensed journeyman electrician under the supervision of a contracting master electrician or master of record. Candidates should apply for the credential that matches their current experience and career goal.

License holders should also follow North Dakota State Electrical Board requirements for renewal, continuing education, and license maintenance. Continuing education and code updates help electricians stay current with electrical code changes, state rules, and safe installation practices.
