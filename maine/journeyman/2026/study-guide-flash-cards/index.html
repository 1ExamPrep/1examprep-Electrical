Exam Details

Maine electrician licensing is handled through the State of Maine Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Electricians’ Examining Board. The Board has contracted with Prov to provide examinations for approved candidates. Candidates must submit an examination application to the Electricians’ Examining Board and receive approval before scheduling an exam appointment.

The Maine Journeyman Electrician exam contains 80 questions and allows 3 hours for testing. The passing score is 70%. The exam is designed to test a candidate’s knowledge of planning, installation, repair, alteration, and maintenance of electric conductors, raceways, and equipment for light, heat, and power while employed by a Master Electrician.

The published Maine Journeyman Electrician exam subject areas include General Electrical Theory and Principles, Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Motors and Controls, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Special Conditions, and Communication Systems.

The exam outline assigns 10 questions to General Electrical Theory and Principles, 16 questions to Wiring and Protection, 16 questions to Wiring Methods and Materials, 10 questions to Motors and Controls, 10 questions to Equipment for General Use, 5 questions to Special Occupancies, 5 questions to Special Equipment, 5 questions to Special Conditions, and 3 questions to Communication Systems.

This combo supports preparation for those subject areas by giving students study guide review, repeated practice exams, full final exams, and flash card review. Students can use the practice exams to build exam familiarity, the final exams to simulate longer review sessions, and the flash cards to reinforce definitions, theory, calculations, wiring methods, equipment concepts, and code-related topics between longer study blocks.

Closed Book Test

Maine electrician examinations are delivered in closed-book format unless references are specifically marked as permitted for the exam. The Maine Journeyman Electrician exam materials list references for study, but the current candidate bulletin states that electrician examinations are closed book unless the listed references are marked as permitted.

Closed-book testing requires a different kind of preparation than an open-book exam. Licensing Steps

Maine journeyman electrician licensing begins with meeting the education and experience requirements established by the Electricians’ Examining Board. A Journeyman Electrician is a licensed individual making electrical installations in the employment of a Master Electrician, Limited Electrician, or electrical company.

Maine provides multiple pathways for journeyman electrician eligibility. One common pathway requires at least 8,000 hours of service as a licensed apprentice or helper and completion of a 576-hour program of study approved by the Electricians’ Examining Board or completed through an accredited institution. The 576 hours include required course areas such as Electricity I, Electricity II, Math I, Controls I, and a current National Electrical Code course, along with approved elective study.

Another pathway applies to licensed apprentices who have completed an approved apprenticeship program of study and have worked at least 4,000 hours as a licensed apprentice electrician. Those candidates may qualify to sit for the journeyman examination under the Board’s requirements.

Maine also recognizes certain electrical education pathways. Graduates of an accredited applied technology high school two-year electrical program may qualify with 7,000 hours of field experience under appropriate supervision and completion of a current National Electrical Code course. Graduates of a Maine community college electrical program or a vocational-electrical program of the Department of Corrections may qualify with 4,000 hours of field experience under appropriate supervision and completion of a current National Electrical Code course.

After meeting the applicable education and experience requirements, candidates submit an examination application to the Electricians’ Examining Board. Board approval is required before scheduling the exam. Once approved, the candidate receives exam scheduling instructions and completes the examination through Prov.

Students can use it while completing apprenticeship training, while documenting work hours, before applying for exam approval, after receiving approval, or during final review before the scheduled exam date.

State Requirements

Maine has statewide electrician licensing through the Electricians’ Examining Board under the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation. The licensing structure includes classifications such as apprentice or helper, journeyman electrician, senior journeyman electrician, master electrician, and limited electrician classifications.

For journeyman electrician applicants, Maine requirements are tied to documented electrical experience, approved education, and successful completion of the required examination. Candidates should follow the Board’s current instructions for application type, education documentation, work-hour documentation, exam approval, scheduling, licensing, renewal, and license status.

Students should keep accurate records of apprentice or helper registration, qualifying work experience, approved classroom training, National Electrical Code coursework, employer documentation, and exam approval notices. The licensing process is separate from exam preparation, and applicants must complete all state forms, documentation, fees, and Board requirements for the license to be issued.
