How to choose the right package for the Maine master exam

Journeyman or master

The journeyman exam tests the code knowledge a working electrician needs every day: branch circuits, feeders, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, box and conduit fill, and the motor and appliance articles. The master exam covers the same ground and then goes further into services, load calculations, transformers, planning and supervision, and the parts of the code a contractor signs off on. Pick the level that matches the license you are applying for. The study guide, calculations guide and flash cards are written separately for each level, so the two packages are not interchangeable.

What the five levels add

Every package starts with the same core: the 2026 state study guide with twelve practice exams and two full-length finals. The Study Guide + Flash Cards level adds flash cards for quick drills. Study Guide + NEC with Tabs swaps the flash cards for the 2026 National Electrical Code paperback with pre-printed tabs, which is the single biggest time saver on an open-book test. Study Guide + Calculations + NEC adds the calculations workbook, where most people lose points. The Super Combo puts all of those together, and the Ultimate Combo adds the online test success kit so you can practise timed exams on a phone or laptop between study sessions.

A simple way to decide

If you already own the current code book with tabs, start with Study Guide + Flash Cards and add the calculations guide if arithmetic under time pressure worries you. If you own nothing yet, the Super Combo is the cheapest way to get the whole paper set, and the Ultimate Combo is the right call if you have more than four weeks before the test and want the online practice. People who fail usually fail on time, not knowledge, so the tabs and the timed practice matter more than the number of books.

Before you buy

Which NEC edition are these based on?

Every product in this collection is built on the 2026 National Electrical Code. If your state is still testing on the 2023 or 2020 NEC, check your candidate bulletin first; our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep, also carries earlier editions.

Are these the actual exam questions?

No. Real exam questions are confidential. These are practice questions written to the same subjects, format and difficulty, with explanations.

What's the difference between Journeyman and Master?

Journeyman guides cover the journeyman-level exam; Master guides go deeper on calculations, planning and supervision topics for the master or contractor exam. Pick the license you are applying for.

When will I get it?

Online test success kits and courses are delivered by email after purchase. Books and code books ship from our fulfillment center, 1 Exam Prep; shipping options are shown at checkout.

Does buying make me eligible to test?

No. Eligibility, experience hours and exam approval come from your state licensing board. These materials get you ready for the test itself.
