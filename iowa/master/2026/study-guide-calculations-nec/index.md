The Iowa Master Electrician exam is administered through PSI for the Iowa Electrical Examining Board. The exam is computer-based and is designed to measure knowledge in the electrical trade, the National Electrical Code, and related master electrician subject areas.

## Licensing Steps

Iowa electrical licensing is handled through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board. Candidates seeking a master electrician license should begin by reviewing the license type they are applying for and confirming that they meet the applicable qualification requirements before testing.

A typical Iowa master electrician licensing path includes submitting the required license application or testing sponsorship request, receiving authorization to test, scheduling the examination with PSI, preparing with code-based study materials, passing the exam, and completing final licensing instructions from the Board. Candidates who are already licensed by the Board may follow different application steps depending on their status and license classification.

Once the Board approves a candidate to test, the candidate receives authorization to schedule the examination. The candidate is then responsible for contacting PSI, paying the required examination fee, and selecting an available testing appointment. PSI provides computer examination centers in Iowa and other available testing locations.

Preparation should begin before the exam is scheduled whenever possible. Candidates can use this combo to create a weekly study plan that rotates between master electrician review, calculation practice, and NEC navigation. This helps prevent one-sided preparation and gives candidates repeated exposure to the topics they are most likely to encounter.

After passing the examination, candidates should follow the Board’s instructions for completing the licensing process. Licensing approval, final credential issuance, and any additional requirements are handled by the Iowa Electrical Examining Board.
