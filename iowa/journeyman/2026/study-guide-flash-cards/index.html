Exam Details

Iowa electrician licensing is administered through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board under the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing. Iowa has statewide electrical licensing for individual electricians, including journeyman electrician licensing. The Iowa electrical examinations are administered through PSI Services LLC.

The Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam is a timed, open-book examination. The exam contains 80 scored questions, and the time allowed for the scored portion is 180 minutes. The passing score is 70%. Some Iowa exam information also identifies additional non-scored items that may appear for exam development, with additional time allowed when they are included.

The Iowa journeyman exam content outline includes General Electrical Knowledge, Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, and Special Conditions. Candidates should prepare for both code lookup and applied electrical understanding. The exam is designed to test whether candidates can use electrical knowledge, code concepts, formulas, and installation rules in a journeyman-level testing environment.

Open Book Test

The Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam is an open-book test. Open book does not mean the exam is easy. Candidates still need to understand electrical concepts, recognize how exam questions are written, move through approved references efficiently, and answer questions within the time allowed.

Open-book electrical exams reward preparation and reference familiarity. Students should practice using the table of contents, index, article structure, definitions, tables, notes, exceptions, and common code sections before exam day. The more comfortable a student is with the reference material, the less time is wasted searching during the test.

Students should use only approved reference materials during the actual exam and follow the current PSI and Iowa Electrical Examining Board rules for tabs, highlighting, notes, loose papers, calculators, and exam-room materials. Study guides, practice exams, flash cards, handwritten notes, and unapproved materials should be used before test day unless the current exam rules specifically allow them in the testing room.

Licensing Steps

Iowa journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board. Candidates should create or access their licensing account, follow the Board’s application instructions, and complete the proper pathway for the license they are pursuing.

Iowa provides multiple application paths, including application by examination, application by apprentice program, and application by proof of work experience. Candidates applying by examination must satisfy the Board’s qualification requirements and complete the required testing process. The correct application route depends on the candidate’s training, apprenticeship history, work experience, and current status.

The traditional journeyman pathway generally includes completion of an approved apprenticeship program or other training and experience accepted by the Board. Iowa recognizes registered apprenticeship training and also recognizes certain approved technical school programs that may provide a path toward journeyman licensing when combined with documented on-the-job training.

Students should keep accurate records of apprenticeship participation, classroom training, approved schooling, employer documentation, work experience, and exam results. The licensing process is separate from exam preparation, and applicants must complete all state forms, documentation, fees, and Board requirements for the license to be issued.

After a candidate is eligible and approved for testing, the examination is scheduled through PSI. Once the exam is passed, the candidate follows the Iowa Electrical Examining Board’s instructions for the license application or license issuance process. Passing the exam is an important step, but candidates must still complete all remaining Board requirements for licensure.

This study guide and flash card combo supports the exam preparation stage of the licensing process. Students can use it while completing apprenticeship training, while documenting work hours, before scheduling the exam, after receiving testing approval, or during final review before the scheduled exam date.
