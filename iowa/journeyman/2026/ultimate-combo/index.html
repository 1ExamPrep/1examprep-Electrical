Exam Details

The Iowa journeyman electrician examination is part of the statewide licensing process through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board. The Board’s electrical examination program is administered through PSI Services LLC. Candidates must follow the current Iowa and PSI instructions for examination registration, scheduling, testing rules, and licensing steps.

The Iowa journeyman electrician exam requires a passing score of 70%. The State of Iowa testing information states that passing the PSI exam with a score of 70% or higher can qualify a candidate for Iowa electrical license classifications such as Class A Master, Class A Journeyman, Residential Master, Residential Electrician, and certain Special Electrician licenses, depending on the license type and qualification pathway.

Iowa journeyman candidates should prepare for an NEC-based examination that measures both electrical code knowledge and electrical theory. The exam may include questions involving general electrical knowledge, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and electrical calculations. Candidates should also be prepared to use the National Electrical Code as a technical reference and apply code rules under timed testing conditions.

Exam fees, scheduling windows, reference rules, code editions, and candidate procedures are controlled by Iowa and PSI. Candidates should make sure they are registered for the correct exam classification and should review the current candidate information before test day.

Open Book Test

The Iowa electrical licensing exam program uses open-book testing for electrical exams. Candidates are expected to follow the approved reference rules issued by the testing provider and the state. Open book testing allows the use of approved references, but it still requires strong preparation and efficient code navigation.

Open book does not mean the exam is easy. The National Electrical Code is a large technical reference, and candidates who are unfamiliar with its layout can lose time searching through the wrong sections. A prepared candidate should know how to identify the subject of the question, locate the correct article or table, understand the wording, and apply the rule correctly.

The included National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback with Tabs is designed to help candidates practice code navigation before exam day. During study, candidates can use the tabbed NEC to become more familiar with definitions, general requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, communications systems, tables, and calculation-related provisions.

Good open-book preparation includes timed lookup practice. Candidates should work through practice questions, identify the topic, find the likely NEC article or table, answer the question, and then review the reasoning. This process builds speed and confidence for code-based exam questions.

Exam-room rules may control what references are allowed, how books may be tabbed, whether notes are permitted, and which code edition applies. The NEC included with this package is intended for study and code-navigation preparation. Candidates should follow the official exam instructions for the materials allowed during the actual Iowa examination.

Licensing Steps

Iowa electrical licensing is handled through the statewide electrical licensing system under the Iowa Electrical Examining Board. Candidates applying for a new license may apply by examination, by apprentice program, or by proof of work experience, depending on the license type and qualification path.

A typical Iowa journeyman electrician path includes completing qualifying supervised electrical work experience, completing an approved apprenticeship or accepted training pathway when applicable, gathering documentation, applying for the proper license classification, taking the required PSI examination if testing is required, receiving a passing score, and completing the remaining Iowa licensing requirements.

Iowa recognizes Class A and Class B journeyman electrician licenses. A Class A journeyman electrician license is commonly associated with completion of an approved apprenticeship training program or another training and experience path accepted by the Board, along with meeting examination requirements. Class B journeyman electrician licensing is tied to specific experience-based pathways and state rules.

Candidates should organize work history, apprenticeship records, classroom training records, employer documentation, identification, and any other materials required for the license application. The licensing application is reviewed by the state, and applicants may be contacted for payment or additional information after submission.
