## Licensing Steps

The Iowa journeyman electrician licensing path begins with selecting the correct license classification and building the experience required for that path. Iowa offers Class A and Class B journeyman electrician licenses, and the requirements differ. The Class A Journeyman Electrician license is the examination-based journeyman credential commonly pursued by candidates who complete apprenticeship training and pass the approved journeyman examination.

A common Class A journeyman pathway includes successfully completing a registered apprenticeship program, completing four years of experience as an apprentice electrician, and passing a supervised written journeyman electrician examination approved by the Board with a score of 70% or higher within the required application period. Candidates should maintain complete records of apprenticeship registration, program completion, work experience, and examination results.

Iowa also recognizes certain experience-based and prior-license pathways under Board rules. A Class B journeyman electrician license may be available to individuals who meet the required experience standards under the applicable rules. Candidates should review the Iowa Electrical Examining Board requirements for the specific license class they are pursuing because Class A, Class B, residential, master, and contractor licenses are not the same credential.

Before testing, candidates must satisfy Iowa’s testing sponsorship requirements. After eligibility is established, the candidate schedules the exam through PSI and follows the current testing instructions. The candidate should review the PSI bulletin for identification requirements, check-in rules, reference material rules, calculator rules, exam format, and score reporting procedures.

After passing the required examination, the applicant completes the license application process through Iowa’s electrical licensing system. The applicant should submit the required application, fees, and documentation according to the current Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing instructions. Passing the exam is an important step, but license issuance still depends on meeting the Board’s requirements and completing the required licensing process.
