## Licensing Steps

Nebraska journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Nebraska State Electrical Division. Candidates begin by confirming that they meet the journeyman electrician qualifications, submitting the appropriate application, receiving approval to test, scheduling the examination through PSI, and completing the remaining licensing process after the exam.

A Nebraska applicant for a journeyman electrician license must have at least four years of verifiable experience, equal to 8,000 hours, acceptable to the board in the electrical trade. Registration as an apprentice electrician for those years may constitute evidence of that experience when approved by the board. Nebraska also allows one year of work experience credit for completion of a two-year post-high school electrical course with an associate degree in electrical technology. Qualified electrical experience gained in military service may also be considered for work experience credit.

If a candidate’s verifiable experience includes less than 4,000 hours in the State of Nebraska, the candidate may receive a restricted license that cannot reciprocate with any other state. Candidates seeking full license mobility should pay attention to Nebraska’s in-state experience and reciprocity rules as they apply to their situation.

The practical path begins with documenting qualifying experience, completing the Nebraska electrician registration or license application, submitting the application fee, receiving exam approval, scheduling the exam through PSI, preparing with the exam content areas and approved references, completing the exam, and finalizing the license process through the state.

Passing the examination is an important milestone, but licensing is completed through the Nebraska State Electrical Division. The state determines whether an applicant has met the qualifications, submitted the required documentation, passed the proper examination, and satisfied all licensing requirements.
