## Licensing Steps

Nebraska journeyman electrician licensing begins with meeting the minimum qualifications established by the Nebraska State Electrical Board. An applicant for a journeyman electrician license must have at least four years of verifiable experience, equal to 8,000 hours, acceptable to the Board, in the electrical trade. Registration as an apprentice electrician for those years may, upon Board approval, constitute evidence of qualifying experience.

A typical Nebraska journeyman path begins with apprentice registration and supervised electrical work. Candidates should maintain accurate records of employment, dates worked, hours completed, supervision, apprenticeship registration, and electrical trade experience. Good documentation helps support the application when the candidate is ready to apply for the journeyman examination.

After completing the required experience, the candidate submits the required application materials through the Nebraska State Electrical Division process. Exam applications must be approved before the candidate may proceed with testing. Candidates should follow state instructions for application forms, supporting documentation, fees, and scheduling procedures.

After approval, the candidate schedules and takes the required examination through the current testing process. Passing the examination is part of the licensing process, but candidates must also complete all licensing steps required by the Nebraska State Electrical Division. Passing candidates may receive continuing education credit toward license renewal as described in Nebraska exam information.

Nebraska also has license classifications beyond journeyman electrician, including contractor-related and residential classifications. Candidates should choose the license type that matches their work goals and qualifications. A journeyman electrician license is not the same as an electrical contractor license, and contractor licensing may involve additional experience, examination, business, and responsibility requirements.
