Open Book Test

The Nebraska journeyman electrician examination is listed as an open-book examination. The candidate bulletin identifies allowed reference materials and explains that reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed, but must otherwise be unmarked and may not contain additional loose or attached papers. It also states that references may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only, and temporary tabs such as Post-it notes are not allowed.

Open-book testing still requires serious preparation. Having reference materials available does not mean the answer will be easy to find. Candidates must know how the NEC is organized, how to identify the subject of a question, which article or table applies, and how to read code language accurately. Many mistakes happen because candidates search in the wrong section, overlook an exception, use the wrong table, or misunderstand the installation condition described in the question.

The included NEC tabs are designed to support open-book study by helping candidates become more comfortable moving through the code book. During study sessions, candidates can use the tabs to locate major chapters, articles, and tables while answering practice questions. The more often candidates practice finding code sections, the more familiar the book becomes.

A good open-book study method is to answer a question, locate the related NEC section, read the full rule, and then review why the answer is correct. Candidates should not rely only on memorizing answer choices. The stronger skill is learning how to find and apply the rule. This is especially important for questions involving general code requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, and communication systems.

Candidates should also practice time management. During an open-book exam, every question requires a decision: answer from knowledge, look up the code, calculate carefully, or mark the question for review. Timed practice helps candidates become more comfortable making those decisions while maintaining accuracy.
