## Licensing Steps

Local requirements may include documented electrical work experience, apprenticeship completion, classroom training, employment verification, affidavits, application forms, fees, and approval before testing.

Once the application is reviewed and approved, the candidate schedules the required examination through the local testing process or approved testing provider. A local journeyman electrician credential may allow the holder to perform electrical work within the issuing jurisdiction under the conditions established by that authority. A local credential may not automatically transfer to every city, township, borough, or county in Pennsylvania, so electricians should confirm where the credential is accepted.
