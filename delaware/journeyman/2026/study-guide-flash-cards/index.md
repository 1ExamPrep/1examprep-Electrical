## Licensing Steps

Delaware journeyperson electrician licensure is handled through the Delaware Division of Professional Regulation and the Board of Electrical Examiners. Delaware requires a professional electrician license to perform electrical services in the state. Electrical services are defined around work covered by the National Electrical Code as adopted by the Delaware State Fire Commission.

A Delaware Journeyperson Electrician license allows the license holder to perform electrical services under supervision while gaining additional experience and developing the skills needed for higher electrical licensure classifications. Delaware provides several application pathways for journeyperson licensure, including examination, reciprocity, and certification.

Candidates applying by examination must first create a DELPROS account and submit the appropriate licensure application through the Delaware online portal. The Board reviews the application before the candidate is approved to sit for the exam. After approval, the candidate receives exam registration information from ProV.

Once approved, the candidate schedules the examination through ProV. Delaware offers traditional in-person testing at Delaware testing locations and online testing through ProV’s Examroom service when available. Testing is arranged by scheduled appointment.

After passing the examination, the candidate must complete the remaining licensing requirements through the Division of Professional Regulation. The Board office receives the official examination score, validates the licensing requirements, and issues the license through the state’s licensing system.

This study guide and flash card combo supports the exam preparation stage of the licensing process. Students can use it while completing apprenticeship requirements, while preparing the DELPROS application, after receiving Board approval, or during final review before the scheduled exam date.
