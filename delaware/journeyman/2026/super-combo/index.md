## Open Book Test

The Delaware Journeyperson Electrician exam is an open book test. The Delaware Division of Professional Regulation candidate bulletin states that all exams are open book and timed. The bulletin also identifies the books that may be used during testing for the Journeyperson Electrician examination.

Prov’s reference material rules allow candidates to prepare reference books by highlighting text and placing permanent tabs on important pages. Handwritten notes are not allowed in any portion of a reference book. Moveable tabs are never permitted. Post-it notes are not permitted. Photocopied documents cannot be added to a reference book, and photocopied versions of a reference book are not permitted.

Test preparation study guides are not approved references and are not permitted in the testing center, including practice examinations. Candidates may not share reference materials during testing and may not highlight, underline, mark, or write any information in reference materials during the examination.

Open book testing still requires serious preparation. ## Licensing Steps

The Delaware journeyperson electrician licensing path begins with choosing the correct application type through DELPROS, the Delaware Professional Regulation online portal. The available journeyperson application paths include examination, reciprocity, and certification. The correct path depends on whether the applicant holds a qualifying certificate of completion, holds a current journeyperson license from another jurisdiction, or needs to apply by examination.

An applicant who applies by examination must pass the journeyperson examination. The applicant creates or logs into a DELPROS account, begins the licensure application, and submits the required documents. The Delaware Board of Electrical Examiners reviews the application before the applicant may sit for the exam.

After Board approval, the candidate receives notification that they have been approved to sit for the examination. The Board liaison forwards the candidate’s registration information to Prov, and Prov contacts the candidate with examination information and scheduling instructions. Delaware offers traditional in-person testing at Delaware testing locations and online testing through Prov’s Examroom service.

The candidate schedules the examination, follows Prov’s test-day rules, brings required identification, and uses only approved reference materials. After passing, the Board office receives the official examination score, validates that the candidate has met the requirements for licensure, and issues the license. The candidate receives email notification once the license has been issued.

A practical preparation path includes reviewing Delaware’s licensing requirements, submitting the correct DELPROS application, waiting for Board approval, studying the exam outline, practicing calculations, building NEC navigation skills, completing flash card review, scheduling the exam, and following the Board’s licensing steps after testing.

## State Requirements

Delaware requires individuals who perform electrical services to hold a Delaware professional license as an electrician. The Board of Electrical Examiners recognizes several types of electrician licensure, including Master, Master Special, Limited, Limited Special, Journeyperson, Residential, and Apprentice.

A Journeyperson Electrician license allows the holder to perform electrical services under supervision while gaining more experience and developing the skills needed to pass examinations for Master, Limited, Master Special, or Limited Special Electrician licensure.

Delaware provides several ways to apply for a Journeyperson Electrician license. Applicants may apply by certificate if they hold a certificate of completion issued by the Department of Labor or an approved apprenticeship program in Delaware or another jurisdiction. Applicants may apply by examination if they do not hold a current qualifying journeyperson license and do not have a qualifying certificate of completion. Applicants may apply by reciprocity if they hold a current journeyperson license issued by another state, U.S. territory, or the District of Columbia and meet Delaware’s reciprocity standards.

For candidates who apply by examination, the Board must approve the applicant before testing. The examination is administered through Prov, and the candidate must obtain the required passing score to proceed through the licensure process.
