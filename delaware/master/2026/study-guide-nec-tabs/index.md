## Open Book Test

The Delaware Master Electrician examination is open book and timed. Prov’s Delaware candidate information states that all examinations are designed to allow candidates to use authorized references while testing. Candidates must use approved references listed for the examination and must follow the reference-material rules provided by Prov.

Reference materials are checked by the Prov test administrator before candidates are permitted into the testing room. Candidates may prepare reference materials by highlighting, underlining in pen only, and attaching permanent tabs. Permanent tabs are allowed when they are fixed to the book. Moveable tabs, repositionable tabs, and Post-it style tabs are not permitted in a reference book.

Candidates may not bring loose notes, unauthorized papers, extra documents, or unapproved materials into the examination. Reference books that do not meet the testing rules may be rejected by the test administrator. Candidates should prepare their books carefully before exam day and make sure their tabs, highlighting, and underlining follow Prov’s rules.

A simple 4-function calculator is permitted. PDAs, tablets, cell phones, communication devices, and special calculators are not allowed in the testing room. Candidates should also follow all identification, check-in, scheduling, testing-center, and conduct rules provided by Prov.

The open-book format makes code-book preparation important. Candidates should not wait until exam day to learn where information is located. A strong preparation routine includes using the NEC paperback regularly, practicing with tabs, locating definitions, reviewing tables, reading exceptions, and learning how related code sections connect. The goal is to become comfortable enough with the NEC that finding information becomes part of a steady problem-solving process.

## Licensing Steps

The Delaware master electrician licensing process begins with selecting the correct license type and applying through DELPROS, the Delaware Professional Regulation Online Services portal. Candidates pursuing Master Electrician, Limited Electrician, Master Electrician Special, or Limited Electrician Special licensure may apply by examination or by reciprocity, depending on whether they hold a current license of the same type from another state, territory, or the District of Columbia.

Applicants who apply by examination must submit the required application and supporting documentation for Board review. The Board of Electrical Examiners must approve the applicant to sit for the examination. After approval, the candidate receives an email notification and separate examination registration information from Prov.

Once approved, the candidate schedules the examination through Prov. Delaware offers in-person testing at Delaware locations and online testing through the Examroom service when available. Testing is arranged by scheduled appointment. Candidates should review the candidate bulletin carefully before the exam, including permitted testing materials, reference rules, exam criteria, sample questions, and score-reporting procedures.

After passing the examination, Master Electrician and Limited Electrician applicants must submit proof of liability insurance of at least $300,000 if it has not already been submitted. The Board office receives the official examination score and validates that the applicant has met the requirements for licensure. After validation, the license is issued through the state process.

Candidates who do not pass may retake the examination according to Prov’s retesting rules. Delaware’s exam page states that a candidate must obtain the minimum passing score and may contact Prov for another attempt if needed. Preparation with this combo can help candidates use retake study time more effectively by identifying weak topic areas and returning to the NEC for focused review.

## State Requirements

The Delaware Board of Electrical Examiners regulates electrician licensing through the Division of Professional Regulation. Delaware requires a professional electrician license to perform electrical services. The state defines electrical services and electrical work by reference to work covered by the National Electrical Code as adopted by the Delaware State Fire Commission.

Delaware’s electrical license types include Master Electrician, Master Electrician Special, Limited Electrician, Limited Electrician Special, Journeyperson Electrician, Residential Electrician, and Apprentice Electrician. Journeyperson Electrician licensure allows a person to perform electrical services under supervision while gaining experience and developing the skills needed for other electrician license types. Master Electrician licensure is the advanced general electrical license category for candidates who meet the state’s education, experience, examination, and insurance requirements.

For Master Electrician, Limited Electrician, and special license applicants, the required proof of experience depends on the license type and whether the applicant completed a registered or approved apprenticeship program. Applicants who completed a registered or approved apprenticeship program may submit a certificate of completion. Applicants who have not completed an approved apprenticeship program must submit verification of employment forms showing full-time experience under the supervision of a licensed master or limited electrician.

Master Electrician applicants should understand that licensing is more than passing the exam. The process may include documenting education or experience, completing the state application, receiving Board approval, passing the examination, submitting required insurance documentation, and waiting for the Board office to validate the application before the license is issued.
