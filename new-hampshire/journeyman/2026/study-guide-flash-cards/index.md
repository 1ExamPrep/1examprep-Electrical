## Licensing Steps

New Hampshire journeyman electrician licensing begins with meeting the qualification requirements established by the Electricians’ Board. Candidates should register properly as apprentices when required, complete supervised electrical work, document qualifying experience, complete required education or training when applicable, and apply through the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification using the appropriate journeyman application materials.

A typical New Hampshire journeyman path includes completing approximately 8,000 hours of qualifying supervised electrical experience. Candidates should maintain accurate records of employment, dates worked, hours completed, job duties, supervision, apprentice status, classroom training, and any supporting documentation needed for the licensing application. Good records help support the application when the candidate is ready to seek approval for the journeyman examination.

New Hampshire candidates may also need to document related education or training as part of the licensing process. Apprenticeship and classroom requirements can be important for demonstrating readiness for the journeyman license. Applicants should use the current state application checklist and Board instructions for the exact documents, signatures, forms, and payment requirements that apply to their path.

After meeting the required qualifications, the candidate submits the required application materials to the state licensing office. Once the candidate is approved for examination, the candidate follows the testing-provider instructions for scheduling and taking the exam. Passing the examination is an important step, but candidates must still complete all licensing requirements before the license is issued.

New Hampshire also has master electrician licensing. A journeyman electrician who later wants to pursue master licensure must meet additional state requirements, including additional experience as a licensed journeyman and successful completion of the master examination when required.
