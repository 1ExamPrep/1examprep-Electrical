## Licensing Steps

New Hampshire journeyman electrician candidates begin by meeting the state’s qualification requirements for the license. A journeyman applicant must obtain **8,000 hours** of practical or field experience as an apprentice to a journeyman or master electrician licensed by the Board.

In addition to the practical experience requirement, the applicant must meet one of New Hampshire’s approved education or alternative qualification paths. One path is completion of **600 hours** of electrical schooling, including at least **24 hours** of electrical safety. This schooling may be completed in approved course blocks or through an associate or higher degree in an electrical curriculum.

New Hampshire also provides other qualification paths for certain applicants. A candidate may qualify through 10 years of experience as a journeyman or master electrician licensed in another jurisdiction, or through having previously taken the journeyman or master examination in New Hampshire, when allowed under the Board’s rules.

After the candidate meets the qualification requirements, the candidate submits the proper application materials to the New Hampshire Office of Professional Licensure and Certification. Journeyman and master applicants use the Board’s application process for electrician licensure. Exam applicants must provide professional experience documentation, including verification of the required on-the-job training.

After the Board determines that the candidate is eligible, the testing information is provided to Prov. The candidate may then schedule the examination through Prov. Candidates should review the current candidate bulletin, exam fee information, approved references, testing format, test-day rules, remote testing procedures when applicable, identification requirements, and rescheduling rules before exam day.

After passing the examination, the candidate completes the remaining licensing requirements through the New Hampshire Electricians’ Board. Passing the exam is an important step, but the licensing process is not complete until the Board’s requirements are satisfied and the license is issued.

## State Requirements

New Hampshire requires state licensing for individuals who perform electrical work or use a title, sign, card, advertisement, or other device indicating that they are engaged in electrical work. Licensing is handled by the New Hampshire Electricians’ Board under the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification.

To qualify for a New Hampshire journeyman electrician license, an applicant must obtain **8,000 hours** of practical or field experience as an apprentice under a journeyman or master electrician licensed by the Board. The applicant must also meet an approved education or alternative qualification requirement.

The education path requires **600 hours** of electrical schooling, including a minimum of **24 hours** on electrical safety. New Hampshire rules also allow the schooling requirement to be met through an associate or higher degree in an electrical curriculum. The Board may provide credit toward practical experience for completion of prescribed electrical installation courses at approved schools, subject to state limits and documentation requirements.

New Hampshire also recognizes certain alternative qualification paths, including 10 years of experience as a journeyman or master electrician licensed in another jurisdiction or having previously taken the New Hampshire journeyman or master examination. Applicants using an alternative pathway should submit the required documentation through the Board’s licensing process.

A New Hampshire journeyman electrician license is different from a master electrician license. The master electrician path requires passing the journeyman examination and obtaining additional field experience as a licensed journeyman before taking the master examination. Candidates should apply for the credential that matches their current experience, work role, and career goal.
