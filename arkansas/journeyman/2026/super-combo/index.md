## Licensing Steps

The Arkansas journeyman electrician licensing process begins with the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners. An applicant must complete the appropriate application with the Board before testing. Applications are posted through the Board’s licensing process, and candidates must be approved before scheduling the examination with Prov.

After the Board approves the applicant for testing, the candidate is notified that they are eligible to contact Prov and schedule the Arkansas Journeyman Electrician examination. The candidate’s eligibility period is listed as six months in the current Arkansas electrical candidate bulletin. Candidates should schedule within the approved eligibility window and take the exam that matches the license classification they are seeking.

The exam is administered through Prov’s testing program. Candidates follow Prov scheduling instructions, bring required identification, follow all test-day rules, and use only approved materials during the examination. Because Prov is not authorized to determine which examination a candidate must take, license classification questions should be resolved directly with the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners before scheduling.

After taking the examination, candidates receive results through the testing process, and Prov shares the test result with the Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing Electrical Examining Board. Candidates who pass continue through the Board’s licensing process. Candidates who do not pass may retake the failed examination according to the retesting rules and eligibility period.

A practical preparation path includes reviewing Arkansas licensing requirements, submitting the correct application, receiving testing approval, studying the exam outline, practicing calculations, building NEC navigation skills, completing flash card review, scheduling the exam, and following the Board’s licensing steps after testing.
