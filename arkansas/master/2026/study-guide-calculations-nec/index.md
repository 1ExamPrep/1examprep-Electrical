## Open Book Test

Arkansas electrical licensing examinations administered by Prov are open book and timed. Candidates may use authorized references during the exam. Reference materials are checked by the test administrator before the candidate is allowed into the testing room. Approved references may be prepared only by highlighting, underlining in pen, and attaching permanent tabs.

Arkansas testing rules do not allow handwritten notes in any portion of a reference book. Moveable tabs are not permitted. Reference books may not contain Post-it notes, photocopied documents, or photocopied versions of reference books. Test preparation study guides and practice examinations are not approved references and are not permitted in the testing center.

Candidates may bring approved references, pencils, and a calculator into the testing center. Only simple four-function calculators are permitted. Tablets, cell phones, PDAs, and special calculators are not allowed. Candidates may not share references during testing and may not highlight, underline, mark, or write anything in their reference materials during the exam.

Open-book electrical exams still require strong preparation. The time limit does not allow candidates to slowly search the code book for every answer. Students need to know how the NEC is organized, how to use the index, how to recognize the correct article, and how to work through tables and calculations efficiently. Practicing with the NEC before test day helps reduce wasted time and improves confidence.
