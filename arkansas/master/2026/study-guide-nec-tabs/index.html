Exam Details

The Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing oversees the licensing process for electricians, and the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners contracts with Prov, Inc. to administer the electrical licensing examinations. Candidates must complete the appropriate application with the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners before scheduling the exam. Once approved for testing, the Board notifies the candidate that they are eligible to contact Prov to schedule the examination.

The Arkansas Master Electrician exam is listed by Prov as an open-book, timed examination. The current Arkansas electrical candidate information bulletin lists the Arkansas Master Electrician exam as 60 questions with a time allowance of 3 hours. The passing score for Arkansas electrical licensing examinations administered through Prov is 70%.

Open Book Test

The Arkansas electrical examinations administered through Prov are open book and timed. Prov states that all Arkansas electrical exams allow the use of authorized references, and the Arkansas Master Electrician exam description identifies the National Electrical Code as the reference for the exam. Candidates are responsible for using authorized reference materials that match the exam description and must bring approved references into the testing center.

Prov allows candidates to prepare reference materials by highlighting, underlining in pen only, and attaching permanent tabs. Permanent tabs are allowed when they are fixed to the book. Moveable tabs are not permitted in a reference book. Repositionable tabs, Post-it style tabs, loose notes, formula sheets, stickers, and added documents are not allowed unless specifically authorized by the licensing jurisdiction.

Handwritten notes are not allowed in any portion of a reference book unless authorized by the licensing or certifying jurisdiction. Candidates may not write in, mark, highlight, underline, or add notes to books during the examination. Books are checked by the Prov test administrator before candidates are permitted to enter the testing room.

These rules make tabbing and code-book organization an important part of preparation. Candidates who practice with a tabbed NEC during study can become more comfortable locating articles, tables, definitions, and requirements. The goal is to build a steady process: read the question carefully, identify the topic, locate the relevant NEC provision, apply the rule to the facts, and select the best answer.

A simple 4-function calculator is permitted under Prov’s approved-items rules. Candidates should follow the testing-center instructions for calculators, identification, approved references, prohibited items, check-in procedures, and conduct during the examination.

Licensing Steps

The Arkansas master electrician licensing process begins with the correct application through the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners. Candidates must apply to the Board before scheduling an exam through Prov. The Board determines eligibility and notifies approved applicants when they may contact Prov to schedule their examination.

After receiving approval, candidates schedule the Arkansas Master Electrician examination through Prov. Prov offers computer-based testing and provides the candidate information bulletin, exam descriptions, scheduling instructions, testing rules, reference-material rules, and exam-day procedures. Candidates select the proper exam for the license they are pursuing and follow the scheduling instructions provided by Prov.

On exam day, candidates bring required identification, approved reference materials, allowed items, and any materials permitted by the bulletin. Reference books are reviewed before entry into the testing room. Candidates complete the timed exam and receive results according to the reporting process used by Prov and the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners.

After the exam, passing results are shared with the state licensing authority. The Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners handles licensing decisions, license issuance, renewal, reciprocity, and state compliance requirements. Candidates who do not pass may retest under the retesting policy provided in the candidate bulletin and any eligibility rules established by the Board.

Preparation should begin before the scheduling window becomes stressful. Consistent preparation helps reduce the chance of wasting time searching through the code book without a clear plan.
