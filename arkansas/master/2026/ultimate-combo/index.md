## Exam Details

The Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing oversees electrician licensing through the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners. Arkansas offers several electrical license classifications, including master electrician, journeyman electrician, residential master electrician, residential journeyman electrician, air conditioning electrician, industrial maintenance electrician, and apprentice registration. The Board of Electrical Examiners is responsible for the administration of Arkansas electrician licensing laws and establishes standards for electrical work in the state.

The Arkansas Master Electrician examination is administered through Prov for licensing through the Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing. The candidate bulletin lists the Arkansas Master Electrician exam in English or Spanish, with 60 questions and 3 hours allowed. The listed subject areas include grounding and bonding, general knowledge of the electrical trade and calculations, service, feeders, branch feeders and overcurrent protection, raceways and enclosures, conductors, motors and controls, utilization and general-use equipment, special occupancies and equipment, low voltage circuits including alarms and communications, and safety.

Arkansas exam candidates must complete the appropriate application process with the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners before testing. After approval, the Board notifies the candidate that they are eligible to contact Prov to schedule the examination. The testing bulletin states that candidates are eligible to take the exam for a limited eligibility period after approval. Exam results are scored at the testing center, and the State is notified of the results.

The passing score listed in the Arkansas electrical testing bulletin is 70%. Candidates who do not pass may retest under the retesting rules described by the testing provider and the Board. Candidates should use the correct application and follow the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners’ current instructions for scheduling, eligibility, identification, exam fees, and license processing.

## Licensing Steps

Arkansas Master Electrician candidates begin by working through the licensing process established by the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners. The licensing path generally includes meeting the required experience or training qualifications, completing the appropriate Board application, receiving approval to test, scheduling the exam with the approved testing provider, passing the required examination, and completing any remaining steps required by the Board before a license is issued.

Candidates should gather work history documentation and any required verification forms before applying. Arkansas provides an electrician application process and work verification affidavit resources through the electrical licensing section. ## State Requirements

Arkansas requires state licensing for individuals who perform electrical work or represent themselves as engaged in electrical work. The Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners administers electrician licensing and registers electrical apprentices. The Board also establishes standards for the performance of electrical work in Arkansas, while code enforcement responsibilities are handled through the Department of Labor and Licensing structure.

Arkansas recognizes multiple electrical license classifications, so candidates should select the correct license type before applying. The Master Electrician classification is separate from the journeyman, residential, air conditioning, industrial maintenance, and apprentice categories. Candidates should not schedule an exam until they are sure which examination is required for the license being sought.

Continuing education is part of maintaining electrical licensure in Arkansas. The electrical licensing section identifies approved continuing education resources and tracking through StateReporting.com. Electricians should keep their license current, follow renewal instructions, and complete Board-approved continuing education as required for their license classification.
