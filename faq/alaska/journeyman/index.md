## The exam

### Is the Alaska Electrical Journeyman exam open book?

Yes. Alaska testing information lists the Electrical Journeyman exam as open book. Candidates should follow the current testing instructions for approved reference materials and exam-day procedures.

### How much time is allowed for the Alaska Electrical Journeyman exam?

Alaska testing information lists a four-hour maximum time limit for exams.

### What score is required to pass?

Alaska testing information states that a score of at least 70 percent is required for passing.

### How many hours are required for the Alaska electrician journeyman certificate of fitness?

Alaska requires 8,000 hours of qualifying experience for the electrician journeyman certificate of fitness. State rules limit how certain types of experience may be counted, including residential work, classroom training, power lineman experience, and maintenance work.

### What score is required to pass the Alaska journeyman electrician exam?

Alaska’s Mechanical Inspection FAQ states that passing the examination with a score of 70% or more allows the state to complete the process and issue the journey-level Certificate of Fitness.

### What is the passing score for Alaska Certificate of Fitness exams?

Alaska testing information states that a score of at least 70 percent is required for passing all tests except Boiler Operator 2nd Class.

### How much time is allowed for Alaska Certificate of Fitness exams?

Alaska testing information lists a four-hour maximum time limit on exams.

### Do I need approval before scheduling the Alaska exam?

Yes. Alaska testing information states that the application packet must be processed and approved before scheduling a test.

### What is the time limit for the Alaska Journeyman Electrician exam?

Alaska’s Mechanical Inspection testing information states that there is a four-hour maximum time limit on all exams.

## Licensing and experience

### Can classroom training count toward the experience requirement?

Alaska application materials allow a maximum of 1,000 hours of trade-related classroom training to be included as part of the 8,000-hour requirement. Students should follow current state instructions for acceptable documentation.

## Code book and materials

### What reference is listed for the Alaska Electrical Journeyman exam?

Current Alaska testing information lists the Electrical Journeyman exam as open book with the National Electrical Code 2020. Students should follow the current state instructions for the exact reference edition required on exam day.

### Why is the National Electrical Code important for Alaska journeyman electrician preparation?

The Alaska electrician journeyman certificate of fitness authorizes work subject to National Electrical Code standards. Studying with the NEC helps candidates understand code requirements, practice lookup, and apply electrical rules during exam preparation.

### Who issues the Alaska journeyman electrician certificate?

The Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development Mechanical Inspection Section issues electrical Certificates of Fitness, including the Electrician Journeyman classification.

### How long is the Alaska Electrical Journeyman exam?

Alaska testing information lists a four-hour maximum time limit for exams.

### Can classroom training count toward the Alaska journeyman hour requirement?

Yes. Alaska allows up to 1,000 hours of trade-related classroom training to be substituted for 1,000 hours of the 6,000 commercial or industrial hour requirement.

### Who issues Alaska electrician Certificates of Fitness?

Alaska electrician Certificates of Fitness are issued by the Alaska Department of Labor and Workforce Development, Labor Standards and Safety, Mechanical Inspection section.

### Where is Alaska Certificate of Fitness testing offered?

Department testing is offered in Anchorage, Juneau, and Fairbanks. Proctored testing in other Alaska cities is available through Department of Labor Job Centers, and Alaska-based plumbing and electrical unions may also offer testing at their facilities.
