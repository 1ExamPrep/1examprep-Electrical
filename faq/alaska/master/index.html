The exam

Is the Alaska electrical administrator exam open book?

Yes. PSI’s Alaska Electrical Administrators Candidate Information Bulletin lists approved reference materials that candidates may bring into the examination center. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references and must follow PSI’s rules for highlighting, annotation, indexing, and tabs.

How many questions are on the Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam?

PSI lists the Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam as 100 questions with a required passing score of 70% and a time allowance of 240 minutes.

How much time is allowed for the Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam?

The Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator examination is listed with a 240-minute time limit.

What score is required to pass?

The current PSI candidate bulletin lists 70% as the required passing score for the Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam.

Do I need state approval before scheduling the Alaska electrical administrator exam?

Yes. The State of Alaska states that electrical administrator examination applicants must obtain approval before taking the examination and must submit a complete application with supporting documentation before registering for the exam.

Does Alaska require approval before scheduling the exam?

Yes. Alaska Electrical Administrator applicants must obtain approval from the State of Alaska before scheduling the examination through PSI.

How long are passing Alaska Electrical Administrator exam results valid?

Passing examination results are valid for 12 months from the pass date.

Who administers Alaska electrical administrator licensing?

Alaska electrical administrator licensing is administered through the Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing under the Department of Commerce, Community, and Economic Development.

What score is required to pass the Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam?

The Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator examination is listed with a required passing score of 70%.

What NEC edition is listed in the current Alaska PSI bulletin?

The current Alaska PSI electrical administrator bulletin lists the 2020 National Electrical Code for code questions. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for current-code study and practice.

Can I use temporary tabs during the Alaska PSI exam?

No. The Alaska PSI bulletin allows permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it notes or removable tabs, are not allowed.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

The current PSI candidate bulletin lists 240 minutes for the Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam.

Licensing and experience

Does Alaska have a license officially called Master Electrician?

Alaska uses the Electrical Administrator license structure rather than a statewide license titled “Master Electrician.” The Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator category is the Alaska master-level path most closely connected to broad inside wiring supervision.

Who regulates electrical administrators in Alaska?

The Alaska Division of Corporations, Business and Professional Licensing staffs the electrical administrator program, and the Department of Commerce, Community, and Economic Development adopts regulations governing electrical administrators.

Who regulates electricians in Alaska?

Electricians are regulated by the Alaska Department of Labor, Labor Standards and Safety, Mechanical Inspection Section.

Does Alaska use a master electrician license title?

Alaska’s advanced electrical licensing structure centers on electrical administrator licensing by category. This product supports master-level electrical preparation and Alaska electrical administrator exam study.

Code book and materials

Can I write in my reference books during the exam?

No. Approved references may be prepared before the exam according to the rules, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index references during the examination session.

Are temporary tabs allowed?

No. Alaska exam rules allow permanent tabs only. Temporary removable tabs are not allowed.

Are tabs allowed during the exam?

PSI states that references may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including removable Post-it style tabs, are not allowed and must be removed before the examination begins.

How should I use the NEC tabs while studying?

Use the tabs to organize major NEC sections and practice locating information quickly. The best approach is to combine tabbed navigation with repeated practice so you understand both where information is located and how to apply it to exam-style questions.

Can I write in my references during the Alaska exam?

No. Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index references during the examination session.

More questions

How should I use the 12 practice exams?

Use the practice exams throughout your study plan. Review missed questions carefully, identify weak topics, and return to the NEC or other study material until the reasoning is clear.

When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of preparation.

What topics should I study for the Alaska master electrician exam?

Important study areas include general electrical knowledge, grounding and bonding, services, feeders, branch circuits, raceways, enclosures, conductors, cables, special occupancies, motors, lighting, low voltage, photovoltaics, alarm systems, safety, electrical power, and Alaska statutes and regulations.

What topics are on the Alaska Unlimited Commercial Wiring Administrator exam?

Topics include general electrical knowledge, grounding and bonding, services, feeders, branch circuits, raceways and enclosures, conductors and cables, special occupancies, electrical power, motors, low voltage, lighting, photovoltaics, alarm systems, safety, and Alaska statutes and regulations.
