## The exam

### Is the Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson exam open book?

Yes. The Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson exam is listed as an open-book examination.

### How many questions are on the Connecticut E-2 exam?

The current Connecticut electrical examination bulletin lists the E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson exam as 80 questions with 3.5 hours allowed.

### How much time is allowed for the exam?

The time allowed for the Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson exam is 3.5 hours.

### What score is required to pass the Connecticut E-2 exam?

The current Connecticut examination bulletin lists a 70% required passing score for the E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson examination.

### How many hours are required for the Connecticut E-2 license?

Connecticut lists 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and 720 classroom hours for the E-2 license. Applicants may also use an equivalent experience and training path when they can prove the required hours, curriculum, and work experience.

### Is the Connecticut E-2 exam open book?

Yes. The current Connecticut electrical examination bulletin identifies the E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson examination as open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing allowed references and must follow the current reference rules.

### Who administers the Connecticut E-2 electrical exam?

PSI Services LLC administers Connecticut electrical trades examinations for the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection.

### What experience and classroom hours are required for the Connecticut E-2 license?

Connecticut lists 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and 720 classroom hours for the E-2 license application path based on equivalent experience and training.

## Licensing and experience

### Who oversees Connecticut electrician licensing?

Connecticut electrician licensing is administered through the Connecticut Department of Consumer Protection.

### What is the Connecticut E-2 license?

The E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license allows the holder to perform electrical work as defined by Connecticut law, but only while employed by a properly licensed contractor.

### What is the Connecticut journeyman electrician license called?

The unlimited journeyperson electrical license classification is the E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license.

### What experience is required for the Connecticut E-2 license?

The E-2 path is commonly tied to 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and 720 hours of classroom instruction, or approved equivalent experience and training accepted for the license classification.

### What is the Connecticut E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license?

The E-2 Unlimited Electrical Journeyperson license is Connecticut’s unlimited journeyperson-level electrical license. The holder may perform electrical work while employed by a properly licensed contractor.

## Code book and materials

### What topics are covered on the Connecticut E-2 exam?

Published subject areas include general electrical knowledge, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, electrical power, motors, low voltage, lighting, illuminated signs, fire detection and alarm systems, safety information, overcurrent protection, high voltage, and photovoltaics.

### What reference books are allowed for the Connecticut E-2 exam?

The Connecticut candidate bulletin lists references including the National Electrical Code or National Electrical Code Handbook, Code of Federal Regulations - 29 CFR Part 1926, Ugly's Electrical References, NFPA 72, Photovoltaic Systems, Convert Your Home to Solar Energy, and the National Electrical Safety Code. Candidates should follow the current bulletin for the exact editions allowed on exam day.

### How long is the Connecticut E-2 exam?

The Connecticut E-2 exam is listed with a testing time of 3.5 hours.

### Are temporary tabs allowed in Connecticut electrical exam references?

No. The PSI bulletin states that references may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, such as Post-it notes, are not allowed.

### Can I write in my reference books during the exam?

No. Reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, annotated, or indexed before the exam session, but candidates may not write in the references during the exam.

### What topics should I study for the Connecticut journeyperson electrician exam?

Study areas include general electrical knowledge, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, electrical power, motors, low voltage, lighting, illuminated signs, fire detection and alarm systems, safety, overcurrent protection, high voltage, and photovoltaics.
