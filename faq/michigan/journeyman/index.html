The exam

Is the Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam open book?

Yes. The Michigan Electrical Journeyman examination is open book with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current PSI instructions for allowed references, code edition, tabs, markings, and exam-day procedures.

How many questions are on the Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam is listed with 80 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam allows 150 minutes of testing time.

What score is required to pass?

The Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam is listed with a required passing score of 75%.

What passing score is required for the Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam?

A minimum passing score of 75% is required for the Michigan Electrical Journeyman examination.

Who administers the Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam is administered by PSI for candidates approved through the State of Michigan Bureau of Construction Codes.

What score is required to pass the Michigan Electrical Journeyman exam?

Michigan requires candidates to pass the electrical journey examination with a score of at least 75%.

Licensing and experience

Does Michigan have statewide journeyperson electrical licensing?

Yes. Michigan has statewide electrical licensing through the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs, Bureau of Construction Codes.

What experience is required for Michigan journeyperson electrical licensing?

The standard Michigan journeyperson pathway requires at least 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience obtained over at least 4 years under the direct supervision of a properly licensed individual. Equivalent military experience may also qualify under state rules.

Who licenses Michigan journeyman electricians?

Michigan journeyman electricians are licensed through the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs, Bureau of Construction Codes.

What experience is required for Michigan journeyman electrician licensure?

The standard Michigan journeyman path requires at least 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience obtained over at least 4 years under the direct supervision of a licensed electrician.

What experience is required for a Michigan electrical journeyman license?

The standard pathway requires at least 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience obtained over at least four years under the direct supervision of an individual licensed under Michigan’s electrical licensing law.

What age is required for Michigan Electrical Journeyman licensure?

An applicant for the Michigan Electrical Journeyman license must be at least 20 years old.

Does Michigan have electrician license reciprocity?

No. Michigan does not hold reciprocity with any other state or country for electrical licensing.

Code book and materials

What code edition is Michigan using for electrical examinations?

Michigan electrical examinations are currently based on the 2023 National Electrical Code, 2023 Michigan Electrical Code Rules Part 8, state construction code law, skilled trades law, and associated rules.

Are NEC tabs allowed during the Michigan electrical exam?

The PSI bulletin states that the National Electrical Code book may have factory markings or highlights with factory tabs only. Materials printed from the Bureau of Construction Codes website may not contain highlighting, marking, or tabs.

What NEC edition is used for current Michigan electrical examinations?

Michigan currently bases electrical examinations on the 2023 National Electrical Code, along with Michigan Electrical Code Rules Part 8, 1972 PA 230, 2016 PA 407, and associated rules.

What references are allowed for the Michigan journeyman electrician exam?

Approved references include the National Electrical Code, 2016 PA 407, 1972 PA 230, and Michigan Electrical Code Rules Part 8. The NEC Handbook is not allowed in the examination room.

Can I write in my references during the Michigan exam?

No. Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index reference materials during the examination. Additional loose or attached papers are not allowed with approved references.

Exam topics include Michigan electrical rules, electrical theory, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods and installations, boxes and cabinets, services, feeders, motors and motor controls, special occupancies, load calculations, lighting, appliances, box and raceway fill, power-limited circuits, and general electrical trade knowledge.
