The exam

Is the Michigan Master Electrician exam open book?

Michigan Master Electrician preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current Michigan and testing-provider instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

How many questions are on the Michigan Master Electrician exam?

The published Michigan PSI bulletin lists the Master Electrician exam with 76 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Michigan Master Electrician exam?

The published Michigan PSI bulletin lists 180 minutes of testing time for the Master Electrician exam.

What is the passing score for the Michigan Master Electrician exam?

The minimum passing score for the Michigan Master Electrician exam is 75%.

What score is required to pass the Michigan Master Electrician exam?

The published Michigan PSI bulletin lists a 75 percent minimum passing score for the Master Electrician exam.

Who administers the Michigan electrical licensing exams?

PSI administers the Bureau of Construction Codes examination program for approved Michigan electrical candidates.

Licensing and experience

What experience is required for a Michigan Master Electrician license?

Michigan master electrician applicants must meet the state’s age, experience, and journeyperson licensure requirements. The state requires at least 12,000 hours of qualifying supervised electrical experience over 6 years or more and at least 2 years holding a journeyperson electrician license.

Is Michigan electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. Michigan electrical licensing is administered statewide through the Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs, Bureau of Construction Codes.

Does Michigan have state electrical licensing?

Yes. Michigan electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the Michigan Department of Licensing and Regulatory Affairs, Bureau of Construction Codes.

Does a Michigan Master Electrician license automatically allow electrical contracting?

No. Michigan regulates electrical contractor licensing separately. An electrical contractor license requires an associated master electrician, and a master electrician may only be associated with one electrical contractor at a time.

Should I confirm Michigan licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

What references are allowed for the Michigan Master Electrician exam?

The published Michigan PSI bulletin lists the National Electrical Code, 2016 PA 407, 1972 PA 230, and Michigan Electrical Code Rules Part 8 as allowed reference materials for the Master Electrician exam. The NEC Handbook is not allowed in the examination room.

Can I use tabs in my NEC for the Michigan exam?

The Michigan PSI bulletin states that the NEC code book may have factory markings or highlights with factory tabs only. Printed documents from the Bureau of Construction Codes website may not have highlighting, marking, or tabs.

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because the Michigan Master Electrician exam includes load calculations, electrical theory, services, feeders, box and raceway fill, and other areas that require accurate problem-solving. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice those skills more consistently.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current candidate bulletin and testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

More questions

What topics are covered on the Michigan Master Electrician exam?

The exam includes state-related rules and code knowledge, electrical theory, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods and installations, boxes and cabinets, services, feeders, motors and motor controls, special occupancies, load calculations, lighting, appliances, box and raceway fill, power-limited circuits, and general electrical trade knowledge.
