## The exam

### Is the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Texas Journeyman Electrician examination is administered as an open book examination with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current TDLR and testing-provider instructions for allowed references, code edition, book condition, calculators, and exam-day procedures.

### How is the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam structured?

The exam has two separately scored portions: an NEC Knowledge portion and a Calculations portion. Candidates must pass each portion with a minimum score of 70%.

### How many questions are on the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Texas Journeyman Electrician exam is split into two portions. The NEC Knowledge Portion includes 59 items, including 3 non-scored items, and the Calculations Portion includes 26 items, including 2 non-scored items.

### How much time is allowed for the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam?

The NEC Knowledge portion allows 130 minutes. The Calculations portion allows 110 minutes. Time remaining from one portion does not roll over into the next portion.

### What score is required to pass?

The Texas Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a required passing score of 70%.

### What score is required to pass the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam?

Candidates must pass each portion with a minimum score of 70%. Both the NEC Knowledge Portion and the Calculations Portion must be passed.

### How many hours are required for a Texas Journeyman Electrician license?

Texas requires 8,000 hours of qualifying on-the-job training for journeyman licensure. TDLR allows applicants to apply for the exam after completing at least 7,000 hours under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician.

### Who administers the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Texas Journeyman Electrician examination is administered through PSI for the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation.

### What is the current Texas Journeyman Electrician exam format?

The Texas Journeyman Electrician examination has been reformatted into two portions: NEC Knowledge and Calculations. Candidates should prepare for both code-based questions and calculation questions.

### Can I apply to take the Texas journeyman exam before reaching 8,000 hours?

Yes. A candidate may apply with at least 7,000 hours of on-the-job training under the supervision of a Texas-licensed Master Electrician, but the full 8,000 hours are required before licensure.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Texas have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Texas has statewide electrician licensing through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation.

### What experience is required for Texas journeyman electrician licensing?

Applicants must complete at least 7,000 hours of on-the-job training under the supervision of a Texas licensed Master Electrician to apply and test. The full 8,000 hours are required for licensure.

### Who licenses Texas journeyman electricians?

Texas journeyman electricians are licensed through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation.

### Who regulates Texas journeyman electrician licensing?

Texas journeyman electrician licensing is regulated by the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation.

### Does Texas have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Texas electrical licensing is handled at the state level through TDLR, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

### Is a journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license in Texas?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contractor licensing is separate and applies to offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

### Should I confirm Texas licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What NEC edition is used for the Texas Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Texas Journeyman Electrician exam is based on the 2023 National Electrical Code.

### What materials are not allowed in the Texas exam room?

Loose-leaf, spiral-bound, or ring-bound NEC copies are not allowed. Homemade or non-NEC tabs are not allowed. Additional paper is not allowed. The NEC Handbook is not allowed in the examination room.

### Are NEC tabs useful for Texas exam preparation?

Yes. Tabs can help students organize the NEC and practice faster lookup during study. For the actual examination, candidates must follow TDLR and PSI rules for the code edition and the type of tabs allowed in the examination room.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Texas testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study Texas rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Texas electrical laws, TDLR rules, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, and any Texas-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.
