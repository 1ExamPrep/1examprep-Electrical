## The exam

### Is the Indiana master electrician exam open book?

Open book rules depend on the local jurisdiction and exam provider. Many NEC-based master electrician exams used for licensing, including the ICC National Standard Master Electrician exam, are open book. Candidates should follow the reference rules for the exact local exam they are taking.

### How many questions are on an Indiana Master Electrician exam?

Question counts vary by jurisdiction and testing provider. A common ICC Master Electrician format uses 100 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for an Indiana Master Electrician exam?

Time limits vary by local authority. A common ICC Master Electrician format allows 5 hours.

### What score is required to pass?

Passing scores vary by jurisdiction and exam provider. Some master electrician exam formats require 75%, while local authorities may set their own passing requirements.

### How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Master Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Master Electrician exam outline identifies 100 multiple-choice questions with a 5-hour open book time limit.

### Is the Indiana electrical exam open book?

Open-book rules depend on the local jurisdiction and testing provider. Many municipal electrical exams are NEC-based, but candidates should follow the exact open-book or closed-book rules for their specific local exam.

### Is the exam open book?

Many NEC-based electrical exams are open book, but candidates must follow the official rules issued by their local jurisdiction or testing provider. The allowed references, tabs, notes, and code edition may vary.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Indiana have one statewide master electrician license?

Indiana electrical licensing is commonly handled at the local level. Cities, counties, towns, and local authorities may set their own requirements for electrical licenses, registrations, exams, insurance, bonds, permits, and renewals.

### Should I check my local Indiana licensing requirements?

Yes. Since Indiana licensing requirements vary locally, candidates should check the official rules for the jurisdiction where they plan to test or apply.

### Do I need to check my local Indiana licensing authority?

Yes. Because Indiana electrical licensing is local, candidates should confirm the exact license title, application steps, exam provider, reference rules, code edition, fees, insurance requirements, bond requirements, renewal rules, and work authorization rules for the jurisdiction where they plan to work.

## Code book and materials

### Can I use the 2026 NEC to study for an Indiana electrical exam?

Yes. The 2026 NEC is useful for updated code-cycle study, code-navigation practice, and master-level electrical review. Candidates should also confirm the NEC edition required by their specific local exam.

### Why are tabs helpful for NEC study?

Tabs help students move through the NEC more efficiently during practice. They can make it easier to locate major articles, chapters, tables, definitions, and commonly tested sections while building code-book familiarity.

### Can I use tabs in my NEC book during an Indiana electrical exam?

Tab rules depend on the local exam provider. Many testing providers allow permanent tabs and prohibit temporary tabs, loose notes, or added materials, but candidates should follow the rules issued for their specific exam.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules depend on the specific exam authority. Candidates should review the official candidate bulletin to confirm which books and materials are permitted during testing.

## More questions

### What topics should I study for an Indiana master electrician-level exam?

Candidates should review NEC navigation, electrical theory, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, conductors, raceways, boxes, overcurrent protection, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, safety, and calculations.

### How should I use the 12 practice exams?

Use the practice exams throughout your study plan. Review missed questions carefully, identify weak topics, and return to the NEC-based material until the reasoning is clear.

### When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.

### Are the flash cards useful for Indiana electrical exam preparation?
