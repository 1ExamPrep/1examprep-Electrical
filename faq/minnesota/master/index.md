## The exam

### Is the Minnesota Master Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Minnesota Master Electrician exam is an open-book examination. Candidates should follow the current Minnesota exam instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

### How many questions are on the Minnesota Class A Master Electrician exam?

The Minnesota Class A master electrician examination consists of 80 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the Minnesota Class A Master Electrician exam?

The time allowed to complete the examination is 5½ hours.

### What is the passing score for the Minnesota electrical license exam?

The passing score for Minnesota electrical license examinations is 70%.

### What score is required to pass the Minnesota Class A Master Electrician exam?

The passing score for Minnesota electrical license examinations is 70%.

### Who administers Minnesota electrical licensing?

Minnesota electrical licensing is administered by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry.

### How much time is allowed for the Minnesota electrical license examination?

Minnesota allows 5½ hours to complete electrical license examinations.

### What score is required to pass?

The passing score for Minnesota electrical license examinations is 70 percent.

### What code edition is used for Minnesota electrical license exam questions?

Minnesota’s electrical license examination guide states that the 2026 National Electrical Code is the code edition used for electrical license examination questions beginning July 1, 2026.

## Licensing and experience

### What experience is required for a Minnesota Class A Master Electrician license?

Minnesota’s Class A Master Electrician license requires a minimum total of 60 months of qualifying electrical experience across state-recognized experience categories. Candidates should review the current Minnesota requirements to confirm how their work history applies.

### Is Minnesota electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. Minnesota electrical licensing is administered statewide by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry.

### How long is the Minnesota electrical license examination session?

All examination types administered at the St. Paul office are allotted 5 1/2 hours for completion.

### Does Minnesota have state electrical licensing?

Yes. Minnesota electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry.

### Does a Minnesota Master Electrician license automatically allow electrical contracting?

No. Minnesota regulates electrical contractors separately. Candidates who plan to operate an electrical contracting business should review the state electrical contractor license requirements.

### Should I confirm Minnesota licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### Can I bring my own tabbed NEC to the Minnesota exam?

No. Minnesota provides the exam reference materials. The department-provided NEC is in soft-cover format and does not include tabs or other aids.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because Minnesota electrical exams can include theory, branch circuit work, feeder and service calculations, conductor sizing, box fill, raceway fill, motors, transformers, and other calculation-based topics. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice these skills consistently.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Minnesota testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

## More questions

### Can an electrical engineering degree qualify someone for the Minnesota Class A Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Applicants with a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering from an accredited university or college qualify for the Class A Master Electrician examination without being required to have practical installation experience.
