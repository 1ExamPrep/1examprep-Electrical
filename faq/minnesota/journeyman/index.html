The exam

Is the Minnesota Class A journeyworker electrician exam open book?

Yes. The examination is reference-based. The Department provides the National Electrical Code, a Laws and Rules Booklet, and an electronic calculator for use during the examination. Personal reference materials are not allowed.

How many questions are on the Minnesota Class A journeyworker electrician exam?

The Minnesota electrical license examination guide lists the Class A journeyworker electrician examination as 80 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Minnesota electrical license exam?

The time allowed to complete the examination is 5½ hours.

What score is required to pass?

The passing score for Minnesota electrical license examinations is 70%.

Licensing and experience

Does Minnesota have statewide journeyworker electrician licensing?

Yes. Minnesota has statewide electrical licensing through the Department of Labor and Industry, including the Class A Journeyworker Electrician license.

What code edition is used for Minnesota electrical license examinations?

The 2026 National Electrical Code became effective July 1, 2026, and is the code edition used for Minnesota electrical license examination questions.

Who oversees Minnesota electrician licensing?

Minnesota electrician licensing is overseen by the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry.

What experience is required for the Minnesota Class A journeyworker electrician license?

Minnesota’s minimum experience schedule identifies a minimum total of 48 months of acceptable experience for the Class A journeyworker electrician license, including required experience in wiring for and installing electrical wiring, apparatus, and equipment.

What NEC edition is used for Minnesota electrical license examinations?

Minnesota’s electrical license examination guide states that the 2026 National Electrical Code is the code edition used for electrical license examination questions beginning July 1, 2026.

Does Minnesota require registration before working as an unlicensed electrician?

Yes. A person performing electrical work without a personal license must generally be registered with DLI as a registered unlicensed electrician and work under proper supervision.

What is the Minnesota journeyman electrician license called?

Minnesota uses the title Class A Journeyworker Electrician. Many candidates also refer to this license as the Minnesota journeyman electrician license.

No. Minnesota provides the NEC book used during the examination. The NEC 2026 Paperback with Tabs included in this combo is for study preparation, code familiarity, and practice before exam day.

More questions

What topics should I study for the Minnesota journeyman electrician exam?

Students should study NEC structure, definitions, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, communications, calculations, Minnesota laws, and Minnesota rules.

What is the Minnesota Class A journeyworker electrician exam?

The Class A journeyworker electrician exam is the Minnesota licensing examination for candidates pursuing the state journeyworker-level electrician license. It measures electrical code knowledge, theory, calculations, and practical application within the license scope.

What topics should I study for the Minnesota Class A Journeyworker Electrician exam?

Important study areas include electrical theory, NEC introduction, general requirements, wiring and protection, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, wiring methods, raceways, boxes, equipment for general use, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, communication systems, tables, and calculations.
