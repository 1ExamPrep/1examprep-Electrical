## The exam

### Are Pennsylvania journeyman-level electrical exams open book?

Many Pennsylvania local journeyman-level electrical exams are prepared for as open-book, NEC-based exams. Candidates must follow the exact rules from the local authority or testing provider administering their exam.

### Is the Pennsylvania journeyman electrician exam open book?

There is no single statewide Pennsylvania journeyman electrician exam. Many NEC-based electrical trade exams use approved references, and local exam providers may identify open or closed book status in their exam instructions. Candidates should follow the rules for their specific local exam.

### How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician examination has 80 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician examination has a 4-hour time limit.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Pennsylvania have one statewide Journeyman Electrician license?

Pennsylvania does not have one single statewide journeyman electrician license for every electrician. Journeyman-level requirements are commonly handled locally, so candidates should confirm the requirements for the city, township, borough, county, or local authority connected to their exam path.

### Is a Philadelphia electrical license the same as a Pennsylvania statewide journeyman license?

No. Philadelphia has its own electrical licensing structure through the City of Philadelphia. A local credential from one Pennsylvania jurisdiction should not be treated as a statewide license unless the receiving jurisdiction specifically recognizes it.

### Who controls Pennsylvania journeyman electrician licensing requirements?

Requirements are generally controlled by the city, municipality, county, or local authority where the electrical work will be performed. Candidates should follow the rules of the local licensing office responsible for their area.

### Does Philadelphia have its own electrical license process?

Yes. Philadelphia has its own electrical contractor license process through the Department of Licenses and Inspections, including experience, insurance, tax compliance, examination, and continuing education requirements.

### Does Pittsburgh have its own electrical trade license?

Yes. Pittsburgh requires an Electrical Trade License to perform electrical work within the city. New license applicants must provide proof of passing a certified standardized test and documentation of qualifying experience and education.

### Does Pennsylvania issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Pennsylvania does not issue one single statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Individual electrician licensing and qualification are generally handled by local jurisdictions.

### Is a local journeyman credential the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. A local journeyman credential generally supports hands-on electrical work under local rules. Electrical contractor licensing or registration may be a separate local requirement and may involve business, insurance, examination, and permitting responsibilities.

### Do Philadelphia and Pittsburgh have their own electrical licensing rules?

Yes. Philadelphia and Pittsburgh have local electrical licensing requirements. Candidates should follow the application, exam, renewal, and work authorization rules for the specific city or municipality where they plan to work.

### Is journeyman-level recognition the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. Journeyman-level recognition, local registration, master electrician licensing, and electrical contractor licensing are different credential concepts. Candidates should confirm the credential that matches their work goal.

### Should I confirm Pennsylvania licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required credential, registration path, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What reference book should I study?

The National Electrical Code is the primary reference for NEC-based electrical exam preparation. Students should also study any Philadelphia electrical code provisions, local amendments, municipal rules, apprenticeship materials, or testing-provider references required for their specific exam.

### Why are NEC tabs useful for studying?

Tabs help candidates move through the National Electrical Code more efficiently during study. They can make it easier to locate important articles, tables, and definitions while practicing code lookup.

### Can I use the 2026 NEC for my Pennsylvania exam?

This combo is built around the 2026 NEC. Candidates should confirm that the 2026 NEC is the code edition required by their local licensing authority or exam provider before test day.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current testing instructions for the local exam being taken. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study local rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review local electrical laws, permit-related requirements, inspection expectations, and any jurisdiction-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.
