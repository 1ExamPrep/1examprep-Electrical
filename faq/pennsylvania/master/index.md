## The exam

### Is the Philadelphia Standard Master Electrician - 211 exam open book?

Yes. The Philadelphia ICC Standard Master Electrician - 211 exam is an open book test with approved references allowed during the examination.

### How many questions are on the ICC Standard Master Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Master Electrician outline lists 100 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the ICC Standard Master Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Master Electrician outline lists a 5-hour time limit.

### Is the Pennsylvania master electrician exam open book?

Open-book status depends on the local exam being taken. Many ICC master electrician contractor and trade examinations are open book, and Philadelphia-focused master electrician preparation is commonly based on open-book code navigation. Candidates should follow the exam-room rules issued for their specific local exam authorization.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Pennsylvania issue a statewide Master Electrician license?

No. Pennsylvania does not issue one single statewide master electrician license. Electrical licensing is generally handled at the local city, county, or municipal level.

### What exam is used for the Philadelphia Electrical Contractor License?

Philadelphia requires proof of successful completion of the Philadelphia Electrical Contractor Examination administered by the International Code Council. The correct exam is the Pennsylvania, Philadelphia Standard Master Electrician – 211 exam.

### Can one Pennsylvania electrical license be used everywhere in the state?

Not automatically. Because Pennsylvania licensing is local, a license or exam accepted in one city or municipality may not satisfy another jurisdiction’s requirements.

### Does Pittsburgh have its own electrical licensing process?

Yes. Pittsburgh requires an Electrical Trade License to perform electrical work within the City of Pittsburgh. Pittsburgh’s requirements are separate from Philadelphia and other Pennsylvania municipalities.

### Does Pennsylvania have one statewide master electrician license?

No. Pennsylvania electrical licensing is commonly handled at the local level by cities, townships, boroughs, and municipalities. Candidates should confirm the exact licensing authority and exam requirement for the jurisdiction where they plan to work.

### Do Pennsylvania electrical licenses transfer between municipalities?

A license or registration issued by one Pennsylvania municipality may not automatically authorize electrical work in another. Electricians should follow the licensing and permit rules of each jurisdiction where they plan to work.

### Do Pennsylvania electrical license requirements vary by city?

Yes. Requirements may vary by jurisdiction. Philadelphia, Pittsburgh, Allentown, Erie, Scranton, and other municipalities may have different applications, exams, fees, experience requirements, and local rules.

### Does a Pennsylvania local license automatically allow work statewide?

No. A license or registration issued by one Pennsylvania jurisdiction may not automatically authorize work in another jurisdiction. Candidates should confirm local requirements wherever they plan to work.

### Should I confirm Pennsylvania licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required local credential, application process, code edition, reference rules, exam format, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What references are used for Pennsylvania master-level electrical exam preparation?

Common master-level electrical exam references include the National Electrical Code and Ugly’s Electrical References. Candidates should use the exact references and code edition required by the local licensing authority and testing provider.

### Can I use the 2026 NEC to study for Pennsylvania electrical exams?

Yes. The 2026 NEC is useful for updated code-cycle study, code-navigation practice, calculations, and long-term electrical knowledge. Candidates should use the official NEC edition required for their scheduled local exam.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include branch circuit calculations, feeder calculations, service calculations, conduit fill, load calculations, conductor sizing, motor calculations, transformer calculations, electrical theory, and other math-based topics. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice these skills consistently.

### How do NEC tabs help during study?

Tabs help organize the code book and make major NEC sections easier to locate during study. They are most useful when paired with practice questions, repeated code lookup, and careful reading of NEC rules, tables, notes, and exceptions.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the local licensing authority or testing provider. Candidates should confirm which books, tabs, highlights, notes, calculators, and code editions are allowed for their scheduled exam.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study local rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review any local ordinances, amendments, licensing rules, permit requirements, inspection procedures, and contractor responsibilities listed by the jurisdiction or testing provider.
