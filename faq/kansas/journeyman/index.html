The exam

Are Kansas journeyman electrician exams open book?

Kansas Standard Journeyman Electrician exams commonly used for local licensing are open book and reference-based. Candidates should follow the official exam bulletin or local licensing instructions for the specific jurisdiction where they are applying.

Who administers Kansas journeyman electrician exams?

Kansas journeyman electrician exams are handled locally. The administering authority may be a city, county, municipal licensing office, local board, or third-party testing provider selected by that jurisdiction.

How many questions are on the Kansas Journeyman Electrician exam?

A commonly listed Kansas Standard Journeyman Electrician exam format includes 80 questions. Local jurisdictions may use different exams, so candidates should follow the exam bulletin for the specific license or certificate they are pursuing.

How much time is allowed for the Kansas Journeyman Electrician exam?

A commonly listed Kansas Standard Journeyman Electrician exam format includes a 4-hour time limit. Time limits may vary by jurisdiction and testing provider.

What score is required to pass the Kansas Journeyman Electrician exam?

A commonly listed Kansas Standard Journeyman Electrician exam format uses a 75% passing score. Candidates should follow the passing standard listed by the local licensing jurisdiction or testing provider.

How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam includes 80 multiple-choice questions.

How much time is allowed for the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

Licensing and experience

Does Kansas have a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Kansas does not use one uniform statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Journeyman-level electrical licensing and recognition are commonly handled by local cities, counties, municipalities, or building departments.

Does Kansas issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Kansas does not issue one single statewide journeyman electrician license through a centralized state electrical licensing board. Electrical licensing is commonly handled by local cities, counties, and municipalities.

Who handles electrician licensing in Kansas?

Electrician licensing in Kansas is usually handled at the local level. Candidates should work with the local building department, contractor licensing office, city office, county office, or municipal authority for the area where they plan to work.

Is a journeyman credential the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. A journeyman credential generally supports hands-on electrical work under local rules. Independent electrical contracting may require a separate contractor license, master license, business registration, insurance, bonding, or other local approval.

Can one Kansas electrical license cover every municipality?

Kansas electrical licensing is local, so electricians may need to confirm licensing, certificate, or registration requirements in each city, county, or municipality where they plan to work.
