The exam

Is the Oklahoma Unlimited Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Oklahoma Unlimited Journeyman Electrician examination is administered as an open book examination with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current Oklahoma Construction Industries Board and PSI instructions for allowed references, code edition, book condition, calculators, and exam-day procedures.

Who administers Oklahoma electrical licensing exams?

Oklahoma electrical licensing examinations are administered through PSI after the candidate is approved by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

What score is required to pass the Oklahoma electrical journeyman exam?

Current Oklahoma information states that the passing grade for electrical contractor and electrical journeyman licensing tests is 70% for exams taken on or after November 1, 2024.

Who administers Oklahoma journeyman electrician licensing?

Oklahoma journeyman electrician licensing is administered by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

How many questions are on the Oklahoma Journeyman Electrician exam?

Current exam information commonly identifies the Oklahoma Journeyman Electrician examination as an 80-question exam. Candidates should confirm the current PSI bulletin for the exam code they are approved to take.

What score is required to pass?

The Oklahoma Unlimited Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a required passing score of 75%.

How much time is allowed for the Oklahoma Journeyman Electrician exam?

Current exam information commonly lists a 3.5-hour time allowance for the Oklahoma Journeyman Electrician examination. Candidates should follow the current PSI bulletin for their scheduled exam.

Licensing and experience

Does Oklahoma have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Oklahoma has statewide electrical licensing through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

What experience is required for the Oklahoma Unlimited Electrical Journeyman license?

The Unlimited Electrical Journeyman category requires 8,000 verifiable hours of on-the-job experience in the electrical construction trade as a registered apprentice under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed contractor. Of those hours, 4,000 must be in commercial or industrial work, and 2,000 hours may be satisfied by formal electrical education.

What experience is required for the Oklahoma Residential Electrical Journeyman license?

The Residential Electrical Journeyman category requires 4,000 verifiable hours of on-the-job experience in the electrical construction trade as a registered apprentice under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed contractor. One thousand hours may be satisfied by formal electrical education.

Who licenses Oklahoma journeyman electricians?

Oklahoma journeyman electricians are licensed through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board.

What experience is required for Oklahoma journeyman electrician licensure?

The standard Oklahoma journeyman path commonly includes 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience. Candidates should follow the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board’s current requirements for exact experience categories and documentation rules.

Does Oklahoma have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Oklahoma electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

Is a journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license in Oklahoma?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contractor licensing is separate and applies to offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

Should I confirm Oklahoma licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

More questions

What topics should I study for the Oklahoma journeyman electrician exam?

Students should study the National Electrical Code, electrical theory, calculations, wiring methods, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, controls, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, communication systems, safety, and Oklahoma electrical rules.

Can a journeyman electrician contract electrical work independently in Oklahoma?

A journeyman electrician may perform electrical work within the licensed category while employed by a licensed electrical contractor. A journeyman license does not authorize the person to contract independently in the journeyman’s own name.

How long is the Oklahoma Unlimited Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Oklahoma Unlimited Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a testing time of 4 hours.

What topics should I study?

Study areas include the NEC, Oklahoma laws and rules, electrical theory, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, communication systems, safety, and calculations.
