## The exam

### Is the Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician examination is open book with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current PSI instructions for code edition, tabs, notes, and exam-day procedures.

### How many questions are on the Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam is listed with 70 questions. A small number of unscored experimental questions may also be administered.

### How much time is allowed for the Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The current Maryland PSI bulletin lists a 210-minute time limit for the Journeyperson Electrician exam.

### What score is required to pass?

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam is listed with a required passing score of 70%.

### What passing score is required for the Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam requires 49 correct answers, which equals a 70% passing score.

### Who administers Maryland journeyperson electrician exams?

### Who administers Maryland electrical licensing?

Maryland electrical licensing is administered by the Maryland State Board of Electricians under the Maryland Department of Labor.

### What score is required to pass the Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The Maryland Journeyperson Electrician exam requires 49 correct answers out of 70 questions, which equals a 70% passing score.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Maryland have statewide journeyperson electrician licensing?

Yes. Maryland has statewide electrician licensing through the Maryland State Board of Electricians, including journeyperson electrician licensing.

### What experience is required to take the Maryland journeyperson electrician exam?

Maryland requires at least four years of qualifying experience providing electrical services for all types of electrical equipment and apparatuses under the direction and supervision of a Maryland licensed master electrician or a similarly qualified employee of a governmental unit.

### Can an apprenticeship program help with Maryland journeyperson licensing?

Yes. Maryland provides a waiver pathway for applicants who complete an approved electrician apprenticeship program with at least 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of work experience and meet the Board’s timing requirements.

### Who licenses Maryland journeyperson electricians?

Maryland journeyperson electricians are licensed through the Maryland State Board of Electricians under the Maryland Department of Labor.

### Can a Maryland apprenticeship affect the journeyperson license requirements?

Yes. The Board may waive the experience and examination requirements for an applicant who completes an approved electrician apprenticeship program with at least 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of work experience and meets the certificate timing requirement.

### Can a Maryland apprenticeship program qualify for a journeyperson license without the exam?

Maryland may waive the experience and examination requirements for applicants who complete an approved apprenticeship program with at least 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of work experience and who meet the Board’s timing requirements.

### Does Maryland waive the journeyperson exam for approved apprenticeship completion?

Maryland waives the experience and examination requirements for applicants who complete an approved electrician apprenticeship program with at least 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of work experience and receive the certificate within two years of applying.

## Code book and materials

### What reference book is allowed for the Maryland exam?

The listed exam-room reference is the National Electrical Code, NFPA 70, 2020 Edition. Candidates must bring the approved reference because it is not provided at the test center.

### Are NEC tabs allowed during the Maryland electrician exam?

Permanent tabs are allowed when they meet PSI’s rules. Post-It Index Flags and Post-It Flags are not acceptable, and tabs that can be removed without ripping the page must be removed before testing.

### What NEC edition is listed for the current Maryland exam?

The current Maryland PSI bulletin lists NFPA 70, National Electrical Code 2020, as the allowed reference for the examination. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for current-code study and practice.

### Can I bring study guides into the Maryland electrician exam?

No. Maryland’s PSI exam rules state that study guides are not allowed in the testing room. Study guides, flash cards, and online practice tools are for preparation before exam day.
