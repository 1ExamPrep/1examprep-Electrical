## The exam

### Is the Colorado Master Electrician exam open book?

The Colorado Master Electrician exam uses provided reference material at the test center. The NEC book and Formula Page are provided during the exam, but candidates may not use outside materials or personal marked references.

### How many questions are on the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

The Colorado Master Electrician exam includes 90 scored items with a time allowance of 240 minutes. The exam may also include up to 10 non-scored items with an additional 30 minutes.

### How much time is allowed for the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

The current Colorado Master Electrician exam outline lists 240 minutes for the scored portion, with additional time included for non-scored items.

### What score is required to pass the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

The passing standard is 70%, which equals 63 correct scored items out of 90 scored items.

### What is the passing score for the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

The passing standard is 70%, which equals 63 correct scored items on the 90-item scored examination.

### Who administers the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

PSI Services LLC administers Colorado electrical licensing examinations for the Colorado State Electrical Board.

### What score is required to pass?

The Colorado Master Electrician examination uses a 70 percent passing standard, which equals 63 correct scored items out of 90 scored items.

### Does passing the exam automatically issue my Colorado Master Electrician license?

No. After passing the examination, candidates must still file the license application with the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations and meet the minimum qualifications for licensure under Colorado law.

## Licensing and experience

### Who regulates Colorado electricians?

The Colorado State Electrical Board within the Division of Professions and Occupations licenses, registers, and regulates electricians, apprentices, and electrical contractors in Colorado.

### Who regulates master electricians in Colorado?

Colorado master electricians are regulated by the Colorado State Electrical Board within the Division of Professions and Occupations.

## Code book and materials

### When does Colorado begin using the 2026 NEC for electrical exams?

PSI’s Colorado electrical bulletin states that effective August 1, 2026, Colorado electrical examination questions are consistent with the 2026 National Electrical Code.

### Can I bring my own NEC book into the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

The testing center provides the NEC reference and Formula Page for the exam. Candidates may not use other materials during the examination, and the provided references may not be written in, highlighted, underlined, or indexed.

### Can I write, highlight, underline, or index the provided references during the exam?

No. Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index on the references provided at the test center.

### Can I bring my own NEC book to the Colorado Master Electrician exam?

No. PSI states that the NEC book and formula page are provided at the test center and that candidates may not use any other materials. The NEC paperback included in this combo is for study preparation before exam day.

### Does Colorado use the 2026 NEC for the Master Electrician exam?

Effective August 1, 2026, Colorado electrical licensing examination questions are consistent with the 2026 National Electrical Code. The 2026 NEC book and Formula Page are provided at the test center for the examination.

Exam topics include general electrical knowledge, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special conditions, special occupancies, special equipment, electrical devices, motors, transformers, and electrical calculations.
