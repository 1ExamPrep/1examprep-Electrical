## The exam

### Is the Utah Journeyman Electrician written exam open book?

Yes. The Utah Journeyman Electrician written exam should be prepared for as an open-book, NEC-based exam under the current exam provider reference rules. Candidates should follow the current bulletin for approved references, tabs, calculators, markings, and prohibited materials.

### Who administers Utah electrician licensing exams?

Utah electrician licensing exams are handled through Prov under the Utah Division of Professional Licensing exam process.

### How many experience hours are required for a Utah Journeyman Electrician license?

Utah lists 8,000 hours of licensed electrical experience under the supervision of a licensed journeyman or master electrician, along with completion of a 4-year, 576-classroom-hour electrical apprenticeship education program. Utah also lists an alternative path using 16,000 hours of licensed electrical experience.

### How many questions are on the Utah journeyman electrician written exam?

The Utah journeyman electrician written examination includes 100 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the Utah journeyman electrician written exam?

The Utah journeyman electrician written examination allows 240 minutes.

### What score is required to pass the Utah journeyman electrician written exam?

The passing score for the Utah journeyman electrician written examination is 75%.

### Did Utah change the electrician exam format?

Yes. Beginning August 1, 2025, Utah updated its electrician licensing exams so journeyman and residential journeyman candidates take a single combined written exam covering theory and code.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Utah have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Utah has statewide electrician licensing through the Utah Division of Professional Licensing.

### What experience is required for Utah journeyman electrician licensing?

The standard Utah pathway requires completion of a four-year electrical apprenticeship education program with 576 classroom hours, 8,000 hours of licensed electrical experience under a licensed journeyman or master electrician, and passing the required written and practical exams. Utah also provides an alternate pathway with 16,000 hours of licensed electrical experience under qualifying supervision and passing the required exams.

### What experience is required for Utah journeyman electrician applicants?

Utah applicants may qualify through completion of a four-year electrical apprenticeship education program with 576 classroom hours and 8,000 hours of licensed supervised electrical experience, or through 16,000 hours of licensed supervised electrical experience.

### Who regulates Utah journeyman electrician licensing?

Utah journeyman electrician licensing is regulated by the Utah Division of Professional Licensing.

### Can a Utah candidate qualify for testing before completing the full licensing experience?

Utah exam information identifies testing eligibility through completion of a four-year electrical apprenticeship education program, or competency exam equivalence, plus 6,000 hours of electrical experience as a licensed apprentice electrician, or through 16,000 hours of electrical experience as a licensed apprentice electrician.

### Does Utah have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Utah electrical licensing is handled at the state level through DOPL, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of Utah’s state electrical licensing structure.

### Is a journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license in Utah?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contractor licensing is separate and applies to offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

### Should I confirm Utah licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### Why are NEC tabs useful for Utah electrical exam preparation?

Tabs help students organize the NEC and practice faster lookup during study. This is useful for code questions involving services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, raceways, boxes, motors, and special equipment.

### Why should I study with the NEC paperback?

The NEC paperback helps candidates practice code navigation, review article structure, and become more comfortable finding electrical code information during study. Familiarity with the NEC is an important part of journeyman electrician exam preparation.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Utah testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study Utah rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Utah electrical laws, DOPL rules, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, and any Utah-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

Beginning August 1, 2025, Utah journeyman and residential electrician licensing exams changed to a single combined written exam covering both Theory and Code.
