## The exam

### Is the Maine Master Electrician exam open book or closed book?

The current State of Maine candidate information bulletin states that electrician examinations are delivered in closed book format unless references are specifically marked as permitted. The Maine Master Electrician references are not marked as permitted in that bulletin.

### How many questions are on the Maine Master Electrician exam?

Maine’s published information lists the Master Electrician exam as 100 multiple-choice questions. Candidates must score at least 70 percent to pass.

### Do I need approval before scheduling the Maine Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Candidates must submit an examination application to the Maine Electricians’ Examining Board and receive approval before scheduling the exam with Prov.

### How much time is allowed for the Maine Master Electrician exam?

The current Maine candidate bulletin lists 4 hours for the Master Electrician exam.

### What score is required to pass the Maine Master Electrician exam?

Candidates must score 70% correct to pass the Maine Master Electrician exam.

## Licensing and experience

### What experience is required for the Maine Master Electrician examination pathway?

Maine requires at least 4,000 hours of service as a journeyman electrician or at least 12,000 hours of experience in electrical installations, along with completion of the required 576-hour program of study.

### Is Maine electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. Maine electrical licensing is administered statewide through the Maine Electricians’ Examining Board within the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

### What experience is required for Maine Master Electrician examination eligibility?

Maine identifies a pathway requiring at least 4,000 hours of service as a journeyman electrician or at least 12,000 hours of experience in electrical installations, along with completion of an approved program of study.

### Who provides the Maine electrician licensing examinations?

Maine has contracted with Prov, Inc. to provide examinations for approved candidates in the state professional licensing examination program.

### Does Maine have state electrical licensing?

Yes. Maine electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the Electricians’ Examining Board within the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

### Should I confirm Maine licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### Is the Maine Master Electrician exam closed book?

Yes. The Maine electrician examinations are delivered in a closed book format unless a reference is specifically marked as permitted. The Master Electrician exam does not list exam-room permitted books.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams often require candidates to apply formulas, code concepts, conductor sizing methods, service and feeder requirements, and load calculation procedures. A dedicated calculations guide helps candidates practice these skills more consistently.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current candidate bulletin and testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

## More questions

### What subjects are covered on the Maine Master Electrician exam?

The exam covers general electrical knowledge, communication systems, equipment for general use, service feeders and branch circuits, motors and controls, special conditions, special equipment, special occupancies, wiring and protection, and wiring methods and materials.

### What topics are covered on the Maine Master Electrician exam?

The published exam outline includes General Electrical Knowledge, Communication Systems, Equipment for General Use, Service Feeders and Branch Circuits, Motors & Controls, Special Conditions, Special Equipment, Special Occupancies, Wiring & Protection, and Wiring Methods & Materials.
