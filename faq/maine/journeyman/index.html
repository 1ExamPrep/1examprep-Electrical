The exam

Is the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. Maine electrician examinations administered through Prov are delivered in open-book format. The candidate bulletin states that the allowed electrician exam book is the softcover National Electrical Code edition listed for the exam.

How many questions are on the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam?

The current Maine candidate bulletin lists the Journeyman Electrician exam as 80 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam?

The current Maine candidate bulletin lists a 3-hour time limit for the Journeyman Electrician exam.

What score is required to pass?

The Maine Journeyman Electrician exam requires a score of 70% or higher to pass.

Who administers the Maine journeyman electrician exam?

Maine’s electrical examination program is administered through Prov. Candidates should follow the current Maine and Prov instructions for scheduling, test-day rules, reference materials, and score reporting.

Who administers Maine electrician exams?

Prov administers Maine electrician examinations for approved candidates through the Maine Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Electricians’ Examining Board.

Who administers Maine electrical licensing?

Maine electrical licensing is administered by the Electricians’ Examining Board under the State of Maine Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

Licensing and experience

Does Maine have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Maine has statewide electrician licensing through the Electricians’ Examining Board under the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

How much experience is required for the Maine journeyman electrician license?

A common Maine pathway requires at least 8,000 hours of service as a licensed apprentice or helper and 576 hours of approved study. Maine also recognizes certain apprenticeship, high school electrical program, community college, and vocational-electrical program pathways with specific work-hour and NEC-course requirements.

Who oversees Maine electrician licensing?

Maine electrician licensing is overseen by the Electricians’ Examining Board within the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

Is a Maine journeyman electrician license the same as a master electrician license?

No. A journeyman electrician license supports qualified electrical installation work while employed under the proper licensing structure. A master electrician license is a higher-level credential with additional requirements and responsibilities.

Who licenses Maine journeyman electricians?

Maine journeyman electricians are licensed through the Electricians’ Examining Board within the Office of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

Does Maine require continuing education for journeyman electricians?

Yes. Maine journeyman electricians must complete 15 hours of an approved current National Electrical Code update course before renewal.

Code book and materials

Why should I study with a tabbed NEC?

A tabbed NEC can make study sessions more organized by helping candidates locate important code sections more efficiently. It supports repeated lookup practice and helps candidates become more familiar with the structure of the National Electrical Code.

Are NEC tabs allowed during the Maine electrician exam?

Yes. The allowed NEC book may have tabs, be written in, highlighted, or underlined. The book cannot have anything stapled, taped, glued, or otherwise inserted into it.

What book is allowed for Maine electrician exams?

The current Maine candidate bulletin states that the only book allowed for electrician tests is the softcover version of the National Electrical Code edition listed in the bulletin. The hard cover Handbook to the NEC is not allowed.

What NEC edition is listed for the current Maine exam?

The current Maine candidate bulletin lists NFPA 70, National Electrical Code 2023, as the exam reference for electrical examinations. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for current-code study and practice.

More questions

What topics are covered on the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam?

Published subject areas include General Electrical Theory and Principles, Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Motors and Controls, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Special Conditions, and Communication Systems.

What is a Maine Journeyman Electrician?

A Maine Journeyman Electrician is a licensed individual who makes electrical installations while employed by a Master Electrician, Limited Electrician, or Electrical Company.

How long is the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Maine Journeyman Electrician exam is listed with a testing time of 3 hours.

What topics should I study for the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam?

Important study areas include general electrical theory and principles, wiring and protection, service feeders and branch circuits, wiring methods and materials, motors and controls, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.

What are the main subject areas on the Maine Journeyman Electrician exam?

Subject areas include General Electrical Theory and Principles, Wiring and Protection, Service Feeders and Branch Circuits, Wiring Methods and Materials, Motors and Controls, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Special Conditions, and Communication Systems.
