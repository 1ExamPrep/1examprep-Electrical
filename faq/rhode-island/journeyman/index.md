## The exam

### What score is required to pass?

Rhode Island electrical licensing examinations are commonly associated with a 70% passing score. Candidates should follow current state examination instructions for the license classification they are pursuing.

### What is the passing score for Rhode Island electrician licensing exams?

The Rhode Island electrician application materials state that individuals who achieve a passing score of 70 or greater will be invoiced for the applicable license fee.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Rhode Island have statewide journeyperson electrician licensing?

Yes. Rhode Island electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Professional Regulation Unit, and the Board of Examiners of Electricians.

### What experience is needed for Rhode Island journeyperson electrician licensing?

Rhode Island journeyperson candidates commonly prepare through apprenticeship, supervised electrical work, and related classroom instruction. Applicants should document qualifying experience and follow the current Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training application instructions.

### Who licenses Rhode Island journeyperson electricians?

Rhode Island journeyperson electricians are licensed through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Division of Professional Regulation, and the Board of Examiners of Electricians.

### What experience is required for the Rhode Island Journeyperson Electrician exam?

Rhode Island journeyperson electrician applicants must verify at least four years of experience in the electrical trade and 576 hours of related instruction approved by the Department of Labor and Training.

### Does Rhode Island require continuing education?

Yes. Rhode Island Certificate A and Certificate B electricians must complete at least 15 hours of approved continuing education as a condition of renewal.

### Who handles Rhode Island electrician licensing?

Rhode Island electrician licensing is handled through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Division of Workforce Regulation and Safety, Professional Regulations Unit.

### Does Rhode Island have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Rhode Island electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

### Is a journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license in Rhode Island?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contractor or business responsibilities may be separate when offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

### Should I confirm Rhode Island licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### Is the Rhode Island Journeyman Electrician exam closed book?

Yes. The Rhode Island electrical license exam is prepared for as a closed-book examination. Students should build strong recall of electrical theory, code concepts, calculations, safety rules, and trade knowledge before test day.

### What reference should I use while studying?

The Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training recommends the current National Electrical Code book using the latest state-approved edition for electrician exam study. Students should also review Rhode Island electrical code requirements and licensing materials connected to their application.

### Is the Rhode Island Journeyperson Electrician exam closed book?

Rhode Island regulations state that a person found referring to notes or books during an examination, except by permission of the Board, will be disqualified. Candidates should prepare as though the exam is closed book unless they receive specific instructions permitting approved references.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Rhode Island testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study Rhode Island rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Rhode Island electrical laws, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, state rules, and any Rhode Island-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.
