## The exam

### Is the Rhode Island Master Electrician exam open book?

The Rhode Island Master Electrician exam includes code-book-related preparation areas and no-book recall areas. Candidates should prepare for NEC reference use as well as direct recall of electrical theory, trade knowledge, and licensing rules.

### What score is required to pass the Rhode Island electrical exam?

Rhode Island requires applicants to obtain an average of at least 70% to obtain a license.

### Is Rhode Island master-level electrician preparation open book?

Rhode Island master-level electrical contractor preparation is treated as open book NEC-based preparation. Candidates should follow the current Department of Labor and Training exam instructions for approved materials and exam-room procedures.

### Who administers Rhode Island electrical licensing?

Rhode Island electrical licensing is administered through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training Professional Regulation unit and the Board of Examiners of Electricians.

### Is Rhode Island electrical exam preparation open book?

Rhode Island electrical exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training exam instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

## Licensing and experience

### Who regulates electrical licensing in Rhode Island?

Rhode Island electrical licensing is administered by the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Division of Professional Regulation, through the Board of Examiners for Electricians.

### What is the Rhode Island master-level electrical license called?

Rhode Island identifies the electrical contractor’s license as Certificate A. This is the master-level contractor classification commonly associated with electrical contracting authority.

### What experience is required for Rhode Island Electrical Contractor testing?

Rhode Island application instructions state that Electrical Contractor tests require verification of at least six years of experience in the respective trade and possession of a Rhode Island Journeyperson’s license. The journeyperson license must be valid for at least two years before applying for a contractor’s examination.

### What experience is required for a Rhode Island journeyperson Certificate B license?

Applicants for a Certificate B journeyperson license must have at least 8,000 hours, or 4 years, of experience as a registered apprentice and must submit required employer verification.

### Does Rhode Island license electricians statewide?

Yes. Rhode Island uses statewide electrical licensing through the Department of Labor and Training and the Board of Examiners of Electricians.

### Does Rhode Island license electrical contractors?

Yes. Rhode Island licenses electrical contractors and other electrical professionals through the state licensing system. Contractor-level applicants must follow the current state application and experience documentation requirements.

### Who handles Rhode Island electrician licensing?

Rhode Island electrician licensing and testing are handled through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Professional Regulation unit, and the Board of Examiners of Electricians.

### What experience is required for Rhode Island Electrical Journeyperson testing?

Rhode Island application instructions state that Electrical Journeyperson tests require verification of at least four years of experience in the respective trade and 576 hours of related instruction approved by the Department of Labor and Training.

### Does Rhode Island have state electrical licensing?

Yes. Rhode Island electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Professional Regulation unit, and the Board of Examiners for Electricians.

### What experience is required before applying for a Rhode Island contractor electrical exam?

Rhode Island electrical contractor test applicants must verify experience in the electrical trade for at least six years and must have a Rhode Island journeyperson license that has been valid for at least two years before applying for the contractor examination.

### Should I confirm Rhode Island licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What code is the Rhode Island electrician exam based on?

Rhode Island regulations state that electrician examinations are based on the edition of the National Electrical Code adopted by the Rhode Island State Building Code Standards Committee at the time of the examination.

### What NEC edition should I study for Rhode Island?

Rhode Island identifies the current National Electrical Code book, latest state-approved edition, as recommended electrician exam study material. This combo uses the 2026 NEC for updated code-cycle study and code-navigation practice.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Can I use the 2026 NEC to study for Rhode Island electrical exams?

Yes. The 2026 NEC is useful for updated code-cycle study, reference-navigation practice, calculations, and long-term electrical knowledge. Candidates should use the official state-approved NEC edition required for their scheduled exam.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, branch circuit work, service and feeder calculations, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, voltage drop, electrical theory, and other math-based topics. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice these skills consistently.

### How do NEC tabs help during study?

Tabs help organize the code book and make major NEC sections easier to locate during study. They are most useful when paired with practice questions, repeated code lookup, and careful reading of NEC rules, tables, notes, and exceptions.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Rhode Island testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.
