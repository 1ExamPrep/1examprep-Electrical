The exam

Is the Alabama Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Alabama electrical examination bulletin identifies the exams in the program as open book and timed. Candidates should still practice code navigation before exam day because open-book testing requires speed, accuracy, and familiarity with authorized references.

How many questions are on the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam is listed with 80 questions and 4 hours of testing time.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

The time allowed for the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam is 4 hours.

What score is required to pass?

The Alabama electrical examination bulletin states that candidates must achieve a 75% score to pass the exams.

How many hours of experience are required for the Alabama statewide journeyman examination?

The Alabama journeyman examination application identifies a minimum of 8,000 hours of experience. Certain approved education or apprenticeship program completion may be substituted for part of that experience, subject to Board approval.

What score is needed to pass?

The Alabama Electrical Contractor Board examinations list a passing score of 75%.

How much time is allowed for the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam is listed with a 4-hour time allowance.

What score is required to pass the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam?

The passing score listed for Alabama electrical exams is 75%.

Who administers the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Alabama Electrical Contractors Board has contracted with Prov, Inc. to administer the licensing examination program for state licensed electrical contractors and electrical journeymen.

Licensing and experience

Does Alabama require experience before applying for the statewide journeyman exam?

Yes. Alabama statewide journeyman applicants must document 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical experience. Approved electrical education or apprenticeship training may substitute for part of the experience requirement, up to the amount allowed by the Board.

What experience is required to apply for the Alabama statewide journeyman examination?

Applicants must document at least 8,000 hours of qualifying experience. Alabama allows certain approved electrical education or apprenticeship training to substitute for part of the experience requirement, up to the Board’s stated limit.

Code book and materials

Does Alabama currently test on the 2026 NEC?

The Alabama Electrical Contractors Board states that current exams reference the 2023 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code and that beginning January 1, 2027, the Board will begin testing on the 2026 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code. This combo is based on the 2026 NEC.

Why should I study with a tabbed NEC?

A tabbed NEC can help make code navigation more efficient during study. Tabs help candidates become familiar with the structure of the code book and return more quickly to important articles, tables, and sections while practicing.

What NEC edition is Alabama currently using for exams?

Alabama’s current Board testing information states that all exams currently reference the 2023 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code and that testing on the 2026 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code begins January 1, 2027.

More questions

What topics are covered on the Alabama Electrical Journeyman exam?

Published subject areas include conductors and cables, electrical power, fire detection and alarm systems, general electrical knowledge, grounding and bonding, illuminated signs, lighting, low voltage, motors, overcurrent protection, raceways and boxes, safety information, service, feeders, branch circuits, special occupancies and equipment, wiring and protection, and wiring methods and materials.
