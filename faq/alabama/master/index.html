The exam

Is the Alabama master electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Alabama electrical licensing examinations listed in the current candidate bulletin are open book and timed. Candidates should bring only authorized reference materials for the exam assigned to them.

How many questions are on the Alabama Electrical Contractor exam?

The Alabama Electrical Contractor examination listed in the current Prov bulletin contains 110 questions.

How many questions are on the NASCLA Electrical Contractor Master Electrician exam?

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor exam listed as Master Electrician/Unlimited Electrician contains 110 questions, with 4.5 hours allowed.

What score is required to pass?

The candidate bulletin states that candidates must achieve a 75% score to pass the exams.

Is the Alabama electrical exam open book?

Alabama electrical examinations administered through the Alabama electrical contractor testing program are listed as open book and timed. Candidates must follow the current testing rules for authorized references, tabs, highlighting, notes, and other test-day material requirements.

Does open book mean I do not need to study?

No. Open book exams still require preparation. Candidates need to understand electrical concepts, know how the NEC is organized, and practice finding information quickly. A tabbed code book is helpful, but it works best when you have already practiced using it.

What score is needed to pass?

The listed passing score for Alabama Electrical Contractor Board examinations is 75%.

Is the Alabama master-level electrical exam open book?

Yes. The Alabama electrical examination bulletin identifies the Board’s exams as open book and timed. Candidates should practice using the NEC before exam day so they can find information quickly and accurately.

How much time is allowed for the Alabama Electrical Contractor exam?

The Alabama Electrical Contractor examination has a 5-hour time limit.

What score is required to pass the Alabama electrical exams?

The current Prov bulletin states that candidates must achieve a 75% score in order to pass the exams.

Who administers Alabama electrical licensing exams?

Alabama electrical licensing exams are administered by Prov, Inc. for the Alabama Electrical Contractors Board.

Licensing and experience

Is Alabama’s master electrician exam the same as the electrical contractor exam?

Alabama uses the Electrical Contractor classification in connection with master-level electrical licensing. The Alabama Electrical Contractors Board states that the term Electrical Contractor may also be interchanged with Master Electrician.

How much experience is required for Alabama Electrical Contractor licensing?

Alabama Electrical Contractor applicants must document 8,000 hours of qualifying experience in overall design, planning, layout, and direct supervision of electrical construction activities and the soliciting and installing of electrical power or control systems.

What topics are covered on the Alabama Electrical Contractor exam?

The exam outline includes business organization, risk management, project management, estimating, safety and environmental, labor laws, payroll and employment taxes, financial management, contracts, lien laws, licensing laws, general electrical knowledge, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, electrical power, motors, low voltage, lighting, illuminated signs, fire detection and alarm systems, safety information, overcurrent protection, and service, feeders, and branch circuits.

What experience is required for the Alabama electrical contractor examination pathway?

Alabama’s application materials identify a requirement of four years or 8,000 hours of supervisory electrical construction experience. The experience must show involvement in designing, planning, laying out, and directly supervising electrical construction activities and installing electrical components.

Can education count toward Alabama’s experience requirement?

Yes. Alabama allows qualifying education in an electrical curriculum to substitute for part of the experience requirement, with limits established by the Board.

Code book and materials

Can I take this book into the exam room?

Only materials authorized by the testing provider and licensing authority may be used in the exam room. Candidates should review the current rules for their specific Alabama exam before test day.

Are handwritten notes allowed in reference books during the Alabama exam?

No. The Prov bulletin states that handwritten notes are not allowed in any portion of a reference book and must be erased before using the materials unless authorized by the licensing jurisdiction.

Are moveable tabs or Post-it notes allowed in reference books?

No. The Prov bulletin states that moveable tabs are never permitted in a reference book and that a reference book cannot contain Post-it notes.

Are test preparation study guides allowed in the testing center?

No. The Prov bulletin states that test preparation study guides are not approved references and are not permitted in the testing center, including practice examinations.

What NEC edition is Alabama currently using for exams?

Alabama’s current Board testing information states that all exams currently reference the 2023 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code and that testing on the 2026 NFPA 70 National Electrical Code begins January 1, 2027.

How should I study for an open-book electrical exam?

Use the NEC while answering practice questions, look up the supporting code sections, review tables and exceptions, practice calculations, and complete timed question sets. Open-book exams require both code familiarity and efficient pacing.
