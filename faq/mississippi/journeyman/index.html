The exam

Are Mississippi electrical exams open book?

Mississippi contractor electrical exams are generally open book with approved references. Many local journeyman-level exams are also prepared for as NEC-based open-book exams, but candidates should follow the exam instructions from the local authority or testing provider administering their exam.

Are Mississippi journeyman electrician exams open book?

Open book rules depend on the local jurisdiction or testing provider. Many code-based electrical exams are open book or reference-based. Candidates should follow the reference rules for the exact exam they are approved to take.

Who administers Mississippi contractor exams?

Mississippi State Board of Contractors examinations are administered through PSI after MSBOC determines the applicant’s eligibility to test.

Can I write in my references for a Mississippi PSI exam?

For MSBOC and PSI examinations, references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed, but they may not be written in. Additional loose or attached papers are not permitted.

Are temporary tabs allowed for Mississippi PSI exams?

Temporary tabs, such as Post-it notes, are not allowed for MSBOC and PSI examinations and must be removed before the exam begins.

How many questions are on the NASCLA electrical trade exam accepted by Mississippi?

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors is listed with 100 questions and a 270-minute time limit.

What passing score is listed for the NASCLA electrical trade exam?

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors is listed with a 75% passing score.

How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam includes 80 multiple-choice questions.

How much time is allowed for the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

Licensing and experience

Does Mississippi have a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Mississippi does not use one uniform statewide individual journeyman electrician license for every local credential. Journeyman and master electrician credentials are commonly handled by local cities, counties, municipalities, or building departments, while statewide contractor licensing is handled separately.

Does Mississippi issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Mississippi does not use one single statewide journeyman electrician license structure like some states. Statewide contractor licensing is handled by the Mississippi State Board of Contractors, while journeyman-level requirements may be handled locally.

Is a journeyman credential the same as a Mississippi electrical contractor license?

No. A journeyman credential generally supports hands-on electrical work under local rules. Electrical contractor licensing is handled separately through the Mississippi State Board of Contractors when state contractor licensing requirements apply.

What exams may apply to Mississippi electrical contractor licensing?

Electrical contractor applicants may need the Mississippi Law and Business Management exam and an electrical trade examination. Electrical trade options may include the MSBOC Master Electricians state-specific exam or the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors when accepted.

Code book and materials

What reference book should I study?

The National Electrical Code is the primary reference for NEC-based electrical exam preparation. Candidates should also study Ugly’s Electrical References and local amendments when required by the Mississippi jurisdiction or testing provider.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help organize the code book by making important chapters, articles, and tables easier to locate. They are especially helpful during study sessions because repeated navigation builds familiarity with the structure of the National Electrical Code.
