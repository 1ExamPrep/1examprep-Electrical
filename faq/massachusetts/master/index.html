The exam

Is the Massachusetts Master Electrician exam open book?

Massachusetts Master Electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current Massachusetts testing instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

How many questions are on the Massachusetts Master Electrician exam?

Massachusetts Master Electrician Part 1 is listed with 90 questions. The Business and Law portion for Master Electrician and Systems Contractor licensing is listed with 50 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Massachusetts Master Electrician exam?

Massachusetts Master Electrician Part 1 is listed with 240 minutes of testing time. The Business and Law portion is listed with 120 minutes of testing time.

What score is required to pass?

Each listed Massachusetts master electrician examination part requires a 70 percent passing score.

Licensing and experience

What experience is required before applying for the Massachusetts Master Electrician exam?

A licensed Journeyman Electrician applying for the Master Electrician license examination must document at least one year of experience as the holder of a Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician license while actively engaged in electrical work.

Is Massachusetts electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. Massachusetts electrical licensing is administered statewide through the Board of State Examiners of Electricians within the Division of Occupational Licensure.

Who oversees Massachusetts electrical licensing?

The Massachusetts Board of State Examiners of Electricians oversees electrical licensing and standards for electrical work in the Commonwealth.

Does Massachusetts have a state Master Electrician license?

Yes. Massachusetts has a state Master Electrician license regulated through the Board of State Examiners of Electricians.

Does a Massachusetts Master Electrician license differ from a systems contractor license?

Yes. Massachusetts electrician and systems credentials have different scopes and responsibilities. Candidates should confirm the credential that matches the type of work they plan to perform, supervise, or qualify for.

Should I confirm Massachusetts licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

Can I use tabs in my NEC for the Massachusetts exam?

Massachusetts exam materials allow references to be tabbed or untabbed, and highlighting or underlining of original text is allowed. Handwritten notes are not allowed in exam references.

What references are used for the Massachusetts Master Electrician exam?

Massachusetts exam materials identify the National Electrical Code and Massachusetts General Laws and regulations applicable to examination questions as allowed reference materials, subject to the current candidate bulletin and examination rules.

Why study with the 2026 NEC?

The 2026 NEC helps students build updated code-cycle knowledge and practice navigating the newest NEC structure. Candidates should also use the NEC edition required by the current Massachusetts exam instructions for their scheduled test.

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because electrical exams often require candidates to apply formulas, tables, service rules, feeder rules, conductor sizing methods, and load calculation procedures. A dedicated calculations guide helps candidates practice these skills more consistently.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Massachusetts testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Should I study Massachusetts rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Massachusetts electrical laws, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, state rules, and any Massachusetts-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

More questions

How many parts are on the Massachusetts Master Electrician exam?

The Massachusetts Master Electrician exam is a two-part exam. Part 1 covers technical electrical knowledge, and Part 2 covers business and law topics.

What education is required for the Massachusetts Master Electrician examination?

Massachusetts requires 150 clock hours of classroom instruction as the education prerequisite for the Master Electrician examination.

What topics are covered on Massachusetts Master Electrician Part 1?

Part 1 includes State Specific Electrical Requirements, Advanced Electrical Knowledge and Electrical Theory, Services, Grounding and Bonding, Wiring Methods and Devices, Motors, Transformers, Low-Voltage Distribution, Special Occupancies and Equipment, Overcurrent Protection, Lighting, Alarm Systems, Photovoltaic, and Energy Storage Systems.
