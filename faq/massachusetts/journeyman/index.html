The exam

Is the Massachusetts journeyman electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Massachusetts journeyman electrician examination is an open book test. Candidates may use approved references according to the current PSI Candidate Information Bulletin and Board rules.

How many questions are on the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

The commonly listed format includes 70 questions for Part 1 and 30 questions for Part 2, for a total of 100 questions across both parts.

How much time is allowed for the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

Part 1 allows 180 minutes of testing time, and Part 2 allows 60 minutes of testing time.

Who administers the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam is administered by PSI for candidates approved through the Board of State Examiners of Electricians.

How many hours of education are required before the Massachusetts journeyman exam?

Massachusetts requires 600 hours of approved electrical education as part of the eligibility requirements for the journeyman electrician examination.

What passing score is required for the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

A passing score of 70% is required for the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician examination.

Who administers Massachusetts electrical licensing?

Massachusetts electrical licensing is administered by the Board of State Examiners of Electricians under the Division of Occupational Licensure.

What score is required to pass the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

Each part requires a 70% passing score. Part 1 requires 56 correct answers out of 80, and Part 2 requires 21 correct answers out of 30.

Licensing and experience

Does Massachusetts have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Massachusetts has statewide electrician licensing through the Board of State Examiners of Electricians under the Division of Occupational Licensure.

What experience and education are required for Massachusetts journeyman electrician licensing?

The Massachusetts journeyman pathway commonly requires 8,000 hours of supervised electrical work experience and 600 hours of Board-approved electrical education before examination approval.

Who oversees Massachusetts electrician licensing?

Massachusetts electrician licensing is overseen by the Board of State Examiners of Electricians within the Division of Occupational Licensure.

Is a Massachusetts journeyman electrician license the same as a master electrician license?

No. The Class B Journeyman Electrician license is a journeyman-level individual license. The Class A Master Electrician license is a higher-level credential with additional requirements and responsibilities.

Does Massachusetts have reciprocity for electrician licenses?

Massachusetts has reciprocity with New Hampshire for qualifying journeyman and master electricians, subject to Board requirements.

Code book and materials

Why should I study with a tabbed NEC?

A tabbed NEC can make study sessions more organized by helping candidates locate important code sections more efficiently. It supports repeated lookup practice and helps candidates become more familiar with the structure of the National Electrical Code.

Are NEC tabs allowed during the Massachusetts electrician exam?

Approved reference materials may be tabbed or untabbed when they comply with the current Massachusetts examination bulletin. Candidates should make sure tabs do not include unapproved notes or inserted materials.

Can I have handwritten notes in my reference book?

No. Highlighting and underlining of original text are allowed, but handwritten notes are not admissible under the Massachusetts examination reference rules.

Can I highlight or underline my books for the Massachusetts exam?

Massachusetts examination rules allow highlighting and underlining of the original text. Handwritten notes are not allowed.

More questions

How many parts are on the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam is a two-part examination. Part 1 focuses on code and trade knowledge, while Part 2 focuses on applied material, Massachusetts amendments, calculations, plans, troubleshooting, licensing laws, and regulations.

What topics are covered on the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

Commonly published subject areas include general knowledge, services, grounding and bonding, wiring methods and devices, motors, transformers, low-voltage distribution, special occupancies and equipment, overcurrent protection, lighting, alarm systems, calculations, schematics, plans, materials, troubleshooting, testing, Massachusetts amendments, licensing laws, and regulations.

What Massachusetts-specific materials should I study?

Candidates should study the Massachusetts Electrical Code and amendments, applicable Massachusetts General Laws, and Board Rules and Regulations in addition to the National Electrical Code.

How long is the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

Part I has a time allowance of 180 minutes. Part II has a time allowance of 60 minutes.

What topics should I study for the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam?

Important study areas include electrical theory, NEC navigation, Massachusetts rules, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, boxes, overcurrent protection, motors, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, communication systems, and calculations.

Is the Massachusetts Journeyman Electrician exam a two-part exam?

Yes. Massachusetts examination information identifies a two-part examination process for electrician licensure after the candidate’s application and supporting documentation are approved.

What education is required before the Massachusetts journeyman examination?

Massachusetts regulations identify 600 hours of required electrical education as a prerequisite to sit for the journeyman examination.

How do the flash cards help with journeyman electrician preparation?

Flash cards support quick review of electrical terms, formulas, code concepts, safety topics, Massachusetts preparation subjects, and exam-relevant knowledge areas. They are useful for short daily study sessions and repeated recall practice.
