The exam

Is the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam open book?

Yes. New Hampshire electrical licensing examinations administered through Prov are open-book and timed. Candidates should follow the current New Hampshire and Prov exam instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

How many questions are on the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

Published New Hampshire master electrician exam preparation information identifies the Master Electrician exam as 125 questions.

How much time is allowed for the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

Published New Hampshire master electrician exam preparation information identifies the Master Electrician exam as a 5-hour examination.

What is the passing score for the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

The New Hampshire Master Electrician exam is scored against a 70% cut score. Candidates who achieve 70% or higher receive a passing grade.

What score is required to pass the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

New Hampshire electrician exams are scored against a 70% cut score. Candidates who score 70% or higher receive a passing grade.

Who administers New Hampshire electrician exams?

New Hampshire electrician licensing examinations are administered through Prov for the New Hampshire Office of Professional Licensure and Certification and the New Hampshire Electricians’ Board.

Licensing and experience

What experience is required before taking the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

New Hampshire requires master electrician candidates to pass the journeyman examination and obtain 2,000 hours of field experience as a journeyman performing electrical installations before taking the master examination.

Is New Hampshire electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. New Hampshire electrical licensing is administered statewide through the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification and the Electricians’ Board.

Who provides New Hampshire electrician licensing exams?

Prov, Inc. administers the New Hampshire electrician licensing examination program for approved candidates.

Does New Hampshire require electricians to be licensed?

Yes. New Hampshire law requires state licensing for individuals who are electricians, who are engaged in electrical work, or who represent themselves as being engaged in electrical work.

Does New Hampshire have state electrical licensing?

Yes. New Hampshire electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the New Hampshire Office of Professional Licensure and Certification and the Electricians’ Board.

Should I confirm New Hampshire licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

What references are used for the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

References include the National Electrical Code, American Electrician’s Handbook, Ugly’s Electrical References, New Hampshire laws and rules, and New Hampshire amendment materials listed for the examination.

What NEC edition is currently listed for the New Hampshire Master Electrician exam?

The current New Hampshire candidate bulletin lists the Master Electrician exam references using the 2020 NEC and related New Hampshire amendments. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for updated code-cycle study and reference-navigation practice.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, service and feeder work, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, electrical theory, and other math-based topics.
