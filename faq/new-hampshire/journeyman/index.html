The exam

Is the New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current New Hampshire testing instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

Who administers the New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam?

Prov administers the New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician examination for the New Hampshire Office of Professional Licensure and Certification and the State of New Hampshire Electrician Board.

How many questions are on the New Hampshire journeyman electrician exam?

The New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician examination is listed with 110 questions and a 4.5-hour time allowance. Candidates should review the current exam bulletin before testing for any updates to exam format or reference requirements.

How much time is allowed for the New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam?

The New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with 4 hours allowed for testing.

What score is required to pass?

The passing score for the New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed as 70%.

Licensing and experience

Does New Hampshire have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. New Hampshire has statewide electrician licensing through the Electricians’ Board under the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification.

What experience is required for New Hampshire journeyman electrician licensing?

The common New Hampshire journeyman pathway includes approximately 8,000 hours of qualifying supervised electrical experience, along with the required application, examination, and licensing steps established by the Electricians’ Board.

Who licenses New Hampshire journeyman electricians?

New Hampshire journeyman electricians are licensed through the New Hampshire Electricians’ Board under the Office of Professional Licensure and Certification.

What education is required for New Hampshire journeyman electrician licensure?

One qualification path requires 600 hours of electrical schooling, including at least 24 hours of electrical safety. The schooling requirement may also be met through an associate or higher degree in an electrical curriculum.

Is the journeyman license the same as a master electrician license?

No. The journeyman license and master electrician license are separate classifications. New Hampshire requires additional licensed journeyman experience before a candidate may take the master electrician examination.

Does New Hampshire have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. New Hampshire electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

Should I confirm New Hampshire licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

Can I use tabs in my reference book during the exam?

Permanent tabs are allowed. Moveable tabs and Post-it notes are not permitted in reference books used during the New Hampshire electrician exam.

Can I write notes in my reference book?

No. Handwritten notes are not allowed in any portion of a reference book used for testing. Candidates may prepare approved references with highlighting, underlining in pen, and permanent tabs.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current New Hampshire testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Should I study state rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review New Hampshire electrical laws, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, state rules, and any New Hampshire-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

More questions

What topics should I study for the New Hampshire journeyman electrician exam?

Students should study the National Electrical Code, electrical theory, calculations, wiring methods, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, communication systems, safety, and New Hampshire electrical laws and rules.

What topics should I study?

Study areas include the NEC, New Hampshire laws and rules, electrical theory, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, equipment, special occupancies, special conditions, communication systems, safety, and calculations.

What topics are covered on the New Hampshire Journeyman Electrician exam?

The exam covers general electrical knowledge, equipment for general use, motors and generators, control devices, special conditions, special equipment, special occupancies, services and service equipment, branch circuits and conductors, feeders, wiring methods and materials, and administrative topics.

Are the flash cards useful for New Hampshire electrical exam preparation?

Yes. Flash cards are useful for quick review of formulas, definitions, electrical theory, safety concepts, NEC topics, and trade knowledge. They work well alongside longer study sessions and timed practice.
