## The exam

### Is the Arizona electrical exam open book?

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician exam pathway is open book. Arizona electrical trade exams also use approved reference materials according to the applicable PSI and state exam instructions. Candidates must follow the rules for the specific exam they are approved to take.

### How many questions are on the Arizona C-11 or CR-11 Electrical Commercial exam?

PSI lists the Arizona C-11 and CR-11 Electrical Commercial exam as 110 questions with a minimum passing score of 70% and a time allowance of 240 minutes.

### How many questions are on the Arizona R-11 Electrical Residential exam?

PSI lists the Arizona R-11 Electrical Residential exam as 110 questions with a minimum passing score of 70% and a time allowance of 240 minutes.

### How many questions are on the AZ ROC electrical trade exam?

PSI lists 110 questions for the AZ ROC R-11 Electrical Residential examination and 110 questions for the AZ ROC C-11 Electrical Commercial examination.

### What score is required to pass the Arizona electrical trade exam?

PSI lists a 70 percent minimum passing score for the AZ ROC R-11 Electrical Residential and C-11 Electrical Commercial examinations.

### How much time is allowed for the Arizona electrical trade exam?

PSI lists 240 minutes for the AZ ROC R-11 Electrical Residential examination and 240 minutes for the AZ ROC C-11 Electrical Commercial examination.

### Who administers Arizona electrical contractor exams?

Arizona Registrar of Contractors examinations are administered through PSI for applicants taking Arizona ROC contractor exams.

### How many questions are on the AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam?

The AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam is listed as 110 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam?

The AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam is listed with a 240-minute time limit.

### What score is required to pass the AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam?

The AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam is listed with a 70% minimum passing score.

### Can I use temporary tabs during an Arizona PSI exam?

No. Arizona PSI reference rules allow permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it notes or other removable tabs, are not allowed.

### Can I write in my references during the Arizona PSI exam?

No. Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, annotated, and indexed before the examination, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index references during the examination session.

### How many questions are on the Arizona electrical trade exam?

The Arizona R-11 Electrical Residential and C-11 Electrical Commercial content outlines list 110 questions for each exam.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Arizona have a license officially called Master Electrician?

Arizona does not issue a statewide license officially titled “Master Electrician” in the same way many other states do. Arizona electrical professionals typically pursue contractor licensing through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors under classifications such as R-11, C-11, or CR-11 Electrical.

### Does Arizona have a statewide master electrician license?

Arizona licenses electrical work through contractor classifications administered by the Arizona Registrar of Contractors. Electrical candidates commonly prepare for residential, commercial, or dual electrical contractor classifications instead of a separate statewide master electrician license format used in some other states.

### Does Arizona issue a statewide master electrician license?

Arizona does not use one single statewide master electrician license structure like some states. Arizona regulates electrical contracting through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors.

### Does Arizona issue a separate master electrician license?

Arizona electrical contracting is licensed through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors by contractor classification. Electrical candidates commonly prepare for residential, commercial, or dual electrical contractor classifications rather than a separate statewide individual master electrician card.

## Code book and materials

### Are NEC tabs allowed during the Arizona electrical exam?

PSI allows references to be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Permanent tabs are tabs that would tear the page if removed. Temporary or removable tabs are not allowed.

### Can I write in my references during the exam?

No. PSI allows approved references to be prepared before the exam, but candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index references during the examination session.

## More questions

### Which Arizona electrical classifications should I know?

The common Arizona electrical contractor classifications are R-11 Electrical for residential electrical work, C-11 Electrical for commercial electrical work, and CR-11 Electrical for dual residential and commercial electrical work.

### Does Arizona accept the NASCLA electrical exam?

Arizona applicants for CR-11 Electrical, C-11 Electrical, and R-11 Electrical may have the option to participate in the NASCLA electrical examination program in place of the Arizona ROC trade exam, subject to the state’s current licensing requirements.

### When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.

### What topics should I study for the Arizona electrical exam?

Important study areas include general electrical knowledge, service feeders and branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, devices, motors, low voltage, lighting, fire detection and alarm systems, safety, overcurrent protection, and photovoltaics.

### What topics are on the AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam?

Topics include general electrical knowledge, service feeders and branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, devices, motors, low voltage, lighting, illuminated signs, fire detection and alarm systems, safety, overcurrent protection, and photovoltaics.

### Can Arizona C-11 or CR-11 applicants use the NASCLA electrical exam?

Arizona exam information identifies that C-11 and CR-11 Electrical Commercial applicants may choose between the NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors and the AZ ROC Electrical Commercial exam.
