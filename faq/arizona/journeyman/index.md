## The exam

### Is the Arizona electrical contractor trade exam open book?

Arizona trade-specific contractor exams use PSI content outlines that identify exam references, and PSI construction testing supports secure open-book testing. Candidates should follow the current reference rules for the exact Arizona ROC classification being tested.

### Who administers Arizona contractor trade exams?

The Arizona Registrar of Contractors has contracted with PSI to conduct its trade examination program. PSI provides computer-based testing through examination centers and other approved testing formats.

### What score is required on the Arizona C-11 Electrical Commercial exam?

The Arizona C-11 Electrical Commercial content outline lists a 70% minimum passing score.

### How are Arizona PSI exam scores reported?

PSI displays the score on screen at the end of the examination and emails a score report. Failing score reports include diagnostic information by examination type.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Arizona issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Arizona primarily licenses electrical contractors through the Arizona Registrar of Contractors rather than issuing one single statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Electrical workers may still use journeyman-level study materials for trade competency, local requirements, employer expectations, and contractor exam preparation.

### Is the Arizona electrical contractor trade exam closed book?

Yes. PSI examination security rules prohibit outside references and resources during the exam. Candidates should use the NEC heavily during preparation, but they should not rely on outside reference materials being available in the exam room.

### What electrical classifications are commonly associated with Arizona ROC licensing?

Arizona electrical contractor classifications include residential electrical, commercial electrical, and dual electrical classifications. Applicants should select the classification that matches the scope of work they intend to perform.

### Which Arizona electrical license classifications are commonly used?

Common Arizona electrical contractor classifications include R-11 Electrical Residential, C-11 Electrical Commercial, and CR-11 Electrical Dual. Candidates should select the classification that matches the type of electrical work they plan to perform.
