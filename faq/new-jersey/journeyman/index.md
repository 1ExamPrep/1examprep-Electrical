## The exam

### Is the New Jersey Electrical Contractor exam open book?

Yes. The New Jersey Electrical Contractor examination is open book. The NEC reference for the electrical contractor examination is provided at the test center, and candidates may not use their own copy of the NEC during that examination.

### How many questions are on the New Jersey Electrical Contractor exam?

The New Jersey Electrical Contractor examination has 100 questions.

### What score is required to pass the New Jersey Electrical Contractor exam?

The required passing score for the New Jersey Electrical Contractor examination is 70%.

### Is the New Jersey Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

New Jersey journeyman electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current testing instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

## Licensing and experience

### Does New Jersey have statewide electrical licensing?

Yes. New Jersey has statewide electrical regulation through the New Jersey Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors under the Division of Consumer Affairs.

### Is the New Jersey Class A journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. The Class A journeyman electrician license is an individual journeyman credential. The electrical contractor license is a separate credential connected to contracting authority and business permit requirements.

### Why should I use practice exams if the New Jersey journeyman license is documentation-based?

Practice exams help strengthen NEC knowledge, electrical theory, calculations, code navigation, and trade confidence. Those skills are valuable for classroom training, field work, employer evaluations, apprenticeship advancement, contractor-level preparation, and long-term electrical career growth.

### What experience is required for the New Jersey Class A journeyman electrician license?

New Jersey materials identify 8,000 hours of practical electrical experience working with tools in the installation, alteration, or repair of wiring for electric light, heat, or power, along with 576 classroom hours of related instruction. At least 4,000 practical hours must be within five years of the application date.

### Who regulates New Jersey electrical licensing?

New Jersey electrical licensing is regulated through the New Jersey Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors under the Division of Consumer Affairs.

### Does New Jersey license journeyman electricians?

Yes. New Jersey has a Class A journeyman electrician license regulated by the New Jersey Board of Examiners of Electrical Contractors.

### What topics are covered on the New Jersey Electrical Contractor exam?

The exam covers general electrical knowledge, raceways and enclosures, services, feeders, branch circuits, overcurrent protection, conductors and cables, grounding and bonding, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment and conditions, motors and controls, low voltage and communications circuits, and safety.

### Is a journeyman-level credential the same as an electrical contractor license in New Jersey?

No. Journeyman-level recognition and electrical contractor licensing are separate. Contractor licensing is connected to offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

### Should I confirm New Jersey licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required credential, registration path, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What reference book should I study?

The National Electrical Code is the primary reference for journeyman-level electrical study. Students should use the NEC edition required by their school, apprenticeship program, employer, local authority, or official examination provider connected to their credential path.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current testing instructions for the exam being taken. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study New Jersey rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review New Jersey electrical laws, board rules, permit-related requirements, inspection expectations, and any state-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

New Jersey Class A journeyman electrician application materials emphasize documented practical experience and related classroom instruction. The electrical contractor licensing examination is a separate PSI-administered examination for applicants approved by the Board for the contractor licensing path.
