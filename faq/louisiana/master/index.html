The exam

Is the Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination open book?

Yes. The Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination is identified as an open book examination. Candidates must follow the testing provider’s rules for approved references, permanent tabs, notes, highlighting, and materials brought into the exam room.

How many questions are on the Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination?

Published PSI information for the Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination lists 100 questions, a minimum passing score of 70 percent, and 240 minutes of testing time.

How much time is allowed for the Louisiana Electrical Statewide exam?

The Louisiana Electrical Statewide exam allows 240 minutes of testing time.

What is the passing score for the Louisiana Electrical Statewide exam?

The listed minimum passing score is 70%.

Is the Louisiana electrical exam open book?

The Louisiana statewide electrical contractor examination is open book. Local master electrician exam rules may vary, so candidates should follow the instructions from the authority administering their exam.

Is the Louisiana Master Electrician exam open book?

Many NEC-based electrical exams use approved references, but Louisiana candidates should follow the official rules issued by the parish, municipality, testing agency, or state contractor licensing process connected to their specific exam.

Licensing and experience

Does Louisiana issue a statewide master electrician license?

Louisiana statewide contractor licensing is handled by the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors, while journeyman and master electrician credentials may be handled at the local parish or municipal level. Electricians should follow the licensing path that applies to their work and location.

Does Louisiana have one statewide master electrician license?

Louisiana electrical contractor licensing is handled at the state level, while individual journeyman and master electrician credentials are commonly issued by local parishes or municipalities. Candidates should confirm the requirements for the jurisdiction where they plan to work.

Does Louisiana have local and state electrical licensing requirements?

Yes. Master electrician licensing is commonly handled locally, while statewide electrical contractor licensing is administered through the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.

Should I check my local Louisiana licensing requirements?

Yes. Candidates pursuing a local master electrician license should review the official requirements for the city, parish, municipality, or local board where they plan to apply.

Should I confirm my local Louisiana licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, exam name, code edition, reference rules, application steps, experience requirements, and local or state licensing requirements before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

Can I use tabs in my reference books for the Louisiana exam?

Yes. References may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Temporary removable tabs, including Post-it notes, are not allowed.

What references are listed for the Louisiana Electrical Statewide exam?

The listed references include NFPA 70, National Electrical Code; Ugly’s Electrical References; and Code of Federal Regulations, 29 CFR Part 1926, or the approved PSI selections version. Candidates should use the editions approved for their scheduled exam.

Why is calculation practice important?

Calculation practice is important because electrical exams often require candidates to apply formulas, tables, load calculation rules, conductor sizing requirements, and service or feeder methods. A dedicated calculations guide helps candidates practice these skills more consistently.

How do NEC tabs help during study?

Tabs help organize the code book and make major sections easier to locate during study. They are most useful when paired with practice questions, repeated code lookup, and careful reading of NEC rules, tables, notes, and exceptions.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the specific exam authority. Candidates should review the official exam bulletin before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

What topics are covered on the Louisiana electrical exam?

The exam outline includes general electrical knowledge, installation requirements, services, feeders, branch circuits, overcurrent protection, grounding and bonding, conductors, cables, raceways, boxes, special occupancies, low voltage systems, lighting, signs, safety, motors, and transformers.
