## The exam

### Are Louisiana electrical exams open book?

The Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination is open book. Many local journeyman-level electrical exams are also prepared for as open-book, reference-based exams, but candidates should follow the exam instructions from the local authority or testing provider administering their exam.

### Is the Louisiana Electrical Statewide examination open book?

Yes. Louisiana Electrical Statewide examination information identifies the exam as open book. Candidates should follow the current exam bulletin for the exact references, code edition, tabs, notes, calculator rules, and test-day procedures allowed for their exam.

### How many questions are on the Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination?

The Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination is listed as 100 questions.

### What passing score is required for the Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination?

The Louisiana Electrical Statewide Examination requires a minimum passing score of 70%.

### Are Louisiana journeyman electrician exams open book?

Open book rules depend on the local jurisdiction or testing provider. National electrical examinations used by some jurisdictions are open book and reference-based. Candidates should follow the reference rules for the exact exam they are approved to take.

### Who administers Louisiana electrical contractor licensing?

Louisiana electrical contractor licensing is administered by the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors.

### Who administers Louisiana journeyman electrician exams?

Journeyman-level electrician exams may be administered by a local city, parish, municipality, or testing provider selected by that local authority. Statewide electrical contractor exams are connected with the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors and approved testing procedures.

### How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam includes 80 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Louisiana have a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Louisiana does not use one uniform statewide individual journeyman electrician license for every local credential. Journeyman and master electrician credentials are commonly handled by local cities, parishes, or municipal authorities, while statewide contractor licensing is handled separately.

### Does Louisiana issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Louisiana does not use one single statewide journeyman electrician license structure like some states. Electrical contractor licensing is handled by the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors, while journeyman-level requirements may be handled locally by cities, parishes, or municipalities.

### Who regulates statewide electrical contractor licensing in Louisiana?

Statewide electrical contractor licensing is regulated by the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors. This is different from a local journeyman electrician credential.

### Is a journeyman credential the same as a Louisiana electrical contractor license?

No. A journeyman credential generally supports hands-on electrical work under local rules. Statewide electrical contractor licensing is handled separately through the Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors when state contractor licensing requirements apply.

## Code book and materials

### What reference book should I study?

The National Electrical Code is the primary reference for NEC-based electrical exam preparation. Candidates should also study OSHA safety references, Ugly’s Electrical References, and local amendments when required by the Louisiana jurisdiction or testing provider.

### Why should I study with a tabbed NEC?

A tabbed NEC can make study sessions more organized by helping candidates locate important code sections more efficiently. It supports repeated lookup practice and helps candidates become more familiar with the structure of the National Electrical Code.

### Are NEC tabs allowed during the Louisiana electrical exam?

Permanent tabs are allowed. Temporary tabs, including Post-it style tabs, are not allowed and must be removed before the examination begins.

### What code edition should I study for a Louisiana electrical exam?

The required code edition depends on the local licensing authority, Louisiana State Licensing Board for Contractors, PSI, or approved testing provider instructions for the specific exam. This Super Combo is based on the 2026 NEC.
