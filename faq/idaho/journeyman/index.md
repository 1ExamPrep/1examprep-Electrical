## The exam

### Is the Idaho journeyman electrician exam open book?

Yes. Idaho electrical examination information states that all electrical license exams are open book. Candidates may highlight books, tab different sections, and leave notes in reference material, but loose paper in reference material is not allowed.

### What score is required to pass?

The passing score listed for the Idaho Journeyman Electrician exam is 70%.

### How many questions are on the Idaho Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Idaho journeyman exam breakdown lists 100 total questions across National Electrical Code subject areas and calculations.

### What exam must an Idaho Electrical Journeyman applicant pass?

Initial Idaho Electrical Journeyman applicants must meet the requirements for licensure and pass the required NASCLA examination through PSI before submitting the journeyman license application to DOPL.

### What score is required to pass the Idaho Journeyman Electrician exam?

DOPL’s electrical exam information lists the minimum passing score for the Journeyman Electrician exam as 70%.

### Who administers the Idaho electrical exam process?

Idaho’s electrical examination process is offered through PSI. Candidates should follow the current DOPL and PSI instructions for registration, scheduling, and exam rules.

### Who administers the Idaho Journeyman Electrician exam?

DOPL states that Electrical Journeyman applicants must register for and pass the NASCLA examination through PSI before applying for licensure through DOPL.

### Do I apply for the Idaho license before or after passing the exam?

DOPL states that applicants seeking initial Electrical Journeyman licensure must meet the requirements for licensure and pass the NASCLA examination before submitting an application to DOPL.

### How much time is allowed for the Idaho Electrical Journeyman exam?

The Idaho Electrical Journeyman exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

## Licensing and experience

### What are the Idaho journeyman electrician experience requirements?

Idaho’s initial journeyman pathway includes 8,000 hours of work experience and 4 years of approved schooling, or an alternate pathway of 16,000 hours of electrical work experience.

### Do Idaho applicants take the exam before applying for licensure?

Yes. Idaho states that initial Electrical Journeyman applicants must meet the requirements for licensure and pass the required NASCLA examination through PSI before submitting the application to DOPL.

### Who oversees Idaho electrician licensing?

Idaho electrician licensing is administered through the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses and the Idaho Electrical Board.

### What experience is required for Idaho journeyman electrician licensure?

The standard Idaho journeyman path requires 8,000 hours of work experience and 4 years of approved schooling. Idaho also provides an alternate path using 16,000 hours of electrical experience.

### Does Idaho require continuing education for journeyman electrician renewal?

Yes. Idaho rules require continuing education for journeymen and master electricians during the renewal cycle, including Idaho Electrical Code training and additional approved code-related, industry-related, or independent study hours.

### Who licenses Idaho journeyman electricians?

Idaho journeyman electricians are licensed through the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses and the Idaho Electrical Board.

### Does Idaho require apprentice registration?

Yes. Idaho’s initial journeyman process includes applying for and maintaining an active apprentice registration while working toward the required experience and education path.

### What experience is required for Idaho Electrical Journeyman licensure?

DOPL lists 8,000 hours of work experience and four years of approved schooling, or 16,000 hours of work experience, as the initial journeyman licensure path before passing the required examination and applying for licensure.

### Who handles Idaho journeyman electrician licensing?

Idaho journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Idaho Division of Occupational and Professional Licenses and the Idaho Electrical Board.

### What are the main Idaho journeyman electrician license requirements?

Initial Idaho Electrical Journeyman applicants must meet the state’s licensure requirements, pass the required examination, and apply for licensure through DOPL. The listed pathway includes 8,000 hours of work experience and four years of approved schooling, or 16,000 hours of work experience.
