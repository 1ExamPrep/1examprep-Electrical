## The exam

### Is the Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Virginia Journeyman Electrician examination is open book under PSI and Virginia DPOR reference rules. Candidates should follow the current bulletin for approved references, tabs, calculators, markings, and prohibited materials.

### How many questions are on the Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam is listed as 70 questions with a 210-minute time limit.

### How much time is allowed for the Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

Candidates are allowed 210 minutes to complete the Virginia Journeyman Electrician portion.

### What score is required to pass the Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

The passing score is 70%, which equals 49 correct answers on the 70-question journeyman electrician exam.

### What score is needed to pass the Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

The passing score for the Virginia Journeyman Electrician portion is 49 correct answers.

### What reference rules apply to the Virginia open book exam?

Approved references may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam. References may use permanent tabs only. Removable tabs are not allowed, and reference materials containing writing are not permitted.

### Who administers the Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Virginia Journeyman Electrician examination is administered by PSI for the Commonwealth of Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Virginia have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Virginia has statewide tradesman licensing through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Board for Contractors Tradesmen Program.

### What experience and training are commonly required for Virginia journeyman electrician licensure?

A common pathway is four years of practical experience in the electrical trade and 240 hours of formal vocational training. Virginia also recognizes other combinations of experience, training, and qualifying education under the Board’s application requirements.

### Is a Virginia journeyman electrician license the same as a contractor license?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual tradesman license. A Virginia contractor license is a separate business license with a license class and specialty classification.

### What experience is required for a Virginia journeyman electrician license?

Virginia offers multiple qualification paths. A common route is four years of practical experience with 240 hours of formal vocational training. Other routes use different combinations of experience, training, education, or verified experience.

### Who regulates Virginia journeyman electrician licensing?

Virginia journeyman electrician licensing is regulated through the Virginia Department of Professional and Occupational Regulation and the Board for Contractors.

### Can a Virginia-approved apprenticeship completion exempt a candidate from the journeyman exam?

Yes. Virginia regulations provide an examination exemption for an individual who successfully completes an apprenticeship program approved by the Commissioner of the Department of Labor and Industry and submits the required official documentation.

No. PSI rules for Virginia tradesman exams state that study guides are not allowed. Study guides are for preparation before the exam. Candidates should bring only approved reference materials that comply with the current rules for their exam appointment.
