The exam

Is the Florida electrical contractor exam open book?

Yes. Florida electrical contractor certification examinations are open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing approved references and following all exam-room rules for books, tabs, notes, calculators, and markings.

How many questions are on the Florida Unlimited Electrical Contractor Technical/Safety exam?

The Technical/Safety section for the Unlimited Electrical Contractor exam has 100 scored questions.

How much time is allowed for the Florida Technical/Safety section?

The Technical/Safety section allows 5 hours of testing time.

What score is required to pass the Florida electrical contractor exam?

Florida requires a passing score of at least 75% on each of the two examination parts required for certification.

How many questions are on the Florida Certified Electrical Contractor exam?

The Florida Certified Electrical Contractor exam is offered in two parts. The Business section contains 50 scored questions, and the Technical/Safety section contains 100 scored questions.

Who administers Florida electrical contractor exams?

Florida electrical contractor exams are scheduled through Pearson VUE after the candidate receives approval from the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

Who administers Florida electrical certification exams?

Florida electrical certification examinations are scheduled through Pearson VUE after the candidate is approved by the Department of Business and Professional Regulation.

What score is required to pass?

The minimum percentage score required to pass both the Business section and the Technical/Safety section is 75 percent.

How much time is allowed for the Florida electrical contractor exam?

The Business section has a time allowance of 2.5 hours. The Technical/Safety section has a time allowance of 5 hours.

Licensing and experience

Does Florida have a license officially called Master Electrician?

Florida does not issue a statewide license officially titled “Master Electrician.” The Certified Electrical Contractor, also known as the Unlimited Electrical Contractor license, is the Florida statewide electrical contractor credential most closely aligned with master-level preparation.

What topics are covered on the Florida Unlimited Electrical Contractor exam?

The Technical/Safety outline includes general theory and electrical principles, plan and specification reading, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, special occupancies and situations, OSHA and safety, life safety and ADA, electrical signs and outline lighting, and alarms and limited energy.

Does Florida have a statewide Master Electrician license?

Florida does not issue one statewide license titled “Master Electrician” in the same format used by some other states. Florida licenses electrical contractors at the state level through DBPR and the Electrical Contractors’ Licensing Board, while some local jurisdictions use journeyman or master electrician terminology for local programs.

How long is the Florida electrical contractor exam?

The Business section has a time allowance of 2.5 hours. The Technical/Safety section has a time allowance of 5 hours.

What topics should I study for the Florida Unlimited Electrical Contractor Technical/Safety exam?

Important study areas include general theory and electrical principles, plan and specification reading, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, special occupancies and situations, OSHA and safety procedures, testing and tools, life safety, ADA topics, electrical signs, outline lighting, structural considerations, alarms, and limited energy.

What is the difference between a certified and registered electrical contractor in Florida?

A certified electrical contractor may work anywhere in Florida within the license scope. A registered electrical contractor is limited to the county or municipality where the registration is issued.

What Florida license is closest to master electrician-level preparation?

The Florida Certified Electrical Contractor license is the statewide electrical contractor credential most closely aligned with advanced contractor-level electrical preparation.

Code book and materials

Are permanent tabs allowed in Florida exam references?

Yes. Permanently attached tabs may be used. Post-it notes, pull-off labels, and removable tabs are not allowed.

Can I bring notes or study guides into the exam room?

No. Class notes, formulas, sample questions, bound or loose-leaf study materials, notes, and similar materials are not allowed in the examination room.

Can I use tabs in my reference books during the Florida exam?

Permanently attached tabs may be used in reference books. Moveable tabs, Post-it flags, pull-off labels, and removable tabs are not allowed.

Can I write in my reference books during the exam?

No. Candidates are not permitted to make marks in their references during the examination. Handwritten and typewritten notes are not allowed in exam references.

More questions

Does Florida require a Business exam?

Yes. The Business section is required for Florida electrical contractor certification categories. It includes 50 scored questions and allows 2 1/2 hours of testing time.

How should I use the 12 practice exams?

Use the practice exams throughout your study plan. Review missed questions carefully, identify weak topics, and return to the NEC-based material or approved references until the reasoning is clear.

When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.

Are flash cards useful for Florida electrical exam preparation?

Yes. Flash cards are useful for repeated review of formulas, definitions, safety concepts, code topics, and trade knowledge. They work well alongside longer study sessions and timed practice.
