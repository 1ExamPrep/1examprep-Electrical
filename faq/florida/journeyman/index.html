The exam

Are Florida journeyman electrician exams open book?

Many Florida local electrical trade examinations are open book and timed, including Prov-administered local exams such as Miami-Dade County testing. Candidates should follow the official rules issued by the local jurisdiction and testing vendor for their specific exam.

How many questions are on the Florida Journeyman Electrician exam?

A commonly used journeyman electrical exam outline lists 80 questions. Local jurisdictions may use different exams, so candidates should follow the exam bulletin for their specific county, city, or licensing board.

How much time is allowed for the Florida Journeyman Electrician exam?

A commonly used journeyman electrical exam outline lists a 3-hour time limit. Time limits may vary by jurisdiction and testing provider.

What score is required to pass the Florida Journeyman Electrician exam?

A commonly used journeyman electrical exam outline and local application instructions list a 75% passing score. Candidates should follow the passing standard listed by their local licensing jurisdiction.

Who administers Florida journeyman electrical exams?

Florida journeyman electrical exams are commonly arranged through local jurisdictions and third-party testing agencies. The specific provider depends on the county, city, or local board.

Licensing and experience

Does Florida have a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Florida does not use one uniform statewide journeyman electrician license for all individual electricians. Journeyman-level electrical credentials are commonly handled by local jurisdictions, while the state regulates electrical contractor licensing through the Department of Business and Professional Regulation and the Electrical Contractors’ Licensing Board.

Does Florida issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Florida does not issue one single statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Journeyman electrician credentials are commonly issued through local counties, municipalities, or construction trades boards.

Who handles state electrical contractor licensing in Florida?

State electrical contractor licensing is handled through the Florida Department of Business and Professional Regulation and the Electrical Contractors’ Licensing Board.

Can a local Florida journeyman license be recognized in another county or municipality?

Florida law provides recognition for certain valid, active local journeyman licenses in the electrical or alarm system trade when the statutory conditions are met, including passing an approved proctored examination and meeting required apprenticeship or experience standards.

Is a journeyman credential the same as a Florida electrical contractor license?

No. A local journeyman credential is not the same as a state-certified or registered electrical contractor license. Contracting for electrical work requires the proper contractor license.

Can a Florida journeyman electrician work independently as a contractor?

A journeyman credential generally does not authorize independent contracting. Electrical contracting authority in Florida is regulated through the proper contractor licensing process, and journeyman electricians typically work under a properly licensed electrical contractor.

Students should review electrical theory, NEC navigation, wiring methods, conductors, raceways, boxes, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, motors, equipment, calculations, special occupancies, and electrical safety.
