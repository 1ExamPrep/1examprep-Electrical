## The exam

### How many questions are on the NYC Master Electrician written exam?

The New York City Master and Special Electrician written examination contains 80 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the NYC Master Electrician written exam?

The written examination allows 3 hours for completion.

### What is the passing score for the NYC Master Electrician written exam?

The written examination requires 56 correct answers, which equals a 70% passing standard.

### Is the New York City Master Electrician written exam open book?

No. The New York City Master and Special Electrician written examination is a closed-book examination. Other New York jurisdictions may use different exam-room rules.

## Licensing and experience

### Does New York have one statewide master electrician license?

New York electrical licensing is commonly handled at the local level by cities, counties, towns, and municipalities. Candidates should confirm the exact licensing authority and exam requirement for the jurisdiction where they plan to work.

### Who issues the New York City Master Electrician license?

The New York City Master Electrician license is issued by the New York City Department of Buildings.

### Who licenses master electricians in New York City?

New York City Master Electricians and Special Electricians are licensed through the New York City Department of Buildings.

### Do New York electrical licenses transfer between municipalities?

A license issued by one New York municipality may not automatically authorize electrical work in another municipality. Electricians should follow the licensing and permit rules of each jurisdiction where they plan to work.

### Does a New York City license automatically allow work everywhere in New York State?

No. Licensing authority is local in New York. Candidates who plan to work in multiple jurisdictions should confirm whether separate local licensing, registration, or permit approval is required.

### Should I confirm New York licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required credential, local jurisdiction rules, application process, code edition, reference rules, exam format, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### Is the NYC Master Electrician written exam closed book?

Yes. The New York City Master and Special Electrician written examination is closed book. Reference materials are not permitted during the written exam.

### Can I use the 2026 NEC to study for New York electrical exams?

Yes. The 2026 NEC is useful for updated code-cycle study, code-navigation practice, calculations, and long-term electrical knowledge. Candidates should also study any local code edition, amendments, or rules required by their licensing authority.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code familiarity and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference during preparation.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include branch circuit calculations, feeder calculations, service calculations, conduit fill, load calculations, conductor sizing, motor calculations, transformer calculations, electrical theory, and other math-based topics. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice these skills consistently.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules depend on the specific local exam. Candidates should review the official candidate bulletin or local testing instructions before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

## More questions

### Do other New York jurisdictions use the same exam rules as New York City?

No. Other New York jurisdictions may use different applications, exam formats, reference rules, licensing requirements, and renewal procedures. Candidates should follow the local authority’s instructions.

### What topics are covered on the New York City Master Electrician written exam?

The written examination outline includes Wiring and Protection; Wiring Methods, Materials, and Equipment for General Use; Equipment for General Use, Communications, and Conduit Fill; and Special Occupancies, Equipment, and Conditions.
