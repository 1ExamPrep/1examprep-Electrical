## The exam

### Is the North Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The North Dakota Journeyman Electrician examination is administered as an open book examination with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current North Dakota State Electrical Board instructions for allowed references, code edition, book condition, calculators, and exam-day procedures.

### What score is required to pass?

The North Dakota State Electrical Board lists a passing grade of 70% for electrical examinations.

### Who administers the North Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam?

The North Dakota Journeyman Electrician examination is administered through the North Dakota State Electrical Board. Applicants must apply and be approved before selecting an exam date.

### What score is required to pass the North Dakota electrical exam?

The North Dakota State Electrical Board lists a passing grade of 70% for its electrical examinations.

### How many questions are on the North Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam?

The North Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with 80 questions.

## Licensing and experience

### Does North Dakota have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. North Dakota has statewide electrician licensing through the North Dakota State Electrical Board.

### What materials does the North Dakota State Electrical Board provide for the exam?

The North Dakota State Electrical Board provides code books, calculators, and pencils for the exam.

### What experience is required for North Dakota journeyman electrician licensing?

The standard journeyman pathway requires 8,000 hours of experience registered as an apprentice electrician, completed in not less than three years, under qualifying supervision. The Board may grant certain credits for approved electrical school, Class B experience, specialty experience, or qualifying military electrical construction experience.

### Who licenses North Dakota journeyman electricians?

North Dakota journeyman electricians are licensed through the North Dakota State Electrical Board.

### Does the Board provide materials for the exam?

Yes. The North Dakota State Electrical Board provides code books, calculators, and pencils for the examination.

### Can electrical school count toward the North Dakota journeyman experience requirement?

Yes. The Board may grant 2,000 hours of credit for a graduate of a two-year or longer electrical school accepted by the Board.

### Does North Dakota have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. North Dakota electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

### Is a journeyman electrician license the same as a contractor license?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Contractor or business requirements may be separate depending on the work being offered, advertised, bid, contracted, or performed.

### Should I confirm North Dakota licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.
