Licensing and experience

Who regulates electrician licensing in North Dakota?

Electrician licensing in North Dakota is regulated by the North Dakota State Electrical Board.

What experience is required for a North Dakota Master Electrician license?

A North Dakota master electrician applicant must have completed one year as a licensed journeyman and must have at least 2,000 hours of experience working as a licensed journeyman electrician under the supervision of a contracting master electrician or master of record.

Is North Dakota electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. North Dakota electrical licensing is regulated statewide by the North Dakota State Electrical Board.

Does North Dakota issue a Master Electrician license?

Yes. North Dakota issues Master Electrician licenses. The state recognizes contracting master, master of record, and non-contracting master categories.

Does North Dakota have state electrical licensing?

Yes. North Dakota electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the North Dakota State Electrical Board.

Does a North Dakota Master Electrician license automatically make someone a contracting master?

No. North Dakota recognizes different master electrician categories, including contracting master, master of record, and non-contracting master. Candidates should review the requirements for the specific category they plan to pursue.

Should I confirm North Dakota licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license category, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, wiring standards, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, branch circuit work, service and feeder calculations, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, electrical theory, and other math-based topics. More questions

What is the North Dakota Master Electrician exam based on?

The examinations are based on fundamental electricity, the Laws, Rules, and Wiring Standards of North Dakota, and the current edition of the National Electrical Code.

What are the North Dakota master electrician categories?

North Dakota identifies contracting master, master of record, and non-contracting master categories. Each category has different responsibility and work limitations under North Dakota rules.

What subjects are covered on the North Dakota electrical exam?

North Dakota electrical examinations are based on fundamental electricity, North Dakota laws, North Dakota rules, Wiring Standards of North Dakota, and the current edition of the National Electrical Code.
