The exam

Is the Missouri master electrician exam open book?

Many Missouri master electrician and contractor exams are open book when administered through recognized contractor and trades testing programs. Candidates should follow the exact reference rules for the jurisdiction and exam they are taking.

How many questions are on the Missouri Master Electrician exam?

The Missouri Master Electrician exam listed through Prov contains 51 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Missouri Master Electrician exam?

The Missouri Master Electrician exam listed through Prov allows 3 hours for completion.

What is the passing score for Missouri electrical exams?

Missouri electrical exams listed through Prov are graded against a 70% passing score.

How many questions are on common master electrician exams used in Missouri?

Commonly used master electrician exams, including ICC-style master electrician exams, are often listed with 100 multiple-choice questions and a 5-hour time limit. Local exam details may vary.

Is the Missouri electrical exam open book?

The NASCLA electrical examinations used for Missouri statewide electrical contractor licensing are open book. Local master electrician exams may have their own rules, so candidates should follow the official instructions issued for the specific exam they are taking.

Is the Missouri electrical contractor or master electrician exam open book?

The NASCLA electrical trade examinations accepted through participating state agencies are open book examinations. Candidates should follow the current PSI, NASCLA, and Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors rules for approved references, tabs, notes, and exam-room materials.

Who administers the Missouri NASCLA electrical examination?

The Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors contracts with PSI to conduct the NASCLA examination program. Candidates must be approved before scheduling through PSI.

How many questions are on the NASCLA electrical contractor exam?

The NASCLA Accredited Trade Examination for Electrical Contractors includes 100 questions, requires 75 correct answers to pass, and allows 270 minutes to complete the exam.

Licensing and experience

Does Missouri have a statewide electrical contractor license?

Yes. The Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors issues statewide electrical contractor licenses. Local master electrician and journeyman electrician requirements may still apply depending on the jurisdiction.

What experience is required for Missouri statewide electrical contractor licensing?

Missouri recognizes several qualifying pathways, including 12,000 verifiable practical hours installing equipment and associated wiring, reduced hour pathways with approved apprenticeship or education, a supervising experience pathway with an electrical engineering degree, and a qualifying local license history pathway.

Does Missouri have one statewide master electrician license?

Missouri individual master electrician credentials are commonly handled at the local level. Missouri also offers a statewide electrical contractor license path through the Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors.

Who regulates statewide electrical contractors in Missouri?

The Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors regulates statewide electrical contractor licensing. This is separate from local individual master or journeyman electrician licensing.

Do I need a local license and a statewide electrical contractor license?

That depends on where the work is performed and the type of work being offered. Missouri’s statewide electrical contractor license does not automatically replace local craft-level license requirements in every jurisdiction.

Should I confirm Missouri licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, local jurisdiction rules, statewide contractor requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

Can I use the 2026 NEC to study for Missouri electrical exams?

Yes. The 2026 NEC is useful for updated code-cycle study, NEC navigation practice, and long-term electrical knowledge. Candidates should also use the NEC edition required by their actual exam authority for test day.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because electrical exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, service and feeder work, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, and other math-based topics. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice these skills consistently.

How do NEC tabs help during study?

Tabs help organize the code book and make major sections easier to locate during study. They are most useful when paired with practice questions, repeated code lookup, and careful reading of NEC rules, tables, notes, and exceptions.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the specific exam authority. Candidates should review the official candidate bulletin before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

More questions

Are local Missouri electrical requirements still important?

Yes. Local jurisdictions may have their own master electrician, journeyman electrician, permit, registration, inspection, or licensing requirements. Candidates should review the rules for the city, county, or municipality where they plan to work.

Does Missouri have local master electrician requirements?

Yes. Missouri cities and counties may set local requirements for master electrician licensing, contractor registration, permits, inspections, and local electrical work. Candidates should confirm the requirements of the jurisdiction where they plan to work.

Does a local Missouri master electrician credential automatically allow statewide electrical contracting?

No. Local individual credentials and statewide electrical contractor licensing are separate considerations. Candidates who plan to contract for electrical work should review Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors requirements and any local rules that apply.
