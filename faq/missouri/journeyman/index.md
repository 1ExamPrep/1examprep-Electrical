## The exam

### Are Missouri electrical exams open book?

Many Missouri journeyman-level electrical exams are prepared for as open-book, NEC-based exams. Candidates should follow the exact exam instructions from the local authority or testing provider administering their exam.

### Are Missouri journeyman electrician exams open book?

Open book rules depend on the local jurisdiction or testing provider. Many code-based electrical exams are open book or reference-based. Candidates should follow the reference rules for the exact exam they are approved to take.

### Who administers Missouri journeyman electrician exams?

Journeyman electrician exams are commonly administered by a local city, county, municipality, or testing provider selected by that local authority.

### How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam includes 80 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Missouri have a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Missouri does not use one uniform statewide individual journeyman electrician license for every local credential. Journeyman and master electrician credentials are commonly handled by local cities, counties, municipalities, or building departments, while statewide electrical contractor licensing is handled separately.

### Does Missouri issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Missouri does not issue one single statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Journeyman and master electrician credentials are generally handled by local cities, counties, or municipalities.

### Is a journeyman credential the same as a Missouri statewide electrical contractor license?

No. A journeyman credential generally supports hands-on electrical work under local rules. Missouri statewide electrical contractor licensing is handled separately through the Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors.

### Who handles statewide electrical contractor licensing in Missouri?

Statewide electrical contractor licensing is handled by the Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors.
