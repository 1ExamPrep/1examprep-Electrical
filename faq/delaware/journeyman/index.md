## The exam

### Is the Delaware journeyperson electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Delaware Candidate Information Bulletin identifies the electrical examinations as open book and timed. Candidates must follow the current reference material rules for highlighting, tabs, notes, and approved materials.

### How many questions are on the Delaware Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The current Delaware Candidate Information Bulletin lists the Journeyperson Electrician exam as 70 questions with 3 hours allowed.

### How much time is allowed for the exam?

The Delaware ProV candidate bulletin lists 3 hours for the Journeyperson Electrician exam.

### What score is required to pass?

The Delaware Division of Professional Regulation examination page lists a minimum passing score of 75%.

### How many experience hours are required for Delaware journeyperson electrician licensure?

Delaware rules address 8,000 hours of full-time electrical experience for journeyperson licensure, including requirements related to commercial and industrial applications and supervision by a licensed master electrician.

### Who administers the Delaware journeyperson electrician exam?

Delaware uses Prov, Inc. to administer required licensure examinations for the electrical trade after the Board approves the candidate to sit for the exam.

### What score is required to pass the Delaware electrical exam?

Delaware electrical examinations require a minimum passing score of 75%.

### How many experience hours are required for Delaware journeyperson applicants by examination?

Delaware examination applicants must document at least 8,000 hours of full-time electrical work experience under the required licensed supervision.

### Who administers Delaware electrical licensing?

Delaware electrical licensing is administered through the Delaware Division of Professional Regulation and the Board of Electrical Examiners.

### How much time is allowed for the Delaware Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The Delaware Journeyperson Electrician examination has a 3-hour time limit.

### Do I need Board approval before scheduling the Delaware exam?

Yes. The Delaware Board of Electrical Examiners must approve a candidate to sit for the examination before the candidate schedules through Prov.

### What score is needed to pass the Delaware electrician exam?

Delaware electrician examinations require a 75% passing score.

## Licensing and experience

### Who oversees Delaware electrician licensing?

Delaware electrician licensing is administered through the Delaware Division of Professional Regulation and the Board of Electrical Examiners.

### What does a Delaware journeyperson electrician license allow?

A Delaware journeyperson electrician license allows the holder to perform electrical services under supervision while gaining additional experience and building the skills needed for higher-level electrician licensure.

### What experience is required for Delaware journeyperson electrician examination applicants?

Examination applicants must submit Verification of Employment forms showing at least 8,000 hours of full-time experience performing electrical work under the supervision of a licensed master electrician, master electrician special, limited electrician, or limited electrician special.

### What is the Delaware journeyman electrician license called?

Delaware uses the Journeyperson Electrician license for electricians who perform electrical services under supervision while gaining additional experience and advancing their trade skills.

## Code book and materials

### What references are listed for the Delaware Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The Delaware ProV candidate bulletin lists the American Electrician's Handbook, 17th Edition, and the National Electrical Code as references for the Journeyperson Electrician examination. Candidates should follow the current exam instructions for the exact edition required on exam day.

### Are moveable tabs or Post-it notes allowed in Delaware exam references?

No. Prov’s reference rules state that moveable tabs are never permitted and that reference books cannot contain Post-it notes.

### Are test preparation study guides allowed in the Delaware testing center?

No. Prov’s reference rules state that test preparation study guides are not approved references and are not permitted in the testing center, including practice examinations.

### Can I bring study guides into the Delaware journeyperson electrician exam?

No. Test preparation study guides and practice examinations are not approved references in the testing center. Study guides are for preparation before exam day.

### What books are listed as approved references for the Delaware Journeyperson Electrician exam?

The listed exam-room references for the Delaware Journeyperson Electrician exam are American Electrician's Handbook, 17th Edition, and NFPA 70 - National Electrical Code.
