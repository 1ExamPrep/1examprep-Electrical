## The exam

### Is the Delaware Master Electrician exam open book?

Yes. Delaware electrical licensing exams are open book and timed. Candidates must follow ProV’s reference rules for approved books, tabs, highlighting, underlining, notes, and exam-room materials.

### How many questions are on the Delaware Master Electrician exam?

The Delaware Master Electrician exam contains 80 questions with a time allowance of 3 hours.

### How much time is allowed for the Delaware Master Electrician exam?

The time allowed for the Delaware Master Electrician examination is 3 hours.

### What score is required to pass the Delaware Master Electrician exam?

A minimum score of 75% is required to pass the Delaware electrical licensing examination.

### What passing score is required for Delaware electrician exams?

Delaware electrician examinations require a minimum passing score of 75%.

### Who administers Delaware electrical licensing exams?

Prov administers Delaware electrical licensing examinations for the Delaware Division of Professional Regulation.

### Do I need approval before scheduling the Delaware Master Electrician exam?

Yes. The Delaware Board of Electrical Examiners must approve a candidate to sit for the examination before the candidate receives registration information from Prov.

### Who administers the Delaware Master Electrician exam?

Delaware uses ProV as the external testing vendor for required electrical licensure examinations after approval by the Delaware Board of Electrical Examiners.

### What score is required to pass?

Delaware requires a minimum score of 75 percent to pass the electrical licensing examination.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Delaware require liability insurance for Master Electrician licensure?

Yes. Master and Limited Electrician applicants must submit proof of liability insurance of at least $300,000 as part of the licensing process.

### Who regulates master electricians in Delaware?

Delaware master electricians are regulated by the Delaware Board of Electrical Examiners through the Division of Professional Regulation.

### What experience is required for the Delaware Master Electrician license?

The Delaware Master Electrician pathway includes six years of full-time experience, or four years of full-time experience under a Master Electrician and an official transcript showing two years of technical training. Applicants who completed a registered or approved apprenticeship program submit the Certificate of Completion of Apprenticeship.

### Is liability insurance required for the Delaware Master Electrician license?

Yes. Master and Limited Electrician applicants must submit proof of general liability insurance of at least $300,000 before the license can be issued.

## Code book and materials

### Are handwritten notes allowed in reference books?

No. Handwritten notes are not allowed in reference books used during the Delaware electrical examination.

### Are permanent tabs allowed?

Yes. Permanent tabs are allowed. Moveable tabs and Post-it notes are not allowed.

### Are NEC tabs allowed during the Delaware electrical exam?

Permanent tabs are allowed under Prov’s reference-material rules. Moveable tabs, repositionable tabs, and Post-it style tabs are not permitted in a reference book.

### Can I use tabs in my reference books during the exam?

Permanent tabs are permitted under ProV open-book reference rules. Moveable items such as repositionable tabs or Post-it notes are not permitted in a reference book.

### Can I write in my books during the Delaware exam?

No. Candidates may not mark in books during the test. Books may have highlighting and underlining in pen before the exam, but handwritten notes are not allowed unless authorized by the licensing jurisdiction.

## More questions

### What topics are covered on the Delaware Master Electrician exam?

The exam covers general electrical knowledge, plan reading, equipment for general use, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, motors and controls, special conditions, special equipment, special occupancies, and communication systems.

### When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.
