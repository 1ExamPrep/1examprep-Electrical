## The exam

### Is the New Mexico EE-98J exam open book?

The EE-98J Part 1 Code examination is open book. The EE-98J Part 2 Theory examination is closed book. Candidates must follow current PSI rules for references, tabs, markings, calculators, and exam-room materials.

### How many questions are on the New Mexico EE-98J written exams?

The EE-98J Part 1 Code examination is listed with 50 questions, and the EE-98J Part 2 Theory examination is listed with 50 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the EE-98J exams?

The EE-98J Part 1 Code examination is listed with 145 minutes allowed. The EE-98J Part 2 Theory examination is listed with 120 minutes allowed. The practical examination is listed with 120 minutes allowed.

### What score is required to pass?

The New Mexico EE-98J journeyman residential and commercial electrical code examination is commonly listed with a required passing score of 75%.

### Is the New Mexico Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

New Mexico journeyman electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current testing instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

### Is the New Mexico journeyman electrical code exam open book?

Yes. The New Mexico journeyman residential and commercial electrical code examination is administered as an open book exam with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current PSI instructions for the exact exam they are taking.

### How many questions are on the New Mexico EE-98J code exam?

The New Mexico EE-98J journeyman residential and commercial electrical code examination is commonly listed with 80 scored questions.

### Who administers New Mexico journeyman electrical exams?

New Mexico journeyman certification examinations are administered through PSI after the candidate is approved to test by the New Mexico Construction Industries Division.

## Licensing and experience

### What experience is required for the EE-98J journeyman classification?

The EE-98J Journeyman Residential and Commercial Electrical classification commonly requires four years, or 8,000 hours, of qualifying electrical experience.

### Who regulates New Mexico journeyman electrical certification?

New Mexico journeyman electrical certification is regulated through the Construction Industries Division of the New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department.

### Does New Mexico regulate electrical licensing at the state level?

Yes. New Mexico electrical licensing and qualification requirements are handled through the state construction licensing structure, including electrical classifications and credential requirements.

### Is a journeyman-level credential the same as an electrical contractor license in New Mexico?

No. Journeyman-level qualification and electrical contractor licensing are separate. Contractor licensing is connected to offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

### Should I confirm New Mexico licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required credential, classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## More questions

### Does New Mexico have statewide journeyman electrician certification?

Yes. New Mexico journeyman certification is handled through the Construction Industries Division under the New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department.

### What is the EE-98J journeyman classification?

EE-98J is the New Mexico Journeyman Residential and Commercial Electrical classification. It is one of the main journeyman electrical classifications used for residential and commercial electrical work.

### What topics should I study for the New Mexico journeyman electrician exam?

Students should study the National Electrical Code, New Mexico Electrical Code, general installation requirements, services, feeders, branch circuits, overcurrent protection, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, motors, transformers, generators, low voltage, alarms, special occupancies, special equipment, calculations, and practical trade skills.

### What is the EE-98J classification?

EE-98J is the New Mexico residential and commercial electrical journeyman classification. Candidates should confirm that this classification matches the work they intend to perform before applying.

### What topics should I study?

Study areas include the NEC, New Mexico state code content, electrical theory, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, transformers, generators, lighting, signs, special occupancies, special equipment, safety, and calculations.

### Does New Mexico require journeyman certification for electrical work?

Yes. Electrical work in New Mexico must be performed by, or under the supervision of, someone who has a journeyman certificate for the classification of work being performed.

### Are the flash cards useful for New Mexico electrical exam preparation?

Yes. Flash cards are useful for quick review of formulas, definitions, electrical theory, safety concepts, NEC topics, and trade knowledge. They work well alongside longer study sessions and timed practice.
