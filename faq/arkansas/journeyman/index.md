## Licensing and experience

### Who oversees electrician licensing in Arkansas?

The Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners is responsible for administering Arkansas laws governing electrician licensing. The Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing provides the state electrical inspection and licensing program.

### How much experience is required for an Arkansas journeyman electrician license?

Arkansas journeyman electrician applicants generally need four years, or 8,000 hours, of electrical work experience, or a four-year combination of board-approved training and experience.

### How much experience is needed for an Arkansas journeyman electrician license?

Arkansas journeyman electrician applicants are commonly associated with four years or 8,000 hours of qualifying electrical work experience under appropriate supervision.
