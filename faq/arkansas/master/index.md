## The exam

### Is the Arkansas Master Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Arkansas electrical examinations administered through Prov are open book and timed. Candidates may use authorized references that meet the reference-material rules provided by Prov and the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners.

### How many questions are on the Arkansas Master Electrician exam?

The Arkansas Master Electrician exam is listed as 60 questions with a time allowance of 3 hours. The passing score for Arkansas electrical exams administered through Prov is 70%.

### How much time is allowed for the Arkansas Master Electrician exam?

The current Arkansas candidate bulletin lists a 3-hour time limit for the Arkansas Master Electrician exam.

### What score is required to pass the Arkansas Master Electrician exam?

Arkansas electrical exams are scored against a cut score of 70%. Candidates who achieve that score or higher receive a passing grade.

### Who administers the Arkansas electrical licensing exams?

Prov, Inc. administers Arkansas electrical licensing examinations under contract with the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners and the Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing.

### Do I need approval before scheduling the Arkansas Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Candidates complete the appropriate application with the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners. After approval, the Board notifies the candidate that they are eligible to contact Prov to schedule the exam.

### What score is required to pass?

Arkansas electrical exams administered by Prov are scored against a 70 percent passing score.

### Who administers the Arkansas Master Electrician exam?

The Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners has contracted with Prov, Inc. to develop and administer the electrical licensing examination program.

## Licensing and experience

### Who regulates electrician licensing in Arkansas?

Arkansas electrician licensing is administered through the Arkansas Department of Labor and Licensing and the Arkansas Board of Electrical Examiners.

## Code book and materials

### Are handwritten notes allowed in reference books?

No. Arkansas exam-room rules do not allow handwritten notes in reference books. Candidates should follow the current reference preparation rules for the exam.

### Are permanent tabs allowed?

Yes. Permanent tabs are allowed under the Arkansas testing rules. Moveable tabs and Post-it notes are not allowed.

### Are NEC tabs allowed during the Arkansas electrical exam?

Permanent tabs are allowed under Prov’s reference-material rules. Moveable tabs, repositionable tabs, Post-it style tabs, loose notes, stickers, formula sheets, and added documents are not allowed unless specifically authorized.

### Can I write notes in my NEC book for the exam?

Handwritten notes are not allowed in any portion of a reference book unless authorized by the licensing or certifying jurisdiction. Books may be highlighted and underlined in pen before the exam, but candidates may not mark in books during the test.

### Can I bring study guides into the exam room?

No. Test preparation study guides and practice examinations are not approved references and are not permitted in the testing center.

### Can I use tabs in my code book?

Permanent tabs are allowed. Moveable tabs are not permitted. Reference materials may be prepared with highlighting, underlining in pen, and permanent tabs.

### What NEC edition is listed for the current Arkansas Master Electrician exam?

The current Arkansas candidate bulletin lists NFPA 70, National Electrical Code 2023, as the reference for the Arkansas Master Electrician exam. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for current-code study and practice.
