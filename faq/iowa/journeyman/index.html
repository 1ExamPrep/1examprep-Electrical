The exam

Is the Iowa journeyman electrician exam open book?

Yes. Iowa electrical examination information identifies the exam as open book. Candidates should follow the current Iowa and PSI rules for allowed references, tabs, notes, highlighting, calculators, and other test-day materials.

How many questions are on the Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam is listed with 80 scored items. It may also include 8 non-scored items used for examination development.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

The Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam allows 180 minutes for the scored portion of the exam.

What score is required to pass?

The Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam is listed with a required passing score of 70%.

Who administers Iowa electrical licensing?

Iowa electrical licensing is administered through the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing and the Iowa Electrical Examining Board.

What score is required to pass the Iowa journeyman electrician exam?

Iowa testing information identifies a score of 70% or higher as the passing standard for the applicable State of Iowa PSI electrical examination.

Who administers the Iowa Journeyman Electrician exam?

Iowa electrical licensing examinations are administered by PSI for the Iowa electrical licensing program.

Licensing and experience

Does Iowa have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Iowa has statewide electrical licensing through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board, including journeyman electrician licensing.

Who oversees Iowa electrician licensing?

Iowa electrician licensing is overseen by the Iowa Electrical Examining Board within the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing.

What is the common path to a Class A Journeyman Electrician license in Iowa?

A common Class A journeyman path includes completing a registered apprenticeship program, completing four years of experience as an apprentice electrician, and passing a Board-approved journeyman electrician examination with a score of 70% or higher.

Who licenses Iowa journeyman electricians?

Iowa journeyman electricians are licensed through the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing and the Iowa Electrical Examining Board.

What training is required for an Iowa Class A Journeyman license?

The Class A Journeyman path requires completion of a registered apprenticeship training program or a Board-recognized equivalent training pathway, such as an approved post-secondary electrical program path with documented on-the-job training.

What Iowa journeyman license classes are available?

Iowa offers journeyman-level electrical licensing classifications, including Class A Journeyman Electrician and Class B Journeyman Electrician.

Who handles Iowa journeyman electrician licensing?

Iowa journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Iowa Electrical Examining Board and the statewide electrical licensing system.

What is the difference between a journeyman electrician license and an electrical contractor license in Iowa?

A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contracting authority may require a separate electrical contractor license or business-related authorization, depending on the work being performed.
