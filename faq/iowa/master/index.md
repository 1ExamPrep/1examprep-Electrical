## The exam

### Is the Iowa Master Electrician exam open book?

Iowa electrical exam preparation includes open-book, code-based study. Candidates should follow the current PSI and Iowa Electrical Examining Board rules for the exact references, tabs, highlights, notes, and materials allowed during their specific exam.

### How many questions are on the Iowa Master Electrician exam?

The Iowa Master Electrician exam includes 100 scored questions. The examination also includes non-scored questions used for exam development.

### How much time is allowed for the Iowa Master Electrician exam?

The scored portion of the Iowa Master Electrician examination has a time allowance of 240 minutes. An additional 30 minutes may be provided for non-scored items.

### What score is required to pass the Iowa Master Electrician exam?

The passing standard for the Iowa Master Electrician examination is 70% correct.

### What is the passing score for the Iowa Master Electrician exam?

The current Iowa exam bulletin lists 70% correct as the passing score for the Master Electrician exam.

### Who administers the Iowa Master Electrician exam?

PSI administers the Iowa Master Electrician examination for the Iowa Electrical Examining Board.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Iowa have Class A and Class B master electrician licenses?

Yes. Iowa recognizes Class A and Class B electrical license structures. Candidates should follow the current state instructions for the exact license classification they are pursuing.

### Does Iowa have statewide electrical licensing?

Yes. Iowa has a statewide electrical licensing program overseen by the Iowa Electrical Examining Board through the Iowa Department of Inspections, Appeals, and Licensing.

### What experience is required for Iowa Master Electrician exam sponsorship?

Master exam candidates must have at least one year of experience acceptable to the Board as a licensed Class A or Class B journeyman electrician, be a State of Iowa-licensed Master Class B electrician, or qualify through the state’s documented prior master electrician experience pathway.

### Should I confirm my Iowa licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, exam authorization process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### Can I use tabs in my reference book during the Iowa exam?

References may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it notes or removable tabs, are not allowed.

### Can I write in my reference books during the Iowa exam?

No. References may not be written in before or during the examination. Candidates may not bring additional loose or attached papers with approved references.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current PSI and Iowa Electrical Examining Board instructions. Candidates should review their exam bulletin and authorization materials before test day.

### Why are NEC tabs useful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

## More questions

### What topics are covered on the Iowa Master Electrician exam?

The exam covers general electrical knowledge, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and communication systems.

### How should I use the 12 practice exams?

Use the practice exams throughout your study plan. Review missed questions carefully, identify weak topics, and return to the NEC-based material until the reasoning is clear.

### When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.
