The exam

Is the South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The South Dakota Journeyman Electrician examination is administered as an open book examination with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current South Dakota Electrical Commission instructions for allowed references, code edition, book condition, calculators, and exam-day procedures.

How many questions are on the South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam?

The South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly identified as an 80-question examination.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

The South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed as a 4-hour exam.

What score is required to pass?

The South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a required passing score of 70%.

How many hours of experience are required before the South Dakota journeyman exam?

South Dakota states that a candidate is ready to write the journeyman exam after completing four years, totaling 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor.

How much time is allowed for the South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam?

The South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam has a 4-hour time limit.

What score is required to pass the South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam?

The passing score for the South Dakota Journeyman Electrician exam is 70%.

Licensing and experience

Does South Dakota have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. South Dakota has statewide electrician licensing through the South Dakota Electrical Commission.

What experience is required for South Dakota journeyman electrician licensing?

South Dakota requires four years, totaling 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor before a candidate is ready to write the journeyman exam.

Who licenses South Dakota journeyman electricians?

South Dakota journeyman electricians are licensed through the South Dakota Electrical Commission under the Department of Labor and Regulation.

Who regulates South Dakota electrician licensing?

South Dakota electrician licensing is regulated by the South Dakota Electrical Commission under the South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation.

Is South Dakota electrician licensing statewide?

Yes. South Dakota has a state-level electrical licensing structure administered through the South Dakota Electrical Commission.

What experience is required before taking the South Dakota journeyman exam?

South Dakota requires four years, totaling 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor before a candidate is ready to write the journeyman examination.

Does South Dakota have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. South Dakota electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

Is a journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license in South Dakota?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contractor or business responsibilities may be separate when offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

Should I confirm South Dakota licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.
