## The exam

### Is the South Dakota Master Electrician exam open book?

South Dakota Master Electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current South Dakota Electrical Commission exam instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

### Can unlicensed work hours count toward South Dakota exam eligibility?

No. South Dakota states that experience hours must be licensed in order for working time to count toward the hours required to take the exams.

### Is the South Dakota electrical exam open book?

South Dakota electrical exams are code-based, and candidates should prepare for reference navigation when approved references are allowed. Candidates should follow the current South Dakota Electrical Commission instructions for approved references and exam procedures.

### Who administers South Dakota electrical licensing?

South Dakota electrical licensing is administered through the South Dakota Electrical Commission.

### Is the South Dakota master-level electrical exam open book?

South Dakota master-level electrical preparation includes open book, code-book related study. Candidates should build strong NEC navigation skills and follow the South Dakota Electrical Commission’s current exam instructions for the code edition and exam materials used on their scheduled exam.

### Are continuing education hours required for South Dakota electrical license renewal?

Yes. South Dakota identifies 16 hours of continuing education for renewal, including a minimum of 8 code hours.

## Licensing and experience

### Who regulates electrical licensing in South Dakota?

Electrical licensing in South Dakota is administered by the South Dakota Electrical Commission within the South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation.

### What experience is required before the South Dakota journeyman exam?

South Dakota identifies four years, totaling 8,000 hours, as a licensed apprentice electrician working under the employment and supervision of an electrical contractor before a candidate is ready to write the journeyman’s exam.

### What experience is required before the South Dakota electrical contractor exam?

South Dakota identifies two additional years as a licensed journeyman, totaling 4,000 additional hours under the employment and supervision of a contractor, including at least 2,000 hours of commercial work, before writing the electrical contractor’s exam.

### Does South Dakota license electricians statewide?

Yes. South Dakota uses statewide electrical licensing through the South Dakota Electrical Commission.

### Does South Dakota have a Master Electrician license?

South Dakota’s advanced electrical licensing path is tied to the Electrical Contractor license rather than one simple Master Electrician title. This combo supports master-level electrical study and contractor-level exam preparation.

### Who handles South Dakota electrical licensing?

South Dakota electrical licensing is handled through the South Dakota Electrical Commission under the South Dakota Department of Labor and Regulation.

### Does South Dakota have state electrical licensing?

Yes. South Dakota electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the South Dakota Electrical Commission.

### Should I confirm South Dakota licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What NEC edition is South Dakota currently using for exams?

South Dakota states that, as of December 2, 2024, all tests are on the 2023 NEC code. Candidates should use the code edition required by the South Dakota Electrical Commission for their scheduled exam.

### What NEC edition is used for South Dakota electrical exams?

South Dakota Electrical Commission exam information states that all tests are based on the 2023 NEC starting December 2, 2024. This combo uses the 2026 NEC for updated code-cycle study and code-navigation practice.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master-level electrical exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, branch circuit work, service and feeder calculations, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, voltage drop, electrical theory, and other math-based topics. ### How do NEC tabs help during study?

Tabs help organize the code book and make major NEC sections easier to locate during study. They are most useful when paired with practice questions, repeated code lookup, and careful reading of NEC rules, tables, notes, and exceptions.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

## More questions

### Does South Dakota require approval before taking the exam?

Yes. Candidates must submit an application and receive prior written approval from the South Dakota Electrical Commission before taking the exam.

### How long does a South Dakota candidate have to arrange the exam after approval?

Once the application is approved, the Commission sends exam-site details, and the candidate has 90 days to make the necessary arrangements to sit for the exam.
