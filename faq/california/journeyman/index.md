## The exam

### Is the California General Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The California General Electrician exam is treated as an open book test because reference materials are available during the exam. Current exam preparation information identifies the references as provided at the testing center.

### How many questions are on the California General Electrician exam?

The current California General Electrician exam outline lists 100 questions with 4 hours and 30 minutes allowed.

### How much time is allowed for the exam?

The time allowed for the California General Electrician exam is 4 hours and 30 minutes.

### What score is required to pass?

A minimum score of 70% is required to pass the California electrician certification examination.

### How many hours are required for California General Electrician certification?

California identifies 8,000 hours of qualifying work experience for the General Electrician classification. The required hours must include work in approved categories, with maximum credit limits for specific types of work.

### What score is required to pass the California electrician certification exam?

The current California Candidate Information Bulletin states that candidates must achieve a minimum score of 70% to pass the examination.

### Who administers California electrician certification?

California electrician certification is administered by the Department of Industrial Relations, Division of Labor Standards Enforcement, Electrician Certification Unit.

### How much time is allowed for the California General Electrician exam?

The California General Electrician certification exam is commonly listed with a 4 hour and 30 minute time limit.

### What score is required to pass the California General Electrician exam?

The California General Electrician certification exam is commonly listed with a 70% passing score.

## Licensing and experience

### Does California require continuing education for renewal?

Yes. California electrician certification must be renewed every three years, and the current bulletin states that certificate holders must complete 32 hours of continuing education before renewal from an approved provider in California.

### Is this the same as a California C-10 contractor license?

No. California General Electrician certification applies to the individual electrician. A C-10 Electrical Contractor license is a separate contractor license issued through the Contractors State License Board.

## Code book and materials

### Can I bring my own NEC book to the California exam?

No. The testing center provides the approved reference materials used during the examination, and candidates may not bring or use their own reference materials.
