The exam

Is the California C-10 Electrical exam open book?

No. The California C-10 Electrical contractor examination is closed book. No reference materials may be used during the exam, and a calculator is provided when computation is required.

How many questions are on the California General Electrician certification exam?

The California General Electrician certification exam is listed as 100 questions with a time allowance of 4 hours and 30 minutes. The exam content includes safety, determination of electrical system requirements, installation, and maintenance and repair.

Who administers California electrician certification?

California electrician certification is administered through the Department of Industrial Relations, Division of Labor Standards Enforcement, Electrician Certification Unit.

Who schedules California electrician certification exams?

For exams taken on or after June 1, 2026, scheduling is handled through CPS HR Consulting and Pearson VUE after the applicant is approved by DLSE.

Is the California electrician certification exam open book?

Yes. Specified references are provided in the testing center and are available during the examination. Candidates may not bring or use their own reference materials in the testing room.

How much time is allowed for the California General Electrician certification exam?

Current California electrician certification materials list a 4-hour and 30-minute time limit for the General Electrician certification exam.

What score is required to pass California electrician certification exams?

California electrician certification examinations require a minimum score of 70% to pass.

Who administers California electrical contractor licensing?

California electrical contractor licensing is administered through the Contractors State License Board.

Licensing and experience

Does California have a license officially called Master Electrician?

California does not issue a statewide license officially titled “Master Electrician” in the same way many other states do. California uses the C-10 Electrical contractor classification through CSLB and electrician certification categories through DLSE.

Does California have a statewide Master Electrician license?

California does not use a single statewide license titled “Master Electrician” in the same format used by some other states. Electrical contractors are licensed through CSLB under the C-10 Electrical classification, and individual electrician certification is handled through the DIR Electrician Certification Unit.

Does California issue a statewide master electrician license?

California does not use one single statewide master electrician license title like some states. California uses electrician certification through DIR and electrical contractor licensing through CSLB.

Is the California C-10 Electrical Contractor exam closed book?

Yes. Current CSLB C-10 Electrical study material states that the examination is closed book and that no reference materials may be used during the examination.

Code book and materials

How do the flash cards help with closed-book exam prep?

Flash cards support quick recall of formulas, definitions, safety concepts, trade terms, and key electrical ideas. Repeated flash card review is useful when the exam does not allow reference materials.

Can I bring my own NEC book to the California electrician certification exam?

For the current California electrician certification exams, the candidate information identifies references that are provided in the testing center and states that candidates may not bring or use their own reference materials. Candidates should follow the instructions for their exact certification category.

Can I bring my own NEC into the California General Electrician certification exam?

Current California electrician certification materials state that references are provided in the testing center and candidates may not bring or use their own reference materials.

More questions

What topics are covered on the California C-10 Electrical exam?

The C-10 Electrical exam covers Planning and Estimating, Rough Wiring, Finish Wiring and Trim, Startup, Troubleshooting, and Maintenance, and Safety.

How should I use the 12 practice exams?

Use the practice exams throughout your study plan. Review missed questions carefully, identify weak areas, and return to those topics before moving to the next practice exam.

When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They are best used as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.

What topics should I study for the California C-10 Electrical exam?

Important study areas include planning and estimating, rough wiring, finish wiring and trim, startup, troubleshooting, maintenance, safety, electrical calculations, code requirements, grounding and bonding, raceways, panels, equipment installation, photovoltaic systems, energy storage, and jobsite safety.

What are the main sections of the California C-10 Electrical exam?

The CSLB C-10 Electrical study guide lists Planning and Estimating, Rough Wiring, Finish Wiring and Trim, Startup, Troubleshooting, and Maintenance, and Safety as major exam sections.
