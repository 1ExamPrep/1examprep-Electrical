## The exam

### Is the Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Nebraska Journeyman Electrician examination is open book with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current Nebraska State Electrical Division and PSI instructions for allowed references, code edition, tabs, markings, calculators, and exam-day procedures.

### Who administers the Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam?

Nebraska uses PSI as the exam provider for Electrical Contractor, Journeyman, and Residential Wireman examinations. Candidates must receive state approval before scheduling through PSI.

### How many questions are on the Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with 80 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with 240 minutes allowed for testing.

### What score is required to pass?

The Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a required passing score of 70%.

### Does Nebraska use an open-book electrical exam format?

The Nebraska journeyman electrician examination is listed as an open-book examination. Candidates should review the current PSI candidate bulletin and Nebraska State Electrical Division instructions for the approved reference list, marking rules, tabbing rules, and code edition required for their exam.

### What NEC edition is listed in Nebraska exam information?

The Nebraska State Electrical Division currently lists the National Electrical Code 2023 in its exam information. This Super Combo is based on the 2026 NEC for candidates who want 2026 NEC-based study materials and code navigation practice.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Nebraska have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Nebraska has statewide electrical licensing through the Nebraska State Electrical Division and Nebraska State Electrical Board.

### What experience is required for Nebraska journeyman electrician licensing?

Nebraska requires at least four years of verifiable experience, equal to 8,000 hours, acceptable to the Board, in the electrical trade. Apprentice registration for those years may be accepted as evidence of experience when approved by the Board.

### Who licenses Nebraska journeyman electricians?

Nebraska journeyman electricians are licensed through the Nebraska State Electrical Division.

### Can education count toward Nebraska journeyman experience?

One year of work experience credit may be given for completion of a two-year post-high school electrical course with an associate degree in electrical technology. Qualified military electrical experience may also be considered.

### Does Nebraska have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Nebraska electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

### Should I confirm Nebraska licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

### What topics should I study for the Nebraska journeyman electrician exam?

Students should study the National Electrical Code, Basic Electricity and Theory, the Nebraska State Electrical Act, Blueprint Reading, Emergency Circuits including Fire Alarm Circuits, calculations, wiring methods, grounding and bonding, services, feeders, branch circuits, overcurrent protection, motors, and equipment.

### How long is the Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a testing time of 4 hours.

### What topics should I study?

Study areas include the NEC, electrical theory, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, equipment, special occupancies, special conditions, communication systems, emergency and fire alarm circuits, blueprint reading concepts, Nebraska licensing concepts, and calculations.

### What does the Nebraska Journeyman Electrician exam cover?

Nebraska exam information lists the National Electrical Code, basic electricity theory, the Nebraska State Electrical Act, blueprint reading, emergency circuits, and fire alarm circuits as exam content areas.
