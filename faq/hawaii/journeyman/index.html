The exam

Is the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The current PSI Candidate Information Bulletin identifies the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician examination as open book, with the National Electrical Code reference provided by the examination center.

How many questions are on the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam?

The Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam contains 70 questions. A small number of unscored experimental questions may also be administered for examination development.

How much time is allowed for the exam?

The time allowed for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam is 180 minutes.

What score is required to pass?

The minimum passing score for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam is 70%.

How many experience hours are required for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician license?

Hawaii requires at least five years of full-time experience, or its equivalent, but not less than 10,000 hours in residential or commercial wiring under the supervision of a journey worker electrician or supervising electrician.

What score is required to pass the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam?

The current PSI Candidate Information Bulletin lists a minimum passing score of 70% for the Journey Worker Electrician examination.

Who administers Hawaii electrician licensing?

Hawaii electrician licensing is administered through the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Professional and Vocational Licensing Division, Board of Electricians and Plumbers.

How much time is allowed for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam?

The Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician examination has a 180-minute time limit.

Licensing and experience

Who oversees Hawaii electrician licensing?

Hawaii electrician licensing is administered by the Department of Commerce and Consumer Affairs, Professional and Vocational Licensing Division, Board of Electricians and Plumbers.

What is the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician license?

The Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician license is the state electrician classification commonly referred to as a journeyman electrician license. A licensed journey worker electrician is authorized to perform electrical work within the scope of Hawaii law.

How much experience is required for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician license?

Hawaii requires at least five years of full-time experience or the equivalent, but not less than 10,000 hours, in residential or commercial wiring under the supervision of a journey worker or supervising electrician. Hawaii also requires 240 hours of accepted electrical academic coursework.

What is the Hawaii journeyman electrician license called?

Hawaii uses the Journey Worker Electrician license for the individual electrician credential commonly associated with journeyman-level electrical work.

What experience is required for Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician eligibility?

Hawaii requires at least 5 years full-time or equivalent experience in residential or commercial wiring, but not less than 10,000 hours, under the supervision of a journey worker or supervising electrician.

Does a Hawaii electrician license allow me to contract for electrical work?

No. Hawaii DCCA application materials state that an electrician license does not allow the holder to contract to perform electrical work. Electrical contracting requires the appropriate contractor license.

Code book and materials

What reference is provided for the Hawaii exam?

The examination center provides the National Electrical Code, 2020 edition, for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam. This product includes flash cards based on the 2026 National Electrical Code for expanded study and current-code review.

What NEC edition is currently provided for the Hawaii exam?

The current PSI bulletin lists the National Electrical Code, 2020 edition, as the provided reference material for the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician examination.

Can I bring my own NEC book into the Hawaii exam?

No. For the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam, the NEC reference material is provided by the examination center. The included 2026 NEC with tabs is for study and code-navigation preparation.

What topics are covered on the Hawaii Journey Worker Electrician exam?

Published subject areas include general electrical knowledge, service, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies, conditions, and equipment, electrical power, motors, equipment for general use, low voltage and communication circuits, lighting, and safety information.

Does Hawaii require classroom coursework?

Yes. Hawaii requires 240 hours of electrical academic coursework accepted by a University of Hawaii Community College offering an appropriate program of study.

Does Hawaii require electrical academic coursework for Journey Worker Electrician applicants?

Yes. Hawaii lists 240 hours of electrical academic coursework accepted by a University of Hawaii Community College offering an appropriate program of study.
