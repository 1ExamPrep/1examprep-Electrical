The exam

Is the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam open book?

Yes. The published Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor outline identifies the examination as open book and lists the National Electrical Code or National Electrical Code Handbook as allowed reference material. Candidates must follow the current PSI and Nevada State Contractors Board rules for approved references and exam-room materials.

How many questions are on the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam?

The current Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam outline lists 80 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam?

The published Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor outline lists 3.5 hours of testing time.

What is the passing score for the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam?

The minimum passing score is 56 correct answers out of 80 questions, which equals 70%.

Who administers Nevada contractor licensing?

Nevada contractor licensing is administered by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

What score is required to pass the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam?

The published Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor outline lists a minimum passing score of 56.

Licensing and experience

What references are allowed in the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam room?

The approved references include the National Electrical Code or National Electrical Code Handbook and NFPA 72 - National Fire Alarm Code or National Fire Alarm Code Handbook. Candidates must follow the edition and reference rules listed for their scheduled exam.

Does Nevada issue one statewide Master Electrician license?

Nevada electrical contractor licensing is handled statewide by the Nevada State Contractors Board. Individual journeyman or master electrician credentials may be handled by local jurisdictions, depending on where the work is performed.

Who provides Nevada contractor exams?

PSI provides contractor examination services for the Nevada State Contractors Board.

Does Nevada have a statewide master electrician license?

Nevada electrical licensing is primarily handled through contractor classifications such as C-2 Electrical Contracting, and local jurisdictions may have additional individual electrician requirements. Candidates should confirm the exact license or credential they need.

What topics are covered on the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam?

The content outline includes general electrical knowledge, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies and equipment, electrical power, motors, low voltage, lighting, illuminated signs, fire detection and alarm systems, safety information, transmission lines, and overcurrent protection.

Should I confirm Nevada licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, local jurisdiction rules, state contractor requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

Can I use tabs in my reference books for the Nevada exam?

Yes. Nevada PSI rules allow permanent tabs. Temporary removable tabs, including Post-it notes, are not allowed.

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because electrical exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, service and feeder work, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, electrical theory, and other math-based topics. A dedicated calculations study guide helps candidates practice these skills consistently.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the specific exam authority. Candidates should review the official candidate bulletin before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

More questions

Does Nevada require a business and law exam?

Nevada requires contractor applicants to pass the required trade examination and a general business and law, or CMS, examination when required by the Nevada State Contractors Board.

Does a local Nevada master electrician credential automatically allow statewide electrical contracting?

No. Local individual credentials and state contractor licensing are separate considerations. Candidates who plan to contract for electrical work should review Nevada State Contractors Board requirements and any local rules that apply.
