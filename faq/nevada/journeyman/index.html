The exam

Are Nevada journeyman-level electrical exams open book?

Nevada journeyman-level electrical qualification exams are commonly prepared for as open-book, NEC-based exams. Candidates should follow the exact exam instructions from the qualification card program, local authority, or testing provider administering their exam.

Is the Nevada journeyman electrician exam open book?

Yes. The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician examination used for journeyman-level electrical qualification testing is open book. Candidates should follow the current ICC and Pearson VUE rules for approved references, tabs, calculators, and exam-room materials.

How many questions are on the National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician examination has 80 multiple-choice questions.

How much time is allowed for the journeyman electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician examination has a 4-hour time limit.

Who administers the Nevada journeyman electrician exam?

Journeyman electrician qualification testing in Nevada commonly uses ICC electrician examinations, which are scheduled through ICC and administered through Pearson VUE testing options.

Licensing and experience

Does Nevada have a single statewide journeyman electrician license?

Nevada does not use one uniform statewide individual journeyman electrician license for every journeyman credential. Individual journeyman-level cards or credentials may be handled through local authorities, qualification card programs, municipalities, counties, or authorities having jurisdiction, while statewide contractor licensing is handled separately.

Is the Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam the same as a journeyman exam?

No. The Nevada C-2 Electrical Contractor exam is connected to contractor licensing through the Nevada State Contractors Board. A journeyman-level electrical exam or qualification card is typically connected to individual trade qualification and local or program requirements.

Does Nevada issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

Nevada does not issue one single statewide individual journeyman electrician license through a state electrical journeyman board. Individual journeyman credentials are commonly handled by local jurisdictions.

Does Nevada license electrical contractors separately from journeyman electricians?

Yes. Electrical contractors are licensed through the Nevada State Contractors Board. Journeyman and master electrician qualification cards are commonly connected to electrical qualification card requirements and ICC electrician examinations.

Does Nevada use state and local electrical licensing requirements?

Nevada electrical requirements may involve state contractor licensing and local journeyman-level requirements. Candidates should confirm the specific authority or jurisdiction connected to their exam path.

Should I confirm Nevada licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

What reference book should I study?

The National Electrical Code is the primary reference for NEC-based electrical exam preparation. Candidates should also study any ICC, local jurisdiction, or qualification card program reference materials required for their specific exam.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current testing instructions for the exam being taken. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Should I study local rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review the Nevada or local electrical rules, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, and any jurisdiction-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

More questions

What topics should I study?

Students should review electrical theory, NEC navigation, calculations, wiring methods, conductors, raceways, boxes, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, motors, controls, transformers, lighting, safety, emergency systems, fire alarm systems, special occupancies, and local amendments when required.

How should I use the practice exams?

Use the 12 practice exams to test your knowledge, improve pacing, and identify weak areas. Review every missed question and return to the related study guide section or NEC topic before moving forward.

How should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing topic review and several practice exams. Take them in a focused setting, track time, review missed answers, and return to weak topics before test day.

How should I use the flash cards?

Use the flash cards for daily active recall. Separate cards into topics you know, topics you partly know, and topics that need more review. Study the weakest group most often.

What topics should I study for a Nevada journeyman electrician exam?

Common study topics include electrical theory, services, feeders, branch circuits, conductor sizing, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, boxes, raceways, overcurrent protection, motors, calculations, equipment, special occupancies, and NEC table use.

What topics are covered on the journeyman electrician exam?

The exam covers general knowledge, services and service equipment, feeders, branch circuits and conductors, wiring methods and materials, equipment and devices, control devices, motors and generators, and special occupancies, equipment, and conditions.

Are the flash cards useful for Nevada electrical exam preparation?

Yes. Flash cards are useful for quick review of formulas, definitions, electrical theory, safety concepts, NEC topics, and trade knowledge. They work well alongside longer study sessions and timed practice.
