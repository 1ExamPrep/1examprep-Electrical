## The exam

### Is the Montana Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

The Montana Journeyman Electrician exam is a code-based PSI examination with reference rules established by the Montana State Electrical Board and PSI. Candidates should follow the current exam information sheet, admission letter, and PSI instructions for allowed references and exam-day procedures.

### Who administers the Montana Journeyman Electrician exam?

PSI administers the examination for the Montana State Electrical Board after the applicant has been approved by the Board.

### Does Montana use an open-book electrical exam format?

Montana electrical exam instructions allow specified reference material in the examination site, including the National Electrical Code edition listed for the exam. Candidates should follow the exact reference, tabbing, and marking rules provided with their exam authorization.

### How many questions are on the Montana Journeyman Electrician exam?

Montana law states that the journeyman electrician examination must consist of at least 60 questions designed to test knowledge and technical application skills in the National Electrical Code, Board rules, and applicable laws.

### How much time is allowed for the Montana Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Montana Journeyman Electrician examination has a time allowance of 180 minutes.

### What score is required to pass the Montana Journeyman Electrician exam?

The required passing score for the Montana Journeyman Electrician examination is 75% correct.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Montana have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Montana has statewide electrician licensing through the Montana State Electrical Board under the Montana Department of Labor and Industry.

### What experience is required for Montana journeyman electrician licensing?

Montana provides multiple qualification paths, including completion of an approved inside wireman apprenticeship or equivalent training program, 8,000 hours of legally obtained practical electrical experience, or a qualifying electrical maintenance pathway requiring 20,000 maintenance hours with at least 8,000 practical hours.

### Who licenses Montana journeyman electricians?

Montana journeyman electricians are licensed through the Montana State Electrical Board under the Department of Labor and Industry.

### Does Montana have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Montana electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

### Should I confirm Montana licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What code edition is Montana using for electrical examinations?

Montana State Electrical Board exam information currently identifies examinations as based on the 2020 National Electrical Code. Candidates should follow current Board and PSI instructions for the exact edition required for their exam.

### What code edition is used for the Montana exam?

Montana’s current examination information states that exams are based on the 2020 NEC as of June 10, 2022. This product is based on the 2026 NEC, and candidates should follow the current Montana and PSI instructions for the exam they are taking.

### Can I use highlighted or handwritten notes in my reference book?

No. Montana exam reference materials must be unmarked. Highlighting, underlining, writing, indexing, and additional papers are not allowed unless specifically permitted under the exam rules for the reference material.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Montana testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### Should I study state rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Montana electrical laws, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, state rules, and any Montana-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.
