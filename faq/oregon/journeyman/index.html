The exam

Is the Oregon General Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Oregon General Journeyman Electrician examination is administered as an open book examination with approved reference rules. Candidates should follow the current Oregon Building Codes Division and testing-provider instructions for allowed references, code edition, book condition, calculators, and exam-day procedures.

What score is required to pass the Oregon electrical licensing exam?

The passing score for Oregon licensing examinations is 75 percent correct or better.

Who administers Oregon journeyman electrician licensing?

Oregon journeyman electrician licensing is administered by the Oregon Building Codes Division under the Department of Consumer and Business Services.

How many questions are on the Oregon Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Oregon journeyman examination is commonly identified as a 52-question examination.

How much time is allowed for the Oregon General Journeyman Electrician exam?

Oregon’s General Journeyman Electrician examination information lists a 3-hour testing period.

What score is required to pass?

The Oregon General Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed with a required passing score of 75%.

Who administers Oregon electrical licensing?

Oregon electrical licensing is administered through the Oregon Building Codes Division under the Department of Consumer and Business Services.

What score is required to pass the Oregon Journeyman Electrician exam?

The passing score is 75%, which equals 39 correct answers on a 52-question examination.

Licensing and experience

Does Oregon have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Oregon has statewide electrical individual licensing through the Oregon Building Codes Division.

What references are approved for Oregon electrical license exams?

Approved materials include the National Electrical Code/NFPA 70 and errata with Oregon amendments, NEC Handbook, Oregon Revised Statutes chapter 479, Oregon Administrative Rules division 918, American Electrician’s Handbook, Electrical Black Book, Ugly’s Electrical References, Ferm’s Fast Finder Index, Tom Henry’s Key Word Index, and a silent, non-printing, non-programmable calculator.

What experience documentation is needed for Oregon electrical licensing?

Oregon applicants must complete the work history and submit experience verification forms. One Electrical Experience Verification Form is required for each employer, and experience must be listed in the required work categories under Oregon Administrative Rule 918-282.

What experience is required for the Oregon General Journeyman Electrician license?

Oregon lists completion of an approved Oregon apprenticeship as one qualification path. Out-of-state qualification paths may include 576 hours of classroom training with 8,000 hours of on-the-job experience, or an alternate experience-based path with 16,000 hours of documented on-the-job experience.

Who licenses Oregon journeyman electricians?

Oregon journeyman electricians are licensed through the Oregon Building Codes Division.

What experience is required for Oregon journeyman electrician licensure?

The Oregon General Journeyman Electrician path commonly includes 8,000 hours of qualifying on-the-job electrical experience and 576 hours of related classroom instruction through an approved apprenticeship or accepted equivalent pathway.

Does Oregon have a master electrician license?

Oregon uses supervising electrician classifications for higher-level electrical authority. The General Supervising Electrician license is different from the General Journeyman Electrician license and has additional requirements.

Does Oregon have a state Journeyman Electrician license?

Yes. Oregon electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and journeyman electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

Is a journeyman electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license in Oregon?

No. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license. Electrical contractor or business requirements may be separate when offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

Should I confirm Oregon licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

Are tabs allowed for the Oregon electrical exam?

Oregon electrical license examination guidelines allow approved published materials to be altered for candidate use, including tabs and personal handwritten or printed notes within the margins of approved materials.

Why are NEC tabs useful for studying?

Tabs help candidates move through the National Electrical Code more efficiently during study. They can make it easier to locate important articles, tables, and definitions while practicing code lookup.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Oregon testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Should I study Oregon rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Oregon electrical laws, Building Codes Division rules, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, and any Oregon-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

Students should study the National Electrical Code, Oregon amendments, Oregon electrical laws and rules, electrical theory, calculations, wiring methods, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, controls, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, communication systems, and safety.

How should I use the practice exams?

Use the 12 practice exams to test your knowledge, improve pacing, and identify weak areas. Review every missed question and return to the related study guide section, NEC topic, Oregon amendment, or Oregon rule topic before moving forward.

How should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing topic review and several practice exams. Take them in a focused setting, track time, review missed answers, and return to weak topics before test day.

How should I use the flash cards?

Use the flash cards for daily active recall. Separate cards into topics you know, topics you partly know, and topics that need more review. Study the weakest group most often.
