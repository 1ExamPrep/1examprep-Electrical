## The exam

### Does passing the exam automatically give me a Kentucky Master Electrician license?

No. Passing the exam is one part of the licensing process. Kentucky also requires applicants to submit a completed application, proof of qualifying electrical experience, passing test results, the required fee, and a passport-sized color photo.

### Is the Kentucky Master Electrician exam open book?

Kentucky master electrician preparation should include open-book code navigation. Candidates should follow the current exam instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

### How many questions are on the Kentucky Master Electrician exam?

The published Kentucky Master Electrician exam information lists 100 questions with a 4-hour time limit.

### How much time is allowed for the Kentucky Master Electrician exam?

The Kentucky Master Electrician exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

### What is the passing score for the Kentucky Master Electrician exam?

The Kentucky Master Electrician exam is scored against a 70% cut score.

### Who administers the Kentucky electrical licensing exam?

Kentucky electrical licensing examinations are administered through Prov, Inc. for the Commonwealth of Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division.

## Licensing and experience

### How much experience does Kentucky require for a Master Electrician license?

Kentucky requires proof of experience totaling 16,000 hours in the electrical trade since the applicant’s sixteenth birthday. The state also identifies certain education, apprenticeship, and teaching experience options that may substitute for a portion of verifiable electrical trade experience.

### Is Kentucky electrical licensing statewide?

Yes. Kentucky electrical licensing is administered at the state level through the Commonwealth of Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division within the Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

### Does Kentucky have state electrical licensing?

Yes. Kentucky electrical licensing is handled at the state level through the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

### Should I confirm Kentucky licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

### Can I use tabs in my reference book for the Kentucky exam?

Yes. Kentucky reference rules allow permanent tabs. Moveable tabs and Post-it notes are not permitted in reference books used during the exam.

### What references are listed for the Kentucky Master Electrician exam?

The listed references include NFPA 70, National Electrical Code, and Ugly’s Electrical References. Candidates should use the edition approved for the exam they are scheduled to take.

## More questions

### Which testing options does Kentucky accept for the Master Electrician exam?

Kentucky’s application checklist identifies PROV, NASCLA, and Pearson VUE as accepted testing options for the Master Electrician exam. Candidates should follow the current scheduling and testing instructions from the provider they use.

### What topics are covered on the Kentucky Master Electrician exam?

The exam outline includes General Electrical Theory & Principles, Plan Reading, Wiring & Protection, Wiring Methods & Materials, Motors & Controls, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Special Conditions, and Communication Systems.
