## What is the Kentucky journeyman electrician exam like?

### Is the Kentucky journeyman electrician exam open book?

Yes. Kentucky electrical examination information identifies the exams as open book and timed. Candidates should follow the current Kentucky and Prov rules for allowed references, tabs, notes, highlighting, calculators, and other test-day materials.

### How many questions are on the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

Some commonly used Kentucky approved provider formats list 80 questions for journeyman electrician testing. Candidates should follow the active bulletin for the exact exam format.

### How much time is allowed for the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

Some commonly used Kentucky approved provider formats list a 4-hour time limit for journeyman electrician testing. Candidates should follow the active provider bulletin for the scheduled exam.

### What score is required to pass?

The Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is listed with a required passing score of 70%.

### Who administers Kentucky electrical licensing?

Kentucky electrical licensing is administered through the Commonwealth of Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

### What passing score is required for the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is scored against a passing cut score of 70%.

### What score is required to pass the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

Kentucky administrative regulations require a minimum passing score of 70% on the examination for an electrician license.

### Who administers the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is administered through Prov for licensing through the Commonwealth of Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division.

## How do you get a Kentucky journeyman electrician license?

### Does Kentucky have statewide electrician licensing?

Yes. Kentucky has statewide licensing for electricians and electrical contractors through the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

### What experience is required for a Kentucky electrician license?

Kentucky’s electrician license checklist identifies proof of experience totaling 8,000 hours in the electrical trade since the applicant’s sixteenth birthday, along with the required application materials and passing exam results.

### Who oversees Kentucky electrician licensing?

Kentucky electrician licensing is overseen by the Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction. The state licenses electricians, master electricians, and electrical contractors.

### Is a Kentucky electrician license the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. An electrician license supports individual electrical work within the scope allowed by law. An electrical contractor license relates to contracting authority and business responsibility. Candidates should apply for the credential that matches the work they plan to perform.

### Who licenses Kentucky journeyman electricians?

Kentucky journeyman electricians are licensed through the Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

### What experience is required for Kentucky journeyman electrician licensure?

Kentucky journeyman applicants commonly qualify through 4 years of supervised electrical experience and 576 hours of approved electrical education, or through additional supervised experience when the education path is not used.

## Which code book and materials are allowed on the Kentucky exam?

### Why should I study with a tabbed NEC?

A tabbed NEC can make study sessions more organized by helping candidates locate important code sections more efficiently. It supports repeated lookup practice and helps candidates become more familiar with the structure of the National Electrical Code.

### Are NEC tabs allowed during the Kentucky electrical exam?

Permanent tabs are allowed under Prov’s reference-material rules. Moveable tabs and Post-it notes are not permitted in a reference book.

## What else do Kentucky journeyman candidates ask?

### What topics are covered on the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

Published subject areas include General Electrical Theory and Principles, Plan Reading, Wiring and Protection, Wiring Methods and Materials, Motors and Controls, Equipment for General Use, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Special Conditions, and Communication Systems.

### What topics should I study for the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

Important study areas include general electrical knowledge, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, conductors, raceways, boxes, grounding and bonding, services, feeders, branch circuits, motors, safety, and calculations.

### How long is the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is listed with a testing time of 3 hours.

Flash cards support quick review of electrical terms, formulas, code concepts, safety topics, and exam-relevant knowledge areas. They are useful for short daily study sessions and repeated recall practice.
