The exam

Is the West Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

West Virginia electrical examinations are treated as open book NEC-based examinations. Candidates should prepare by learning how to navigate the National Electrical Code efficiently and by following the exam-room material rules for their specific test date.

How many questions are on the West Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

West Virginia’s 2026 electrical exam update states that the Master, Journeyman, and Single Family Dwelling exams contain 50 multiple-choice questions. The questions are valued at two points each, and calculation-based questions are incorporated directly into the multiple-choice format.

Who administers electrician licensing in West Virginia?

West Virginia electrician licensing is administered by the West Virginia State Fire Marshal’s Office.

What score is needed to pass the West Virginia electrician exam?

A passing grade of at least 70% is required for West Virginia electrician licensing examinations.

Licensing and experience

Does West Virginia have statewide electrician licensing?

Yes. West Virginia electrician licensing is administered statewide by the West Virginia State Fire Marshal’s Office, Regulatory and Licensing Division.

Who regulates West Virginia journeyman electrician licensing?

West Virginia journeyman electrician licensing is administered by the West Virginia State Fire Marshal’s Office through its Regulatory and Licensing Division.

What experience is required for a West Virginia journeyman electrician license?

West Virginia defines a journeyman electrician as a person qualified by at least one year of electrical work experience and licensed by the West Virginia State Fire Marshal.

Code book and materials

What code edition is used for West Virginia electrical exams in 2026?

Beginning January 1, 2026, West Virginia electrical examinations are based on the 2023 edition of NFPA 70, National Electrical Code. This product package is based on the 2026 NEC and includes the National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback for study and professional reference.

Why are NEC tabs helpful for West Virginia electrical exam preparation?

Tabs help students organize the NEC and locate important sections faster during study. They are useful for practicing questions involving services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, and special equipment.

More questions

Where are West Virginia electrical exams offered?

West Virginia 2026 exam locations include Charleston, Beckley, Clarksburg, Martinsburg, and Wellsburg. Candidates should use the current State Fire Marshal exam schedule for available dates.

What should I study for the West Virginia Journeyman Electrician exam?

Students should study the 2023 National Electrical Code, electrical theory, calculations, wiring methods, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, equipment, special occupancies, special equipment, and safe installation practices.

How should I use the practice exams?

Use the 12 practice exams to test your knowledge, improve pacing, and identify weak areas. Review every missed question and return to the related study guide section, NEC topic, or calculation method before moving forward.

How should I use the flash cards?

Use the flash cards for daily active recall. Separate cards into topics you know, topics you partly know, and topics that need more review. Study the weakest group most often.

What are the West Virginia journeyman electrician qualification paths?

Journeyman applicants must meet at least one approved qualification path: at least 1 year of experience as an assistant/helper electrician under a master or journeyman electrician, completion of a United States Department of Labor registered electrical apprenticeship program, or completion of an approved West Virginia Board of Education electrical vocational program of at least 1,080 hours with the required testing and instructor affidavit.

Can a West Virginia journeyman electrician design electrical systems?

No. West Virginia’s journeyman electrician classification is not authorized to design electrical systems. Design authority is associated with the master electrician classification.

Are the flash cards useful for West Virginia electrical exam preparation?

Yes. Flash cards are useful for quick review of formulas, definitions, electrical theory, safety concepts, NEC topics, and trade knowledge. They work well alongside longer study sessions, calculation drills, and code lookup practice.
