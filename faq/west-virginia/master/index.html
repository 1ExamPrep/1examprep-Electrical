How many questions are on the West Virginia Master Electrician exam?

The West Virginia State Fire Marshal’s 2026 electrical exam update states that the Master, Journeyman, and Single Family Dwelling exams contain 50 multiple-choice questions.

Who administers electrician licensing in West Virginia?

Electrician licensing in West Virginia is administered through the West Virginia State Fire Marshal’s Office, Regulatory and Licensing Division.

Are calculation questions part of the West Virginia electrical exam?

Yes. The 2026 exam update states that calculation-based questions are incorporated directly into the multiple-choice format.

Licensing and experience

What experience is required to apply for a West Virginia master electrician license?

West Virginia’s electrician licensing rule states that an applicant for a master electrician license must have two years of experience as a journeyman electrician in electrical work that demonstrates competence to perform all types of electrical work and instruct other electricians.

Who handles West Virginia master electrician licensing?

West Virginia electrician licensing is handled through the West Virginia State Fire Marshal’s Office, Regulatory and Licensing Division.

Is a license required to perform electrical work for hire in West Virginia?

Yes. West Virginia law requires the proper license and certificate for electrical work performed, offered, or engaged in for compensation or hire, subject to the supervised helper provisions allowed by law.

Does West Virginia have a Master Electrician license?

Yes. West Virginia recognizes master electrician licensing as part of its state electrical licensing structure.

Does a West Virginia Master Electrician license automatically equal an electrical contractor license?

No. A master electrician license is an individual electrical credential. Electrical contracting activity may also require the proper contractor license or business credential, depending on the work being offered or performed.

Should I study contractor licensing topics?

Candidates who plan to operate, qualify, advertise, bid, or contract through an electrical business should review West Virginia contractor licensing requirements in addition to individual electrician licensing requirements.

Should I confirm West Virginia licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

What NEC edition is West Virginia using for 2026 electrical exams?

As of January 1, 2026, West Virginia electrical examinations are based on the 2023 Edition of NFPA 70, National Electrical Code. Candidates should use the code edition required by the State Fire Marshal’s Office for their scheduled exam.

What code edition does West Virginia list for 2026 electrical exams?

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, branch circuit work, service and feeder calculations, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, voltage drop, electrical theory, and other math-based topics.

More questions

What does a West Virginia Master Electrician do?

A West Virginia Master Electrician is licensed by the State Fire Marshal and is competent to instruct and supervise electrical work performed by journeyman electricians and assistant or helper electricians.

What is the difference between a master electrician and a journeyman electrician in West Virginia?

A master electrician is expected to perform all types of electrical work and direct or instruct journeyman electricians and assistant or helper electricians. A journeyman electrician performs electrical work under the licensing rules but does not carry the same master-level supervisory role.

What should I study for the West Virginia Master Electrician exam?

Candidates should study NEC navigation, electrical theory, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, wiring methods, raceways, boxes, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, safety, state licensing topics, and electrical calculations.

What does the West Virginia electrical exam update say for 2026?

West Virginia’s published 2026 electrical exam update states that, as of January 1, 2026, all West Virginia electrical examinations are based on the 2023 edition of NFPA 70, National Electrical Code. The update also states that the Master, Journeyman, and Single Family Dwelling exams contain 50 multiple-choice questions valued at two points each.

Where are West Virginia electrical exams offered in 2026?

Published 2026 West Virginia electrical exam locations include Charleston, Beckley, Clarksburg, Martinsburg, and Wellsburg.

What does West Virginia expect a master electrician to know?

The licensing rule states that a master electrician must have comprehensive knowledge and understanding of the National Electrical Code and the ability to read electrical plans, drawings, and designs to calculate demand loads in compliance with the NEC.
