## The exam

### Is the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam is listed as an open book exam. Candidates should follow the current ICC, Pearson VUE, and Wyoming rules for approved references, code edition, tabs, notes, book format, and prohibited items.

### How many questions are on the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam is listed with 80 questions and a 4-hour time allowance.

### How much time is allowed for the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam has a 4-hour time allowance.

### What score is required to pass the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam?

Wyoming journeyman electrician applicants must receive a passing grade of 70% on the examination.

### How many hours are required to qualify for the Wyoming journeyman electrician exam?

Wyoming journeyman applicants must present evidence of a four-year, 8,000-hour apprenticeship or experience in the electrical wiring industry, along with the required classroom instruction.

### What score is needed to pass the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam?

A score of 70% is required to pass the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Wyoming have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Wyoming electrician licensing is administered statewide through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety under the State Fire Marshal’s Office.

### What experience is required for Wyoming journeyman electrician licensing?

Wyoming requires evidence of a four-year, 8,000-hour apprenticeship or experience in the electrical wiring industry, obtained over not less than four years, under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed master electrician.

### Who handles electrical licensing in Wyoming?

Wyoming electrical licensing is handled through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety under the Wyoming State Fire Marshal’s Office.

### Who regulates Wyoming journeyman electrician licensing?

Wyoming journeyman electrician licensing is administered through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety under the Wyoming State Fire Marshal’s Office.

### Is Wyoming electrician licensing statewide?

Yes. Wyoming uses a statewide electrical licensing structure for electrician credentials, including apprentice, journeyman, master, technician, and contractor-related categories.

## More questions

### What topics are on the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam?

The exam outline includes General Knowledge, Services and Service Equipment, Feeders, Branch Circuits and Conductors, Wiring Methods and Materials, Equipment and Devices, Control Devices, Motors and Generators, and Special Occupancies, Equipment, and Conditions.

### What classroom instruction is required?

Wyoming requires evidence of 144 hours per year, or 576 hours over a four-year apprenticeship, of successfully completed electrically related classroom instruction from approved electrical apprenticeship training programs.
