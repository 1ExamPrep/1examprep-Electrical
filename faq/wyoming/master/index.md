## The exam

### Who administers the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

The Wyoming Master Electrician exam is part of the ICC contractor/trades testing program and is scheduled through Pearson VUE after the Wyoming licensing agency approves the candidate’s eligibility.

### Is the Wyoming Master Electrician exam open book?

Wyoming Master Electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current Wyoming testing instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

### How many questions are on the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

The ICC Wyoming Electrical Contractor/Trades bulletin lists the Wyoming Master Electrician exam as 100 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

The current ICC Wyoming electrical bulletin lists a 5-hour time limit for the Wyoming Master Electrician exam.

### Do I need approval before scheduling the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Candidates must first apply with the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. After approval, the candidate may schedule the examination.

## Licensing and experience

### Who regulates Wyoming Master Electrician licensing?

Wyoming Master Electrician licensing is administered by the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety through the Wyoming State Fire Marshal’s Office.

### What experience is required for the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

Wyoming requires 8 years, or 16,000 hours, of work experience in the electrical wiring industry, including 4 years, or 8,000 hours, documented as a licensed journeyman.

### How often does the Wyoming Master Electrician license renew?

The Wyoming Master Electrician license is valid for 3 years and renews on July 1 of every third year.

### Does Wyoming require continuing education for Master Electrician renewal?

Yes. Wyoming requires 16 hours of approved update education before renewal, including at least 8 hours from a course that specifically covers the National Electrical Code.

### Who handles electrical licensing in Wyoming?

Electrical licensing in Wyoming is handled through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety.

### Who handles Wyoming master electrician licensing?

Wyoming electrical licensing is handled through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety under the Wyoming State Fire Marshal’s Office.

### Does Wyoming have a state Master Electrician license?

Yes. Wyoming electrical licensing is handled at the state level, and master electrician licensing is part of the state electrical licensing structure.

### Should I confirm Wyoming licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## Code book and materials

### What references are allowed for the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

Approved references include the National Electrical Code, Ugly’s Electrical References, Ferm’s Fast Finder Index, National Electrical Code Handbook, and Tom Henry’s Key Word Index.

### Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

### What NEC edition does Wyoming currently list for examinations?

Wyoming licensing information states that examinations are based on the current edition of the National Electrical Code, and the current ICC Wyoming electrical bulletin lists the 2023 National Electrical Code as the exam reference.

### Can I look up every answer during the open-book exam?

The ICC bulletin explains that time limits make it unrealistic to look up every answer. Candidates should be familiar with their references before exam day.

### What references are associated with the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

The Wyoming 701 Master Electrician exam reference list includes the National Electrical Code and Ugly’s Electrical References. Candidates should use the current exam bulletin for the exact editions and exam-room rules.

### Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, branch circuit work, service and feeder calculations, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, voltage drop, electrical theory, and other math-based topics.

### Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Wyoming testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

### Should I study state rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Wyoming electrical laws, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, state rules, and any Wyoming-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

## More questions

### Does Wyoming require classroom instruction for the Master Electrician exam?

Yes. Wyoming requires 576 hours of electrically related classroom instruction from approved apprenticeship training programs unless an approved education exemption applies.

### What topics are covered on the Wyoming Master Electrician exam?

The exam outline includes general knowledge, electrical theory, load calculations, code definitions, plan reading, services, feeders, branch circuits, conductors, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, equipment, controls, motors, generators, special occupancies, emergency systems, signaling systems, and fire alarm systems.
