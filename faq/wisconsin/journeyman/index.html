The exam

Is the Wisconsin Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. Wisconsin’s DSPS Journeyman Electrician exam information states that the exam is open book. Candidates must follow the current DSPS and Pearson VUE rules for permitted reference materials, binders, tabs, printed notes, and prohibited items.

Licensing and experience

Does Wisconsin have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Wisconsin journeyman electrician licensing is administered by the Wisconsin Department of Safety and Professional Services.

What experience is required for Wisconsin journeyman electrician licensing?

Wisconsin applicants may qualify through completion of a recognized electrical construction apprenticeship or through experience installing, repairing, and maintaining electrical wiring during at least 48 months with at least 8,000 hours, or at least 1,000 hours per year for at least five years. Limited electrical-related education credit may apply.

Does Wisconsin offer reciprocity for journeyman electricians?

Wisconsin identifies reciprocity pathways for applicants holding a valid, unexpired Journeyman Electrician license acquired through a state examination in Iowa or New Hampshire, when current Wisconsin requirements are met.

Who regulates electrician licensing in Wisconsin?

Electrician licensing in Wisconsin is administered by the Wisconsin Department of Safety and Professional Services.

Does Wisconsin allow education credit toward experience?

Yes. A full-time semester in an electrical-related program at an accredited college, university, technical school, or vocational school may count as 500 hours of experience, subject to Wisconsin’s limits on education credit.

Does Wisconsin have journeyman electrician reciprocity?

Wisconsin has a reciprocity pathway for applicants holding a valid, unexpired Journeyman Electrician license acquired through a state examination in Iowa or New Hampshire, subject to DSPS requirements.

Is Wisconsin electrician licensing statewide?

Yes. Wisconsin electrical credentials are administered through DSPS, and candidates follow the state licensing and examination process for the Journeyman Electrician license.

Code book and materials

What references are allowed for the Wisconsin Journeyman Electrician exam?

Allowed reference materials include Wisconsin Administrative Code SPS 305, Wisconsin Administrative Code SPS 316, printed notes in one binder, the 2017 National Electrical Codebook or Handbook, and up to two printed, bound reference books such as Ugly’s, Ferm’s Fast Finder Index, Tom Henry series books, or Mike Holt series books.

Are practice exams allowed in the Wisconsin exam room?

No. Practice exams and previous exams do not qualify as notes and are not allowed in the Wisconsin Journeyman Electrician exam room.

What NEC edition is currently listed for the Wisconsin exam?

Wisconsin’s current journeyman electrician exam reference information lists the 2017 National Electrical Codebook or Handbook as an allowed exam reference. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for updated code study and navigation practice.

Are printed notes allowed on the Wisconsin electrical exam?

Yes. Wisconsin’s exam reference rules allow printed notes as long as they are three-hole punched and placed in the binder. Practice exams and previous exams do not qualify as notes and are not allowed.

Are removable tabs or sticky notes allowed?

No. Wisconsin’s exam reference rules do not allow loose papers, removable tabs, sticky notes, or paperclips. Tabs are acceptable if they come with a bound code book, and three-ring paper dividers with tabs are permitted.
