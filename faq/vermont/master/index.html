The exam

Is the Vermont Master Electrician exam open book?

Vermont Master Electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current Vermont Division of Fire Safety exam instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

How many questions are on the Vermont Master Electrician exam?

The Vermont Master Electrician exam outline used in exam-preparation materials lists 105 questions.

How much time is allowed for the Vermont Master Electrician exam?

The Vermont Master Electrician exam outline used in exam-preparation materials lists a 5-hour testing window.

Who administers Vermont electrical licensing?

Vermont electrical licensing is administered through the Vermont Division of Fire Safety under the Department of Public Safety, with licensing connected to the Electricians’ Licensing Board.

Licensing and experience

Who regulates Vermont Master Electrician licensing?

Vermont Master Electrician licensing is handled through the Vermont Department of Public Safety, Division of Fire Safety, and the Vermont Electricians’ Licensing Board.

What experience is required for a Vermont Master Electrician license?

Vermont law requires a master electrician applicant to have been licensed as a journeyman electrician for at least 2 years, or to have comparable experience and training acceptable to the Board, and to pass the required examination.

Does Vermont have a journeyman electrician license?

Yes. Vermont licenses journeyman electricians, and the journeyman license is a common step toward qualifying for the Vermont Master Electrician license.

Does Vermont have Type-S electrician licenses?

Yes. Vermont recognizes Type-S journeyman electrician licensing categories for certain specialty electrical work classifications.

Does Vermont issue a Master Electrician license?

Yes. Vermont issues a statewide Master Electrician license for applicants who meet the state’s experience or comparable training requirements and pass the master electrician examination.

Does Vermont license master electricians?

Yes. Vermont licenses master electricians, journeyman electricians, apprentice electricians, and specialist electricians through the state electrical licensing system.

Who handles Vermont master electrician licensing?

Vermont electrician licensing is handled through the Vermont Department of Public Safety, Division of Fire Safety, with the Electrical Licensing Board connected to the licensing process.

Does Vermont require continuing education for electrician license renewal?

Yes. Vermont electricians must meet continuing education requirements for renewal. Master electricians should follow the current Division of Fire Safety renewal and continuing education instructions for their license cycle.

Does Vermont have a state Master Electrician license?

Yes. Vermont has state electrical licensing and recognizes a Master Electrician license through the Vermont Division of Fire Safety.

Should I confirm Vermont licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license classification, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

Code book and materials

What is a Vermont Master Electrician allowed to do?

A Vermont Master Electrician may design, install, repair, maintain, and replace electrical installations, including lightning rods, fire alarms, and fire detection systems, and may employ other licensed individuals or electrician’s helpers under the master electrician’s direction.

Can I use the 2026 NEC to study for Vermont electrical exams?

Yes. The 2026 NEC is useful for updated code-cycle study, reference-navigation practice, calculations, and long-term electrical knowledge. Candidates should use the official NEC edition required for their scheduled exam.

Why are NEC tabs helpful?

NEC tabs help candidates locate major articles, chapters, tables, and subject areas faster during study. This supports stronger code-navigation practice and helps candidates become more comfortable using the NEC as a reference.

Why is calculation practice important for this exam?

Calculation practice is important because master electrician exams can include load calculations, conductor sizing, branch circuit work, service and feeder calculations, box fill, raceway fill, motor calculations, transformer calculations, voltage drop, electrical theory, and other math-based topics.

How do NEC tabs help during study?

Tabs help organize the code book and make major NEC sections easier to locate during study. They are most useful when paired with practice questions, repeated code lookup, and careful reading of NEC rules, tables, notes, and exceptions.

Can I bring these books into the exam room?

Exam-room reference rules are controlled by the current Vermont testing instructions. Candidates should review the official rules before test day to confirm which books and materials are permitted.

Should I study state rules in addition to the NEC?

Yes. Candidates should review Vermont electrical laws, licensing requirements, inspection expectations, state rules, and any Vermont-specific subjects listed in the current examination instructions.

More questions

What should I study for the Vermont Master Electrician exam?

Candidates should study NEC navigation, electrical theory, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors, wiring methods, raceways, boxes, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, safety, state licensing topics, and electrical calculations.
