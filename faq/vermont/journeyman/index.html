The exam

Is the Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Vermont Journeyman Electrician examination is prepared for as an open-book, NEC-based exam under the current exam provider reference rules. Candidates should follow the current bulletin for approved references, tabs, calculators, markings, and prohibited materials.

How many questions are on the Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam?

Published Vermont journeyman electrician exam information commonly lists the exam as 80 multiple-choice questions with a 4-hour time limit.

How much time is allowed for the Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam has a 4-hour time allowance.

What score is required to pass the Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam?

The Vermont journeyman electrician exam is commonly listed with a 70% passing score.

How many experience hours are required for a Vermont journeyman electrician license?

The common Vermont journeyman path includes 8,000 hours of documented on-the-job electrical experience and 576 hours of related classroom instruction.

What score is needed to pass the Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam?

Vermont Division of Fire Safety exams administered by Prov are scored against a 70% cut score. Candidates who achieve 70% or higher receive a passing grade.

Why is code navigation important for an open book electrical exam?

Code navigation helps candidates locate definitions, tables, article sections, and requirements efficiently. On a timed exam, being familiar with the NEC can help candidates avoid wasting time searching through unfamiliar sections.

Licensing and experience

Does Vermont have statewide journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Vermont has statewide electrical licensing through the Vermont Division of Fire Safety.

What experience is commonly required before taking the Vermont journeyman exam?

Vermont journeyman preparation commonly includes 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and 576 hours of classroom instruction before a candidate sits for the journeyman licensing exam.

Who regulates Vermont journeyman electrician licensing?

Vermont journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Vermont Department of Public Safety, Division of Fire Safety.

Is Vermont electrician licensing statewide?

Yes. Vermont has a statewide electrical licensing structure for electrician classifications, including apprentice, journeyman, master, and specialist categories.

What experience is required for Vermont journeyman electrician licensing?

A common Vermont journeyman path includes 8,000 hours of on-the-job electrical experience as a registered apprentice and 576 hours of approved related classroom instruction.

Does Vermont have a master electrician license?

Yes. Vermont has a master electrician license. The master license is separate from the journeyman license and requires additional qualifying experience or another approved pathway.

Does Vermont have specialist electrician licenses?

Yes. Vermont recognizes specialist electrician categories for limited scopes of electrical work. A specialist license is not the same as a full journeyman electrician license.

Code book and materials

What references are listed for the Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam?

The listed references include the 2020 State of Vermont Electrical Safety Rules, NEC Notes, NFPA 70 - National Electrical Code 2020, Ugly’s Electrical References, and Vermont Statutes Title 26 Chapter 15.

Why are NEC tabs helpful for Vermont electrical exam preparation?

Tabs help students organize the NEC and locate important sections faster during study. They are useful for practicing questions involving services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, wiring methods, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, and special equipment.

Can I bring the study guides into the testing center?

Test preparation study guides and practice examinations are not approved references and are not permitted in the testing center. Study guides should be used before the exam for preparation.

Why are electrical calculations important for the journeyman electrician exam?

Electrical calculations help candidates solve problems involving load, conductor sizing, voltage drop, overcurrent protection, and other practical electrical topics. The included 2026 Electrician Calculations Study Guide gives candidates focused practice in this important exam-preparation area.
