## The exam

### Is the Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor exam open book?

Yes. The Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor examination is identified as an open book examination. Candidates must follow the exam provider’s rules for approved references, highlighting, underlining, indexing, permanent tabs, written notes, and loose or attached papers.

### How many questions are on the Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor exam?

The Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor examination is listed with 100 questions.

### How much time is allowed for the Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor exam?

The Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor examination is listed with 260 minutes of testing time.

### What score is required to pass the Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor exam?

The Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor examination requires a 73 percent score, which equals 73 correct items.

## Licensing and experience

### Who regulates Tennessee electrical contractor licensing?

Tennessee electrical contractor licensing is regulated by the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors under the Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance.

### When is a Tennessee contractor license required?

A Tennessee contractor’s license is required before contracting, bidding, or negotiating a price whenever the total cost of the project is $25,000 or more.

### What is the Tennessee CE - Electrical Contractor classification?

The CE - Electrical Contractor classification covers electrical systems serving residential, commercial, and industrial buildings, including premises wiring, lighting systems, branch circuits, feeders, services, raceways, grounding and bonding, generators, motor controls, transformers, electrical equipment, fire alarm systems, and communications raceways.

### What references are approved for the Tennessee CE - Electrical Contractor exam?

Approved references include NFPA 70 - National Electrical Code, Ugly’s Electrical References, OSHA 29 CFR 1926, and American Electrician’s Handbook. Candidates should use the exact editions listed in the current exam bulletin for their scheduled exam.

### Does Tennessee have a statewide Master Electrician license?

Tennessee does not use one simple statewide Master Electrician title for all electricians. The higher-level electrical contractor path is the CE Electrical Contractor classification, while smaller covered work may fall under the Limited Licensed Electrician license.

### What is the Tennessee Limited Licensed Electrician license?

The LLE license applies to covered electrical work less than $25,000 per project in municipalities that use the Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections. The LLE license does not authorize a person to act as a contractor.

### Who handles Tennessee electrical contractor licensing?

Tennessee contractor licensing is handled through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors under the Tennessee Department of Commerce and Insurance.

### Does Tennessee use one statewide master electrician license title?

Tennessee’s state system is commonly organized around electrical contractor licensing and the Limited Licensed Electrician credential rather than one single statewide master electrician license title.

### What topics are covered on the Tennessee CE – Electrical Contractor exam?

The content outline includes general knowledge and electrical installation requirements, building code requirements, services, feeders, branch circuits, overcurrent protection, grounding and bonding, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, hazardous locations, special occupancies, special equipment, low voltage, alarms, signaling systems, communications, lighting, signs, general-use equipment, OSHA and NFPA 70E safety, motors, transformers, and generators.

### Does Tennessee have state electrical contractor licensing?

Yes. Tennessee electrical contractor licensing is handled at the state level through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors when the work falls under contractor licensing requirements.

### Does Tennessee have a Limited Licensed Electrician credential?

Yes. Tennessee has a Limited Licensed Electrician path for certain electrical work. Candidates should review the current state requirements and limits before applying or testing.

### Should I confirm Tennessee licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required license or credential, application process, code edition, reference rules, exam format, and post-exam licensing steps before scheduling or taking an exam.

## More questions

### Does Tennessee require a Business and Law exam?

Yes. Tennessee contractor applicants are required to take the Tennessee Contractor Business and Law exam. The exam contains 50 questions and allows 2 hours.

### Who provides Tennessee electrical exams?

PSI provides examination services for Tennessee electrical candidates under the applicable state examination program.

### Is a Tennessee Business and Law exam required?

Yes. Tennessee contractor applicants are required to take the Tennessee Business and Law exam, and a trade exam is required for certain classifications, including Electrical.

### Should I study business and law topics?

Candidates pursuing Tennessee contractor licensing should review business and law topics when required by the licensing authority or testing provider. Electrical contractor preparation often includes both trade knowledge and contractor business responsibilities.

### Do Tennessee local jurisdictions have electrical requirements?

Some local jurisdictions may have electrical registration, inspection, permit, or credential requirements. Candidates should confirm local requirements where they plan to perform or contract electrical work.
