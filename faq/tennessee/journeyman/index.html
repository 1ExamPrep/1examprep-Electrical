The exam

Is the Tennessee LLE electrical exam open book?

Yes. The Tennessee Limited Licensed Electrician exam administered by PSI is open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing approved references and must follow PSI rules for highlighting, underlining, indexing, tabs, and prohibited notes.

How many questions are on the Tennessee LLE exam?

The Tennessee LLE exam contains 40 questions. Candidates must answer 29 questions correctly, or 72.5%, to pass. The time allowed is 130 minutes.

How much time is allowed for the Tennessee LLE exam?

The Tennessee LLE exam allows 130 minutes.

What score is required to pass the Tennessee LLE exam?

The Tennessee LLE exam requires 72.5%, which equals 29 correct items.

Is the Tennessee Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Tennessee journeyman electrician exam preparation should include open-book code navigation and reference-use practice. Candidates should follow the current testing instructions for the exact references, tabs, notes, calculators, and code edition allowed during testing.

Who administers the Tennessee LLE exam?

The Tennessee Limited Licensed Electrician examination is administered by PSI for the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors, Limited Licensed Electrician Division.

Is the Tennessee electrical exam open book?

Yes. Tennessee PSI electrical exams identified as open book allow approved reference materials under PSI rules. Candidates must follow the current bulletin for approved books, tabs, calculator use, markings, and prohibited materials.

Can I write notes in my reference books for a Tennessee PSI electrical exam?

No. PSI rules allow approved references to be highlighted, underlined, or indexed before the exam, but references may not be written in. Candidates may not bring additional loose or attached papers with approved references.

Are temporary tabs allowed on Tennessee PSI electrical exams?

No. PSI rules allow permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it style tabs, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins.

Licensing and experience

What is the Tennessee Limited Licensed Electrician license?

The Limited Licensed Electrician license is a Tennessee state license issued to individuals who pass the LLE trade exam. It applies to work less than $25,000 per project in municipalities that use the Division of Fire Prevention for permits or inspections.

Is the Tennessee LLE license the same as a contractor license?

No. Tennessee law states that a person may not act as a contractor with the LLE license. Contractor licensing is a separate process through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors.

Does the Tennessee LLE license work in every city or county?

No. Tennessee states that electricians should check local government requirements first because the LLE license is not accepted by every local agency for permits and inspections.

Does Tennessee issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Tennessee does not issue one single statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Individual journeyman credentials are generally handled by local cities, counties, or municipalities.

Who regulates Tennessee electrical contractor licensing?

Electrical contractor licensing is regulated through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors when contractor licensing requirements apply. Contractor licensing is separate from local journeyman credentialing.

Is a journeyman credential the same as a Tennessee electrical contractor license?

No. A journeyman credential generally supports hands-on electrical work under local rules. Tennessee electrical contractor licensing is handled separately through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors.

What is the Limited Licensed Electrician license?

The Limited Licensed Electrician license is a Tennessee electrical credential for certain electrical work within the limits of that license. It is separate from a local journeyman credential and separate from a full electrical contractor license.

Does Tennessee have local journeyman electrician licensing?

Yes. Local governments in Tennessee may have their own electrician licensing requirements, including journeyman and master electrician credentials for permits and inspections.

Is the LLE license the same as an electrical contractor license?

No. Tennessee state materials state that a person may not act as a contractor with the LLE license. Electrical contractor licensing is a separate path through the Tennessee Board for Licensing Contractors.

Does Tennessee have one statewide Journeyman Electrician license?

Tennessee electrical requirements may include state contractor licensing and local journeyman-level requirements. Candidates should confirm the requirements connected to the authority, employer, jurisdiction, or exam path they are pursuing.

Is journeyman-level recognition the same as an electrical contractor license in Tennessee?

No. Journeyman-level recognition and electrical contractor licensing are different credential concepts. Contractor licensing is connected to offering, bidding, contracting, advertising, or performing electrical work as a business.

Should I confirm Tennessee licensing requirements before testing?

Yes. Candidates should confirm the required credential, registration path, eligibility requirements, application process, code edition, reference rules, and post-exam steps before scheduling or taking an exam.
