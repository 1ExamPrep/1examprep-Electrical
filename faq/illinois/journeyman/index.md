## The exam

### Is the Illinois Journeyman Electrician exam open book?

Many Illinois local electrical exams use open-book reference rules when listed by the local licensing authority or testing provider. Candidates should follow the current exam bulletin for the specific city, county, or municipality where they are testing.

### Is the Chicago Supervising Electrician exam open book?

Yes. The Chicago Supervising Electrician examination is open book. The allowed reference is the Chicago Electrical Code, and tabs may be used to mark sections. Notes, highlighting, or other changes to the published text are not permitted for that exam.

### Are all Illinois electrical exams open book?

Illinois electrical exam rules vary by local jurisdiction and testing provider. Candidates should follow the exam bulletin or local licensing instructions for the specific city, county, or municipality where they are applying.

### Who administers Illinois journeyman electrician exams?

Illinois journeyman electrician exams are handled locally. The administering authority may be a city, county, municipal licensing office, local board, or third-party testing provider selected by that jurisdiction.

### How many questions are on the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam includes 80 multiple-choice questions.

### How much time is allowed for the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam?

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam allows 4 hours of testing time.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Illinois have a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Illinois does not issue one statewide individual journeyman electrician license. Licensing and examination requirements are commonly handled by local cities, villages, counties, municipalities, or building departments.

### Does Illinois issue a statewide journeyman electrician license?

No. Illinois does not issue one single statewide journeyman electrician license through a centralized state electrical licensing board. Electrical licensing is commonly handled by local cities, counties, and municipalities.

### Who handles electrician licensing in Illinois?

Electrician licensing in Illinois is usually handled at the local level. Candidates should work with the local building department, electrical commission, licensing office, or municipal authority for the area where they plan to work.

### What is the Chicago Supervising Electrician license?

The Chicago Supervising Electrician license is a local City of Chicago credential for individuals who have demonstrated knowledge of electrical installation, alteration, repair, and maintenance under the Chicago Electrical Code. It is separate from a statewide Illinois journeyman license.

### Can one Illinois electrical license cover every municipality?

Illinois electrical licensing is local, so electricians may need to confirm licensing or registration requirements in each city, county, or municipality where they plan to work.
