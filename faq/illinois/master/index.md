## The exam

### Is the Illinois Master Electrician exam open book?

Many Illinois municipal electrical exams are open book and use the National Electrical Code as a reference. Exam-room rules vary by jurisdiction, so candidates should follow the instructions for the exact local exam they are taking.

### Is the Illinois electrical exam open book?

Exam rules depend on the local jurisdiction. The Chicago Supervising Electrician examination is open book and allows only the specified Chicago Electrical Code reference. Candidates should follow the rules issued by their local licensing authority.

## Licensing and experience

### Does Illinois have one statewide Master Electrician license?

Illinois does not issue one single statewide master electrician license for individual electricians in the same way many other states do. Electrical licensing and testing are commonly handled by local jurisdictions.

### What reference is used for the Chicago Supervising Electrician exam?

Chicago candidates are instructed to bring a clean copy of the 2018 Chicago Electrical Code published by NFPA. Tabs marking the start of code sections are permitted, and no other books or materials are allowed during the exam.

### Is there one statewide Illinois master electrician license?

Illinois electrical licensing is generally handled by local jurisdictions rather than one single statewide master electrician license. Candidates should check the requirements for the city, county, or municipality where they plan to apply.

### Should I check my local Illinois licensing requirements before studying?

Yes. Because Illinois electrical licensing varies by jurisdiction, candidates should confirm the exact license type, exam name, NEC edition, application requirements, and testing rules required by the local authority where they plan to work.

### Do I need to check my local Illinois licensing authority?

Yes. Because Illinois electrical licensing is local, candidates should confirm the exact license title, application steps, exam provider, reference rules, code edition, fees, insurance requirements, and renewal rules for the jurisdiction where they plan to work.

## Code book and materials

### Why study with the 2026 NEC?

The 2026 NEC supports updated code-cycle study, long-term electrical knowledge, and code-navigation practice. Students should also study any local code edition or amendments required for their specific municipal exam.

### Can I use the NEC paperback for studying?

Yes. The National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback is included so candidates can study directly from the code book, review articles and tables, and improve NEC navigation skills.

### Can I use tabs in my reference book?

Tab rules depend on the local exam. For the Chicago Supervising Electrician examination, tabs are allowed to mark sections of the code, but notes, highlighting, or changes to the published text are prohibited.

### Why is the NEC paperback with tabs useful?

The 2026 NEC paperback with tabs helps candidates practice code navigation. Tabs can make it easier to locate major sections, articles, and tables during study and reference practice.

## More questions

### What topics should I study for an Illinois master electrician-level exam?

Candidates should review NEC navigation, electrical theory, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, conductors, raceways, boxes, overcurrent protection, motors, transformers, special occupancies, special equipment, safety, and calculations.

### How should I use the 12 practice exams?

Use the practice exams throughout your study plan. Review missed questions carefully, identify weak topics, and return to the NEC-based material until the reasoning is clear.

### When should I use the 2 full final exams?

Use the full final exams after completing several practice exams and reviewing your weaker areas. They work best as readiness checks near the end of your preparation.

### Are the flash cards useful for Illinois electrical exam preparation?

Yes. Flash cards are useful for quick review of formulas, definitions, electrical theory, safety concepts, NEC topics, and trade knowledge. They work well alongside longer study sessions and timed practice.
