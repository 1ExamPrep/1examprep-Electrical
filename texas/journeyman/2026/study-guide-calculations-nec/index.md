## Licensing Steps

Texas journeyman electrician candidates begin by registering and working as apprentice electricians under proper supervision. Apprentices should maintain accurate employment and experience records while gaining supervised electrical experience. Keeping organized documentation helps support the journeyman application when the candidate is ready to apply.

The standard Texas Journeyman Electrician path requires at least **8,000 hours** of on-the-job training under the supervision of a master electrician licensed in Texas. This experience is central to eligibility for the journeyman examination. Candidates should make sure their experience is properly documented and meets the TDLR requirements for the license classification.

After meeting the experience requirement, the candidate submits the Journeyman Electrician license application to TDLR. The application should include the required information and experience verification. Candidates should make sure employment dates, supervision information, and work history are accurate before submitting the application.

Once TDLR approves the application, the candidate receives examination eligibility and follows the testing provider’s scheduling process. Candidates should review the current exam bulletin before testing so they understand the exam format, approved references, identification requirements, calculator rules, testing procedures, score reporting, and retake rules.

After passing the examination, the candidate completes the remaining licensing requirements through TDLR. Passing the exam is an important step, but the licensing process is not complete until all state requirements are satisfied and the Texas Journeyman Electrician license is issued.

## State Requirements

Texas requires electricians to be licensed through the Texas Department of Licensing and Regulation. The Journeyman Electrician license is a statewide individual electrical license that authorizes journeyman-level electrical work within the scope of the license. Texas uses a statewide licensing structure rather than leaving journeyman electrician licensing entirely to local jurisdictions.

To qualify for the Texas Journeyman Electrician license, an applicant must have at least **8,000 hours** of on-the-job training under the supervision of a Texas licensed master electrician. This experience requirement supports the candidate’s eligibility to take the journeyman electrician examination.

The Texas Journeyman Electrician license is different from a Residential Wireman license, Maintenance Electrician license, Master Electrician license, or Electrical Contractor license. Each classification has its own scope, eligibility requirements, and licensing purpose. A journeyman electrician license is an individual trade license, while an electrical contractor license is a business-related credential connected to contracting activity.

Texas also requires electrical apprentices to be registered while gaining qualifying experience. Apprentices should work under the proper supervision and keep clear records of their training, employment, and work experience. Accurate documentation helps avoid delays when applying for the journeyman license.

License holders should also follow TDLR requirements for renewal, continuing education, and license maintenance. Continuing education helps electricians stay current with National Electrical Code updates, Texas law and rule changes, safe installation practices, and licensing responsibilities.
