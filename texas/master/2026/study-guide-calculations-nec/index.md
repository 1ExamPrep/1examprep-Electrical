## Licensing Steps

Texas Master Electrician licensing is handled through TDLR. A typical path includes holding a qualifying journeyman electrician license, documenting the required electrical work experience, completing the TDLR master electrician application, receiving eligibility approval, scheduling the exam through PSI, preparing with code-based study materials, passing the required examination, and completing final licensing steps with TDLR.

Texas Master Electrician applicants must meet the state’s experience requirements before approval. The master license path requires substantial on-the-job training under the supervision of a master electrician and a qualifying period as a licensed journeyman electrician. Candidates should organize their employment history, supervision information, license history, and work-hour documentation before applying.

The Texas master electrician license path is not the same as the journeyman electrician path. A journeyman license confirms a lower-level individual electrical credential, while a master electrician license is an advanced credential for electricians who have progressed through additional experience and examination requirements. Candidates should make sure their application matches the license level they are pursuing.

After TDLR approves examination eligibility, candidates follow PSI instructions to schedule the exam. Before test day, candidates should review all examination rules, approved references, calculator requirements, identification requirements, arrival instructions, prohibited items, score reporting, and retesting procedures.
