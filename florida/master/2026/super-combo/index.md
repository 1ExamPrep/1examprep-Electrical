## Exam Details

Florida electrical contractor certification examinations are administered on behalf of the Electrical Contractors’ Licensing Board. The examinations are given in two parts: a Technical/Safety section and a Business section. Candidates approved to test schedule their computer-based examinations through Pearson VUE.

The Electrical Contractors Certification examination includes a Business section with 50 scored questions and a time allowance of 2.5 hours. The Technical/Safety section includes 100 scored questions and a time allowance of 5 hours. Florida requires a passing score of at least 75% on each of the two parts in order to pass the examination for certification.

The Florida electrical certification exam structure is broad. The competency areas include technical knowledge, general business knowledge, and safety knowledge. Florida administrative rules identify the approximate competency distribution as technical knowledge, general business knowledge, and safety knowledge. Candidates preparing for the trade side of the exam should be ready for electrical installation knowledge, code application, safety practices, equipment requirements, and calculation-based questions.

Candidates should make sure they are applying for and scheduling the correct examination classification for their licensing goal.

## Open Book Test

Florida electrical contractor certification examinations are open book. Candidates are responsible for bringing the applicable approved code books, reference materials, and calculators allowed by the Electrical Contractors’ Licensing Board and the examination rules.

Only approved reference materials are allowed at the examination site. The official reference list explains that examination questions are based on the editions listed by the state, along with general trade knowledge and knowledge of trade practices. Earlier or later editions may be brought to the examination at the candidate’s own risk, but only one copy of each reference is allowed.

Reference materials must follow exam-room rules. Handwritten and typewritten notes are not allowed. Existing handwritten notes must be completely blackened out or whitened out so they cannot be read. Moveable tabs, such as Post-it flags, are not allowed. Permanently attached tabs may be used in reference books. Candidates are not permitted to make any marks in their references during the examination.

No scratch paper, class notes, formulas, sample questions, bound or loose-leaf study materials, notes, or similar materials are allowed in the examination room. Only original edition reference materials are allowed unless a specific reference is listed as a permitted PDF file. When a PDF reference is permitted, it must be printed and bound as required by the exam rules.

The open-book format makes code navigation extremely important. Open book does not remove the need to study. Candidates still need to know where to look, how to read the code language, which table applies, and how to avoid wasting time. The National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback with Tabs included in this Super Combo helps candidates practice moving through the code book while studying and build stronger reference habits before test day.
