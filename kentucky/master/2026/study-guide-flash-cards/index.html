The Kentucky Master Electrician exam is part of the licensing process for electricians seeking a master electrician license in Kentucky. Kentucky accepts Master Electrician exam results through approved testing options identified by the state. The state’s new application checklist identifies PROV, NASCLA, and Pearson VUE as accepted testing options for the Master Electrician exam.

Applicants pursuing the Kentucky Master Electrician license must submit a copy of passing test results as part of the licensing application process. Passing the exam alone does not complete the licensing process. Licensing Steps

To pursue a Kentucky Master Electrician license, candidates must follow the state licensing process. The general process includes passing an accepted Master Electrician exam, gathering documentation, completing the required state application, and submitting the required materials to the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

  1. Prepare for the Master Electrician exam. Use a structured study plan, review the 2026 NEC, work through practice exams, and reinforce trade knowledge with flash cards.
  2. Pass an accepted Master Electrician exam. Kentucky identifies PROV, NASCLA, and Pearson VUE as accepted testing options for the Master Electrician exam.
  3. Obtain a copy of passing test results. A copy of passing results must be included with the license application materials.
  4. Document qualifying electrical experience. Kentucky requires proof of electrical trade experience for the Master Electrician license.
  5. Complete the application. The state requires a completed application for the license being requested.
  6. Submit supporting documents. Supporting materials may include experience documentation, test results, fee payment, and a passport-sized color photo.
  7. Follow state instructions for review and approval. Licensing approval is handled by the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction.

This study guide and flash card combo supports the preparation stage of the process. It helps candidates work toward the exam with organized practice, but licensing decisions, application review, and approval are handled by the state.

State Requirements

Kentucky requires applicants for a Master Electrician license to pass the Master Electrician exam through an accepted testing option and submit a copy of passing test results. The state also requires proof of experience totaling 16,000 hours in the electrical trade since the applicant’s sixteenth birthday.

Experience documentation may be provided through a notarized letter from an employer or former employer who can verify the work experience. The letter must include examples of work performed, hours worked, and must be signed by the Electrical Contractor or Master Electrician. Kentucky also identifies other documentation options, including tax documentation showing electrician work, proof of completion of an approved apprenticeship training program, proof of an associate’s degree or diploma in electrical technology from an accepted college, or proof of qualifying teaching experience in electrical technology or a related field.

Kentucky’s checklist also states that completion of an approved apprenticeship training program with a minimum of 288 classroom hours may substitute for 4,000 hours of verifiable electrical trade experience. An associate’s degree or diploma in electrical technology from the Kentucky Community and Technical College System or an accredited college or university may substitute for 6,000 hours of verifiable electrical trade experience. Two years of teaching electrical technology or a related field at an accredited college or university may substitute for 4,000 hours of verifiable electrical trade experience.

Applicants must also submit a completed application, the required application fee, and a passport-sized color photo. Fees are set by the state and should be reviewed on the current application materials before submission.

For electricians who plan to become electrical contractors in Kentucky, the state notes that an electrical contractor license requires a Kentucky Master Electrician license or employment of a Kentucky licensed Master Electrician. Contractor licensing is a separate step from master electrician exam preparation and may require additional documentation and testing.
