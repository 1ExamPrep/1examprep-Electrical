Licensing Steps

The Kentucky journeyman electrician licensing path begins with meeting the state’s qualification requirements for an electrician license. Applicants should use the current Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction licensing forms and instructions for the license classification they are pursuing.

A common Kentucky electrician licensing path includes documenting qualifying electrical work experience or approved training and education. Kentucky licensing materials require applicants to provide documentation and verification of work experience in accordance with Kentucky law and administrative regulations. When a training program or education program is being applied instead of work experience, documentation and verification of completion are required.

After meeting the qualification requirements, the candidate applies or registers according to the Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division and approved examination provider instructions. The candidate then schedules the required journeyman electrician examination, studies the approved references, follows the exam provider’s testing rules, and works toward the required passing score.

After passing the required examination, the candidate completes the remaining license application steps through the Kentucky Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction. Passing the exam is an important step, but the applicant must still satisfy all licensing requirements, submit required documentation, and complete the state licensing process.

A practical preparation path includes gaining qualifying electrical experience, completing any applicable training documentation, reviewing the current exam bulletin, studying the NEC, practicing electrical calculations, building speed with reference navigation, using flash cards for recall, passing the examination, and completing the licensure process through the Kentucky electrical licensing system.
