The Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is administered through Prov for licensing through the Commonwealth of Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division. The exam is computer-based, open book, and timed. Candidates may schedule through Prov and may test at approved testing centers.

## Open Book Test

The Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is an open book test. Candidates may use authorized references during the exam, subject to the rules issued by Prov and the Commonwealth of Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division.

Open book testing still requires serious preparation. The National Electrical Code is a detailed technical reference, and candidates who are not familiar with its layout can lose valuable time searching through unrelated sections. A prepared candidate should know how to identify the topic in the question, locate the correct NEC article or table, understand the wording, and apply the rule accurately.

The Kentucky candidate information allows candidates to prepare reference materials by highlighting text and attaching permanent tabs. Reference materials are checked by the test administrator before entry into the testing room. Moveable tabs, repositionable tabs, Post-it notes, and unauthorized loose materials are not permitted. Candidates may not mark in their books during the exam.

## Licensing Steps

Kentucky journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Commonwealth of Kentucky Electrical Licensing Division. Candidates must complete the required licensing process, pass the journeyman electrical exam, and submit the required documentation for the electrician license.

A typical Kentucky electrician license path includes gaining qualifying electrical trade experience, preparing documentation, taking and passing the Journeyman Electrical Exam through an approved testing provider, submitting a completed electrician license application, sending a copy of passing exam results, providing proof of qualifying electrical trade experience, paying the required application fee, and submitting the required identification and photo materials.

Kentucky’s electrician license application checklist identifies proof of experience totaling 8,000 hours in the electrical trade since the applicant’s sixteenth birthday. Experience documentation may include a notarized letter from an employer or former employer verifying the work performed and hours worked, signed by an electrical contractor or master electrician. Other documentation options may be accepted under state instructions.

Candidates should keep accurate records of work history, employer information, hours worked, apprenticeship or training documentation, dates of employment, job duties, and any previous electrical credentials. Good documentation can make the licensing process easier after the exam is completed.

## State Requirements

Kentucky licenses electricians and electrical contractors through the Department of Housing, Buildings and Construction. The Electrical Division is responsible for electrical licensing, electrical inspections, electrical inspector certification, and educational programs related to electrical codes and standards.

For the electrician license path associated with the journeyman exam, Kentucky requires a completed application, a copy of passing test results, proof of qualifying electrical trade experience, an application fee, a passport-sized color photo, and required identification documentation. The experience requirement listed for an electrician license is 8,000 hours in the electrical trade since the applicant’s sixteenth birthday.

Kentucky’s electrical licensing structure also includes master electrician and electrical contractor licenses. A contractor license allows a business to conduct electrical contracting work, but the contractor license must be associated with a master license holder. Candidates should understand the difference between an individual electrician license, a master electrician license, and an electrical contractor license.

Passing the Kentucky Journeyman Electrician exam is an important licensing step, but it is not the only requirement. Candidates must also complete the state application process, submit documentation, and satisfy the Commonwealth’s licensing requirements. The licensing authority reviews the application materials and determines whether the applicant qualifies for licensure.
