## Open Book Test

The Oklahoma Unlimited Electrical Contractor examination is an **open book test**. Candidates are responsible for bringing their own approved references to the examination center. A silent, nonprinting, nonprogrammable calculator may be used in the examination center.

Approved reference materials may be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the examination session. References may not be written in before or during the examination. Candidates may not bring additional papers with approved references. Any additional materials may be removed from the references and confiscated.

References may be tabbed or indexed with permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, such as Post-it notes, are not allowed and must be removed before the exam begins. Downloaded references must be spiral bound, hole-punched and placed in a binder, or stapled in the left-hand corner according to exam rules.

Open-book testing still requires serious preparation. The exam is not simply about bringing a code book into the room. Candidates need to understand how the NEC is organized, how to identify the topic being tested, how to locate the correct Article or table quickly, and how to apply the rule accurately within the time allowed.

## State Requirements

Oklahoma electrical licensing is regulated by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The Board protects life and property through licensing and inspection of related trades, including electrical work. Electrical contractors must meet Board requirements before engaging in electrical contracting in the state.

All Oklahoma Electrical Contractors must be an owner, partner, or officer in the electrical firm. Electrical Contractor is the highest-ranking electrical license category in Oklahoma. The Unlimited Electrical Contractor category is unlimited in the type of electrical contracting work the licensee may perform.

For the Unlimited Electrical Contractor category, Oklahoma requires **12,000 verifiable hours** of on-the-job experience in electrical construction work. The candidate must also show proof of **4,000 hours of experience as a licensed Unlimited Electrical Journeyman**. Of the total experience, **6,000 hours must be in commercial industrial work** obtained while employed by a licensed Electrical Contractor. A maximum of **2,000 hours** may be satisfied by formal electrical education.

For the Residential Electrical Contractor category, Oklahoma requires **8,000 verifiable hours total**. Residential Electrical Contractors are limited to contracting for wiring buildings as defined in the NEC for one-family and two-family dwellings. The Residential path includes required hours as a Residential or Unlimited Journeyman Electrician under the employment and supervision of an Electrical Contractor.

The Limited Electrical Contractor category applies to individuals who are an owner, partner, or officer in an electrical firm and who are prohibited from engaging in the work of an apprentice or journeyman electrician. Qualification may be based on a degree in electrical engineering with qualifying commercial or industrial experience, or verification of extensive experience in the electrical trade performing electrical work, estimating, or project management in commercial or industrial work.
