## Open Book Test

The Oklahoma electrical examinations are **open book tests**. Candidates may bring approved references into the examination center according to PSI and Oklahoma Construction Industries Board rules. The exam reference rules allow approved materials to be highlighted, underlined, and indexed before the exam session, but references may not contain writing before or during the examination.

Oklahoma testing rules allow permanent tabs only. Temporary tabs, including Post-it notes, are not allowed and must be removed before the examination begins. Candidates may not bring additional loose papers with approved references. Downloaded references must be properly bound, hole-punched in a binder, or stapled in the left-hand corner when allowed by the testing rules.

The current Oklahoma electrical exam reference list includes the **NFPA 70 National Electrical Code, 2023 Edition**, as revised and adopted by the Oklahoma Uniform Building Code Commission, **Code of Federal Regulations 29 CFR Part 1926** or PSI’s OSHA selections, and **Ugly’s Electrical References**. NEC Handbooks and spiral-bound copies of the NEC are not allowed in the test center under the current bulletin.

## Licensing Steps

The Oklahoma electrical licensing path begins with identifying the correct credential. A person entering the trade typically registers as an apprentice and gains supervised experience under a licensed journeyman or licensed contractor. After meeting journeyman experience requirements, the applicant applies for the appropriate journeyman examination. After working as a journeyman and meeting additional contractor requirements, the electrician may pursue an electrical contractor license.

For journeyman licensing, Oklahoma recognizes categories such as Unlimited Electrical Journeyman and Residential Electrical Journeyman. An Unlimited Electrical Journeyman applicant must document **8,000 verifiable hours** of on-the-job electrical construction experience as a registered apprentice under proper supervision, with **4,000 hours** in commercial or industrial work. Formal electrical education may satisfy up to **2,000 hours** when it meets the Board’s requirements.

For contractor licensing, Oklahoma requires Electrical Contractors to be an owner, partner, or officer in the electrical firm. Unlimited Electrical Contractors must document **12,000 verifiable hours** of electrical construction experience, including proof of **4,000 hours** as a licensed Unlimited Electrical Journeyman. The experience must also include **6,000 hours** in commercial or industrial work obtained while employed by a licensed Electrical Contractor. Up to **2,000 hours** may be satisfied by formal electrical education.

Residential Electrical Contractors must document **8,000 verifiable hours** total, including **4,000 hours** as a Residential or Unlimited Journeyman Electrician under the employment and supervision of an Electrical Contractor. The remaining experience may be obtained as a registered apprentice, with limited educational credit allowed under Board rules.

After meeting the experience requirement, the applicant submits the required application to the Construction Industries Board. Once approved, the candidate receives testing information and schedules the exam through PSI. After passing the required exam, the applicant completes the licensing process with the Board, including contractor activation requirements when applicable. Active electrical contractors must maintain bond and insurance requirements and provide workers’ compensation coverage documentation or an approved exemption when required.
