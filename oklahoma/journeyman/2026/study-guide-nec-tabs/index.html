Licensing Steps

The Oklahoma journeyman electrician licensing process begins with apprentice registration and supervised electrical experience. Candidates working toward a journeyman electrician license must document qualifying electrical experience under the rules of the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Oklahoma’s licensing path is designed to ensure that journeyman electricians have both hands-on experience and the knowledge needed to perform electrical work safely.

A common path toward the journeyman level includes registering as an electrical apprentice, working under proper supervision, accumulating the required verifiable electrical experience, applying to take the journeyman examination, receiving examination approval, scheduling the exam through the testing vendor, passing the required examination, and completing the remaining licensing steps required by the Board.

Oklahoma rules identify 8,000 verifiable hours of on-the-job electrical construction trade experience for the unlimited electrical journeyman path. Experience must be properly documented, and Oklahoma experience must be connected to apprentice registration requirements. Formal electrical education may be counted toward a portion of the experience requirement when allowed by Board rules.

After meeting the experience requirements, the candidate submits the application for examination approval. Once approved, the candidate receives instructions for scheduling the examination. Passing the examination is an important step, but candidates must still complete any remaining licensing requirements, fees, and documentation required by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board before the license is issued.

Study preparation should begin before the exam is scheduled. State Requirements

Oklahoma electrical licensing is administered by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The Board regulates licensing for electrical work and provides the application and exam process for electrical journeyman candidates. Candidates should follow the Board’s current electrical rules, forms, licensing instructions, and testing procedures.

For the unlimited electrical journeyman path, Oklahoma identifies 8,000 verifiable hours of on-the-job experience in the electrical construction trade. This experience is tied to registered apprentice work under proper supervision. Oklahoma also recognizes that a limited amount of formal electrical education may be credited toward the experience requirement when it meets Board rules.

A licensed journeyman electrician may perform electrical work within the category of licensure while employed by a licensed electrical contractor. A journeyman license does not authorize the individual to contract independently to furnish labor or labor and materials in the journeyman’s own name. Candidates who plan to operate as an electrical contractor must follow the separate contractor licensing requirements.

Oklahoma also has continuing education rules for electrical licensees and electrical apprentices. Candidates and licensees should review the current Board requirements for continuing education, renewal, license status, and any changes that apply to their license type.
