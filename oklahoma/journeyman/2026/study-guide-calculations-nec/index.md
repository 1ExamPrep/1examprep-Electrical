## Licensing Steps

Oklahoma journeyman electrician candidates begin by gaining qualifying electrical experience and maintaining proper registration status as required by the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Apprentice electricians should register with the Board and work under the proper supervision while gaining experience toward journeyman eligibility.

The standard Oklahoma journeyman path is built around documented electrical experience under proper supervision. A candidate applying for the Unlimited Journeyman Electrician license must provide the required experience documentation and satisfy the Board’s eligibility requirements before being approved for the licensing examination.

After meeting the experience requirement, the candidate submits the proper application materials to the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The application should include the required forms, experience documentation, and any other supporting materials required for the journeyman classification. Candidates should make sure employment records, supervision details, and dates are accurate before submitting the application.

Once the application is approved, the candidate follows the exam scheduling process through PSI. Candidates should review the current candidate information bulletin before testing so they understand the exam format, approved references, identification requirements, calculator rules, testing procedures, score reporting, and retake rules.

After passing the examination, the candidate completes the remaining licensing requirements through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. Passing the exam is an important step, but the licensing process is not complete until all Board requirements are satisfied and the journeyman electrician license is issued.

## State Requirements

Oklahoma requires electricians to be licensed through the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board. The Board regulates electrical licensing classifications, including electrical apprentice, electrical journeyman, electrical contractor, and electrical specialty classifications. This statewide structure applies to electrical licensing throughout Oklahoma.

Oklahoma offers an Unlimited Journeyman Electrician license for individuals who qualify through the required experience and examination process. The journeyman license is an individual trade credential that supports electrical work within the state licensing framework. It is separate from an electrical contractor license, which carries different responsibilities and requirements.

The standard Oklahoma journeyman experience path commonly includes **8,000 hours** of electrical experience, with a portion of the experience in commercial or industrial work. Candidates should follow the Oklahoma Construction Industries Board’s current requirements for the exact experience categories, acceptable documentation, and supervision rules that apply to their application.

Oklahoma also uses apprentice registration as part of the electrical licensing path. Apprentices should maintain registration and keep accurate records of supervised electrical work. Proper documentation can make the journeyman application process smoother when the candidate is ready to apply for examination approval.

An Oklahoma journeyman electrician license is different from an Oklahoma electrical contractor license. A contractor license involves additional responsibility for contracting activity and a separate licensing path. Candidates should apply for the credential that matches their work role, experience, and career goal.
