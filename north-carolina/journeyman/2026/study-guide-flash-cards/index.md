## Open Book Test

North Carolina electrical contractor examinations are open-book examinations using approved references. Many local journeyman-level electrical exams are also prepared for as open-book, NEC-based exams, but candidates must follow the exact instructions issued by the local North Carolina authority, apprenticeship program, employer, or testing provider administering their exam.

Open book does not mean easy. Electrical candidates still need to understand the material, know how questions are written, find code information quickly, apply rules correctly, and manage time during the exam. A student who depends only on searching through the code book for every answer may struggle to complete the test within the time allowed.

Open-book electrical preparation rewards reference familiarity. Students should practice using the table of contents, index, article structure, definitions, tables, exceptions, notes, and common code sections before test day. Students should also practice calculations repeatedly so formulas, load relationships, conductor sizing concepts, grounding rules, bonding rules, motor requirements, transformer concepts, box fill, raceway fill, voltage drop concepts, and overcurrent protection rules become easier to recognize.

Reference rules may vary by exam. Some testing programs allow approved references with limited highlighting or permanent tabs, while others restrict notes, removable tabs, loose pages, copied materials, study guides, or practice exams. Students should use this study guide, the 12 practice exams, the 2 full final exams, and the flash cards before test day. During any official exam, candidates should bring only the references and materials allowed by the authority administering that exam.

A strong open-book strategy includes learning where major NEC topics are located before the exam. Students should practice identifying the correct article, checking definitions first, reading table notes carefully, reviewing exceptions, and confirming whether the question asks for a minimum, maximum, permitted condition, prohibited condition, or calculation result. Flash cards help students recall key concepts faster, while practice exams help build the habit of reading questions carefully and choosing the correct code path.

## Licensing Steps

Contractor licensing should not be confused with local journeyman-level recognition or apprenticeship advancement.
