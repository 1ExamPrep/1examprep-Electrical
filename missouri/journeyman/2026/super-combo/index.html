Exam Details

Local Missouri journeyman electrician exams may be administered directly by the local authority or through an outside testing provider. Depending on the jurisdiction, the exam may include National Electrical Code questions, electrical theory, installation requirements, wiring methods, services, feeders, branch circuits, grounding and bonding, conductors, raceways, boxes, motors, overcurrent protection, safety, and electrical calculations.

Licensing Steps

The Missouri journeyman electrician path begins with identifying the correct licensing authority. For many journeyman-level credentials, the controlling authority may be a city, county, or municipality. For statewide electrical contractor licensure, the licensing authority is the Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors.

A practical journeyman-level path begins with reviewing local application instructions. Many Missouri jurisdictions expect applicants to document electrical work experience, apprenticeship training, classroom education, or a combination of training and field experience before approving the applicant for examination. Some jurisdictions may require proof of work under a licensed journeyman electrician, master electrician, or electrical contractor.

After reviewing the local rules, the candidate submits the required application to the licensing authority or testing provider. Once approved, the candidate schedules the required exam, studies the current reference list, brings the permitted materials when applicable, and follows the test-day procedures established by the testing provider or licensing office.

After passing the exam, the candidate completes the remaining local licensing, certificate, or registration steps. These steps may include submitting the score report, paying local fees, completing registration, receiving a license card or certificate, or renewing according to the local schedule. Electricians who work in more than one Missouri city or county may need to confirm licensing, registration, permit, or reciprocity rules in each jurisdiction.

For statewide electrical contractor licensing, applicants follow the Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors application process. The office may require verification of local licensure, proof of passing an approved examination, business information, insurance documentation, and other materials required for the statewide contractor license. This statewide contractor process is separate from local journeyman licensing but may recognize approved examination history under the office’s rules.
