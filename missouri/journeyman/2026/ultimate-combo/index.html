Exam Details

Missouri does not use one single statewide journeyman electrician exam for every individual electrician. Journeyman electrician licensing, registration, and testing requirements are commonly set by local jurisdictions. A candidate may need to follow the rules of a city, county, municipal licensing board, local building department, contractor licensing office, or other local authority.

A commonly used journeyman-level electrical exam format is the ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam. That exam includes 80 multiple-choice questions, allows 4 hours of testing time, and is open book. Its subject areas include general knowledge, services and service equipment, feeders, branch circuits and conductors, wiring methods and materials, equipment and devices, control devices, motors and generators, and special occupancies, equipment, and conditions.

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam places strong emphasis on wiring methods and materials, branch circuits and conductors, equipment and devices, services and service equipment, and special occupancies, equipment, and conditions. These are practical journeyman-level areas that require candidates to understand both NEC rules and field application.

Some Missouri cities and local boards accept specific ICC exams for journeyman or master electrician licensing, while other jurisdictions may use different approved examinations, local applications, separate passing scores, local code amendments, different testing providers, or specific experience documentation. Candidates preparing for a Missouri journeyman electrician credential should match their study materials to the exam required by the jurisdiction where they are applying.

Open Book Test

The ICC National Standard Journeyman Electrician exam is an open book test. Candidates taking that exam may use approved reference materials during the examination, subject to the exam provider’s rules. Open book testing still requires strong preparation. Licensing Steps

Missouri journeyman electrician licensing steps depend on the local authority that issues or recognizes the credential. A candidate may need to apply through a city licensing office, county department, municipal board, local building department, contractor licensing office, or electrical licensing authority. Since Missouri does not issue one uniform statewide individual journeyman electrician license, identifying the correct local jurisdiction is the first step.

A typical Missouri journeyman electrician path may include completing supervised electrical work experience, finishing apprenticeship or classroom training when required, gathering employer verification, submitting a local application, paying required local fees, scheduling the approved electrical examination, passing the required exam, and completing any remaining local licensing or registration steps.

Local requirements may include a minimum number of years or hours of electrical experience, proof of work under a licensed electrician or electrical contractor, apprenticeship completion, classroom instruction, identification documents, continuing education, or a local sponsor. Some jurisdictions may recognize an exam passed through a national provider, while others may require a specific local exam or local review process.

Missouri statewide electrical contractor licensing is separate from local journeyman-level credentialing. The Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors regulates statewide electrical contractor licenses. A journeyman credential generally supports trade-level work and employment, while contracting authority may require the proper contractor license, business structure, insurance, bonding, and regulatory approval.
