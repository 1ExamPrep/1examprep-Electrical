Open Book Test

The NASCLA Electrical Contractor/Master Electrician examination used for Missouri statewide electrical contractor licensing purposes is an open book test. Open-book electrical exams require strong reference-navigation skills. Candidates must be able to locate the correct article, read section language carefully, check definitions, use tables properly, review notes and exceptions, and apply the code rule to the question being asked.

Open book does not mean easy. Many candidates struggle because they are not fast enough with the NEC or because they do not know where to begin looking for an answer. A prepared candidate understands how the code book is organized, how to use article headings, how to work through tables, and how to avoid wasting time in unrelated sections.

The National Electrical Code 2026 Paperback included in this combo gives candidates the code reference needed for study and navigation practice. Local Missouri master electrician exam rules may vary. Some local exams may allow references, while others may use different testing procedures or reference rules. Candidates should follow the official instructions from the authority administering their exam for allowed books, calculators, tabs, markings, identification, testing center procedures, and exam-day materials.

Licensing Steps

Missouri licensing steps depend on whether the candidate is pursuing a statewide electrical contractor license, a local master electrician credential, or another electrical classification. A typical statewide contractor licensing path includes identifying the correct license classification, reviewing Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors requirements, completing the required application, submitting supporting business or qualifying party information, receiving exam instructions when testing is required, scheduling the appropriate exam, preparing with NEC-based study materials, passing the exam, and completing final licensing steps with the state.

For statewide electrical contractor licensing, applicants work through the Missouri Office of Statewide Electrical Contractors. The process may include selecting the proper license type, submitting the required application, meeting experience or examination requirements, receiving approval when needed, scheduling the applicable examination, and completing final licensing requirements. Candidates should follow the current Office instructions for application forms, approved examinations, documentation, and renewal responsibilities.

For a local master electrician credential, candidates should contact the city, county, municipality, or local authority where they plan to work. Local requirements may include work experience documentation, examination approval, local registration, business licensing, permit requirements, insurance, renewal rules, or continuing education. A local credential may not replace statewide electrical contractor licensing when statewide contractor licensing is required for the work being performed.
