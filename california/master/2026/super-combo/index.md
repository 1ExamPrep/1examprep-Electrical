## Exam Details

California’s electrical structure includes two important tracks. The Department of Industrial Relations, Division of Labor Standards Enforcement, Electrician Certification Unit administers electrician certification for individual electricians. The Contractors State License Board administers C-10 Electrical Contractor licensing for businesses and qualifying individuals who meet contractor licensing requirements.

The California General Electrician certification exam is designed for individuals who perform work for an electrical contractor installing, constructing, or maintaining electrical systems covered by the National Electrical Code. Current California electrician certification materials list the General Electrician exam as 100 questions with a 4-hour and 30-minute time limit. The minimum passing score for California electrician certification examinations is 70%.

The General Electrician certification content outline includes Safety, Determination of Electrical System Requirements, Installation, and Maintenance and Repair. These subject areas connect directly to master-level study because they require knowledge of code application, electrical system design logic, installation methods, troubleshooting, testing, maintenance, and safe work practices.

For contractor-level advancement, the California C-10 Electrical Contractor examination is a separate CSLB licensing examination. Current CSLB study material divides the C-10 Electrical examination into five major sections: Planning and Estimating, Rough Wiring, Finish Wiring and Trim, Startup, Troubleshooting, and Maintenance, and Safety. The C-10 exam includes electrical calculations, code requirements, system layout, grounding and bonding, raceway and panel installation, wire and equipment installation, motor and equipment installation, energy production and storage systems, lockout/tagout procedures, personal protective equipment, and jobsite safety.

## Closed Book Test

The California C-10 Electrical Contractor examination is a closed-book examination. Current CSLB study material states that no reference materials may be used during the C-10 examination. A calculator is provided, and the exam is multiple choice with four choices per question. Some questions require mathematical computation, so candidates should practice calculations before test day rather than relying on reference lookup.

California electrician certification exams use a different testing model. Current California electrician certification materials state that references are provided in the testing center and are available during the examination, and candidates may not bring or use their own reference materials. For the General Electrician certification exam, listed testing-center references include the 2023 National Electrical Code, NFPA 70E, Cal/OSHA materials, and other references used for exam development.

## Licensing Steps

The California advanced electrical path begins by identifying the correct credential. Individual electricians often pursue California electrician certification through the Electrician Certification Unit. Businesses and qualifying individuals who want to contract for electrical work generally pursue the C-10 Electrical Contractor license through the Contractors State License Board.

For electrician certification, an applicant applies to the Electrician Certification Unit and must be approved before scheduling the exam. After approval, eligible candidates receive instructions for scheduling the examination through the testing process used by California’s current exam administrator. The candidate then takes the exam, receives the score result, and receives certification after satisfying the state requirements.

For C-10 Electrical Contractor licensing, the applicant follows the CSLB contractor license process. The process commonly includes selecting the correct license classification, identifying the qualifying individual, documenting qualifying experience, submitting the license application, completing required examination steps, meeting bond and business requirements, and satisfying CSLB licensing rules before the contractor license is issued.

California electrician certification and C-10 contractor licensing are separate. A certified electrician credential supports individual electrical work eligibility within California’s certification system. A C-10 contractor license is tied to contracting authority and business licensing. Experienced electricians should choose the path that matches their work goals, employment role, and business plans.
