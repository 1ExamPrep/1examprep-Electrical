## Exam Details

The Colorado State Electrical Board contracts with PSI Services LLC to administer the Colorado electrical licensing examinations. The Colorado Master Electrician examination is a computer-based exam administered through PSI testing centers.

The Colorado Master Electrician examination includes 90 scored items and has a time allowance of 240 minutes for the scored portion. The passing standard is 70%, which equals 63 correct scored items. The exam may also include up to 10 non-scored items with an additional 30 minutes. Non-scored items are used for examination development and do not count toward the final score.

The Master Electrician examination content outline includes the following areas: general electrical knowledge, services, feeders, and branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special conditions, special occupancies, special equipment, electrical devices, motors, transformers, and electrical calculations. Each area requires more than memorization. Candidates need to understand the topic, apply code rules, work through calculations, and select the best answer under timed testing conditions.

Effective August 1, 2026, Colorado electrical licensing examination questions are consistent with the 2026 National Electrical Code. The test center provides the 2026 National Electrical Code book and Formula Page for the exam. Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index on the references provided at the test center, and other materials may not be used during the exam.

Scores are provided immediately after completion of the examination. Passing the examination is an important licensing step, but applicants must still meet the minimum qualifications for licensure under Colorado law and file the required license application with the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations.
