## Exam Details

The Colorado State Electrical Board licenses, registers, and regulates electricians, apprentices, and electrical contractors in Colorado. The Board’s licensing exams are administered through PSI Services LLC. The Colorado electrical exam program includes exams for Residential Wireman, Journeyman Wireman, Master Electrician, and the Colorado Journeyman Wireman exam for apprentices with six or more years of apprentice registration.

The Colorado Master Electrician exam is a computer-based exam administered by PSI. The current PSI candidate bulletin lists the Master Electrician exam with 90 scored items and 240 minutes of testing time. The bulletin also lists up to 10 non-scored items with 30 additional minutes. The listed passing standard is 70%, which equals 63 correct scored items.

The Master Electrician examination content outline includes General Electrical Knowledge, Services, Feeders, and Branch Circuits, Grounding and Bonding, Overcurrent Protection, Conductors and Cables, Raceways and Boxes, Special Conditions, Special Occupancies, Special Equipment, Electrical Devices, Motors, Transformers, and Electrical Calculations. Grounding and bonding is one of the largest listed categories, and calculations are also a significant part of the exam outline.

The PSI bulletin states that examination questions are currently consistent with the 2023 National Electrical Code. Effective August 1, 2026, the bulletin states that all examination questions are consistent with the 2026 National Electrical Code, and the 2026 National Electrical Code book and Formula Page are provided at the test center. This product is based on the 2026 NEC for candidates preparing for the 2026 code cycle.

PSI provides score results immediately after completion of the examination. Candidates who do not pass may reregister under PSI’s scheduling rules. Passing the exam is part of the licensing process, and applicants must still meet the Colorado State Electrical Board’s license qualification requirements.

## Licensing Steps

Colorado Master Electrician candidates must meet the licensing requirements established by the Colorado State Electrical Board. Practical experience must be documented on the Board’s Affidavit of Experience form and completed by the electrical contractor supervising the work performed. Candidates should gather accurate work documentation before beginning the license application process.

Colorado lists several pathways for Master Electrician experience qualification. Candidates may qualify with 10,000 hours of experience earned in no less than 5 years; a bachelor’s degree in electrical engineering from an accredited college or university plus 2,000 hours of construction experience earned in no less than 1 year; graduation from an electrical trade school or community college plus 8,000 hours of experience earned in no less than 4 years; or 2,000 hours of experience earned in no less than 1 year in addition to the Journeyman Wireman requirements. For the path based on additional experience after the Journeyman Wireman requirements, some hours must include planning and layout, and some must include supervision while in possession of a Journeyman Electrician license.

The Colorado licensing page notes that it is not possible to go directly from Apprentice or Residential Wireman to Master when using experience to qualify for the Master license. Candidates should review which pathway applies to their background before submitting an application.

After meeting the qualification requirements and completing the exam process, applicants file the appropriate license application with the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations. PSI scheduling is handled through the Colorado electrical examination program. Candidates should use the same legal name that appears on their identification, follow PSI’s scheduling instructions, and bring the required identification to the examination site.

## State Requirements

Colorado’s State Electrical Board regulates electricians, apprentices, and electrical contractors. The Board’s mission includes protecting public health and safety by enforcing Colorado electrical statutes. Colorado recognizes license and registration categories that include apprentice registration, Residential Wireman, Journeyman Electrician, Master Electrician, and Electrical Contractor registration.

To perform electrical contracting in Colorado, a business must be registered as an Electrical Contractor with the State of Colorado. The contractor must hold or employ a licensed master electrician to serve as the responsible individual for the company. The master electrician and company owner, when they are separate individuals, must sign the required acknowledgment of responsibility. The selected master electrician may serve as the responsible party for one company only.

Colorado also requires electricians and electrical contractors to renew on the state’s renewal cycle. Active Residential Wireman, Journeyman Electrician, and Master Electrician license holders must complete at least 24 hours of continuing education during each 3-year license period. License holders should track renewal dates, complete required continuing education, and keep their contact information current with the Division of Professions and Occupations.
