## Licensing Steps

Colorado journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations and the Colorado State Electrical Board. PSI administers the examination program for the Board.

Colorado journeyman applicants must meet the experience and education requirements established by the State Electrical Board. Current Colorado guidance identifies the journeyman path as requiring 8,000 hours of experience earned in no less than four years, with 4,000 of those hours in commercial or industrial work. Colorado also requires documented classroom training for applicable applicants, including training in safety, the National Electrical Code and its applications, and other training required by the Board.

After preparing for the exam, candidates register and schedule the Colorado Journeyman Wireman examination through PSI. PSI administers the exam by computer and provides results after completion. Passing the exam does not automatically guarantee licensure; applicants must still file the appropriate license application with the Division of Professions and Occupations and demonstrate that they meet Colorado’s minimum qualifications for licensure.

## State Requirements

The Colorado State Electrical Board regulates electrical licensing in Colorado. The Board’s licensing structure includes Residential Wireman, Journeyman Wireman, and Master Electrician licensing categories. The Journeyman Wireman license is the statewide credential commonly pursued by electricians seeking journeyman-level licensing in Colorado.

Colorado requires candidates to demonstrate qualifying electrical experience and meet the Board’s education requirements before licensure. The current journeyman experience pathway includes 8,000 hours of experience over at least four years, including 4,000 hours of commercial or industrial experience. Candidates should keep accurate documentation of experience and training because those records are part of the licensing process.

Colorado also requires continuing education for active electrical licenses. Active Master Electricians, Journeyman Electricians, and Residential Wiremen must complete at least 24 hours of continuing education during each three-year license period. This continuing education requirement does not apply to the first renewal of a license when the licensing examination was successfully completed as a condition of issuance.

Students should follow current Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations instructions for applications, experience documentation, examination registration, renewal, continuing education, reciprocity, and license status.
