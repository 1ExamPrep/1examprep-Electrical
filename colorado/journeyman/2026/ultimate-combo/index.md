## Exam Details

The Colorado State Electrical Board uses PSI Services LLC for the electrical examination program. The Colorado Journeyman Wireman exam is a computer-based licensing examination for candidates pursuing the journeyman level in Colorado. The exam measures entry-level competence across electrical job task areas and is designed to evaluate whether a candidate can apply electrical knowledge safely and correctly.

Effective August 1, 2026, Colorado electrical exam questions are consistent with the 2026 National Electrical Code. The 2026 National Electrical Code book and Formula Page are provided at the test center. Candidates may not write, highlight, underline, or index on the provided references and may not use any other materials in the examination room.

The Colorado Journeyman Wireman examination includes 90 scored items with 240 minutes of testing time. The passing standard listed in the candidate information is 70%, which equals 63 correct scored items. The exam may also include up to 10 non-scored items, with additional time added for those items. Non-scored items are used for exam development and do not count toward the final pass or fail result.

The Colorado Journeyman Wireman content outline includes the following exam areas: general electrical knowledge, services, feeders and branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies, special equipment, electrical devices, motors, transformers, photovoltaics, and electrical calculations. ## Licensing Steps

Colorado journeyman electrician licensing is handled through the Colorado State Electrical Board within the Division of Professions and Occupations. Candidates pursuing the journeyman level should follow the state application process, document qualifying experience, complete the exam process, and submit licensing materials as required by the Board.

A typical licensing path includes gaining qualifying electrical experience, preparing the required documentation, applying through the Colorado licensing system, scheduling the examination through PSI after eligibility or registration requirements are met, taking the Colorado Journeyman Wireman exam, and completing the remaining state licensing requirements after examination.

Candidates should keep careful records of supervised electrical work experience, dates, categories of work, employer information, and supporting documentation. Experience documentation is an important part of electrical licensing, and incomplete or inaccurate records can slow down the licensing process.

## State Requirements

Colorado’s journeyman electrician license is associated with documented electrical construction wiring experience for light, heat, and power. Journeyman applicants are generally expected to document 8,000 hours of electrical construction wiring experience earned over no less than four years. A portion of that experience must include commercial or industrial work.

Colorado also has education requirements that may apply to apprentices based on registration date and state rules. Candidates should make sure their experience, education, and documentation align with Colorado State Electrical Board requirements before scheduling or completing licensing steps.

The Colorado electrical licensing process is state regulated, and the examination is one part of the overall path. Passing the exam alone does not automatically grant a license. Candidates must still meet the Board’s qualification standards, submit required documentation, and complete the licensing process through the proper state channels.
