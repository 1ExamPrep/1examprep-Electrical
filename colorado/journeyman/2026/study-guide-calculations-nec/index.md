## Exam Details

The Colorado Journeyman Wireman examination is administered through PSI for the Colorado State Electrical Board. The exam is computer-based and is designed to measure entry-level competence in the job task areas where a Colorado journeyman electrician is legally allowed to practice. Passing the examination is part of the licensing process, and applicants must also meet Colorado’s minimum qualifications for licensure.

The Colorado Journeyman Wireman examination is listed with **90 scored items**, **240 minutes** of testing time, and a required score of **70%**, which equals 63 correct scored items. The exam may also include up to **10 non-scored items** with an additional **30 minutes**. Non-scored items are used for examination development and do not count toward the final score.

The Journeyman Wireman content outline includes general electrical knowledge, services, feeders and branch circuits, grounding and bonding, overcurrent protection, conductors and cables, raceways and boxes, special occupancies, special equipment, electrical devices, motors, transformers, photovoltaics, and electrical calculations. These subjects cover both practical electrical work and code-based knowledge, making a balanced study plan important.

The listed exam content includes **11 questions** on general electrical knowledge, **9 questions** on services, feeders, and branch circuits, **14 questions** on grounding and bonding, **6 questions** on overcurrent protection, **8 questions** on conductors and cables, **5 questions** on raceways and boxes, **5 questions** on special occupancies, **6 questions** on special equipment, **5 questions** on electrical devices, **6 questions** on motors, **4 questions** on transformers, **2 questions** on photovoltaics, and **9 questions** on electrical calculations.

This range of exam topics shows why preparation should include more than reading code sections at random. ## Open Book Test

The Colorado Journeyman Wireman examination is a reference-based, open book exam. The PSI bulletin states that all examination questions are consistent with the National Electrical Code book and that the NEC code book and formula page are provided at the test center. Effective August 1, 2026, Colorado examination questions are listed as consistent with the 2026 National Electrical Code, with the 2026 NEC code book and formula page provided at the test center.

Open book testing does not mean the exam is easy. Candidates may not rely on the ability to look up every answer slowly. The exam is timed, and candidates need to understand how to identify the subject of a question, move through the code book efficiently, and apply the correct rule. ## Licensing Steps

Colorado journeyman electrician candidates begin by meeting the experience and education requirements established by the Colorado State Electrical Board. Applicants must document qualifying electrical experience and classroom education before pursuing licensure. The experience must be earned over the required time period and documented according to the Board’s application requirements.

After meeting the required qualifications, the applicant completes the licensing process through the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations. The applicant must submit the required application materials and documentation. The Board reviews the applicant’s qualifications as part of the licensing process.

PSI administers the examination program for Colorado electrical licensing. Candidates schedule and take the examination through PSI according to the testing procedures. The examination is administered by computer, and candidates receive score information after completing the exam.

After passing the examination, the applicant must file the license application with the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations. Passing the exam by itself does not automatically guarantee licensure. The applicant must still demonstrate that the minimum qualifications for licensure are satisfied under Colorado law.

A strong licensing plan should begin before the exam date. Candidates should organize work history documentation, classroom education records, and application materials early. Study should also begin early enough to cover major trade topics, calculations, and NEC navigation. Waiting until the exam is scheduled can make preparation feel rushed, especially for candidates who need additional practice with calculations or code lookup.

## State Requirements

Colorado Journeyman Electrician applicants must document **8,000 hours** of experience earned in no less than **4 years**. Of those hours, **2,000 hours** must be in commercial or industrial work. The experience requirement is intended to show that the applicant has developed practical electrical construction wiring knowledge under appropriate supervision.

Colorado also requires **288 hours of classroom education** for all journeyman electrician applicants. The education must be documented with a transcript or similar statement. Classroom education supports the technical side of electrical training, including safety, electrical theory, code knowledge, and related trade instruction.

Apprentices and other workers moving toward journeyman licensure should keep accurate records of employment, supervised work, and education. Proper documentation is important because the Board must be able to review and verify the applicant’s qualifications. Applicants should use the forms and application process required by the Colorado Division of Professions and Occupations.

Colorado also provides licensing by endorsement and reciprocity options under specific conditions. For journeyman reciprocity, Colorado participates in the National Electrical Reciprocal Alliance with certain states. Applicants using endorsement or reciprocity must satisfy the applicable Colorado requirements for current licensing, examination history, good standing, and documentation.

The Colorado journeyman electrician license is separate from electrical contractor registration. To perform electrical contracting in Colorado, an electrical contractor must be registered with the state and must hold or employ a licensed master electrician to serve as the responsible individual for the company.
