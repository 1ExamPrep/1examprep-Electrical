## Exam Details

Wyoming electrical licensing is handled through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety under the Wyoming State Fire Marshal. Candidates seeking licensure by examination must first apply with the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. After the licensing agency reviews the application and determines that the applicant is eligible to test, the candidate may schedule the examination through the ICC contractor/trades examination program.

The ICC Wyoming electrical bulletin lists the 701 Wyoming Master Electrician examination as a 100-question, multiple-choice exam with a 5-hour time limit. The bulletin identifies the exam as open book. The exam is delivered through Pearson VUE for the ICC contractor/trades examination program.

The Wyoming Master Electrician exam outline includes general knowledge, electrical theory and general electrical load calculations, code definitions and terms, plan reading, services and service equipment, service and fault current, service load calculations, service equipment, service conductors, transformers, system grounding and bonding, temporary service wiring, feeders, branch circuits and conductors, overcurrent protection, required outlets, GFCI and AFCI protection, wiring methods and materials, raceway installations, underground wiring methods, boxes and enclosures, cabinets, panelboards, switchboards, clearance and guarding, flexible cords and cable, equipment and devices, appliances, heating and cooling, control devices, motors and generators, special occupancies, swimming pools and similar installations, mobile home and RV parks, data processing equipment, miscellaneous special equipment, emergency systems, signaling systems, and fire alarm systems.

The current ICC outline gives the largest listed content weight to wiring methods and materials, followed by services and service equipment, branch circuits and conductors, general knowledge, and special occupancies, equipment, and conditions. Students should not ignore smaller categories, but a strong study routine should give extra attention to the areas that represent a larger share of the examination.

The Wyoming Master Electrician exam is not simply a code lookup test. The bulletin notes that candidates will not have time to look up every answer and must be familiar with the references. That makes preparation especially important. Students should practice locating code sections, reading questions carefully, applying formulas, using tables, and answering questions under timed conditions.

## Licensing Steps

The Wyoming master electrician licensing process begins with the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. A candidate must apply for licensure by examination before scheduling the Wyoming Master Electrician exam. The ICC bulletin states that candidates must first make a license application with the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. When the application is satisfactory, ICC is notified of the candidate’s eligibility to test.

Once the candidate receives approval from the licensing agency, the candidate may schedule the examination through Pearson VUE for the ICC contractor/trades examination program. The bulletin recommends allowing time after approval so the eligibility record can be processed before scheduling.

Wyoming’s licensing information states that a reciprocal master electrician applicant must meet Wyoming licensing requirements for master electricians, including holding a journeyman license for 4 years, 8,000 hours of on-the-job training as a journeyman electrician, and 4 years, or 576 hours, of apprenticeship schooling from an approved Department of Labor school. Applicants using the examination path should follow the Department’s current application instructions for documenting qualifications and eligibility.

After passing the examination, the applicant completes the licensing steps required by the Wyoming State Fire Marshal’s electrical licensing process. Wyoming’s licensing information states that after passing the examination, the applicant uploads the pass letter into the eLicense portal account and pays the initial license fee for the license type being issued.

A practical preparation path starts before the exam is scheduled. Candidates should review their eligibility, gather work documentation, submit the required application, wait for approval, schedule the exam, study the current exam outline, practice calculations, and spend time using the NEC so reference navigation becomes familiar before exam day.
