## Open Book Test

The Wyoming Master Electrician exam is listed as open book in the ICC Wyoming Electrical Contractor/Trades bulletin. Open book does not mean every answer can be found quickly without preparation. For open-book preparation, students should practice using the NEC in the same way they expect to use it during testing. ## Licensing Steps

Wyoming electrical licensing is administered by the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. Candidates seeking licensure by examination must begin with the state licensing process before scheduling the exam. The ICC bulletin states that candidates must first make a license application with the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety before scheduling a test through the examination program.

After the application is reviewed, approved candidates receive notification of eligibility. Once approved, the candidate may schedule the examination. The bulletin recommends allowing time after approval so the eligibility record can be processed before scheduling through the testing system.

Wyoming’s licensing page explains that applicants use the state’s online eLicense portal. The process includes creating an account, uploading required identification, completing the correct application, uploading required documents, electronically signing the application, and submitting it for review. Candidates receive email notification after the application has been processed.

After passing the exam, the applicant uploads the pass letter into the eLicense portal account and completes the state licensing process. Wyoming’s licensing information also explains that licenses are emailed rather than mailed, and applicants should monitor the email account connected to the licensing portal.

Students preparing for the Wyoming Master Electrician exam should begin studying well before the desired test date. A good study plan includes reviewing the exam content outline, working through practice questions, practicing code lookup with the NEC, reviewing missed answers, and dedicating extra time to weak areas such as load calculations, grounding and bonding, wiring methods, motors, transformers, or special occupancies.

## State Requirements

Wyoming regulates electrical licensing through the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. The state issues electrical licenses through its licensing system and publishes information for apprentices, technicians, journeyman electricians, master electricians, contractors, reciprocal licenses, license renewals, and licensing by examination.

For the Master Electrician exam path, Wyoming requires applicants to meet state licensing qualifications before exam approval. Wyoming’s licensing information for reciprocal Master Electrician applicants states that a Master Electrician applicant must meet Wyoming licensing requirements, including having held a Journeyman license for 4 years, completing 8,000 hours of on-the-job training as a Journeyman Electrician, and completing 4 years, or 576 hours, of apprenticeship schooling from an approved Department of Labor school.

Wyoming also states that its National Electrical Code is adopted without modification and that license examinations are based on the current edition of the National Electrical Code. This makes code knowledge central to the licensing process and to exam preparation.

Electrical work in Wyoming is tied to state licensing and supervision rules. The state licensing information explains that licensed journeyman or master electricians supervise apprentice electricians and that apprentice supervision ratios apply. These requirements are part of the broader licensing structure that supports safe, code-compliant electrical work throughout the state.
