## Licensing Steps

Wyoming candidates should begin by confirming that the Journeyman Electrician license is the correct license classification for their work goals. Wyoming also licenses master electricians, low voltage technicians, limited technicians, apprentices, and electrical contractors. Each license type has its own qualification rules, work authority, and application requirements.

A practical journeyman licensing path begins with gaining qualifying electrical wiring experience. Wyoming rules require a journeyman electrician applicant by examination to present evidence of a four-year, 8,000-hour apprenticeship or equivalent experience in the electrical wiring industry. The 8,000 hours must be obtained over a period of not less than four years and must be governed by minimum NEC standards under appropriate licensed supervision.

Candidates should keep accurate records of employment, dates worked, supervisors, license numbers when applicable, job duties, and the type of electrical wiring work performed. Accurate documentation matters because the state reviews the applicant’s experience as part of the licensing process.

After meeting the experience requirement, the candidate applies through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety licensing process. The state reviews the application and determines whether the candidate is eligible for examination. Candidates should submit required documents, pay applicable fees, and follow current state instructions for examination approval.

Once approved, the candidate schedules the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam through the current exam provider, prepares approved reference materials, brings required identification, follows testing rules, and completes the exam. After passing the exam and completing the remaining state requirements, the candidate receives the Wyoming journeyman electrician license through the state licensing process.
