Exam Details

Wyoming electrician licensing is administered through the Wyoming Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. The state licenses electrical workers at the journeyman and master levels, and candidates must follow the current Wyoming electrical licensing process before receiving a journeyman electrician license.

The Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam is commonly listed as the Wyoming 385 Journeyman Electrician examination. The exam is open book and includes 80 questions with a 4-hour time allowance. Candidates must earn a passing score of 70%. Open Book Test

The Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam is an open-book exam. Open-book testing does not mean candidates can rely on the code book alone. It means candidates must know how to locate the right information quickly, read the rule carefully, and apply it correctly under time pressure. A candidate who has not practiced code navigation may spend too much time searching and not enough time answering.

The official Wyoming journeyman electrician exam references have included the National Electrical Code and Ugly’s Electrical References. Candidates should bring only the references allowed for their specific exam appointment and should prepare those references according to the current exam-room rules. Code books and references should be clean, organized, and ready for use before test day.
