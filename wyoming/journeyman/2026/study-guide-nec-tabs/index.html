Licensing Steps

  1. Register and gain qualifying electrical experience. Wyoming journeyman applicants must document electrical wiring industry experience obtained under the minimum standards of the NEC and under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed master electrician.
  2. Complete the required work history. A journeyman electrician applicant by examination must present evidence of a four-year, 8,000-hour apprenticeship or equivalent experience in the electrical wiring industry. The 8,000 hours must be obtained over no less than four years.
  3. Complete classroom instruction. Wyoming requires evidence of 144 hours per year, or 576 hours over a four-year apprenticeship, of successfully completed electrically related classroom instruction through approved electrical apprenticeship training programs.
  4. Prepare documentation. Work experience should be documented through the forms or notarized employer letters required by the state. Candidates should keep records of employers, dates, hours, work categories, licenses, supervision, and classroom completion.
  5. Apply through the Wyoming licensing process. Applicants use the Wyoming State Fire Marshal public portal and follow the licensing-by-examination instructions for journeyman electricians.
  6. Receive authorization and schedule the exam. Wyoming electrical contractor and trades exams are administered through ICC and Pearson VUE. Candidates should follow the registration instructions provided after meeting state requirements.
  7. Study the NEC and exam references. Preparation should include NEC navigation, Ugly’s Electrical Reference practice, electrical theory, load calculations, services, feeders, branch circuits, wiring methods, equipment, motors, generators, and special occupancies.
  8. Pass the journeyman electrician exam. Wyoming journeyman electrician applicants must earn a passing score of 70%.
  9. Complete final licensing steps. After passing, the applicant follows Wyoming’s instructions for uploading the pass letter and completing the license process through the state portal.
  10. Maintain the license. Licensed electricians should follow all renewal, continuing education, and compliance requirements that apply to their Wyoming license classification.

State Requirements

Wyoming electrician licensing is administered through the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety. The state licenses apprentice electricians, journeyman electricians, master electricians, low-voltage technicians, limited technicians, and electrical contractors. Wyoming adopts the National Electrical Code without modification, and examinations for electrical licenses are based on the current edition enforced by the state and testing program.

A Wyoming journeyman electrician applicant by examination must present evidence of four years, or 8,000 hours, of apprenticeship or experience in the electrical wiring industry. The required experience must be obtained during a time frame of no less than four years. The work must be governed by NEC minimum standards and performed under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed master electrician.

Wyoming also requires classroom instruction for journeyman and master electrician applicants. The state requires 144 hours per year, or 576 hours over a four-year apprenticeship, of successfully completed electrically related classroom instruction through approved electrical apprenticeship training programs. If the applicant cannot provide 576 hours of classroom instruction, the applicant may qualify for the exam only by providing approved evidence of 10 years and 20,000 hours of on-the-job training experience as a licensed journeyman or master electrician.

Wyoming work experience must include residential, commercial, and industrial installation experience. No more than 75% of the time may be in any one category unless the Board waives that requirement in special circumstances. The state may also allow up to two years, or 4,000 hours, of credit toward work-history requirements for electrical school study exceeding the 576 hours of required classroom instruction.

Wyoming has reciprocal journeyman electrician license agreements with several states. Reciprocity can exempt a qualified applicant from the exam, but it does not remove all licensing requirements. Applicants using reciprocity must meet Wyoming’s reciprocal licensing rules, including requirements related to the state of original licensure, examination score, license standing, and license history.

Students should keep licensing compliance and exam preparation separate. This product helps with electrical study, NEC navigation, and exam-style preparation, while the candidate remains responsible for following Wyoming’s application process, documenting work history and classroom instruction, using approved exam references, and completing all licensing steps required by the state.
