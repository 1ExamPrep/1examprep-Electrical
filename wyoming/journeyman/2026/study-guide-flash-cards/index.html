Candidates still need to understand electrical concepts, recognize how questions are written, locate information quickly, apply code rules correctly, and manage time across 80 questions in 4 hours.

Open-book electrical exams reward reference familiarity. Students should practice using the National Electrical Code table of contents, index, article structure, definitions, tables, exceptions, notes, and common code sections before exam day. Licensing Steps

Wyoming journeyman electrician licensing begins with apprentice licensing, supervised electrical work, classroom instruction, and proper documentation. A person who has never held a Wyoming apprentice license must begin the licensing process within 10 days of starting employment. Apprentices should keep work history and classroom instruction records organized from the beginning of their training.

A typical Wyoming journeyman path includes obtaining an apprentice electrician license, working under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed master electrician, completing qualifying on-the-job experience, completing required classroom instruction, documenting residential, commercial, and industrial work categories, submitting the journeyman examination application, receiving approval, taking the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician exam, passing with the required score, uploading the pass letter into the licensing portal, paying the initial license fee, and receiving the Wyoming journeyman license.

A Wyoming journeyman electrician applicant by examination must present evidence of a four-year, 8,000-hour apprenticeship or experience in the electrical wiring industry. The 8,000 hours must be obtained during a period of not less than four years. The work must be governed by NEC minimum standards and performed under the direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or licensed master electrician.

Wyoming also requires electrically related classroom instruction. A journeyman electrician applicant must present evidence of 144 hours per year, or 576 hours over a four-year apprenticeship, of successfully completed electrically related classroom instruction from approved electrical apprenticeship training programs. If an applicant cannot provide the 576 classroom hours, a separate path may apply when the applicant provides evidence of 10 years and 20,000 hours of on-the-job training experience as a licensed journeyman or master electrician and receives approval from the Chief Electrical Inspector.

Electrical work experience is expected to include residential, commercial, and industrial installation experience. No more than 75 percent of the time may be in any one category, unless the Board waives that requirement in special circumstances. Wyoming may also grant credit toward work history requirements for certain accredited electrical school study exceeding the required 576 hours of classroom instruction.

State Requirements

Wyoming has statewide electrician licensing through the Department of Fire Prevention and Electrical Safety under the State Fire Marshal’s Office. The state adopts the National Electrical Code without modification, and electrical license examinations are based on the current edition of the NEC. This product is focused on the Wyoming Journeyman Electrician license.

For Wyoming journeyman electrician licensing by examination, candidates must document a four-year, 8,000-hour apprenticeship or equivalent electrical wiring industry experience, completed over not less than four years. Work must be performed under direct supervision of a licensed journeyman or master electrician and must meet NEC minimum standards. Candidates must also document 576 hours of electrically related classroom instruction through an approved apprenticeship training program, unless an alternate approved path applies.

Wyoming requires work verification documentation. Acceptable work history may include notarized letters from current or past employers, notarized letters from local unions, official letters from other licensing boards, or required employment verification forms that detail the duration and character of the work. Candidates should keep accurate records of employers, supervision, license numbers when applicable, work categories, hours, classroom instruction, certificates, application materials, exam approval notices, test results, and licensing documents.

Wyoming has reciprocal journeyman electrician license agreements with multiple states. Reciprocal licensing can exempt a qualifying applicant from the exam, but it does not remove all licensure requirements. Reciprocal applicants must meet Wyoming requirements, hold an eligible license obtained by examination with the required score, and provide the license verification information required by Wyoming.
