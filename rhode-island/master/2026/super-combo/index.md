## Licensing Steps

A practical Rhode Island licensing path begins by confirming the correct license category. Rhode Island electrical licensing includes journeyperson and contractor-level categories. Candidates pursuing a master-level or electrical contractor path should confirm the classification that matches the work, supervision, contracting responsibility, and business role they intend to perform.

Rhode Island electrician applications are handled through the Department of Labor and Training. Applicants submit a completed application, identification, fees, and required documentation. Applications that are incomplete or missing required documents may be returned, so candidates should prepare supporting documents carefully before submission.

Rhode Island electrician application instructions state that all Electrical Contractor tests require verification of experience in the respective trade for at least six years and possession of a Rhode Island Journeyperson’s license. The Electrical Journeyperson’s license must be valid for at least two years before applying for a contractor’s examination.

For journeyperson testing, Rhode Island application instructions state that applicants must verify at least four years of experience in the respective trade and 576 hours of related instruction approved by the Department of Labor and Training. Indentured apprenticeship applicants applying for journeyperson exams must provide the required apprenticeship completion documentation issued by the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training Apprenticeship Office.

After the application is submitted and reviewed, the candidate follows the Department’s examination scheduling process. Rhode Island trade exams are given on a first-come, first-served basis, and space is limited. Candidates should prepare before selecting an exam date so that study time is focused, organized, and aligned with the license being pursued.
