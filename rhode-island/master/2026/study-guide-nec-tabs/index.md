## Licensing Steps

The Rhode Island electrical licensing path begins with selecting the correct license category. Electricians commonly move through apprenticeship and journeyperson licensing before pursuing contractor-level qualification. Rhode Island’s contractor-level electrical path requires experience in the trade and possession of a valid Rhode Island journeyperson license for the required period before applying for a contractor examination.

Apprentices gain supervised field experience and classroom instruction through an approved apprenticeship path. Journeyperson candidates submit the required application materials, including apprenticeship completion documentation when applicable, and apply for the journeyperson examination through the Department of Labor and Training.

After becoming licensed as a journeyperson, the electrician may continue gaining experience toward contractor-level qualification. Rhode Island electrical contractor applicants must verify experience in the trade and hold a Rhode Island journeyperson license that has been valid for at least two years before applying for the contractor examination.

The applicant submits the required electrician application materials through the Department of Labor and Training. The application process may include trade experience documentation, licensing history, required forms, fees, identification, and additional materials required by the Board. After the application is reviewed and approved, the candidate proceeds to the examination step.

After passing the required exam and completing the licensing process, the licensee must continue to follow Rhode Island electrical laws, board rules, renewal requirements, continuing education requirements, permit rules, inspection requirements, and the scope of work authorized by the license held.
