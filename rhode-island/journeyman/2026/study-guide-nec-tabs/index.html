Rhode Island requires qualified candidates to pass the applicable electrical journeyperson examination before receiving a journeyperson electrician license. The exam is connected to the Rhode Island Board of Examiners of Electricians and the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training professional regulation process.

Rhode Island rules state that electrical examinations are conducted in writing and may include written and practical demonstrations. Licensing Steps

The Rhode Island journeyman electrician licensing path generally begins with documented electrical experience and related instruction. Electrical journeyperson applicants must meet Rhode Island’s experience and education requirements before sitting for the appropriate examination. Rhode Island application instructions identify electrical journeyperson tests as requiring verification of at least four years of experience in the trade and 576 hours of related instruction approved by the Department of Labor and Training.

After meeting the required experience and instruction standards, candidates submit the appropriate electrician application through the Rhode Island professional regulation process. The application process may require documentation of work experience, proof of related instruction, identification, and other supporting materials requested by the state. Once the application is accepted, the candidate can proceed through the state examination process.

After passing the required journeyman electrician examination, the candidate may complete the remaining licensing steps required by Rhode Island. A Rhode Island journeyperson electrician license allows the license holder to work within the scope of that credential under state rules. Candidates planning to later pursue an electrical contractor license should understand that contractor-level exams and requirements are separate from journeyperson licensing.
