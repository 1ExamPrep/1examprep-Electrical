## Licensing Steps

Rhode Island journeyperson electrician candidates typically begin by entering electrical apprenticeship or supervised electrical employment. Apprentices should work under proper supervision, complete required related instruction, and maintain documentation of job duties, dates of employment, and work experience.

The candidate must build at least four years of qualifying experience in the electrical trade and complete 576 hours of related instruction approved by the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training. Rhode Island indentured apprentices applying for journeyperson testing must provide the Certificate of Completion of Apprenticeship issued by the Department of Labor and Training Apprenticeship Office.

After meeting the experience and instruction requirements, the candidate submits the electrician application materials to the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training, Division of Professional Regulation. The application instructions require supporting documentation, including employer statements on company letterhead describing work-related experience. Applicants should make sure the application is complete, signed, notarized where required, and submitted with the required processing fee.

After the application is reviewed and approved, the candidate receives examination instructions. The candidate should review the current Rhode Island exam information, study material guidance, identification requirements, exam location instructions, and any rules issued by the Board before the test date.

After passing the examination, the candidate completes the remaining licensing requirements through the Rhode Island Department of Labor and Training. Passing the exam is an important step, but the licensing process is not complete until all state requirements are satisfied and the journeyperson electrician license is issued.
