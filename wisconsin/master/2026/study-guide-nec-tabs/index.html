Open Book Test

The Wisconsin DSPS Master Electrician exam information states that the exam is open book. DSPS also states that the passing score is 70%. Because reference materials are allowed under state rules, students should spend time learning how to use their code book efficiently before exam day.

DSPS provides specific reference-material rules for trades exams. Printed notes are allowed when they are three-hole punched and placed in a binder. Printed code may only be brought into the exam if it is bound together in a three-ring binder. Practice exams and previous exams do not qualify as notes and are not allowed. Tabs are acceptable when they come with a bound code book, and three-ring paper dividers with tabs are permitted. Loose papers, removable tabs, sticky notes, and paperclips are not allowed.

DSPS states that Pearson VUE and DSPS exam locations allow three reference item categories for the Master Electrician exam: one binder containing Wisconsin Administrative Code SPS 305, Wisconsin Administrative Code SPS 316, and printed notes; the National Electrical Codebook or Handbook edition listed by DSPS; and up to two printed, bound reference books such as common electrical reference books.

Students using this combo should treat the open-book format as a skill to practice. Fast code lookup comes from repetition. Candidates should practice moving through definitions, general requirements, wiring and protection, wiring methods, equipment rules, special occupancies, special equipment, and tables until the structure of the code book feels familiar.

The tabs included with the NEC 2026 paperback support this type of preparation by making major sections easier to locate during study. Students can use the tabbed code book to build a repeatable process: read the question, identify the subject, locate the correct article, confirm the rule, and apply the answer carefully.

Licensing Steps

Wisconsin trades credentials are handled through DSPS and its online licensing platform, LicensE. DSPS states that initial, reinstatement, and renewal applications for trades credentials must be completed through LicensE.

A typical path for a Wisconsin Master Electrician candidate begins with reviewing the DSPS Master Electrician licensing page and application information. Candidates should confirm the application form or online application pathway that applies to their credential, gather the required information, submit the application through the state’s licensing system, and follow DSPS instructions for examination and credential completion.

DSPS lists Master Electrician License Application Information as Form 3107 and also provides Trades LicensE Information for applicants using the online platform. Candidates with convictions or pending charges may be directed to submit additional trades-related conviction and pending-charge information when applicable.

After the application process is underway, candidates should review the DSPS exam information page and trades examination resources. Exam preparation should begin early enough to allow time for code review, calculation practice, and full practice exams. Master-level preparation often requires repeated review across several electrical subjects, especially for candidates who have not taken a licensing exam recently.

Once licensed, Wisconsin electricians are responsible for following applicable state laws, administrative rules, permit requirements, and licensing requirements. DSPS states that a person who is responsible as a licensed Master Electrician for electrical wiring installation, repair, or maintenance must use appropriately licensed or registered individuals to construct, install, repair, or maintain electrical wiring.

State Requirements

Wisconsin regulates electrician licensing through the Department of Safety and Professional Services. DSPS publishes profession pages, application information, renewal information, exam information, statutes, administrative code resources, and trades licensing guidance.

Wisconsin law provides that a person may not install, repair, or maintain electrical wiring unless the person is licensed as an electrician by the department or enrolled as a registered electrician by the department. DSPS also states that, for electrical installations requiring a uniform building permit under the applicable Wisconsin administrative code, a person holding a Master Electrician license may not commence installation of electrical wiring until a permit is issued for the installation.

For Master Electrician candidates, DSPS exam information confirms that the exam is open book and that a passing score is 70%. DSPS also provides exam reference-material rules that candidates must follow at Pearson VUE and DSPS exam locations.
