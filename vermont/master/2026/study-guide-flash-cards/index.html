Exam Details

The Vermont Master Electrician licensing process is overseen by the Vermont Electricians’ Licensing Board through the Department of Public Safety, Division of Fire Safety. Applicants must meet the eligibility requirements for master electrician licensure and pass an examination to the satisfaction of the Board.

Vermont law states that an applicant for a master electrician license must have been licensed as a journeyman electrician for at least 2 years, or have comparable experience and training acceptable to the Board, and must pass the required examination. After successful completion of the examination and payment of the required fee, the applicant may receive a master electrician license.

A person licensed in Vermont as a master electrician is entitled to design, install, repair, maintain, and replace electrical installations, including lightning rods, fire alarms, and fire detection systems. A master electrician may also employ other licensed individuals or electrician’s helpers to perform electrical installation work under the master electrician’s direction.
