Exam Details

The Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam is designed to measure the knowledge needed to perform electrical installations under the direction of a master electrician and to supervise an apprentice electrician or electrician’s helper working under the master electrician’s direction. The exam covers a broad range of electrical knowledge, with emphasis on code application, safe installation practices, wiring systems, electrical equipment, and local requirements.

The Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam has been listed with 90 questions and a 4-hour time allowance. Subject areas include general electrical theory and principles, wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, motors and controls, equipment for general use, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, and local amendments. Because the exam includes both broad trade knowledge and code-based content, candidates benefit from studying the NEC structure as well as the practical electrical principles behind the questions.

State Requirements

Vermont recognizes the journeyman electrician license for individuals who perform electrical installations under the direction of a master electrician. A licensed journeyman may also supervise an apprentice electrician or electrician’s helper employed by a master electrician under that master electrician’s direction. This makes the journeyman license an important step for electricians who are building professional responsibility, field leadership, and long-term licensing goals.

Vermont journeyman electrician candidates should be prepared to document qualifying electrical experience or approved training according to state requirements. The state’s licensing framework includes apprentices, journeyman electricians, master electricians, and specialty electrical classifications. Candidates pursuing the journeyman credential should focus on the requirements that apply specifically to the journeyman electrician license and the exam associated with that classification.

Because electrical licensing is tied to public safety, the exam emphasizes knowledge that supports safe, code-compliant electrical work. Candidates should understand wiring and protection, wiring methods and materials, equipment rules, motors and controls, special occupancies, special equipment, special conditions, electrical theory, and Vermont-specific requirements. Test Information and Study Materials

The Vermont Journeyman Electrician exam outline includes several major electrical content areas.
